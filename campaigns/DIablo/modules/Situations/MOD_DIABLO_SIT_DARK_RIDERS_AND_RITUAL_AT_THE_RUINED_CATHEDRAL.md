# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_SIT_DARK_RIDERS_AND_RITUAL_AT_THE_RUINED_CATHEDRAL: Dark Riders and Ritual at the Ruined Cathedral

Townsfolk report an invasion by dark riders and strange rituals in the ruined cathedral at the edge of town, but the true nature of the evil is not yet clear.

### Dramatic Premise
A witness who saw something terrible but cannot name it + a threat that is only rumor = a party that must investigate before it can act

### Core Premise
The evil haunting the village is not immediately clear in nature. All the town can say is that dark riders have invaded and strange rituals are happening in the ruined cathedral at the edge of town. The module is about turning fearful hearsay into understanding.

### GM Placement & Synergies
Use it as a clue layer within the opening arrival or as a follow-up in town. It consumes the villagers' willingness to talk and feeds the descent into the dungeon with a sense of mystery. Arrival goes well when the party compares accounts, notices the gaps, and enters the cathedral with questions rather than a ready answer. It goes badly if the GM resolves the mystery early, or if the party writes off the rumors as flavor.

### 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Physical environment:** A ruined cathedral at the edge of town, a place of strange rituals.
- **Behaviors:** An anxious, dwindling populace speaks of invasion by dark riders and of rituals.
- **Relationships:** Townsfolk are bound by shared fear, and the riders and ritualists are known only by hearsay.
- **Attitudes:** Anxiety and uncertainty. The evil is not immediately clear in nature.
- **Values:** Safety and the need to understand what is hunting them.

### Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The anxious townsfolk):** Something is invading and the cathedral is at the heart of it. *Leverage:* They saw and heard the signs and can point the party toward the cathedral. *Blind spot:* Their reports are fearful, secondhand and possibly contradictory.
* **Main opponent (The dark riders):** The town is theirs to invade. *Leverage:* They have already driven the populace to fear and shrinkage. *Blind spot:* Their nature and goals are unknown, so the party may misjudge them.
* **Thematic shadow (The cathedral ritualists):** The ruined sacred place can still be turned to a dark purpose. *Leverage:* They control the unknown ritual, and the cathedral's holy ruin gives it weight. *Blind spot:* Their rites are only rumor, so no one knows whether they are the cause or a symptom.
* **Tertiary outcast (A witness who saw too much):** Telling the truth will only bring the riders sooner. *Leverage:* They may hold the one detail that clarifies the evil. *Blind spot:* Fear keeps them silent or unreliable.

### Output Dial
- **Level 1:** Whispers of riders and odd rites circulate, and the town is uneasy but still functioning.
- **Level 2:** Several townsfolk give overlapping but incomplete accounts, and the cathedral becomes the obvious focus.
- **Level 3:** The rumors harden into fear, people avoid the cathedral edge of town, and misreadings of the evil start to cause harm.
- **Level 4:** The riders and the ritual are no longer rumor but present horror, and the town has no clear answer for what it faces.

### Threat Clocks
- **The evil stays unnamed [4 Ticks]:** advances when The party acts on rumor without investigating or comparing accounts

### Yields
A working hypothesis about the evil, a lead to the cathedral, and local trust when the party shares what it finds.

### Standalone Fallback
Run it as a mystery seed. The party hears conflicting accounts of riders and ritual, then must decide what the evil really is by investigating the cathedral.
