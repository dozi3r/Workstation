# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_STONE_CURSE: Stone Curse

A high-end spell that petrifies enemies over an area, even without line of sight, so they cannot flee and shatter under a few weapon blows.

### What It Does
Stone Curse is the most effective way to keep enemies from running away. It works on an area, and no line of sight is needed. Targets are petrified for a time. Once stoned, a few whacks with any weapon on hand is usually all it takes to reduce the dark ones to rubble. It can also be tried as a ranged attack against Balrogs, though that is described as a matter of luck.

### Source & Teachers
The card does not say who teaches it. It is one of the upper-end spells a Warrior is told to look for as books in the dungeon, and it is a Sorcerer spell that the Rogue can also cast.

### Costs & Limits
The caster must be close, and the petrification is temporary. Because of this, it is best paired with something that finishes the targets while they are stoned.

### Lore
The guide calls Stone Curse and a decent bow a deadly combination for the Rogue. Paired with the Golem, it is perhaps the only way for a Sorcerer to triumph over Diablo's minions on Hell difficulty.
