# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_WARRIOR_REPAIR_SKILL: Warrior Repair Skill

The Warrior's innate skill of repairing weapons and armor in the field, measured by an item's Durability.

### What It Does
Repairs weapons and armor in the field, measured by the item's Durability number. The skill grows with each level of experience, so the more experienced the Warrior, the more effective the repairs.

### Source & Teachers
An innate ability of the Warrior; no teacher is named.

### Costs & Limits
Repairs lower the upper-end Durability number of the item, and an item vanishes when its Durability reaches zero. Repair is therefore a lasting trade-off, not a free restoration.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Warrior
- **Cost narrative:** Each repair lowers the item's upper-end Durability, and an item vanishes at zero.
- **Range note:** Touch-level work on one item.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Reliably useful for keeping gear working, but it only slows decline.
- **Range: Low** (judged). Works on an item the Warrior handles.
- **Cost: Medium** (stated). Each repair lowers the item's maximum Durability, a lasting but gradual price.

### Standalone Fallback
A fighter can patch up weapons and armor in the field, but each item wears down for good over time and vanishes when it has nothing left.
