# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_HEAL_OTHER: Heal Other

A multiplayer spell that heals a weary comrade. The caster highlights the target, then right-clicks to restore their health.

### What It Does
Heal Other restores health to another character. The caster highlights the target to see their current vitality, then right-clicks to heal.

### Source & Teachers
It is available in multiplayer only and is learned from a spell book. It requires a fairly high level of magical aptitude.

### Costs & Limits
It draws on the caster's mana, and the price grows with the spell's mastery. Extra book study increases the healing and lowers the mana cost a little.

### Facts
- **Cost narrative:** Costs mana, with the price rising with spell mastery; further study improves healing and eases the cost.
- **Range note:** Within sight, since the target must be highlighted.
