# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_INFRAVISION: Infravision

Lets the caster see through walls, showing lurking monsters in a reddish hue. It is seldom a significant tactical advantage, except when lining up a Wall of Fire. Scroll only.

### What It Does
The Infravision spell allows the caster to see through walls, displaying lurking monsters in a reddish hue.

### Source & Teachers
It is found on scrolls only.

### Costs & Limits
It is seldom a significant tactical advantage. It helps mainly when lining up a Wall of Fire.

### Facts
- **Range note:** Reveals monsters behind walls nearby.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Low** (stated). Seldom a significant tactical advantage, useful mainly in specific situations.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). It shows monsters beyond walls, within a scene.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). The card gives no cost.

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a one-off scouting charm that reveals hidden creatures behind walls as red shapes. It helps most when the caster plans an area attack.
