# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_SORCERER_STAFF_RECHARGING: Sorcerer Staff Recharging

The Sorcerer's innate ability to recharge staves, analogous to the Warrior's repair skill.

### What It Does
The Sorcerer possesses the ability to recharge staves, an ability analogous to the Warrior's repair. It improves with experience.

### Source & Teachers
An innate ability of the Sorcerer; no teacher is named.

### Costs & Limits
As with the Warrior's repair, the upper-end Charges number lowers with use, so a staff holds fewer charges as it is recharged. The card names no other cost.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer
- **Cost narrative:** The upper-end Charges number is lowered by the process.
- **Range note:** Applies to a staff in hand.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Keeps staves useful for a caster, a niche but reliable benefit.
- **Range: Low** (judged). Applies to a staff the Sorcerer holds.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). The upper-end Charges number lowers with experience-linked recharging; the card is otherwise silent, so this is judged.

### Standalone Fallback
A spellcaster can restore the charges of a magical staff, though each recharge leaves the staff able to hold less than before.
