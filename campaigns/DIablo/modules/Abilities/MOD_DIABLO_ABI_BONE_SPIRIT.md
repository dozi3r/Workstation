# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_BONE_SPIRIT: Bone Spirit

A highest-tier Monster Class spell that strips a large share of a target's remaining vitality with each strike. After a few shots even a formerly tough foe falls quickly. It costs Health as well as Mana.

### What It Does
The Bone Spirit spell reduces a target's Hit Points by a third every time it strikes. After two or three shots even a formerly tough foe can be finished quickly.

### Source & Teachers
It is a highest-tier spell of the Monster Class.

### Costs & Limits
Casting it costs the caster Health as well as Mana, a real price for the damage it deals.

### Facts
- **Cost narrative:** The caster pays in both Health and Mana for each casting.
- **Range note:** Strikes a chosen target; range not stated.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (stated). Each strike takes a large share of the target's remaining vitality, and a few strikes finish a tough foe.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). It strikes a target, but the card gives no range.
- **Cost: High** (stated). It consumes the caster's Health as well as Mana.

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a costly spirit-bolt that gnaws away a big portion of a foe's remaining strength per hit, paid for in the caster's own blood and magic.
