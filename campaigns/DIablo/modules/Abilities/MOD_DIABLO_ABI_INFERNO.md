# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_INFERNO: Inferno

A high-level fire spell whose area and efficiency grow with the caster's experience, far more impressive for a Sorcerer than a Warrior.

### What It Does
Inferno is a fire spell. Combat spells grow in area of effect and mana efficiency as they increase in level, and a high-level Sorcerer's Inferno covers a large area.

### Source & Teachers
It is found as spell books in the dungeon. The Warrior is told to keep an eye out for the books of Stone Curse, Chain Lightning, Inferno, Teleport, and Flame Wave. No teacher is named.

### Lore
The guide stresses how different the spell is in the hands of a Sorcerer and a Warrior who have both advanced a long way. The Sorcerer's version is far more impressive.

### Facts
- **School:** Fire
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer; Warrior
- **Cost narrative:** Costs mana, becoming more efficient as the caster advances.
- **Range note:** Area grows with the caster's advancement; no distance is stated.
