# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_ROGUE_TRAP_DISARMING: Rogue Trap Disarming

The Rogue's innate skill of identifying and disarming traps, specifically booby-trapped chests and doors.

### What It Does
Identifies and disarms booby-trapped chests and doors. A trapped chest shows up as such on the ground when the Rogue highlights it. "SELECt YOUR^DiSARJIlinG SKILL AS YOU WOULD A SPELL, AnD 'CASf' it On tHE OFFEnDinG OBJECf."

### Source & Teachers
An innate ability of the Rogue; no teacher is named. It is selected like a spell.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Rogue
- **Range note:** Targets a single trapped chest or door.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Reliably decisive in its niche of dealing with traps, but narrow.
- **Range: Low** (judged). Works on an offending object the Rogue targets; no wider reach is stated.
- **Cost: Low** (judged). The card names no cost, so it is rated low as a judgement.

### Standalone Fallback
A nimble specialist can spot and defuse trapped chests and doors before they spring, making a dungeon's locked prizes safer to open.
