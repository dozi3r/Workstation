A high-end Sorcerer offensive spell that does not dissipate over distance and so works well at range.

### What It Does
Elemental is one of the high-end spells the Sorcerer uses occasionally as a late-game offense. The guide names it, with Fireball, as a spell that works quite nicely at range because it does not dissipate over distance.

### Source & Teachers
