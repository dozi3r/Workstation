# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_CHAIN_LIGHTNING: Chain Lightning

A high-end lightning spell the Sorcerer relies on as the best defense later in the game, found as books in the dungeon.

### What It Does
Chain Lightning is an offensive high-end spell of the lightning school. Bursts of it can be used as a ranged attack against Balrogs.

### Source & Teachers
It is found as spell books in the dungeon. A Warrior is told to keep an eye out for the books of Stone Curse, Chain Lightning, Inferno, Teleport, and Flame Wave. The Sorcerer casts it.

### Costs & Limits
It is mana-hungry.

### Lore
The Sorcerer uses it, along with Stone Curse, Flame Wave and the occasional dose of Elemental, as the best defense later in the game.

### Facts
- **School:** Lightning
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer
- **Cost narrative:** Draws heavily on the caster's mana.
- **Range note:** Ranged bursts; no distance is given.
