# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_PHASE: Phase

Teleports the caster in a random direction. It is hard to aim and wasteful of Mana, so the guide recommends it for leaping across dungeon sections or escaping swarms.

### What It Does
The Phase spell teleports the caster in a random direction.

### Costs & Limits
The direction is random, so it is poor for reaching a specific spot. It is also wasteful of Mana. The guide recommends it for leaping across dungeon sections or escaping swarms.

### Facts
- **Cost narrative:** Wasteful of mana, with no control over where the caster lands.
- **Range note:** A random direction; distance not stated.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Reliably useful for escape, but unpredictable.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). Moves the caster within a scene; no distance is given.
- **Cost: Medium** (stated). Described as wasteful of Mana, but not as a lasting price.

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a panic jump that throws the caster somewhere nearby and unpredictable. It is good for escaping a crowd or skipping a stretch of a dungeon.
