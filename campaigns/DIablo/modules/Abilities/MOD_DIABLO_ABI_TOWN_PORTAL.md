# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_TOWN_PORTAL: Town Portal

A spell that opens a gateway between the dungeon and a spot just outside Tristram's town square. It lets the caster return to town quickly and come back to the exact point they left.

### What It Does
Town Portal is the quickest route back to town short of taking the stairs. It opens a direct gateway to a spot just outside Tristram's town square. The trip runs both ways. It allows access to the town, then a return to the dungeon from the exact point where the spell was cast. It is useful for all classes, and the guide calls it an indispensable way to return to town without a lot of tedious leg-work.

### Source & Teachers
Adria the Witch keeps a steady supply of Town Portal spells. They are always available in scroll form from her, which makes the Town Portal the primary means of travel between the dungeon and Tristram.

### Costs & Limits
The spell is used in scroll form, so each casting uses up a scroll bought from Adria. Extra scrolls found in the dungeon can pile up. The guide advises throwing them on the ground in Tristram near where the portal opens and picking them up on the next return to town.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Adria the Witch (sells scrolls)
- **Cost narrative:** Each use spends a scroll bought from Adria the Witch. Spare scrolls can be stashed on the ground in Tristram.
- **Range note:** Links the dungeon caster's position to a spot just outside Tristram's town square, and back to the exact casting point.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). It reliably solves the problem of getting back to town, but it does nothing in a fight.
- **Range: High** (judged). It links the dungeon to the town and back, crossing the whole distance between them.
- **Cost: Low** (judged). Scrolls are always available from Adria and the card names no other price.

### Related Modules
- `MOD_DIABLO_SIT_TRISTRAM`

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a reliable escape-and-resupply gate in any delve. The portal leads to a safe hub and returns the party to the exact spot where it was opened.
