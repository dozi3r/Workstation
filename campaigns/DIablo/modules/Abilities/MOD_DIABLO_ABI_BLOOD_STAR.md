# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_BLOOD_STAR: Blood Star

A highest-tier Monster Class spell that works on any monster without Magic resistance. A hit takes a fixed share of the caster's Magic from the foe's vitality rather than rolling a range of harm, and casting costs Health as well as Mana.

### What It Does
The Blood Star Spell whips any monster without Magic resistance. Rather than a range of damage, a hit reduces the foe's hit points by a third of the caster's Magic characteristic number, so its strength follows the caster's magical attainment.

### Costs & Limits
Casting it costs Health as well as Mana, so the caster pays in vitality for every use. It is ineffective on monsters with Magic resistance.

### Facts
- **School:** Monster Class spell
- **Cost narrative:** Paid in both Health and Mana on each casting.
- **Counters:** Monsters with Magic resistance [arcane]

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (judged). A highest-tier spell that reliably harms any non-resistant monster in proportion to the caster's Magic.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). The card gives no range; it is treated as a targeted spell within sight.
- **Cost: High** (stated). Casting consumes Health as well as Mana.

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a costly signature spell: the caster pays in both Mana and Health to strike any foe that lacks magical resistance, with the harm tied to the caster's own magical strength rather than to chance.
