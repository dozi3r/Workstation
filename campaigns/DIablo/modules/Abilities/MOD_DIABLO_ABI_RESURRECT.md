# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_RESURRECT: Resurrect

A multiplayer-only spell, available through staff and scroll, that raises a dead ally who returns barely alive.

### What It Does
Raises a dead ally. The raised character returns with only one Hit Point, though further book study is noted to give more.

### Source & Teachers
Available from scroll and staff only, and only in multiplayer. "Just in case, Pepin always has a Resurrect scroll in his inventory during multiplayer games."

### Costs & Limits
The spell leaves the raised ally nearly dead. "Using the spell only gives the raised character one Hit Point, so don't do it in hostile territory unless you're prepared to cast." It also takes a considerable outlay of Mana and a hard-to-find scroll or staff.

### Facts
- **Cost narrative:** A real Mana outlay, plus the risk of leaving the revived ally nearly dead in danger.
- **Range note:** Targets a dead ally; no range is stated.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (judged). Returns a dead ally to play, which can turn the course of a fight, though the ally is left fragile.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). Targets a fallen ally in the scene; the card gives no range.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). Its Mana outlay is notable and the result is fragile, but the card describes no lasting price.
