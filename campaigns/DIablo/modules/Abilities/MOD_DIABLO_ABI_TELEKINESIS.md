# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_TELEKINESIS: Telekinesis

A spell that lets the caster manipulate distant objects with the mind. The Rogue uses it to open doors from afar and fire arrows through the opening.

### What It Does
This spell allows the caster to manipulate distant objects with the power of the mind. It opens chests, sarcophagi and doors, and it pushes monsters back. The Rogue can open doors with Telekinesis and fire arrows through the portal from a distance.

### Source & Teachers
The card names the Rogue as the caster. It gives no teacher.

### Costs & Limits
The spell needs a fair measure of magical aptitude and spends mana on each use. Its effect is utility and displacement, not damage. The Sorcerer is advised to use Teleport rather than Telekinesis to escape crowds.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Rogue
- **Cost narrative:** Mana is spent each time, and the caster needs enough magical aptitude to learn it.
- **Range note:** Works on distant objects, so doors can be opened from afar.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Low** (judged). It opens things and pushes monsters back, which is situational help rather than a decisive effect.
- **Range: Medium** (stated). It works on distant objects within sight, so the Rogue can act from a distance.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). It has a mana cost and a magic requirement. The card gives no other price.
