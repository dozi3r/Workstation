# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_NOVA: Nova

A devastating radial Lightning attack found only on scrolls and staffs. It is so powerful that even a Sorcerer cannot learn it from a book.

### What It Does
Nova is a radial Lightning attack. It strikes outward from the caster and is described as devastating.

### Source & Teachers
It is available only from scrolls and staffs. It cannot be learned from a book, even by a Sorcerer.

### Costs & Limits
The main limit is access. It cannot be learned and exists only in scroll or staff form, so the user needs the right item.

### Lore
It is so powerful that even a Sorcerer cannot learn it from a book.

- **School:** Lightning
- **Cost narrative:** Needs a scroll or staff as its source, since it cannot be learned from a book.
- **Range note:** Radial, spreading outward from the caster.
