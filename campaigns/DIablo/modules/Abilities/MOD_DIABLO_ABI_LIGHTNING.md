# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_LIGHTNING: Lightning

A Sorcerer spell that does not dissipate over distance and outranges Poison Spitter spitting, but is useless against lightning-immune demons.

### What It Does
Lightning is a single-target stream whose bolt grows longer as the spell advances. It does not dissipate over distance, so the Sorcerer can use it to outdistance the spitting of Poison Spitters. Chain lightning bursts are a related use.

### Source & Teachers
It is on the Sorcerer's spell list. No teacher is named.

### Costs & Limits
It costs mana, and extra books reduce the cost. It has no effect on lightning-immune creatures such as Red Storm, so a Sorcerer needs an alternative against them.

### Lore
The guide notes that Lightning provides unlimited range, though poison balls fall to the ground after a certain distance.
