# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_HEALING: Healing

A restorative spell cast by the Sorcerer that heals the caster at the cost of Mana. Best used outside combat, with stockpiled scrolls covering battle.

### What It Does
Heals the caster and restores Health. Further study of the spell's book improves the healing while lowering the Mana it demands.

### Source & Teachers
The spell is a Sorcerer's practice, learned from a book. The card names Pepin and Adria as sources of Scrolls of Healing, which the Sorcerer is advised to stockpile for use in battle.

### Costs & Limits
It costs Mana. Burning Mana during combat is not advised for Sorcerers, who should use stockpiled Scrolls of Healing in battle and cast with Mana outside combat. The guide notes that "Generally, it's best to go that route, and heal with Mana when you're not in the heat of battle." The spell becomes Mana-efficient for the Sorcerer at some point, which makes the choice more drastic.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer
- **Cost narrative:** Paid in Mana; costly enough in battle that scrolls are preferred there, and it grows cheaper with further book study.
- **Range note:** Heals the caster only.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Reliably restores Health for its caster, a decisive niche role, though it is not described as turning fights.
- **Range: Low** (stated). It heals the caster only.
