# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_MANA_SHIELD: Mana Shield

A protective spell, learned from a book or bought as a scroll, that lets magical reserves absorb harm in place of life.

### What It Does
Mana Shield allows the caster to utilize Mana points as Hit Points, drawing off of Mana instead of health when damage is sustained in combat. For every level of Mana Shield, the damage taken is reduced. For a Sorcerer it is effectively essential. With it, a large magical reserve can stand in for a small store of life, which is why vitality matters to Sorcerers only early in the game.

### Source & Teachers
It is learned from a book of Mana Shield, or bought as a Mana Shield Scroll sold by Adria the Witch in Tristram. The Sorcerer and the Rogue both use it, and the guide advises buying a scroll whenever one appears in the Witch's inventory and counting yourself lucky to find a book.

### Costs & Limits
The cost is mana, drawn in place of health. The spell wears off when the caster moves between levels or returns to town, so it has to be recast before every significant chunk of adventuring. Without the spell itself, scrolls are a constant need.

### Lore
The guide calls it almost as important for the Rogue as for the Sorcerer. Experience poured into Magic early can pay off greatly through Mana Shield.

### Facts
- **School:** Protective magic
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer; Rogue
- **Cost narrative:** Damage is paid from mana instead of health, and the spell must be recast after moving between levels or going to town.
- **Range note:** Self only.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
