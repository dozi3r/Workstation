# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_CHARGED_BOLT: Charged Bolt

A lightning spell that produces multiple projectiles which bounce off walls. It is a poor choice against lightning-resistant creatures, which it may slow but probably will not kill.

### What It Does
This spell produces multiple lightning projectiles, which bounce off the walls until they strike a foe or eventually dissipate. A further book adds more bolts as the caster improves.

### Costs & Limits
The spell asks for only modest magical aptitude and a small amount of mana. Its main limit is lightning resistance. If you throw a Charged Bolt at a lightning-resistant creature, you might slow it down, but you probably won't kill it.
