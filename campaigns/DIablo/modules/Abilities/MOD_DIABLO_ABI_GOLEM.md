# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_GOLEM: Golem

A summoned stone ally that hunts enemies. The Sorcerer sends it into a room to draw attacks so that Stone Curse can be placed on the attackers.

### What It Does
The Golem spell gives you a helper who'll seek out your enemies and destroy them. The Sorcerer also uses it as a decoy. Send your Golem into a room and watch for him to come under attack. Then cast Stone Curse over the area of the magic attack until the magic stops. This combination is perhaps the only way for a Sorcerer to triumph over Diablo's minions on Hell difficulty.

### Source & Teachers
The Sorcerer casts it. The card names no teacher.

### Costs & Limits
Only one Golem can exist at a time. A second summons replaces the old one. A Golem's toughness is based on the caster's mana and spell skill.

### Facts
- **School:** summoning
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer
- **Cost narrative:** Mana is spent to summon it. Only one Golem exists at a time, and its toughness depends on the caster's mana and spell skill.
- **Range note:** Sent ahead into a room, where it seeks out enemies on its own.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (stated). The card calls its combination with Stone Curse perhaps the only way to beat Diablo's minions on the hardest difficulty.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). The Golem is sent into a room ahead of the caster and watched from a distance.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). It uses mana and only one can exist at a time. The card names no lasting price.

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a single summoned decoy and hunter that the caster sends ahead. It pulls enemy attention so the caster can act on the enemies while they are distracted.
