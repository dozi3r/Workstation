# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_FIREBALL: Fireball

A Sorcerer spell that does not dissipate over distance, so it works well at range.

### What It Does
Fireball is one of the Sorcerer's ranged spells. It works well because it does not dissipate over distance.

### Source & Teachers
It belongs to the Sorcerer's spell list. The guide ranks it as a good option next to the mostly lightning-based spells, with Elemental.

### Facts
- **Practitioners:** Sorcerer
- **Range note:** Does not dissipate over distance, so it suits long-range use.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Described as working 'quite nicely', so reliable but not singled out as overwhelming.
- **Range: Medium** (judged). It keeps its force over distance, but the card gives no reach beyond line of sight.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). The card says nothing about its cost.

### Standalone Fallback
Use it as a long-range blast that a spellcaster can hurl across a room or cavern without losing force. It needs no setting beyond a caster and a distant foe.
