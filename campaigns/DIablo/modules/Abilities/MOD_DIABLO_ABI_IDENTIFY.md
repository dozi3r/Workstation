# MODULE MOD_DIABLO_ABI_IDENTIFY: Identify

A spell, available only through scroll or staff, that reveals the nature of magical objects, which may help or harm their owner.

### What It Does
This important spell determines the nature of magical objects: not all magical items assist your persona, and some may even cause you harm.

### Source & Teachers
It comes from scroll and staff only. Adria sells it, and she generally carries a Scroll of Identify. The guide advises buying them whenever they appear, so the player need not return to Tristram to check an item. Cain the Storyteller is also tied to the service.

### Costs & Limits
It cannot be learned from a book, so it depends on finding or buying scrolls and staves. The card gives it no Mana cost.

### Facts
- **Cost narrative:** No Mana is spent; the price is obtaining a scroll or staff.
- **Range note:** Applied to a single magical object.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: Medium** (judged). Reliably decisive in its niche of vetting magical items, but it does not act on the world.
- **Range: Low** (judged). Applies to an object being examined; the card states no wider reach.
- **Cost: Low** (stated). Its Mana figure is zero; the cost is acquiring the scroll.
