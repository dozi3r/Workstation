Monumental counterweighted stone plug sealing the subterranean dwarven kingdom since the Dwarfgate War, reopened only by the Helm of Grallen and Hammer of Kharas (`Q_WIN_01`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 3
- **Provides:** `hammer_of_kharas_authority`, `refugee_winter_sanctuary`
- **Requires:** `helm_of_grallen_relic`
