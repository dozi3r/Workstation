# MODULE MOD_T5_NERAKA_FOUNDATION_PORTAL: THE CRACKED FOUNDATION STONE & TAKHISIS'S ABYSSAL GATE

## Granular Disposition Baseline ([1, 1, 3])

### 1. Executive Summary & Egri Premise
The ultimate cosmic climax in the subterranean vaults of Neraka where the five-headed Queen of Darkness strains against the Foundation Stone—decided by Berem the Everman, Raistlin, and the Companions (`Q_SPR_03`, `CRUC-20`, `CRUC-22`, `CRUC-29`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 5
- **Provides:** `sealed_abyssal_foundation_gate`
- **Requires:** `berem_everman_revelation`, `metallic_dragon_oath_breaker`
- **Standalone Fallback:** Operates as the final campaign climax engine across all three campaign editions.

### 2. 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Physical Environment:** A cracked, jewel-encrusted marble pillar humming with Abyssal void-wind; the radiant emerald on Berem's chest; five chromatic dragon heads pressing through the veil.
- **Behaviors:** Raistlin blasting Dracart with black lightning while Caramon shields Berem's final leap onto the stone.
- **Relationships:** Jasla's forgiving spirit embracing her brother Berem after three hundred years of torment.
- **Attitudes:** Cosmic redemption versus total oblivion.
- **Values:** Forgiveness and free will sealing the dark.

### 3. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment:** Takhisis (The Dark Queen) & High Cleric Dracart (`CRUC-22`)
* **Main Opponent:** Berem the Everman, Caramon & Jasla's Spirit
* **Thematic Shadow:** Raistlin Majere (Defying the Dark Queen to Preserve His Own Ambition, `CRUC-20`)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Gakhan & The Collapsing Temple Garrison

### 4. Severance & Escalation Threat Clock
- **Takhisis Planar Manifestation Clock [6 Ticks]:** Advances each round during the Night of the Eye until the Foundation Stone is sealed or corrupted.
