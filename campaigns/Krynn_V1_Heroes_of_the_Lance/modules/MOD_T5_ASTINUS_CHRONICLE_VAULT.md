# MODULE MOD_T5_ASTINUS_CHRONICLE_VAULT: THE ICONOCHRONOS OF ASTINUS & SHOIKAN GROVE

## Granular Disposition Baseline ([1, 1, 3])

### 1. Executive Summary & Egri Premise
The timeless Great Library of Palanthas where Astinus chronicles all mortal history and where Black-Robe Raistlin claims his destiny as Master of Past and Present (`Q_SPR_01`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 5
- **Provides:** `astinus_chronicle_prophecy`, `palanthas_dragon_orb_vault`, `tower_of_palanthas_key`
- **Requires:** `black_robe_transfiguration`
- **Standalone Fallback:** Provides historical lore and the third Dragon Orb to scholars who pass the Aesthetic monks.

### 2. 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Physical Environment:** Scratching goose-quill pens in absolute silence; across the street, the terrifying aura of the oak trees of Shoikan Grove.
- **Behaviors:** Astinus looking up from his desk to greet Raistlin as "old friend" (Fistandantilus).
- **Relationships:** Raistlin severing his mortal dependence to claim the cursed Tower of Palanthas.
- **Attitudes:** Cosmic neutrality and inexorable time.
- **Values:** The River of Time.

### 3. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment:** Astinus of Palanthas & Order of Aesthetics
* **Main Opponent:** Raistlin Majere (Claimant of the Black Robes)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Unliving Guardians of the Shoikan Grove
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Caramon & Tanis Searching for Their Lost Companion

### 4. Severance & Escalation Threat Clock
