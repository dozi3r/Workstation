# The Mythic Dawn Sleeper Network & Civic Paranoia
*Tier 4 Worldbuilding Module for The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion*

## 1. Premise & Operational Mandate
This module governs the mechanics, sensory cues, and systemic repercussions of the mythic dawn sleeper network & civic paranoia across Cyrodiil.

## 2. Four-Level Output Dial
- **Light:** Minor ambient rumors, distant sulfur odor, flickering candles.
- **Moderate:** Cultist graffiti, unsealed minor gates, heightened guard patrols.
- **Severe:** Full Daedric incursions, burned trade caravans, martial law declared in county seats.
- **Lethal:** Colossal Siege Crawlers, dimensional collapse, assassination of city counts.
