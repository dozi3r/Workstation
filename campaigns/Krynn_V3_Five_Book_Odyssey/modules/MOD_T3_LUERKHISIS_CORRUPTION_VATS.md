# MODULE MOD_T3_LUERKHISIS_CORRUPTION_VATS: THE TEMPLE OF LUERKHISIS & METALLIC DRAGON AWAKENING

## Granular Disposition Baseline ([3, 1, 2])

### 1. Executive Summary & Egri Premise
Volcanic covert operation into Sanction's Temple of Luerkhisis that exposes the corruption of the Good Dragon eggs and brings the Gold, Silver, Bronze, Brass, and Copper Dragons into the war (`Q_SPR_02`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 3
- **Provides:** `metallic_dragon_oath_breaker`
- **Requires:** `silver_clutch_eggshell_evidence`

### 2. 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Physical Environment:** Choking sulfur fumes, glowing magma grates, and the metallic chime of mourning Silver Dragons.
- **Behaviors:** Silvara, Gilthanas, and Fizban creeping through volcanic ducts past Bozak guards.
- **Relationships:** The ancient Gold and Silver Dragons weeping in fury as Takhisis's lie is exposed.
- **Attitudes:** Righteous draconic wrath unleashed after centuries of coerced silence.
- **Values:** Protection of the unborn and liberation of Krynn.

### 3. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)

* **Main Opponent:** Silvara, Gilthanas & Fizban the Fabulous
* **Thematic Shadow:** Draconic Neutrality Oath Enforced by Hostage Fear
