# MODULE MOD_T2_WAYRETH_SHIFTING_GROVE: The Shifting Enchanted Forest of Wayreth

## 1. Executive Summary & Egri Premise
Sentient mobile woodland that physically relocates across southern Ansalon to conceal the obsidian Tower of High Sorcery from the uninvited (`Q_PRE_01`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 2
- **Provides:** `high_sorcery_conclave_mandate`
- **Requires:** None (Root Producer)
- **Standalone Fallback Mode:** Manifests as an impenetrable mist-bound pine forest to non-magical travelers.

## 2. 5D Sensory Pentagon (James Wood Serious Noticing)
- **Physical Environment:** Whispering pine needles, silver-barked oaks moving when unobserved, and polished black obsidian towers.
- **Behaviors:** Forest paths folding back on trespassers while parting for summoned initiates.
- **Relationships:** Neutral sanctuary shared by White, Red, and Black Robe archmages.
- **Attitudes:** Detached arcane vigilance above mortal wars.
- **Values:** Preservation of Magic under the Three Moons.

## 3. Four-Corner Opposition Web
- Establishment: "Par-Salian & The Conclave of Wizards"
- Opponent: "Renegade Mages & Unsanctioned Warlocks"
- Shadow: "Fistandantilus's Lingering Vestige"
- Outcast: "Mundane Guardians & Brothers (Caramon Majere)"

## 4. Four-Level Output Dials
- Level 1: "Welcoming forest paths guiding summoned initiates to the gates"
- Level 2: "Disorienting spatial loops delaying uninvited war-parties"
- Level 3: "Active elemental and illusion wards repelling Dragonarmy scouts"
- Level 4: "Total planar translocation of the grove to a new region"
