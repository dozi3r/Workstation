# MODULE MOD_T5_SOULFORGE_CRUCIBLE: THE SOULFORGE OF WAYRETH & FISTANDANTILUS'S BLOOD-PACT

## Granular Disposition Baseline ([3, 3, 3])

### 1. Executive Summary & Egri Premise
The lethal Test of High Sorcery inside the Tower of Wayreth where Fistandantilus offers young Raistlin survival at the price of his health, golden skin, and hourglass eyes—and where Raistlin destroys the illusion of Caramon (`Q_PRE_01`, `Q_ECHO_01`, `CRUC-03`, `CRUC-04`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 5
- **Provides:** `staff_of_magius_attunement`, `soulforge_memory_projection`
- **Requires:** `high_sorcery_conclave_mandate`
- **Standalone Fallback:** Triggered as a playable Temporal Memory Crucible (`Q_ECHO_01`) whenever a 351 AC party attunes to the Bloodstone or Staff of Magius.

### 2. 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Physical Environment:** Melting stone walls, dark-elf arrows, emerald soul-fire, and the reflected hourglass pupils in a silver mirror.
- **Behaviors:** Raistlin whispering the bargain to Fistandantilus while Caramon weeps behind Par-Salian's one-way glass.
- **Relationships:** The irreversible fracture of the Majere twins' innocence (`CRUC-04`).
- **Attitudes:** Defiant survival at any cost.
- **Values:** Arcane transcendence versus fraternal love.

### 3. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment:** Par-Salian & The Three Orders' Testing Wards
* **Main Opponent:** Liam's Lethal Fireball & Dark Elf Construct
* **Thematic Shadow:** Fistandantilus's Soul-Bargain (`CRUC-03`)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Caramon Majere (The Illusory Twin & Real Witness, `CRUC-04`)

### 4. Severance & Escalation Threat Clock
- **Soulforge Mortality Strain Clock [6 Ticks]:** Advances through each chamber of the Test of High Sorcery.
