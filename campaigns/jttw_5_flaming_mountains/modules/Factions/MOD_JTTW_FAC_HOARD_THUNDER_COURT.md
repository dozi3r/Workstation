# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_HOARD_THUNDER_COURT: The Hoard-Thunder Court

A household bought with a fox king's fortune, defended by a hundred beast-spirits, and run by a demon king who splits his time between two wives.

### Philosophy & Goals
Keep the fortune and the husband; enjoy the library and the banquets; take revenge for the son.

### Structure & Ranks
The Bull King commands; Jade Countenance holds the purse; a captain runs the outer guard.

### Methods & Resources
Wealth, a beast garrison, shape-changing and friends with other courts.

### Allies and Rivals
Allied with the lagoon dragons; at odds with Rakshasi's household; hunted by Heaven.

### Internal Tensions
The first wife's claim, the second wife's money and a husband who leaves one to please the other.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Flattery and gossip about his wives are the quickest keys; he is slow to forgive insults to either.

### Using them in play
A moral-complication stronghold: a siege that can end in a trial of loyalties rather than just a fight.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Golden-eyed beast; Cast-iron rod
- **Ranks:** Demon King; Princess; Guard Captain; Beast-spirits
- **Allies:** Green Wave Lagoon Court
- **Rivals:** Palm-Leaf Cave Household; Heavenly Enforcement Host; Pilgrim Party
- **Doctrine taboos:** Never slight a friend's wife or concubine
- **Headquarters:** The Cloud-Touching Cave

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: Medium** (stated). The Bull King has friends at the lagoon and a name known across the West.
- **Force: High** (stated). A hundred-plus guards plus a demon king who ties the Great Sage.
