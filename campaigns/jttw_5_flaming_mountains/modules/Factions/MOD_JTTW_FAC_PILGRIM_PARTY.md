# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_PILGRIM_PARTY: The Tang Pilgrim Party

Four sworn travellers on a commissioned journey whose one rule is that the road goes west and nothing may turn it aside.

### Philosophy & Goals
Reach the scriptures. The party answers to a Buddhist decree, not to local lords, and the master will not take another road.

### Structure & Ranks
Tripitaka decides; Wukong acts; Eight Rules complains and then fights; Sha Monk guards the luggage and the master.

### Methods & Resources
Disguise, negotiation by kinship, brute force, and appeal to Heaven when force fails.

### Allies and Rivals
Allied with Lingji and the Heavenly host; hostile to the Bull King's two households; welcome but dependent among the villagers.

### Internal Tensions
Eight Rules wants another road; Wukong's old bonds make him spare enemies the others would kill; Tripitaka's fear of the fire is hidden behind duty.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Outsiders cannot join. They can feed them, guide them or bind them with debt; the party always repays.

### Using them in play
Run them as the PCs' travelling companions or as a patron that arrives, solves one problem violently, and leaves the aftermath to the local power.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Palm-Leaf Fan carried on a shoulder; Muckrake; Golden-hooped rod
- **Ranks:** Master; Eldest disciple; Second disciple; Third disciple
- **Allies:** Little Sumeru Temple; Heavenly Enforcement Host
- **Rivals:** Palm-Leaf Cave Household; Hoard-Thunder Court
- **Doctrine taboos:** Never turn away from the West; Wukong's kinship bonds restrain him from killing kin
- **Headquarters:** The Fire-Line Camp (a roadside halt)

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: High** (stated). Heaven, Buddha and a dozen lesser powers act to protect the journey.
- **Force: High** (stated). Wukong matches the Bull King for over a hundred rounds twice.
- **Wealth: Low** (stated). They live on alms and hospitality and bribe no one.
- **Secrecy: Low** (judged). They arrive announcing their commission at every door.
