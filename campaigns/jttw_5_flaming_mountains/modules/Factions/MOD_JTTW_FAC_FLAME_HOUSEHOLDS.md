# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_FLAME_HOUSEHOLDS: The Households of the Mountain of Flames

Red-roofed farming villages that cannot grow a grain without a distant keeper's rain and who have no ruler but a banished spirit.

### Philosophy & Goals
Keep the fan's rain and the fire at bay; every season is hot, and every harvest is borrowed.

### Structure & Ranks
A householder speaks for the village; no ruler; the mountain's spirit is the only official.

### Methods & Resources
Pilgrimage with four hogs, four sheep, flowers, fruit, fowl and wine, once every ten years; a month's round trip.

### Allies and Rivals
Friendly to the pilgrims; neutral toward the fan's keeper, whom they know only by a title.

### Internal Tensions
Some say the fire returns each year, others each decade; the party's arrival makes the households ask if the monks caused the fire.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Offer a gift, bathe, and ask the householder. Outsiders become hosts or guests for a night.

### Using them in play
A safe, rumour-rich start; an NPC well for local knowledge; their harvest clock raises pressure whenever the fire returns.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Red brick and red tile; Rice-pudding cart
- **Ranks:** Householder; Peddler
- **Allies:** Local Spirit Office; Pilgrim Party
- **Doctrine taboos:** Never approach the keeper without gifts; Wash before the pilgrimage
- **Headquarters:** The Red-Brick Village

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: Low** (stated). A string of villages near one mountain; the householder knows only the next stage of the road.
- **Force: Low** (judged). No guards or fighters appear; the local spirit's ghost soldiers are Heaven's, not theirs.
- **Wealth: Low** (stated). Nothing grows without the fan; they offer pigs and sheep once a decade.
- **Secrecy: Low** (judged). Everything about them is public: red roofs, public rituals.
