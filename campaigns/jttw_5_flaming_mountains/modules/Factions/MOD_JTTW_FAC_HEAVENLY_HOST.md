# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_HEAVENLY_HOST: The Heavenly and Buddhist Enforcement Host

A chain-of-command army that moves only on a decree and, once it moves, closes every quarter of the sky.

### Philosophy & Goals
Defend the journey and the decree; arrest the Bull King and lead him to the Buddha.

### Structure & Ranks
A devaraja commands; a prince executes; four guardians hold the quarters.

### Methods & Resources
Nets, a mirror, a fiery wheel, a rope and a sword that cannot kill a head that regrows.

### Allies and Rivals
Allied with the pilgrims and Little Sumeru; hostile to the Bull King.

### Internal Tensions
Nata's methods outpace his father's patience; the guardians wait for a command.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Only via petition: a memorial goes up, a decree comes down, the host arrives.

### Using them in play
A deus ex machina with a cost: the PCs' stand-down is the price of their intervention.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Cosmic nets; Imp-reflecting mirror; Fiery wheel
- **Ranks:** Devaraja; Prince; Diamond Guardian; Gods of Darkness and Light
- **Allies:** Pilgrim Party; Little Sumeru Temple
- **Rivals:** Hoard-Thunder Court; Palm-Leaf Cave Household
- **Doctrine taboos:** No god may withhold protection from the scripture journey
- **Headquarters:** Jade Cloud Mountain (while deployed)

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: High** (stated). Guardians at four mountains and a general in the sky.
- **Force: High** (stated). They seal the Bull King's every escape and reduce him to a roped bull.
- **Wealth: Medium** (judged). Treasure mountains and heavenly weapons, but no coin appears.
- **Secrecy: Medium** (stated). Acts by secret command of Tathagata and the Jade Emperor's decree.
