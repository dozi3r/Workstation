# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_PALM_LEAF_HOUSEHOLD: The Palm-Leaf Cave Household

A strict cultivators' cave with a single treasure that can end a region's drought or send an army across a continent.

### Philosophy & Goals
Hold her cultivation and her fan; avenge Red Boy; keep a husband who is rarely home.

### Structure & Ranks
Rakshasi, her maids and her gate attendant. No male inside.

### Methods & Resources
The fan, two swords, wine and a good memory for grievances.

### Allies and Rivals
The Bull King's first wife and rival of Jade Countenance; hostile to Wukong.

### Internal Tensions
She wants her son and her husband, but cannot hold both; drink loosens her tongue and her secrets.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Approach with Red Boy's news or the Bull King's name; offer, do not demand.

### Using them in play
A diplomacy-and-duel hub with a loot-and-secret ending, and a good source of reversals.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Palm-leaf fan; Two blue swords
- **Ranks:** Princess; Maid
- **Rivals:** Hoard-Thunder Court; Pilgrim Party
- **Doctrine taboos:** No man inside the household; A treasure is not lent lightly
- **Headquarters:** The Palm-Leaf Cave Hall

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: High** (stated). Her fan controls fire, wind and rain over an eight-hundred-li region and can blow a person 84,000 li.
- **Force: Medium** (stated). Swords and the fan; she loses a wrestling bout with Wukong's rod and a stomach assault.
- **Wealth: Medium** (judged). A decade of village tribute and the Bull King's sent jewels, silk and rice.
- **Secrecy: Medium** (stated). Known only by a title; the fan's formulae are oral secrets.
