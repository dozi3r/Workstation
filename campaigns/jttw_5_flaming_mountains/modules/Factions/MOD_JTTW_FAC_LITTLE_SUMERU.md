# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_LITTLE_SUMERU: The Little Sumeru Temple

A single bodhisattva's mountain that holds one unused counter to the fan and gives it freely to those on the journey.

### Philosophy & Goals
Serve Tathagata's order by lending the right tool at the right time.

### Structure & Ranks
One bodhisattva and attendants.

### Methods & Resources
A staff and an elixir, held back until needed.

### Allies and Rivals
Allied with the pilgrims.

### Internal Tensions
Lingji's hospitality is brief by his own account; he will help, but he will not host the party or escort it back.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Arrive by cloud, be announced, explain; receive and go.

### Using them in play
A guaranteed-boon stop that ends a failure streak and gives a sewn-in advantage with a hidden side effect.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Bells; Staff
- **Ranks:** Bodhisattva; Gate worker
- **Allies:** Pilgrim Party; Heavenly Enforcement Host
- **Doctrine taboos:** Items are held for the journey
- **Headquarters:** Little Sumeru Mountain

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: Medium** (stated). Fifty thousand li from the Mountain of Flames.
- **Force: Medium** (stated). Lingji's staff subdued the Yellow Wind fiend.
- **Wealth: Low** (judged). A throne and a gate worker; no treasury appears.
- **Secrecy: Low** (judged). Open gates, loud bells.
