# MODULE MOD_JTTW_FAC_GREEN_WAVE_COURT: The Green Wave Lagoon Court

An aquatic court whose politics is the banquet and whose security is its etiquette.

### Philosophy & Goals
Host; be seen; avoid quarrels that reach the hall.

### Structure & Ranks
The old dragon rules; sons and grandsons enforce; courtiers intercede.

### Methods & Resources
Banquets, music and a water-repelling gate that keeps a dry floor.

### Allies and Rivals
Friend to the Bull King; wary of the Great Sage once he is recognised.

### Internal Tensions
Courtiers will plead for a ridiculous intruder to avoid a scene.

### Joining and Dealing With Them
Arrive by invitation or in humble disguise; speak in verse.

### Using them in play
An infiltration set-piece where the prize is a mount, a rumour or a good enemy.

### Facts
- **Symbols:** Pearl light; Banquet music
- **Ranks:** Old Dragon King; Dragon sons; Attendants
- **Allies:** Hoard-Thunder Court
- **Doctrine taboos:** A guest must follow etiquette; The sentence for a breach is flogging
- **Headquarters:** The Green Wave Dragon Palace

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Reach: Medium** (judged). Hosts the Bull King and attendants from across the region.
- **Force: Medium** (judged). Dragon sons seize intruders, but flog rather than kill.
- **Wealth: High** (stated). Gold roof tiles, jade frames, coral railings, pearl light.
- **Secrecy: Low** (judged). Its hall is a stage for being seen.
