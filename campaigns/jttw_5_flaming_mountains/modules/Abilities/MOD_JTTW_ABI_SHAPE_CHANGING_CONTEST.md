# MODULE MOD_JTTW_ABI_SHAPE_CHANGING_CONTEST: The Contest of Transformations

A pursuit duel in which each shape-shifter takes the form that preys on the other's last form.

### What It Does
Swan, vulture, eagle, phoenix, crane, deer, tiger, leopard, lion, bear, elephant, then bull and giant: each form beats or evades the last.

### Source & Teachers
Both fighters learned the same method; the Bull King is bulkier and slower.

### Costs & Limits
A caught form cannot be left once the mirror is trained on it; size matches size.

### Lore
The bull's last form is ten thousand feet long and eight hundred feet high; Wukong grows to a hundred thousand.

### Facts
- **School:** The seventy-two transformations
- **Practitioners:** Sun Wukong; Bull Demon King
- **Cost narrative:** Exhaustion over a whole day and night of fighting.
- **Counters:** Imp-reflecting mirror pins the creature in its true form [arcane]
- **Range note:** From cave mouth to mountain skies.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (stated). Both fighters have seventy-two transformations; neither wins until the gods arrive.
- **Range: Medium** (stated). The contest moves across the sky and mountain.
- **Cost: Low** (judged). No stated cost beyond time and exhaustion.

### Standalone Fallback
Use as a duel minigame: each round, the pursuer picks a form that beats the fugitive's last form; the fugitive escapes only by changing to something larger or by reverting.
