# MODULE MOD_JTTW_ABI_WIND_ARRESTING_ELIXIR: The Wind-Arresting Elixir

A single pellet that makes the swallower unmovable by a wind artifact for the rest of the arc.

### What It Does
Makes viscera firm and bones fortified; any wind from the fan fails to move the carrier.

### Source & Teachers
Given to Lingji by Tathagata with the Flying-Dragon Staff; never used before.

### Costs & Limits
A tiny silk bag; sew it under the collar to store, hold it in the mouth to use.

### Lore
Wukong later swallows it unknowingly; that is why the Bull King's fan fails at his first try.

### Facts
- **School:** Bodhisattva gift
- **Practitioners:** Lingji (keeper); Sun Wukong (user)
- **Cost narrative:** Single use; swallowed unawares when Wukong becomes a mole cricket.
- **Counters:** Use against the palm-leaf fan only [wind]
- **Range note:** Personal.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (stated). The fan cannot move Wukong even three times in a row.
- **Range: Low** (judged). Self only.
- **Cost: Medium** (stated). One pellet, one use; swallowing it is permanent.

### Standalone Fallback
Use as a one-use counter-item that nullifies the biggest wind or force effect in the scene and has an unnoticed ongoing effect.
