# MODULE MOD_JTTW_ABI_PALM_LEAF_FAN: The Palm-Leaf Fan: Fire, Wind and Rain

A supreme-yin leaf that puts out fire, raises wind or brings rain on three waves, and blows a person across the world on one.

### What It Does
Wave one: fire out. Wave two: breeze. Wave three: rain on the burnt area only. At a creature: drift of 84,000 li. Forty-nine waves end the fire for good.

### Source & Teachers
Born at the back of Mount Kunlun when chaos divided; its keeper shows the enlarging formula only by accident.

### Costs & Limits
Press the seventh red thread with the left thumb and speak the six-syllable breath formula to enlarge; the shrink formula is separate. The false fan makes fire worse.

### Lore
Villagers know it as the Immortal Iron-Fan's treasure; Lingji knows its origin; the Bull King knows how to shrink it.

### Facts
- **School:** Supreme yin
- **Practitioners:** Rakshasi; Bull Demon King; Wukong (partial)
- **Cost narrative:** No stated cost; use depends on knowing both enlarge and shrink formulas.
- **Counters:** Wind-Arresting Elixir [wind]; Fire reversed by a false fan [fire]
- **Range note:** Eighty-four thousand li drift; permanent quenching needs forty-nine waves.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (stated). Quenches an eight-hundred-li fire in three waves; blows a mortal 84,000 li.
- **Range: High** (stated). Rain falls on the whole burnt region only.
- **Cost: Medium** (judged). No cost is stated, but the formula and the shrink secret limit use to those who learned them.
