# MODULE MOD_JTTW_ABI_THREE_HEADS_SIX_ARMS: Three Heads, Six Arms, and the Monster-Binding Kit

A prince's capture routine: take a many-armed war form, cut, burn, pin the shape and rope the nose.

### What It Does
Three heads and six arms; the sword beheads, the wheel burns, the mirror pins, the rope leads.

### Source & Teachers
A Heavenly Prince's gifts; the mirror belongs to his father.

### Costs & Limits
A beheaded bull regrows a head each time, so cutting alone fails.

### Lore
More than ten heads fall before the fiery wheel is hung on a horn.

### Facts
- **School:** Heavenly martial arts
- **Practitioners:** Prince Nata; Devaraja Li
- **Cost narrative:** Needs two cooperating officers.
- **Counters:** Fire against the bull's horn [fire]
- **Range note:** Sight range for the mirror.

### Bands (Low / Medium / High)
- **Potency: High** (stated). Takes a mountain-sized bull that Wukong cannot beat alone.
- **Range: Medium** (stated). Works at melee reach and at sight range with the mirror.
- **Cost: Low** (judged). No cost stated; needs a decree and a second holder for the mirror.
