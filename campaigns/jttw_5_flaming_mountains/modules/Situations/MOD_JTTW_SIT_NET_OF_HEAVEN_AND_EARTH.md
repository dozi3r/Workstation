# MODULE MOD_JTTW_SIT_NET_OF_HEAVEN_AND_EARTH: The Net of Heaven and Earth

A fugitive demon runs to each quarter and finds a guardian waiting; the sky is closed and the end is arrest.

### Dramatic Premise
The more powerful the fugitive, the more certain the net; the net needs a decree, and a decree needs a reason the gods cannot ignore.

### Core Premise
North, south, east and west guardians block the Bull King; Li and Nata block the sky.

### GM Placement & Synergies
Use it to end the arc. It rewards the PCs for earlier restraint and punishes a premature ending.

### 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Sight:** Cosmic nets spread between mountains; gold-eyed bull; fiery wheel on a horn.
- **Sound:** Bellows and roars.
- **Smell:** Burnt hair and immortal fire.
- **Touch:** Heat through a rope.
- **Taste:** Iron and smoke.

### Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (Devaraja Li):** A decree is a decree. *Leverage:* The mirror. *Blind spot:* Bound by orders.
* **Main opponent (The Bull King):** My hatred is deep. *Leverage:* Shape-changing. *Blind spot:* Cornered.
* **Thematic shadow (Prince Nata):** Cut it until it yields. *Leverage:* Many arms and a wheel. *Blind spot:* Impatient.
* **Tertiary outcast (Wukong):** I wanted a fair duel. *Leverage:* The ending is his. *Blind spot:* The old brotherhood.

### Output Dial
- **Level 1:** A lone guardian blocks one road.
- **Level 2:** Two quarters are closed.
- **Level 3:** Four quarters are closed and the sky is blocked.
- **Level 4:** The bull is roped and led to the Buddha; the fan is handed over.

### Threat Clocks
