# MODULE CWT-074: THE TEOTIHUACAN PALACE IMMOLATION AND PYRAMIDAL REVOLT

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Archaeological Grounding
Flourishing between 100 BCE and 650 CE within the volcanic highland Basin of Mexico, Teotihuacan ("The City of the Gods") was the largest pre-Columbian urban metropolis in the Western Hemisphere, boasting a peak population between 100,000 and 125,000 inhabitants. Unlike the dynastic Maya city-states of the Petén lowlands dominated by individualized god-kings, monumental royal stelae, and personal funerary cults, Teotihuacan engineered an unprecedented, hyper-egalitarian corporate urban model. For centuries, this corporate order suppressed individual ruler glorification in favor of collective civic institutions, universal high-quality public housing, and an industrial-scale manufacturing monopoly over Mesoamerica's most vital strategic resource: **green Pachuca obsidian**.

However, between 550 and 650 CE, this egalitarian social pact suffered catastrophic systemic collapse. Exhaustive archaeological excavations and micro-stratigraphic forensic analyses (synthesized by Linda Manzanilla, René Millon, and George Cowgill) demonstrated that Teotihuacan did not fall to nomadic northern invaders (*Chichimeca*). Instead, the metropolis imploded through an internally coordinated, surgical populist revolution: the common citizens rose in armed rebellion to systematically de-sacralize, loot, and incinerate the seats of concentrated aristocratic authority, while leaving their own residential neighborhoods completely untouched.

The physical and institutional architecture of the module rests upon four pillars of archaeological reality:

1. **The Multi-Family Apartment Compound**: Over 80% of Teotihuacan's urban population resided in over 2,000 standardized, single-story, high-walled **apartment compounds** (such as Tetitla, Zacuala, Yayahuala, and Atetelco). Each compound measured between 1,500 and 3,500 square meters, housing 60 to 100 individuals organized into multi-family corporate kin or craft collectives. These compounds featured private open-air central courtyards, sophisticated underground sub-floor ceramic drainage pipes, shared kitchens, domestic shrines, and vibrant polychrome fresco murals painted in brilliant hematite red, malachite green, and cinnabar. Commoners enjoyed a material standard of living, caloric stability, and architectural permanence unknown in any other pre-industrial ancient empire.
2. **The Green Pachuca Obsidian Industrial Monopoly**: Teotihuacan exercised absolute monopoly control over the Sierra de las Navajas geological deposits of **translucent green Pachuca obsidian**—the industrial steel of pre-Columbian Mesoamerica. Through hundreds of specialized urban workshops distributed across domestic quarters, master knappers converted raw volcanic glass into standardized prismatic blade cores, razor-sharp lancets, bifacial spear points, and ritual eccentrics. These cores were exported across trade corridors spanning thousands of kilometers (reaching Kaminaljuyu, Tikal, and Copán), generating astronomical wealth that financed the city's monumental masonry programs.
3. **Talud-Tablero Civic Monumentality without Individual Kings**: The monumental spine of the city—anchored by the 65-meter **Pyramid of the Sun** (over 1.1 million cubic meters of adobe and basalt fill), the **Pyramid of the Moon**, and the 2.5-kilometer **Avenue of the Dead**—employed the standardized *talud-tablero* architectural signature. A sloping battered apron (*talud*) supports a framed, cantilevered rectangular vertical panel (*tablero*), creating alternating bands of light and deep shadow. Crucially, Teotihuacan produced **zero royal tombs, zero named king portraits, zero individualized victory monuments, and no dynastic hieroglyphic inscriptions**. Monumentality served the collective cosmological order—the Feathered Serpent, the Storm God Tlaloc, and the Great Goddess—rather than personal monarchical vanity.
4. **The Forensics of the Selective Burn Horizon (~550–650 CE)**: Following severe environmental strain (the global 536 CE volcanic dust veil event and multi-year highland droughts) and the emergence of an ambitious aristocratic faction attempting to consolidate hereditary power inside the Ciudadela, the corporate pact dissolved. Stratigraphic core sampling proves that **the burn layer is strictly confined to the Avenue of the Dead**:
   * Every administrative palace, elite council hall, and theocratic pyramid temple lining the central ceremonial way was systematically dismantled, looted, and burned with pitch and resin torches.
   * Colossal stone effigies of the Feathered Serpent and aristocratic lineage symbols were deliberately decapitated, battered with stone mauls, and dragged into public plazas.
   * In total contrast, **the surrounding 2,000 apartment compounds show zero evidence of destruction or fire**. The people did not sack their own homes; they conducted a focused, surgical revolutionary strike against the corrupt centralized nodes of theocratic exploitation.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "Our city has no king; the Avenue of the Dead balances all neighborhoods under cosmic equality, and the gods require only that we feed the pyramids with obsidian blades and highland maize."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** Desperate after prolonged drought and crop failure, an aristocratic faction inside the Ciudadela fortifies the Avenue of the Dead, hoards green obsidian cores, halves commoner lime-plaster repair rations, and demands human retainers for private palatial tombs.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Creeping Aristocratic Sclerosis} + \text{Armed Populist Compound Solidarity} = \text{Selective Palatial Immolation \& De-Sacralization of the Pyramids}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** An egalitarian corporate metropolis will not permit the rebirth of despotic kingship. When rulers attempt to elevate their private palaces above the common plaza, the collective will burn the palaces to ash while keeping their neighborhood compounds intact.

#### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The High Priest-Prefect of the Ciudadela):**  
  An entrenched theocratic aristocrat commanding the inner temple complex of the Temple of the Feathered Serpent. Convinced that the devastating 6th-century drought signals the failure of collective council governance, the Prefect attempts to establish an autocratic hereditary dynasty. He seals the Ciudadela's sunken quadrangle, stockpiles thousands of high-grade green Pachuca obsidian blade cores inside subterranean vaults, and hires foreign highland atlatl mercenaries. To fund his monumental palace expansion, he slashes the lime-plaster allocations owed to the apartment compounds, letting commoner homes crumble in the rains.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Apartment Compound Militia Captain):**  
  A battle-tested collective war-leader from the Tetitla apartment compound. Representing the council of neighborhood craft-barrios, she organizes obsidian-blade knappers, pottery guild workers, and market porters into disciplined urban street phalanxes. Armed with long mahogany lances tipped with razor-sharp green obsidian prismatic blades, she rejects royal centralization with incandescent fury. Her objective is uncompromising: breach the barricades along the Avenue of the Dead, drag down the false monarchical thrones, and incinerate every elite palace from the Pyramid of the Moon to the Ciudadela.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (The Obsidian Smuggling Factor):**  
  A cynical pochteca merchant operating from an unmapped compound basement along the southern canal margin. Seeing the inevitable clash between palace and barrio as a supreme commercial opportunity, the Factor smuggles green obsidian blade cores out of state storehouses to sell to the neighborhood militias at extortionate rates, while simultaneously supplying defensive barrier stakes to the Ciudadela guard. During the height of the conflagration, he intends to infiltrate the deepest subterranean caverns beneath the Pyramid of the Sun to plunder thousands of ceremonial green jadeite beads, obsidian mirrors, and marine shell offerings.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (The Highland Lime-Kiln Burner):**  
  A scarred, half-blind elder residing in the stripped, deforested volcanic foothills flanking Mount Gordo. Having spent four decades felling highland pine and burning calcined limestone in subterranean kilns to produce the millions of tons of white lime plaster that coat Teotihuacan's streets and murals, his lungs and eyes are ruined by caustic alkaline dust. He hates both the parasitic theocrats and the violent militia captains. He delivers a grim ecological warning: if the massive plaster-sheathed palaces along the Avenue of the Dead are incinerated, the intense thermal breakdown will release choking clouds of caustic calcium oxide and toxic smoke, poisoning the valley's open-air chinampa canals and drinking cisterns for a generation.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: The Populist Conflagration
The sociopolitical tension and physical destruction of the metropolis escalate across four strictly calibrated hazard tiers:

* **Level 1 (Compound Plaster Rationing & Obsidian Export Tax / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The Ciudadela elite restricts the distribution of quicklime plaster to neighborhood compounds and levies emergency trade tariffs on exported obsidian cores; tension simmers behind compound walls.
  * *Mechanics:* Checkpoints manned by state atlatl guards line the Avenue of the Dead. Passing guarded thresholds without papers or bribing guards requires a **DC 10 Haggling, Deception, or Bureaucracy check**. Navigating crowded compound courtyards during council debates imposes minor delays. Handling fresh prismatic obsidian blade cores without leather wraps requires a **DC 10 Crafting or Dexterity check** to avoid 1d4 slashing damage.
* **Level 2 (Talud-Tablero Temple Barricades & Street Clashes / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Barrio militias erect heavy timber and basalt rubble barricades across the Avenue of the Dead; palace atlatl patrols clash violently with knappers' guilds; theocratic frescoes are defaced with revolutionary slogans.
  * *Mechanics:* Transit across ceremonial plazas is contested by hostile patrols. Scaling or breaching a *talud-tablero* street barricade requires a **DC 13 Athletics or Acrobatics check**; failure leaves the character exposed to atlatl dart skirmishers dealing **2d8 bludgeoning or piercing damage**. Entering crowded, riotous neighborhood plazas requires a **DC 13 Will or Insight check** to avoid being swept into panicked crowd stampedes.
* **Level 3 (Avenue of the Dead Palatial Conflagration / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Thousands of compound militia members storm the central administrative sector with resin torches; timber lintels ignite; monumental palaces along the Avenue of the Dead burn in towering infernos; elite god statues are toppled.
  * *Mechanics:* Active, blazing urban war zone. Moving through burning palace corridors requires a **DC 16 Reflex or Fortitude check** each round to avoid falling *talud-tablero* masonry lintels and superheated smoke, taking **4d10 fire and suffocation damage** on a failure. Caustic lime dust from calcining plaster burns eyes and lungs, imposing disadvantage on all Perception and attack rolls without wet cloth masks. Mercenary atlatl units deploy concentrated dart volleys requiring a **DC 16 Dodge or Cover check**.
* **Level 4 (Total De-Sacralization & Mass Compound Exodus / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The Ciudadela enclosure is completely engulfed; elite palaces are razed to their foundations; stone Feathered Serpent effigies are shattered and cast into the street; the aristocratic council is wiped out; centralized government permanently dissolves.
  * *Mechanics:* Catastrophic urban rupture. Characters caught within the collapsing administrative sector must succeed on a **DC 20 Survival, Athletics, or Fortitude check** or suffer **8d12 violent riot, crushing masonry, and fire damage**. The node loses all imperial and administrative classification permanently. Commoner apartment compounds seal their outer blast-gates, entering self-sufficient communal autarky before regional populations disperse peacefully into autonomous valley settlements.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```

[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Aristocratic Monarchy Consolidation Meter (6 Ticks)
```

* **Palatial Selective Immolation Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each operational period that palace guards execute street protestors, seize neighborhood grain storage, or when the party aids the Compound Militia with tactical sabotage or incendiaries.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Black pitch and dry ocote pine torches are openly distributed across the courtyards of the Tetitla and Zacuala compounds.
    * *Tick 2:* Vanguard militia detachments smash the outer peripheral gates of the Avenue of the Dead, routing state customs scribes.
    * *Tick 3:* Smoke plumes rise from elite administrative compounds flanking the Pyramid of the Sun; aristocratic families flee toward the Ciudadela.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Triggers **Dial Level 3: Avenue of the Dead Palatial Conflagration** immediately. Ten thousand armed commoners storm the central avenue, incinerating the administrative palaces.
* **Aristocratic Monarchy Consolidation Meter [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) whenever the High Priest-Prefect seizes commoner obsidian workshops, successfully secures foreign mercenary reinforcements, or suppresses neighborhood council meetings.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* The Avenue of the Dead is closed to commoner pedestrian transit by heavy palisades and bronze-studded barricades.
    * *Tick 4:* Compound lime-plaster rations are formally cancelled; all masons are conscripted into fortifying the Ciudadela into a private monarchical fortress.
  * *Tick 5:* The Prefect proclaims hereditary divine kingship atop the Temple of the Feathered Serpent, staging show executions of dissident barrio elders.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* Oligarchic autocracy cements. The compound militias are fractured by mercenary terror, commoner apartment compounds are converted into walled tax ghettos, and Teotihuacan transforms into a tyrannical hereditary slave-monarchy.

### 4. Supply Chain Algebra & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Material: Volcanic-Basalt-Lime-Plaster`: Massive regional quarrying of porous volcanic basalt and highland calcined limestone kilns producing hundreds of thousands of tons of durable hydraulic white plaster to seal roofs, floors, and murals against tropical rains.
  * `Agriculture: Valley-Chinampa-Irrigation`: Intensive wetland agriculture, lake-shore raised field chinampas, and spring-fed canal grids across the Basin of Mexico supplying millions of bushels of maize, beans, squash, and amaranth to sustain the dense urban non-agrarian artisan base.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Structure: Talud-Tablero-Pyramidal-Citadel`: Monumental stepped pyramidal platforms utilizing the battered slope (*talud*) and cantilevered vertical frame (*tablero*) architectural grammar, providing peerless elevated command, civic assembly plazas, and defensible strongpoints.
  * `Industry: Green-Pachuca-Obsidian-Blade-Cores`: High-purity prismatic obsidian cores capable of yielding hundreds of razor-sharp surgical blades per core, serving as the supreme cutting tool and lethal edge-weapon standard of Mesoamerica.
  * `Tension: Selective-Palatial-Immolation`: A powerful narrative and sociopolitical engine modeling surgical internal revolution where commoners annihilate centralized political leadership while preserving their own domestic neighborhood infrastructure.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When administrative palaces burn and centralized regional tribute collection halts, the module executes **Autonomous Apartment Compound Commune Mode**:
    * Centralized bureaucracy vanishes instantly; tribute demands and imperial export taxes drop to zero.
    * Over 2,000 walled apartment compounds seal their outer entrances, govern themselves through neighborhood elder councils, and cultivate rooftop gardens and local chinampa plots.
    * Regional trade drops by 70%, but the population survives autonomously without famine or mass internal bloodshed for two generations (~650–750 CE) before gradually dispersing into peaceful regional valley towns.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks (writing-generation)

* **Scene 1: The Obsidian Razor Shakedown (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party enters the Sun Plaza attempting to purchase supplies or meet an administrative contact, but is cornered by Ciudadela palace mercenaries armed with feathered atlatls and hardwood macuahuitl clubs. The mercenary captain invokes an emergency theocratic decree demanding the party surrender all high-grade green obsidian weapons and pack animals; simultaneously, an angry crowd of compound artisans armed with heavy basalt knapping hammers and fire-hardened oak staves surrounds the guards, demanding the party defy the decree and join the neighborhood militia.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party expects to navigate a wealthy commercial trade metropolis, but is instantly plunged into a violent jurisdictional standoff between state confiscators and armed revolutionary artisans.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The palace captain demands absolute submission in the name of the divine cosmic order, but his sandals are caked in charcoal dust and soot from an arson attack he barely survived in the neighboring barrio this morning.
* **Scene 2: The Firebrand in the Mural Courtyard (Resource Dilemma)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Inside a burning administrative palace off the Avenue of the Dead, compound rebels have cornered a dozen teenage scribes and are pouring flammable ocote pine pitch over the walls to incinerate a vast archive of painted deerskin and amatl bark-paper tax rolls, debt registers, and trade agreements. The rebels view these scrolls as instruments of their generational debt bondage.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Save the Archives:* The party can battle the impassioned populist rebels to rescue the bark-paper codices (preserving the irreplaceable written history, astronomical charts, and architectural blueprints of the greatest metropolis in the Americas), but doing so alienates the commoner compounds and marks the party as defenders of elite tyranny.
    * *Let the Fire Burn:* Standing aside ensures safe alliance with the barrio militia, but destroys the historical memory of the civilization forever, leaving future generations with no record of who built the pyramids.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The militia leader shouts that no child should grow up reading the debt-titles that sold their parents into the quarries, holding the roaring pine torch inches from the painted genealogies while a weeping apprentice scribe clutches a pot of cinnabar ink to his chest.
* **Scene 3: The Toppling of the Feathered Serpent (The Severance Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* At the summit platform of the Temple of the Feathered Serpent inside the Ciudadela, populist insurgents have looped thick maguey-fiber hauling ropes around a 4-ton basalt serpent head jutting from the *tablero* facade. Fifty warriors heave in unison to topple the stone monster down the steep 45-degree stairway into the sunken courtyard below, where barricaded palace nobles and their families are trapped.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Sever the Ropes:* Intervening to cut the hauling ropes saves the trapped patricians from being crushed by the rolling monolith, but risks the party being thrown down the 60-meter pyramidal steps by the furious crowd.
    * *Aid the Toppling:* Helping the insurgents heave the monolith smashes the courtyard gates, allowing the mob to butcher the aristocracy and opening a path to the secret subterranean offering vault beneath the pyramid, packed with pristine green Pachuca blade cores and sacred jade.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* As the 4-ton stone effigy groans and cracks against its mortar, the air vibrates with rhythmic chanting from ten thousand commoners packing the plaza below, marking the exact acoustic second that an empire dies and freedom is reclaimed.
