# MODULE CWT-033: HIGH-ALTIPLANO HIGHLAND CAMELID & WOOL HERDS

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Pastoral Circuit
High above the timberline, nestled across a vast wind-raked plateau of red volcanic shale and yellow fescue grass, herds of sure-footed camelids thrive in thin, icy air. These hardy beasts produce dense, moisture-wicking hollow fleece prized by high-altitude travelers and military engineers alike. Clan families follow cyclical pastoral trails between glacial tarns and dry stone shearing corrals, drying strips of spiced meat under the intense ultraviolet mountain sun.

* **The Dry-Stone Pircas:** Circular stone corrals built from unmortared basalt slabs that shield newborn camelids from howling katabatic winds.
* **The High Salar Lick:** Natural mineral flats encrusted with mineral borates and rock salt where herds replenish crucial electrolytes.
* **The Solar Jerky Racks:** Racks of split willow poles where thinly sliced meat dehydrates in the crisp, sterile mountain air, creating rations that resist rot for years.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The High-Pasture Clan Council):** Stern pastoral elders wrapped in crimson ponchos. They allocate seasonal ridge-lines with strict equity, executing anyone caught shearing out of season.
* **Main Opponent (Lowland Textile Monopolies):** Coastal merchant houses that lobby to bar highland weavers from selling finished garments, forcing the clans to sell raw wool bundles at depressed wholesale prices.
* **Thematic Shadow (Borderland Beast Rustlers):** Deserter bands and mountain brigands who strike outlying corrals during thick fog, herding stolen camelids across treacherous border gaps.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Lone Ridge Watchers):** Disgraced herders assigned to isolated windy windbreaks with nothing but a leather sling and dry jerky, tasked with spotting snow-leopards before the herds wake.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 05 (Mountain Watchtower & Signal Pylon)**, **Node 20 (Altiplano Plateau Pass)**, or any high-elevation edge bridging mountain and valley biomes.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Pasture: Sub-Alpine-Fescue-Tussock` and `Mineral: High-Salt-Licks`; outputs `Textile: Thermal-Camelid-Fleece` (vital for surviving frozen high-altitude transit modules like CWT-022) and `Meat: Cured-Jerky-Protein`.
* **Standalone Fallback:** When cut off from trade routes, the clans process their own wool on hand spindles, self-sustaining without external supply.
