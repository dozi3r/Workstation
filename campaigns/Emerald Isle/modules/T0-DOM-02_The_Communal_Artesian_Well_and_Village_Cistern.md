# T0-DOM-02: The Communal Artesian Well and Village Cistern
*Domain: DOM-02 Water & Hydrology | Pipeline Tier: 0 (Baseline Substratum) | Set: Set A*

---

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Hydrological Reality

Water is the absolute chronological ceiling of tabletop survival: a human body survives thirty days without food, but collapses into organ failure within **three days without potable water**. Pre-industrial settlements require **8–10 liters per adult daily** for drinking and cooking, plus **30–50 liters daily per draft ox or plow horse**. This mundane reality relies on three physical engineered elements:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │       VILLAGE ARTESIAN HYDROLOGY CORE        │
                  └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                         │
                 ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
                 ▼                       ▼                       ▼
    [THE STONE-LINED SHAFT]   [THE TIMBER WINDLASS CORE]  [THE OVERFLOW RUNNEL]
    • 40-foot ashlar cylinder • Double-crank bucket hoist • Paved cattle trough
    • Waterproof hydraulic mortar • Pitch-tarred oak buckets • Vegetable patch sluice
    • Deep sand filtering bed • Iron safety grating lock   • Wash-house runnel
```

* **The Dry-Stone Ashlar Shaft**: Dug down 30–60 feet until reaching impermeable limestone bedrock or water-bearing gravel strata. Lined with dressed fieldstone bedded in slaked hydraulic lime mortar, preventing muddy surface seepage from contaminating the sweet deep groundwater.
* **The Counter-Weighted Timber Windlass**: A heavy oak drum turned by iron crank handles, carrying hemp or rawhide rope coiled to drop two 5-gallon iron-banded buckets alternately. An iron padlock grill covers the shaft mouth at night to prevent vandalism, livestock accidents, or intentional carcass sabotage.
* **The Overflow Cattle Trough & Wash Runnel**: Water drawn from the well flows first into drinking buckets, then drains into an external curved stone trough for cattle and horses, and finally discharges into a paved gutter irrigating cabbage patches and hemp gardens.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism

* **The Stasis / The Lie**: "The deep earth springs are inexhaustible and belong to everyone; no landlord can fence the rain that falls from heaven."
* **The Crucible / Conflict**: A 90-day seasonal drought lowers the regional water table below the shaft floor; simultaneously, an upstream tanning pit dumps caustic arsenic broth into the hillside aquifer, sickening half the children in the village.
* **Dramatic Syllogism**:  
  $$\text{Shared Hydrological Commons} + \text{Aquifer Depletion / Upstream Contamination} = \text{Mortal Water Feuds \& Settlement Evacuation}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis**: Potable water is the invisible pivot of human sanity. When clean water ceases to flow from the communal bucket, all laws, kinship loyalties, and civilized morality dissolve into desperate, blood-soaked bucket riots within seventy-two hours.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (Parish Well-Warden):** An incorruptible village bailiff whose family has tended the artesian well for three generations. Ringing a bronze hand-bell at sunrise and dusk, they permit each hearth two wooden pails of water and strike down anyone who tries to water private cattle before common children drink.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (Upstream Tanner & Dyer):** The master of an industrial leather-tanning workshop situated 300 yards uphill. Bound to deliver fifty cured war-saddles to the sovereign cavalry, they flush lime-slurry, crushed dog dung, and arsenic runoff into hillside sinkholes, claiming imperial defense overrides village tea kettles.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (Private Cistern Speculator):** An enterprising tavern owner whose slate roofs channel every raindrop into a locked, subterranean 10,000-gallon cistern. As the communal well scrapes mud, they sell water at two copper pence per pitcher, buying up desperate neighbors' family heirlooms and field plots.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (Blind Water-Dowser & Dredge Diver):** A blind hermit who lives beside the drainage ditch. Armed with a bifurcated hazel fork and copper wire, they are lowered down 50-foot shafts on a hemp sling to dig out choking gravel and silt, knowing the subterranean fault lines that can tap pure artesian veins or open sulfur vents.

### 4. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 4.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Hydrological Stress

* **Level 1 (Sweet Artesian Springhead / Light Severity Tier - DC 10):**  
  * *Condition:* Springhead flowing at full head; stone troughs crystal clear; communal wash-house bustling.
  * *Mechanics:* All short rests recover maximum hydration benefits. Clearing moss or replacing worn leather bucket washers requires a **DC 10 Athletics or Craft check**.
* **Level 2 (Routine Windlass Draw & Silty Drip / Moderate Severity Tier - DC 13):**  
  * *Condition:* Water level normal; slight mineral cloudiness after rainstorms; bucket windlass creaks.
  * *Mechanics:* Standard operational wear. Repairing tangled ropes or mediating queue squabbles requires a **DC 13 Athletics or Persuasion check**; failure causes **2d8 bludgeoning or equipment damage** (snapped crank handle or dropped bucket).
* **Level 3 (Aquifer Drawdown & Brackish Sump / Severe Severity Tier - DC 16):**  
  * *Condition:* Water table plunges 20 feet; bucket scrapes gravel and sulfur-scented grey muck; water rations cut to 4 liters/day; dysentery cases surge.
  * *Mechanics:* Drinking unboiled water requires a **DC 16 Fortitude check**; failure inflicts **4d10 poison damage**, dehydration cramps, and severe weakness. Deepening the shaft requires dangerous **DC 16 Mining or Athletics checks**.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Poisoning & Complete Desiccation / Lethal Climax Tier - DC 20):**  
  * *Condition:* The well runs bone-dry or a rotting carcass/sulfur breach contaminates the reservoir with lethal cholera; animal troughs filled with dead flies; riotous mobs torch the well-warden's cottage.
  * *Mechanics:* Total hydrological failure. Inhabitants without personal water flasks must succeed on a **DC 20 Survival check** every 12 hours; failure inflicts **8d12 lethal dehydration and toxicity damage**. The settlement must migrate within 48 hours or perish.

#### 4.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ] Aquifer Drawdown & Silt Clock (4 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ] Wellhead Contamination & Typhoid Meter (4 Ticks)
```

* **Aquifer Drawdown & Silt Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) during prolonged summer droughts, unseasonal freezes freezing upland feeder snowpacks, or intensive agricultural draining upstream.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Water table drops below the well sump floor, cutting flow to zero and advancing Dial Level to 4.
* **Wellhead Contamination & Typhoid Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) whenever manure floods the well apron, upstream tanning waste seeps into sinkholes, or rotting animal remains are discovered in cistern flumes.
  * *Mitigation:* Boiling water, scrubbing shaft walls with slaked lime, or redirecting upstream dye ditches via a **DC 16 Engineering or Medicine check** removes 1 tick.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Spatial Encounters

* **Encounter 1: The Midnight Well-Chain Tampering (Arrival Collision)**  
  * *Collision ($><$):* Arriving parched at the village well after nightfall, the party catches three shadowy figures attempting to hammer open the iron padlock of the windlass while another readies a sack of rotten sheep offal to drop down the shaft.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* Expecting to draw a harmless drink of water, the party is suddenly caught in a deadly sabotage attempt where swinging a weapon near the open shaft risks shattering the stone coping and losing the bucket forever.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The saboteurs claim they are starving tenants from an un-watered outer thorp whom the Well-Warden locked out, begging for mercy while clutching knives.
* **Encounter 2: The Sinking Dowser in the Sump (The Severance Dilemma)**  
  * *Dilemma:* Lowered 50 feet into the dry well to chisel open a collapsed aquifer seam, the Blind Water-Dowser triggers a violent underground sand pocket that collapses the timber shoring. The rope winch jams, and water mixed with liquid silt begins rising rapidly. The party must either leap down the narrow shaft on a safety rope (**DC 16 Athletics**) to pull the dowser out, or frantically crank the damaged windlass (**DC 13 Strength**) before the wellhead collapses completely.
* **Encounter 3: The Tanner’s Toxic Flume Skirmish (The Outcast Route)**  
  * *Outcast Route:* Tracing the foul sulfur stench upstream, the party infiltrates the uphill tannery where armed mercenaries guard the vats. The party can either fight the guards (**DC 13 Combat**) or sabotage the drainage diversion gate (**DC 16 Stealth/Engineering**) to send the toxic runoff harmlessly into a dead clay quarry.
