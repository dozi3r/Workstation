# MODULE STARTER-06: WHISPERING OLD WOODS & HERBALIST CLOISTER

### 1. Forest Layout & Flora
An old-growth hardwood forest of towering oaks and silver birch trees. At its heart lies a circular clearing ringed by standing stones, where forest herbalists maintain living root cottages.

* **Herbalist's Drying Shed:** Bundles of dried comfrey, willow bark, and bluebell root hanging from rafters; players can purchase healing salves (heals $1\text{d}4+2$ HP) for 5 silver pieces.
* **Whispering Sylvan Shrine:** A hollow grandmother oak whose murmuring leaves provide cryptic guidance to travelers who leave offerings of bread or honey.
* **Game Trails:** Narrow, switchbacking animal trails known only to forest rangers; grants safe passage bypassing road patrols.

### 2. GM Placement & Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 4 (Whispering Old Woods)** or **Node 9 (High Forest Canopy)**.
* **Synergies:** Supplies medicinal salves to **Node 11 (The Sylvan Abbey)** and logs timber for **Node 14 (The River Mill & Weir)**.
