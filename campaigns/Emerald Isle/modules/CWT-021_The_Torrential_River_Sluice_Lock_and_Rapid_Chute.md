# MODULE CWT-021: THE TORRENTIAL RIVER SLUICE LOCK & RAPID CHUTE

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Transit Affordance
A roaring hydraulic highway carved through a narrow limestone canyon. Where the river drops eighty feet over treacherous jagged rapids, an engineered sequence of timber lock-chutes and sluice gates allows flat-bottomed freight barges and skiffs to shoot downstream in minutes rather than portaging for days over rugged cliffs.

* **The Upper Flume Mouth:** Heavy oak guillotine gates held by iron ratchets. Water wardens divert river volume into the timber-lined chute, creating a slick hydraulic cushion.
* **The Devil’s Chute:** A half-mile polished timber flume where boats achieve speeds exceeding thirty miles per hour, navigated entirely with twenty-foot steering sweeps through boiling foam.
* **The Counter-Weight Towpath:** For ascending traffic, water-powered capstans wind heavy hemp cables along the canyon wall, slowly winching empty barges back up to the upper lake.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Water Bailiff Guild):** Bureaucrats who operate the winch capstans and collect cargo-weight tithes. They enforce strict barge inspection rules, routinely confiscating overloaded vessels.
* **Main Opponent (The Gorge Rapscallions):** Littoral wreckers and river pirates stationed on the high bluffs. They drop tethered boulders into the flume to founder rich merchant skiffs and harvest water-logged silks.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Timber Float Monopolists):** Lumber barons who flood the flume with unbundled redwood logs without warning, smashing small passenger boats to clear the chute for raw industrial timber runs.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Drowned-Recovery Scavengers):** Diver families living in driftwood shanties below the final plunge pool. They dredge the river bottom for corpses and lost coin purses, trading river lore for food.

### 3. GM Edge Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Edge Placement:** Recommended on **Edge #2 ➔ #14** or **Edge #14 ➔ #5** (connecting river headwaters down to wetland or delta nodes).
* **Transit Velocity:** Instant downstream transit (1 travel watch); ascending travel requires 2 watches and working capstans.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Converts into an unnavigable Class V whitewater deathtrap if sluice controls are sabotaged.
