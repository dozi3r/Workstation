# MODULE CWT-020: THE SUBTERRANEAN MINECART GRAVITY INCLINE

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Transit Affordance
An audacious feat of subterranean engineering cutting straight through the granite root of the central mountain range. Twin heavy bronze rail lines run fifteen miles down a continuous twelve-degree incline, linking high alpine mining redoubts directly to lowland smelter valleys. Heavy descending ore trains haul ascending passenger gondolas via a five-mile counterbalanced steel-and-hemp cable system.

* **The Incline Head (Upper Station):** A fortified timber-and-iron terminus perched on an alpine ledge. Giant grooved sheave wheels twelve feet in diameter regulate descending velocity using manual water-cooled friction shoes.
* **The Dark Run:** Carriages plunge into a narrow, unlit granite bore. Hand lanterns illuminate rushing rock walls mere inches from passengers' shoulders as air pressure pops ears during the two-thousand-foot drop.
* **The Terminal Bumper Basin:** A massive hydraulic buffer pit at the lower terminus designed to catch runaway carriages before they smash into industrial sorting docks.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Incline Directorate & Rail Bailiffs):** Ruthless concessionaires who own the cables and patents. They charge exorbitant toll-tickets for passage, search every passenger for hidden silver dust, and execute unauthorized rail-walkers.
* **Main Opponent (The Under-Tunnel Saboteurs):** Dispossessed mountain smugglers whose overland pack trails were ruined by the rail. Armed with iron spikes and sledgehammers, they pry loose rail plates to cause devastating derailments.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Black-Carriage Syndicate):** Corrupt brake operators who take bribes from fugitive nobles and assassin rings to smuggle human cargo inside hollowed-out slag vats beneath false coal floors.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Blind Rail Greasers):** Crippled laborers and orphaned children who dwell in hollows carved into the tunnel walls. Working with tallow buckets, they grease the guide rollers in the dark and know every hidden side-tunnel.

### 3. GM Edge Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Edge Placement:** Designed specifically for **Edge #8 ➔ #14** (Outer Alpine Redoubt to Hydro-Industrial Heart) or any steep mountain edge connecting Tier 2/3 alpine nodes to lowland valleys.
* **Transit Velocity:** Reduces standard 2-day overland mountain travel watches to a single 4-hour transit watch.
* **Standalone Fallback:** When cables snap, functions as a perilous foot-tunnel with Moderate Tier climbing and asphyxiation hazards.
