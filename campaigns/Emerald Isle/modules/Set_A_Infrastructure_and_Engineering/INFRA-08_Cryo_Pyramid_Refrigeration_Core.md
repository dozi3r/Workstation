# MODULE INFRA-08: CYCLOPEAN CRYO-PYRAMID & REFRIGERATION COILS

### 1. Thermodynamic Architecture

The mechanical source of the island's unnatural cold, maintaining an eternal pocket of glacial permafrost in the middle of subtropical hardwood forests.

* **Sunken Cyclopean Stepped Pyramid:** Megalithic basalt structure built by pre-convict civilization, partially swallowed by jungle loam.
* **Arcane Heat-Exchange Coils:** Massive humming bronze tubes running through the pyramid's core, venting compressed metaphysical cold into the surrounding ground.
* **Permafrost Ring:** The ground within 300 yards of the pyramid is frozen solid year-round. Trees grow covered in thick icicles, and ground moisture freezes instantly.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Mechanical Utility
* **Preservation Vaults:** Interior chambers function as zero-temperature vaults used to store perishable medicines, corpses awaiting post-mortem soul harvest, and dormant monster eggs.
* **Downstream Cold Wind:** Chilled air sinking off the pyramid creates heavy fog in lower elevation valleys, adding thermal camouflage against heat-seeking stirges.
