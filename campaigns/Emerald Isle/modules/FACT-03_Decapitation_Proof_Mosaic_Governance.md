# MODULE FACT-03: DECAPITATION-PROOF STATE ARCHITECTURE (THE MOSAIC DOCTRINE)

### 1. Governance Architecture

A governance doctrine engineered to ensure that a state cannot be destabilized or dissolved by the assassination or capture of its central leadership.

* **The Core Triad:**
  1. **Parallelism:** The state maintains two redundant armed forces with overlapping jurisdictions (e.g., the Territorial Militia defending borders vs. the Ideological Guard safeguarding the regime's core doctrine).
  2. **Subsidiarity:** Decoupled regional units (burrows, garrisons, guilds) possess standing orders and emergency autonomous budgets to operate as independent micro-states if communication with the capital is severed.
  3. **Institutional Persistence:** Authority is legally and ritually invested in an external artifact (e.g., the *Prime-Scale Soul-Mask* or the *Code of Precepts*). The moment a leader falls, the mask is placed on the designated successor, and state authority transfers in under 60 seconds without a power vacuum.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Player Subversion
* **Antidote to Murderhobo Assassinations:** When PCs assassinate the warlord or governor, the faction does not fracture into panic. Instead, the parallel leadership council immediately ratifies martial law, declares the deceased a martyr, and deploys pre-mobilized retaliation battalions.
* **The Expediency Council:** A deadlock-breaking arbitration body that resolves disputes between administrative bureaucrats and military commanders.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Decapitation Immunity:** Assassinating a Mosaic leader grants zero morale penalties to enemy units; advances regional alert by +2 levels immediately.
