# MODULE CWT-013: THE ESCHATOLOGICAL MIASMA & NECROTIC ECLIPSE

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Climax Trigger
Deep inside the lowest catacombs beneath the ancient necropolis, ancestral wards carved in lead and silver have corroded through. A dense, chilled vapor—the distilled psychic residue of ten generations of plague victims and forgotten slaves—is bubbling up into the municipal aqueducts, turning the living into sleepwalking wraiths and elevating the entombed dead to sovereign rulers.

* **The Stygian Wellspring:** A bottomless cenote where black water wells upward under unnatural pressure, emitting an odorless mist that extinguishes mundane lanterns.
* **The Incorporeal Tide:** As the mist spreads along river valleys and mountain gaps, incorporeal entities walk among the living, collecting "life tithes" measured in years of mortal vitality.
* **The Climax Vector:** When the 8-tick Eclipse Clock fills, the sky darkens permanently, freezing crops and establishing an eternal thanatocratic hegemony where only the dead hold legal citizenship.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Ossuary Arch-Priests):** Embalmers and high clerics who argue that death is merely a predictable administrative status. They register the deceased in municipal census rolls and harvest spectral labor to build cyclopean monuments.
* **Main Opponent (The Hearthflame Dawn-Wardens):** Zealous purifiers carrying naphtha sprayers and mirrors. They wage scorched-earth cremation campaigns, burning infested cemeteries along with any civilian showing pale skin.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Soul-Tax Vulture Syndicates):** Corrupt attorneys who draft posthumous debt contracts, enabling living citizens to sell their post-mortem labor in exchange for immediate grain bags.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Grave-Digger Communes):** Pariah laborers living on the edges of mass burial trenches. Immune to the early mist through exposure, they hide fugitive living refugees in empty sepulchers.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 18 (Deep Subterranean Caverns)** or **Node 21 (Apex Temple / Monastery)**.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Esot: Necrotic-Resonance-Catalyst` and `Geo: Glacial-Quartz-Meltwater`; yields `Arcana: Bound-Wraith-Phalanxes` which ignore overland physical barricades.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Dries into benign grave-dust if cold water conduits are severed.
