# MODULE STARTER-05: THE MOUNTAIN WATCHTOWER & SIGNAL PYLON

### 1. Bastion Layout
A three-story square stone tower built on an outcrop overlooking the high mountain defile. It serves as the primary early-warning defense for the island interior.

* **Stone Beacon Brazier:** A raised iron fire-basket on the battlements filled with pitch and seasoned cedar wood, visible up to 25 miles away.
* **Arrow-Slit Redoubt:** Narrow slits covering the single approach trail; provides three-quarters cover (+5 AC) to archers inside.
* **Provisions Vault:** Lower floor holds dry rations, canteens, and fletching tools to survive a 2-week siege.

### 2. GM Placement & Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 8 (Dwarf Watch Redoubt)** or **Node 21 (Ruined Mountain Fortress)**.
* **Synergies:** Directly guards the switchbacks leading down to **Node 7 (Mountain Waystation)** and warns **Node 13 (Market Cross Township)** of approaching raiders.
