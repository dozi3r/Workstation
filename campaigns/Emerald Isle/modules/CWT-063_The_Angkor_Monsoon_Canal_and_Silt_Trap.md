# MODULE CWT-063: THE ANGKOR MONSOON CANAL AND SILT TRAP

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Hydraulic Engineering Grounding
Drawing from the monumental hydraulic metropolis of Greater Angkor (the Khmer Empire, 9th–15th century CE), this module models the pre-industrial world’s largest water-management machine. Spanning over 1,000 square kilometers across a low-gradient alluvial plain, this artificial landscape was engineered to harness the extreme hydrological swings of the tropical monsoon. By trapping, buffering, and redistributing seasonal flash floods, the system supported a dense urban agglomeration exceeding 750,000 inhabitants and powered a hyper-intensive agricultural machine capable of delivering three to four rice harvests per year.

At the geographic and administrative heart of the machine sit the **Macro-Barays**—monolithic rectangular artificial reservoirs constructed not by excavating deep pits, but by piling colossal earthen dikes atop the flat plains. The West Baray, measuring 8 kilometers in length and 2.1 kilometers in width, holds more than **53 million cubic meters** of water elevated above the surrounding countryside. Fed by monumental stone diversion weirs across mountain rivers descending from the holy Kulen highlands (the Puok, Siem Reap, and Roluos rivers), these massive reservoirs utilize sheer gravity to feed an interconnected grid of thousands of kilometers of linear distribution canals, compartmentalized silt basins, and bamboo-gated paddy flumes.

```
       [Kulen Mountain Catchment]
                   │
                   ▼ (High-Energy Monsoon Runoff & Heavy Laterite Silt)
         [Masonry Diversion Dam]
                   │
         ┌─────────┴─────────┐
         │                   │ (Overland Feeder Canals)
         ▼                   ▼
  [West Baray Dikes]    [Silt Trap Settling Basins]
  (53M m³ Elevated)          │
         │                   ▼ (Scoured Water)
         └─────────► [Spean Thma Stone Sluice Gates]
                             │
                             ▼ (Gravity Distribution)
                 [Quad-Harvest Wetland Rice Grid]
                             │
                             ▼ (Inundation Discharge)
                 [Tonlé Sap Great Lake Pulse]
```

However, this hyper-specialized machine contains a fatal geomorphic vulnerability: **laterite siltation and riverbed incision under climate whiplash**. Tropical soils in the highland catchment are dominated by weathered laterite clays—heavy, iron-rich, and prone to rapid erosion once slopes are deforested for timber and swidden agriculture. During dry cycles, sluggish canal flows drop millions of metric tons of red laterite mud into settling channels, requiring continuous, grueling labor by hundreds of thousands of conscripted corvée workers armed with wooden scoops and wicker baskets.

Dendroclimatological records (*Fokienia hodginsii* tree-ring cores) demonstrate that multi-decade mega-droughts in the 14th and 15th centuries crippled this labor base, leaving canals clogged with baked silt plugs. When unprecedented, hyper-violent mega-monsoons returned, torrential waters carrying floating logs and boulders struck the choked canal mouths. Unable to enter the silted feeder channels, the roaring head smashed heavy masonry spillways (such as the Spean Thma bridge-weir) and carved a new course straight through the soft alluvium. The raging torrent **incised the riverbed 5 to 8 meters below its original elevation**, permanently stranding the stone intake sills of the entire canal grid high and dry above the water level. Deprived of gravity inflow, the world's largest irrigation network suffered systemic, irreversible failure, forcing the abandonment of the imperial capital.

* **The Spean Thma Masonry Sluices & Spillways:** Monumental sandstone and laterite water-gates utilizing heavy mortise-and-tenon stonework and vertical timber drop-beams. Positioned at critical canal junctions, these sluices regulate hydraulic pressure between highland torrents and city channels. Under extreme floods, their narrow spans induce severe water hammer vibration that can shear stone foundation piers.
* **The Elevated Macro-Baray Dikes:** Massive earthen levees standing 10 to 12 meters above grade, retaining tens of millions of tons of water solely through compacted clay mass and grass root systems. Hydrostatic seepage, saturated toe-slopes, or internal piping can trigger sudden catastrophic breach waves that inundate miles of settlements downstream.
* **The Compartmentalized Silt-Trapping Basins:** Wide, stepped rectangular pools built immediately downstream from diversion dams. Designed to drop the flow velocity of incoming mountain runoff so that heavy sediment settles before water enters the primary irrigation arteries, these basins require non-stop corvée dredging to prevent total silt choking.
* **The Quad-Harvest Wetland Rice Grid:** A hyper-engineered checkerboard of dikes, distribution ditches, and bamboo stop-gates spanning hundreds of square kilometers. By precisely controlling water depth throughout the bimodal monsoon cycle, state agronomists achieve four staggered crop yields each year, generating the enormous grain surplus that finances the empire's stone temple architecture.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Royal High Hydraulic Scribe):**  
  A senior imperial bureaucrat of the High Water Office who views the canal network as the sacred earthly reflection of cosmic geometry and divine kingship. Wearing silk vestments and brandishing measuring rods carved from ironwood, they command squads of royal halberdiers to drive 20,000 starving corvée peasants into waist-deep laterite sludge. Rather than divert dangerous flood surges into sacred royal temple moats or aristocratic pleasure parks, the Scribe will gladly sacrifice peasant outlying villages, rigidly adhering to ancient canal regulations written when monsoons were gentle and predictable.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (Upstream Kulen Foresters):**  
  Highland mountain clans dwelling in the forested Kulen catchment who reject imperial corvée edicts. Having clear-cut thousands of hillside acres for swidden farming and timber export, they have stripped the watershed of stabilizing tree roots, sending avalanches of red laterite mud downslope into the lowlands. When royal engineers erect diversion dams that back floodwaters into highland burial grounds, the clans deploy guerrilla saboteurs to dynamite or lever apart masonry weirs with heavy prybars.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (Canal Grain-Corvée Syndicates):**  
  A corrupt guild of canal tax-masters, barge captains, and grain warehouse overseers who operate private, illicit sluice-gates. While tenant farmers watch their communal paddies rot or desiccate, the syndicates quietly redirect stored Baray water to their private betel nut, sugar cane, and aromatic timber plantations. They withhold emergency rice rations during dredge cycles to drive land prices down, foreclosing on indebted peasants and turning free farmers into bonded plantation chattels.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (Tonlé Sap Floating-Village Fisherfolk):**  
  Stilt-dwelling and bamboo-raft foragers native to the Great Lake basin whose lives follow the natural flood-pulse inversion of the Tonlé Sap river. Contemptuous of rigid stone sluices, imperial tax tablets, and the grinding servitude of canal dredging, they navigate floodwaters in agile dugout canoes. They regard the gradual silting and structural rupture of the royal dikes as a holy ecological restoration that will tear down imperial fences and recharge the giant catfish migration corridors.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: The Hydraulic Overload & Riverbed Incision Dial
The operational strain, silt load, and hydrostatic pressure of the canal system are tracked across a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Laterite Silt Shoals & Low Head / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Sluggish water flow causes red laterite sediment to settle across canal bottoms; shallow channels form mud shoals at major junctions; transport skiffs scrape bottom.
  * *Mechanics:* Navigating flat-bottom barges or transport skiffs through secondary channels requires a **DC 10 Athletics or Vehicles check**; failure grounds the craft, imposing 1 hour of delay and minor physical fatigue. Scribe water rationing reduces field hydration; tenant unrest imposes a **DC 10 Insight or Persuasion check** to maintain orderly work shifts.
* **Level 2 (Monsoon Flash Flood & Sluice Vibration / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Torrential seasonal rains drench the plain; embankments turn to slick clay slurries; laterite-rich floodwaters surge through intake gates; wooden winches and stone sluice frames groan under hydrostatic head.
  * *Mechanics:* Traversing narrow earthen dikes requires a **DC 13 Balance (Acrobatics) check**; failure results in slipping into the churning canal, inflicting **2d8 bludgeoning damage** from floating timber and debris. Operating heavy wooden sluice winches or clearing timber jams from intake grates requires a **DC 13 Athletics or Engineering check**. Corvée workers exposed to unending rain and grueling shovel shifts suffer heatstroke/exhaustion checks (1 level of Exhaustion on failure).
* **Level 3 (Masonry Sluice Shear & Riverbed Incision / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Extreme runoff from deforested highlands overwhelms silt traps; hydraulic head shears stone sluice piers at Spean Thma; the raging river carves a new bypass channel that incises the bed 5 meters deep, stranding irrigation intake sills above the waterline and inundating adjacent residential quarters.
  * *Mechanics:* Raging flood currents sweep across low-lying terrain. Characters caught in the torrent must succeed on a **DC 16 Fortitude or Athletics (Swimming) check** or be swept downstream, suffering **4d10 bludgeoning water damage** and lasting injury (broken limb or lost gear). Gravity water flow into the secondary canal grid ceases permanently; surrounding paddies begin drying or rotting into stagnant bogs.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Baray Embankment Rupture / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Hydrostatic pressure breaches the eastern earthen levee of the 8-kilometer West Baray; over 50 million cubic meters of elevated water collapse in a roaring tsunami-like wall of mud, masonry debris, and uprooted trees that sweeps across the urban center.
  * *Mechanics:* Cataclysmic inundation. Characters and installations within the breach path must succeed on a **DC 20 Reflex or Survival check**; failure inflicts **8d12 lethal crushing and drowning damage** (instant character death or total devastation of structures). The hydraulic grid is completely shattered; the urban node permanently loses its agricultural production capacity, and the surviving population flees into the jungle hinterland.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Laterite Silt Choke Clock (6 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ]       Earthen Baray Hydrostatic Breach Clock (4 Ticks)
```

* **Laterite Silt Choke Clock [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each campaign cycle that corvée dredging gangs are disrupted, strike, or are reallocated to temple construction, or when flash runoff from deforested Kulen slopes flushes un-trapped laterite mud into distribution flumes.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* Feeder canal depth drops by 50%; transport barges cannot pass without towing lines; grain shipping times double across the city.
    * *Tick 4:* Secondary sluice gates become wedged shut with compacted red clay; emergency sluice winches strip their gear teeth under torque.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* The primary silt traps plug solid into immovable laterite hardpan. Feeder canals back up and spill over their banks, turning inhabited residential compounds into foul, knee-deep mud bogs and cutting agricultural yield by half.
* **Earthen Baray Hydrostatic Breach Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each monsoon deluge during which masonry spillways remain blocked, sluice release gates are held closed by scribes, or floodwaters exceed the safe levee crest height.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Seepage boils appear at the base of the eastern levee, bubbling brown silt-laden water into adjacent paddies.
    * *Tick 2:* Internal piping cavities collapse along the earthen embankment; top crest slumps by 2 meters.
    * *Tick 3:* Water begins overtopping the earthen crest along a 300-meter front, scouring deep rills into the downslope retaining wall.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Triggers **Dial Level 4: Catastrophic Baray Embankment Rupture** immediately. The earthen levee collapses catastrophically, unleashing a 50-million-ton tsunami wave across the plain.

### 4. Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Labor: Corv-e-Canal-Dredging-Crews`: Sustained mass labor of 10,000 to 20,000 conscripted workers equipped with digging poles, shovels, and reed baskets to continuously remove laterite sludge from traps and canal beds.
  * `Climate: Predictable-Bimodal-Monsoon-Cycle`: Regular alternation between the southwest summer monsoon (heavy rains) and northeast winter monsoon (dry season) to enable the timed fill-store-release cycle of the barays and paddies.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Agriculture: Quad-Harvest-Wetland-Rice`: Staggered hydrological flooding across hundreds of square kilometers yielding four annual crops of deep-water and wetland rice, producing massive caloric surpluses capable of feeding an imperial capital.
  * `Hydrology: Macro-Baray-Reservoir-Network`: Massive elevated gravity water storage (over 50 million cubic meters in the West Baray alone) providing year-round drought insulation, urban drinking water, and temple moat stabilization.
  * `Engineering: Masonry-Silt-Scouring-Sluice`: Advanced hydraulic masonry infrastructure comprising sandstone weir gates, scour flumes, and stepped diversion spillways capable of managing high-velocity flash floods.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When corvée dredging labor fails or mega-monsoon floods destroy sluice gates, the installation collapses into *Feral Swampland Mode*:
    * Canals choke on millions of tons of laterite silt; the river incises 5 meters below canal intake beds, permanently draining the irrigation grid into malarial marsh and reducing rice output by **85%**.
    * The geometric canal grid breaks into stagnant pools and mud slicks infested with mosquitoes and liver flukes, imposing **DC 13 Fortitude checks** against tropical swamp fever (1 level of Exhaustion and blurred vision) on all inhabitants every 48 hours.
    * Urban transport collapses as waterways become impassable; stone temples and timber dwellings are engulfed by invasive wetland bamboo, strangler figs, and flood debris.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks

* **Encounter 1: The Sluice Winch Mutiny (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* As the party arrives at the Grand Sluice Gate Junction seeking boat passage or trade goods, they find the installation in chaos. An upstream surge threatens to burst the masonry abutments, and the Royal High Hydraulic Scribe commands his armored halberdiers to commandeer the party’s pack animals, heavy carts, and muscle, chaining them to the colossal ironwood emergency winches to crank open the scouring gates. Simultaneously, a mob of three hundred desperate peasant farmers armed with bamboo staves and iron rice-sickles attacks the winch platform, fighting to smash the chains—because opening the royal gates will vent the floodwaters directly across their ancestral villages and drown their families to spare the King’s sacred lotus basins.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party enters seeking standard logistical river passage but is immediately forced into a lethal confrontation between desperate state engineers and insurgent tenant farmers.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The Hydraulic Scribe shouts frantic imperial proclamations about celestial harmony and divine order, but his trembling hands and bloodshot eyes betray the reality: he knows the masonry beneath his feet is fracturing and that the ancient flood manuals are completely useless against this mega-monsoon.
* **Encounter 2: The Silt Plug in the Grand Feeder (The Severance Crisis / Dilemma)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* At the monumental stone bridge-weir of Spean Thma, a catastrophic 200-ton logjam intertwined with red laterite clay has plugged the primary canal intake. The roaring river is already backing up, beginning to scour a deep bypass gully around the stone abutments.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Blast the Silt Plug with Alchemical Fire:* The party can use alchemical explosives or demolitions to blow the logjam apart. This clears the intake, preserves the canal grid, and prevents riverbed incision; however, the violent wall of water and debris unleashed downstream will completely wipe out an unmapped shantytown of 500 corvée dredge families living on the sandbars, drowning hundreds.
    * *Refuse Demolition / Attempt Manual Clearing:* Manual clearing requires dangerous labor amidst creaking timber (DC 13 Athletics/Engineering checks under falling logs), but will take too long. Within 6 hours, the floodwaters carve a catastrophic 5-meter incision in the soft riverbed, permanently stranding the intake sills, leaving 50,000 citizens downstream without irrigation water, and condemning the province to famine.
* **Encounter 3: The Lake-Pulse Pilgrimage (The Outcast Sanctuary)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Stranded in the flooded lower basin as canals silt over and roads wash away, the party is intercepted by Tonlé Sap Stilt Fisherfolk navigating in narrow dugout canoes through drowned jungle canopy. The fisherfolk offer to guide the party through secret flood bypasses and provide dry shelter on their floating bamboo platforms—in exchange for iron tools, salt, or help neutralizing an imperial river patrol that has been seizing their giant catfish traps to feed starving garrison troops.

### 6. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies

* **Recommended Placement:**

  * **Node 02 (River Waterfall & Water Intake / Chokepoint)**: Can represent the highland catchment and diversion weir descending from the Kulen hills.
* **Topological Synergies:**
  * **Macro Network Integration**: Directly outputs `Agriculture: Quad-Harvest-Wetland-Rice`, fulfilling grain prerequisites for urban nodes (such as `FACT-01` Sovereign Hubs or `CWT-015` Peasant Levee cores).
  * **Hydraulic Cascades**: Pairs with downstream river or delta modules (e.g. `CWT-021` Torrential River Sluice Lock or maritime port hubs), controlling downstream water flow and flood hazard levels.
  * **Industrial Synergy**: Can supply bulk food and timber floating capacity to heavy manufacturing modules like `CWT-060A` (Cypriot Copper Smelting Slag Foundries) or `CWT-043` (Colossal Foundry).
