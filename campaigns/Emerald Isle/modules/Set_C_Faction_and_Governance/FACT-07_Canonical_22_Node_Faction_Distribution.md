### 3. Canonical the campaign network Node Sociological Profiles

#### Node #1: The Silver Outpost (Perimeter Bastion)
* **Sociological Baseline:** **Hierarchy (Deliver Profile)** | *Alignment:* White
* **Operational Reality:** A fortified military harbor under permanent martial law. Stirges swarm the tree-line nightly, demanding continuous acetylene flame screens (`INFRA-03`) and blue-burning Zim-Sharr braziers (`INFRA-09`).
* **Encounter Atmosphere:** Mandatory 12-hour quarantine in damp iron cells upon arrival; weapons peace-bonded with stamped lead seals; three-tier bureaucratic clearance required for grain rations.

#### Node #5: The Blackmire Swamp (Arcane Chemical Refineries)
* **Sociological Baseline:** **Adhocracy / Market Hybrid** | *Alignment:* Blue/Black
* **Operational Reality:** A cutthroat boomtown constructed on elevated timber boardwalks over boiling swamp water. Rival distillers race to extract enzyme secretions from carnivorous pitcher plants (`INFRA-05`).
* **Encounter Atmosphere:** Constant patent disputes in soggy waterfront taverns; bounties offered hourly for live venom glands; regular risk of rotten plank collapses and sulfur steam blowouts.

#### Node #11: The Temple Sanctum (Panopticon High Monastery)
* **Sociological Baseline:** **Extreme Hierarchy (Deliver Matrix)** | *Alignment:* White/Gold
* **Operational Reality:** The theocratic summit of island governance. Monks chant in unison around massive resonant bells that ward off aerial fauna. Citizens are issued silver bracelets under the guise of holy benedictions (`CONF-05`).
* **Encounter Atmosphere:** Extreme psychological conformity; open dissent diagnosed as spiritual contamination; sealed archives guarded by mute inquisitors; punishment involves dredging toxic sludge from lower brazier flues.

#### Node #14: The Industrial Waterfall & Dynamo (Arc Smelting Core)
* **Sociological Baseline:** **Market Production Mill (Produce Profile)** | *Alignment:* Blue/Red
* **Operational Reality:** A deafening gorge complex driven by a 50-foot Pelton wheel dynamo (`INFRA-01`) powering carbon arc smelting furnaces (`INFRA-02`).
* **Encounter Atmosphere:** Deafening hydraulic roar requiring hand signals; soot-stained laborers suffering from chronic metal-fume cough; foreman contracting mercenaries to violently disrupt clandestine labor organizing.

#### Node #20: Corsair Freehold / Pirates Cove (Naval Salvage)
* **Sociological Baseline:** **Clan / Adhocracy Hybrid** | *Alignment:* Red/Black
* **Operational Reality:** A jagged sea-arch haven shielded by coral reefs. Run by a council of smuggler captains through oral agreements and shared prize distribution.
* **Encounter Atmosphere:** Raucous tavern hospitality for those carrying black-market silver or munitions; thieves tied to tidal rocks at low tide; active recruitment for raids on imperial transport barges.
