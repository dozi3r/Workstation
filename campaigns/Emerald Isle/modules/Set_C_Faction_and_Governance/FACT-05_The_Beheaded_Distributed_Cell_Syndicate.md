# MODULE FACT-05: THE BEHEADED DISTRIBUTED CELL SYNDICATE

### 1. Insurgent Architecture

A revolutionary criminal syndicate engineered to survive totalitarian crackdowns by eliminating central hierarchy entirely.

* **Headless Command:** "The Beheaded" has no king, general, or grandmaster. Orders are issued through mathematical code ciphers chalked on tavern walls, disguised radio broadcasts (`INFRA-06`), or coded verses in folk songs.
* **Cell Autonomy:** Each cell consists of 3 to 5 operatives tasked with a single objective (e.g., stealing black powder, assassinating an overseer, forging shipping manifests). If one cell is captured and tortured, they genuinely cannot reveal the names or locations of any other cell.
* **The Black Tide Connection:** Operates the primary black-market currency exchange, trading illegal Zim-Sharr torches and "Lung-Wash" for stolen convict silver.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Interaction
* **Subversive Infiltration:** Drops into any high-oppression node (e.g., Node 1 Outpost or Node 6 Quarries) as an underground resistance network.
* **Contract Work:** Players needing forged travel permits or untraceable weapons must prove their discretion through blind dead-drop trials.
