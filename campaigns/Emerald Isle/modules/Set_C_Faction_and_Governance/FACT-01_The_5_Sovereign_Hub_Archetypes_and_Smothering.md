# MODULE FACT-01: THE 5 SOVEREIGN HUB ARCHETYPES & ADJACENCY SMOTHERING

### 1. Macro-Political Architecture

The geopolitical balance of power is defined by the election of exactly **5 Sovereign Hubs** from an eligible pool of 14 candidate nodes.

* **The 14 Hub Candidates:** Nodes #1 through #11, Node #13, Node #14, and Node #20.
* **The Smothering Law:** Sovereignty is an aggressive, zero-sum gravitational force. When a candidate wins Sovereign Hub status, its economic and military weight crushes the political independence of every adjacent eligible neighbor, reducing them to subordinate **Tier 2 Satellites**.
* **The 5-Hub Council:** The top 5 non-smothered nodes become the regional superpowers, dividing the island into competing spheres of influence.

### 2. The 3 Systemic Geopolitical Gaps
Procedural smothering inevitably creates three structural fault-lines:
1. **The Core Vacuum:** Interior mountain nodes cut off from coastal trade, creating desperate resource raids.
2. **The Asymmetric Corridor:** Nodes caught between two competing hegemons forced into double taxation or guerrilla warfare.
3. **The Buffer Wilderness:** Smothered border zones where imperial law is absent, becoming havens for smugglers and escaped convicts.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Hub Influence Checks:** Hubs roll with Advantage when asserting legal jurisdiction or trade embargoes over adjacent satellites.
* **Satellite Rebellion Clock [6 Ticks]:** Smothered nodes harbor deep resentment; each failure to deliver promised grain or silver advances the rebellion clock.
