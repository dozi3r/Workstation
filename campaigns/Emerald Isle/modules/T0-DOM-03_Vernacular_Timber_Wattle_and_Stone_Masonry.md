# T0-DOM-03: Vernacular Timber, Wattle & Stone Masonry
*Domain: DOM-03 Shelter & Civic Municipal | Pipeline Tier: 0 (Baseline Substratum) | Set: Set A*

---

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Vernacular Reality

Mundane survival against the elements is not heroic; it is tedious, mud-spattered maintenance. A settlement's civic fabric protects its citizens from hypothermia, predator incursions, windstorms, and smoke-inhalation blindness. Pre-industrial shelter relies on three localized material technologies:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │          VERNACULAR SHELTER FABRIC           │
                  └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                         │
                 ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
                 ▼                       ▼                       ▼
    [SPLIT-OAK TIMBER CRUCKS] [WATTLE-AND-DAUB SCREENS]  [SLATE & THATCH ENVELOPE]
    • Heavy curved timber pairs• Hazel-rod interwoven laths • Water-shedding reeds
    • Mortise-and-tenon joints • Clay, cow-dung, and straw • Lime-wash fire barrier
    • Stone pad foundations    • Draft-proofing plaster   • Chimney flue stack
```

* **Cruck Timber Framing**: Built from paired, curved oak limbs split down the center to form natural pointed arches. Resting on dry-stone footings to prevent ground moisture from rotting the base timbers, crucks support the entire roof weight without requiring expensive sawmills or iron fasteners.
* **Wattle-and-Daub Infill Panels**: Flexible hazel or willow branches woven horizontally through vertical oak studs, then plastered thickly on both sides with a mix of sticky river clay, chopped straw, and fresh cattle dung. Once dried, panels are whitewashed with slaked lime, creating windproof, moderately fire-resistant walls.
* **The Thatch & Shingle Weatherproof Cap**: Roofs thatched with water-reed bundles 14 inches thick, or layered with split slate shingles bedded in sheep wool. Crucially, a well-built chimney flue draws wood smoke upward, preventing blinding soot accumulation inside sleeping lofts.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism

* **The Stasis / The Lie**: "Stone and oak stand forever once raised; as long as our hearth fires burn, the winter cold can never penetrate our walls."
* **The Crucible / Conflict**: An unseasonal autumn gale tears the thatch off forty family cottages; simultaneously, dry chimney sparks ignite the miller's hayloft, and the village carpenter discovers dry-rot beetle larvae hollowing the central parish rafters.
* **Dramatic Syllogism**:  
  $$\text{Vernacular Timber-Thatch Density} + \text{High-Wind Hearth Sparks / Neglected Maintenance} = \text{Catastrophic Settlement Conflagration \& Freezing Winter Exposure}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis**: Warmth and shelter are a precarious covenant between flammable thatch and glowing hearth coals. A single uncared-for chimney spark or rotted king-post can render three hundred souls shivering refugees overnight.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (Parish Master Carpenter):** A meticulous master craftsman who inspects every cottage roof annually. Knocking on king-posts with a bronze mallet, they will declare a family's ancestral home a fire hazard and order it pulled down with iron hooks to save the street.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (Charcoal-Burner & Shingle Splitter):** An aggressive woodsman who claims the ancient, tabu highland pine grove must be clear-cut immediately to replace rotten roof timbers, threatening to lynch any druid or forest warden who stands between shivering children and warm timber.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (Slate Quarry Cartel Factor):** An opportunistic stone merchant who controls the local slate quarry. While thatched cottages burn, they refuse to barter slate shingles for grain, waiting until burned-out families surrender their land deeds in exchange for fireproof stone roofing.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (Chimney Sweep & Mud-Dauber):** An orphaned laborer coated in chimney soot and clay who crawls through 12-inch stone flue vents to scrape creosote pitch. Ignored and reviled, they know which houses are one spark away from incinerating, and which walls hide secret priest-holes or hoard caches.

### 4. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 4.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Shelter & Municipal Integrity

* **Level 1 (Weatherproof Ashlar & Pitch-Sealed Thatch / Light Severity Tier - DC 10):**  
  * *Condition:* Roofs tight against rain; lime-wash fresh; chimneys draw clean smoke; sleeping quarters dry and warm.
  * *Mechanics:* Long rests completely remove mundane exhaustion. Minor maintenance requires a **DC 10 Craft (Carpentry) or Athletics check**.
* **Level 2 (Drafty Gables & Damp Hearth Flues / Moderate Severity Tier - DC 13):**  
  * *Condition:* Minor leaks in bedroom lofts; smoke fills kitchens on windy days; wet firewood smolders.
  * *Mechanics:* Characters resting without private blankets make a **DC 13 Constitution save** or suffer disadvantage on initiative from shivering joints. Shoring leaky thatch requires a **DC 13 Athletics or Survival check**; failure inflicts **2d8 bludgeoning or cold damage**.
* **Level 3 (Timber Rot & Collapsed Rafters / Severe Severity Tier - DC 16):**  
  * *Condition:* Heavy roof beam fractures under wet snow or gale winds; icy rainwater floods earthen floors; hypothermia cough spreads.
  * *Mechanics:* Evading falling roof timbers requires a **DC 16 Reflex save** or takes **4d10 bludgeoning damage**. Sleeping in soaked cottages prevents natural hit point recovery without magical heating.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Civic Conflagration / Lethal Climax Tier - DC 20):**  
  * *Condition:* High winds carry chimney sparks into dry thatch; an entire residential quarter erupts in an uncontrollable firestorm; burning beams collapse across narrow streets.
  * *Mechanics:* Catastrophic municipal inferno. Escaping burning streets requires a **DC 20 Reflex or Athletics check**; failure inflicts **8d12 fire and smoke inhalation damage**. Over 50% of inhabitants are rendered homeless in winter, triggering mass refugee flight.

#### 4.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ] Municipal Fire Conflagration Clock (4 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ] Rafter Rot & Structural Decay Meter (4 Ticks)
```

* **Municipal Fire Conflagration Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) during dry summer droughts, gale-force windstorms, or whenever open fire hazards are ignored near thatched roofs.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Triggers **Dial Level 4: Catastrophic Civic Conflagration**.
* **Rafter Rot & Structural Decay Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each prolonged rainy season without fresh pine-tar applications or thatch maintenance.
  * *Mitigation:* Sponsoring timber replacements or scraping chimney soot via a **DC 16 Craft check** clears 1 tick.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Spatial Encounters

* **Encounter 1: The Rafter Collapse on the Cradle (Arrival Collision)**  
  * *Collision ($><$):* As the party enters the village inn during a torrential downpour, a violent crack echoes overhead as the central oak tie-beam snaps under the weight of waterlogged slate, sending heavy rafters and slate tiles crashing down toward the crowded common room.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party enters seeking dry food, but is instantly forced to catch a 500-lb timber beam (**DC 16 Strength check**) or tackle trapped commoners out of the crush path.
* **Encounter 2: The Spark on the Thatch Ridge (The Severance Dilemma)**  
  * *Dilemma:* A chimney spark ignites the dry thatch ridge of a cottage while sixty-mile-an-hour gale winds blow straight toward the communal granary. The party must choose whether to climb the burning cottage roof with wet blankets (**DC 16 Athletics**) to smother the flames, or demolish the adjacent connecting hay-shed with axes (**DC 13 Strength**) to create a firebreak, sacrificing an old widow's winter fodder.
* **Encounter 3: The Chimney Sweep’s Secret Flue (The Outcast Route)**  
  * *Outcast Route:* To escape hostile bailiffs or bypass a locked barricade, the Chimney Sweep guides the party through interconnected attic crawls and ancient stone chimney flues (**DC 13 Acrobatics/Stealth**), bypassing perimeter guards completely.

### 6. GM Placement, Settlement Scale & Topological Synergies

* **Recommended Placement:**
  * **All Settlement Categories**: Standard infrastructure for Thorps, Hamlets, Villages, and Market Towns.
  * **Highland Timber Coupling**: Sockets directly to timber-producing forestry nodes (`Set B`), consuming lumber to maintain civic stability.
* **Topological Synergies:**
  * **Dual-Mode UX**: In "Smooth Generic" mode, satisfies all civic shelter needs implicitly. In "Granular Mode", provides `Shelter: Weatherproof-Civic-Fabric`, preventing cold weather penalties for troops garrisoned in the node.
