# MODULE FACT-02: THE 2-AXIS SOVEREIGN FACTION TENSION MATRIX

### 1. Sociological System Design

Factions on the campaign network are not simple good vs. evil alignments; they exist along two fundamental systemic axes of survival:

```text
                        [AXIS 1: ORDER / LAW]
                        The Argent Covenant
                        Imperial Magistrates
                                 ▲
                                 │
[AXIS 2: ECOLOGICAL STEWARDSHIP] │ [AXIS 2: EXTRACTION / SIPHON]
The Sylvan Fey Court             ┼  Iron-Hob Legion Foundries
The Backslope Herders            │  The High Mint & Siphon Masters
                                 │
                                 ▼
                       [AXIS 1: ANARCHY / FREEDOM]
                       The Black Tide Smugglers
                       Canopy Glider Exiles
```

### 2. The 4 Dynamic Faction Quadrants
* **Quadrant I (Law & Extraction):** Imperial Colonial Administration. Believes survival requires total discipline, forced labor quotas, and continuous feeding of the Soul Siphon.
* **Quadrant II (Law & Stewardship):** Templars of the Sacred Lake. Believes survival requires moral purity, strict hydrological conservation, and ritual quarantine.
* **Quadrant III (Anarchy & Stewardship):** Fey Glade Clans & Highland Herders. Believes survival requires living in harmony with island ecology and destroying all ferrous machines.
* **Quadrant IV (Anarchy & Extraction):** Black Tide Corsairs & Bandit Toll-Lords. Believes survival is purely opportunistic, fencing contraband silver and selling arms to all sides.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Diplomatic Leverage Check (2d6):** When negotiating between factions, PCs gain +2 if appealing to shared positions on either axis; suffer -2 if proposing compromises that bridge opposing poles.
