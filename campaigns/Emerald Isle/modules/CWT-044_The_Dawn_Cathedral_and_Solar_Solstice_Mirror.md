# MODULE CWT-044: THE DAWN CATHEDRAL & SOLAR SOLSTICE MIRROR

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Heliocentric Sanctum
Crowning an east-facing cliff overlooking the capital valley, the Dawn Cathedral is a marvel of architecture and optics. Its eastern transept is dominated by a forty-foot parabolic mirror assembled from hundreds of hand-burnished, gilded bronze segments. At dawn, as the first rays crest the mountain horizon, the mirror captures the golden light and concentrates it into a sunken marble healing pool at the center of the nave, filling the vast stone basilica with blinding, incandescent warmth.

* **The Gilded Parabola:** A monumental concave dish mounted on oiled bronze gimbals, turned each morning by four massive water-filled counterweight pistons.
* **The Solstice Crucible:** A raised basalt altar at the parabolic focal point where silver ingots can be melted into holy coins and alchemical plasma vials are charged.
* **The Nave Amphitheater:** Semicircular tiers of white marble seating three thousand citizens, arranged so every worshiper receives direct reflected sunlight.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The High Solar Prelates):** Charismatic clergy clad in gold-threaded chasubles who preach that order, truth, and warmth are identical; they command absolute civic obedience during morning prayers.
* **Main Opponent (The Nocturnal Crypt-Covenant):** An underground movement worshiping the cool silence of deep caverns, plotting to shatter the gilded mirror with catapulted stones during solstice mass.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Inquisitorial Ray-Wardens):** Zealous magistrates who use the concentrated focal beam as an execution engine, burning condemned heretics and prisoners of war to white ash in public ceremonies.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Blind Sun-Gazers):** Destitute ascetics who stared into the mirror's focus during ecstasy and lost their vision, now begging for crumbs outside the south bronze gates.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 01 (Sovereign Cathedral / Capital)**, **Node 09 (High Sun Ridge)**, or any cultural high point commanding valley views.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Material: Gilded-Bronze-Parabola` and `Geography: East-Facing-Sun-Ridge`; outputs `Governance: Radiant-Civic-Unity` (suppresses civil unrest across all connected settlements) and `Esoteric: Focused-Solar-Plasma`.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Maintains profound moral authority through ceremonial candlelight even during heavy winter cloud cover.
