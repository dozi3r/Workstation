# MODULE SPARK-05: INDEPENDENT CONSPIRACY: THE HOLLOW ARCHITECTS

### 1. Conspiracy Arc Design

An independent modular conspiracy engine where the true threat is an extradimensional bureaucratic foreclosure of reality itself.

* **Rule of Use:** The GM picks **ONE** independent conspiracy at the start of the campaign. That faction is the real mastermind; all other factions and rumors are pawns, myths, or unwitting fronts.
* **The Core Horror:** The ruling class is not corrupt—they are literally non-human parasites wearing human skin, systematically harvesting the population to balance a cosmic energy debt.
* **The Climax:** Storming the grand city hall or cathedral, discovering that the masonry walls bleed, the furniture is alive, and the Archpriest is molting out of a human corpse to notarize the eviction of the human race.
