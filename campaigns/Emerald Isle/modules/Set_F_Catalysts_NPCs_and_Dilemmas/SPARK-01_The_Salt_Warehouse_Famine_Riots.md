# MODULE SPARK-01: THE SALT WAREHOUSE FAMINE RIOTS

### 1. Catalytic Scenario Seed

A high-tension urban crisis triggered when colonial administrators withhold life-preserving mineral salt from starving convict workers.

* **The Core Grievance:** Without salt, fish catches rot in 24 hours and laborers suffer severe heat cramps and dehydration. The Outpost Magistrate has locked the central stone salt silos, hoarding salt exclusively for export to imperial warships.
* **The Mob:** Hundreds of emaciated convicts armed with iron pry-bars, fish gaffs, and heavy dock hooks surround the warehouse perimeter.

### 2. GM Clock & Escalation
* **Tick 1:** Shouted insults, stone throwing, guards lowering crossbows to half-cock.
* **Tick 2:** Convicts ramming the iron-reinforced warehouse oak doors with a shipyard spar.
* **Tick 3:** Guards fire a volley into the crowd; 3 convicts killed; mob surges forward in blood fury.
* **Tick 4:** Warehouse breached and set ablaze; salt bags destroyed; full-scale urban mutiny.

### 3. Resolutions & Fallout
* **Diplomatic De-escalation:** PCs negotiating a ration compromise or exposing magistrate corruption can halt the clock.
* **Violent Suppression:** Backing the guards protects the salt, but turns the working population permanently hostile.
