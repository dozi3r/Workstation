# MODULE SPARK-02: THE LIVING STATUE WARD & THE BLUE-ROT HORROR

### 1. Bodily Horror Architecture

A clinical and psychological horror scenario exploring the tragic fate of convicts afflicted with the island's endemic lithic fungus.

* **The Quarantine Barracks:** A row of drafty timber sheds filled with dozens of petrified victims in various stages of mineral transformation.
* **Stage 3 Torment (The Living Statue):** Victims in Stage 3 are completely rigid stone sculptures, yet their eyes remain moist and darting. They hear every word, feel every touch, but cannot breathe, weep, or scream.
* **The Black Tide Harvest:** The criminal syndicate harvests the active fungal crystals from victims between Stage 3 and Stage 4. Removing the crystal kills the host, but maximizes torch potency.

### 2. GM Placement & Player Interaction
* **Medical Race:** PCs afflicted with Blue-Rot have 48 hours to secure a dose of "Lung-Wash" (`INFRA-05`) before advancing to the irreversible Stage 3.
* **The Mercy Dilemma:** A Stage 3 convict's family begs the PCs to euthanize their petrified loved one before the smugglers arrive with chisels.
