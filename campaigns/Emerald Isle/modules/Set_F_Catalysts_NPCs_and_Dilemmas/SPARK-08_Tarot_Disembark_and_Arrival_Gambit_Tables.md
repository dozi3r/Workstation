# MODULE SPARK-08: TAROT DISEMBARK & ARRIVAL GAMBIT TABLES

### 1. Conceptual Engine & Usage

When the player party first arrives at the campaign's entry node (harbor pier, gatehouse, or staging outpost), the Game Master draws one Major Arcana card to set the initial tone, environmental hazard, starter perk, and institutional friction.

* **Upright Card:** The landing operates under favorable conditions. The party receives an immediate tactical perk or social advantage.
* **Reversed Card:** The landing suffers chaotic complications. The party begins with an institutional debt, minor injury, or social friction.

### 2. Universal System-Agnostic Disembark Table (22 Major Arcana)

| Card | Upright Arrival (The Event & Party Advantage) | Reversed Arrival (The Complication & Burden) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0 The Fool** | **Fresh Slate:** Dockworkers assist with heavy baggage. *Advantage:* Party gains a bonus general survival or repair kit. | **Lost Cargo:** An unsecured crate tumbles into deep water. *Burden:* A non-essential piece of starting luxury or gear is lost. |
| **I The Magician** | **Flawless Wards:** Defensive perimeter braziers burn steady and bright. *Advantage:* Immediate insight into local ward mechanics. | **Flickering Spark:** Fuel line sputters; wards briefly fail. *Burden:* Arcane or technical casting focus dulled until first short rest. |
| **II The High Priestess** | **Consecrated Port:** Local ritual sanctum at peak potency. *Advantage:* A cryptic prophetic omen reveals a hidden hazard. | **Psychic Static:** Unsettling ground tremors or whispers. *Burden:* Disorientation imposes friction on vigilance and perception. |
| **III The Empress** | **Bountiful Harbor:** Fresh provisions and herbal air dispel fatigue. *Advantage:* Party gains initial resilience against environmental stress. | **Stifling Air:** Heavy smog and humidity choke lungs. *Burden:* Physical stamina taxed; party incurs lingering respiratory fatigue. |
| **IV The Emperor** | **Iron Discipline:** Garrisons offload cargo with clockwork precision. *Advantage:* Assigned light duties or rapid transit clearance. | **Martial Friction:** Guards harass newcomers aggressively. *Burden:* Party singled out by authorities for heightened scrutiny. |
| **V The Hierophant** | **Sanctuary's Blessing:** Sympathetic chaplain provides quiet aid. *Advantage:* Social protection or blessing granting defensive resistance. | **Dogmatic Tithe:** Corrupt religious officers demand extortionate tolls. *Burden:* Starting coin depleted or labeled heretical exiles. |
| **VI The Lovers** | **Common Cause:** Veteran laborers and exiles bond over shared work. *Advantage:* Immediate friendly contact and aid from dock hands. | **Sudden Betrayal:** An opportunistic crewmember steals valuables. *Burden:* Mistrust festers; social friction within allied factions. |
| **VII The Chariot** | **Momentum:** Logistics flow smoothly without bottleneck. *Advantage:* Party receives priority transport or freight voucher. | **Broken Axle:** Heavy cart tips on stairs; supplies scatter. *Burden:* Overexertion reduces overland movement rate until rest. |
| **VIII Strength** | **Surge of Resolve:** Laborers haul freight effortlessly. *Advantage:* Party is emboldened, immune to fear effects for 24 hours. | **Crushing Dread:** Bleak surroundings overwhelm morale. *Burden:* Hesitation imposes friction on reaction times and initiative. |
| **IX The Hermit** | **Secret Guide:** An eccentric scholar offers a concealed path. *Advantage:* Knowledge of a hidden tunnel bypassing customs checkpoints. | **Lost in the Smog:** Dense fumes separate the party for several minutes. *Burden:* Party begins their first encounter disorganized and isolated. |
| **X Wheel of Fortune** | **Unexpected Salvage:** An unclaimed cargo container sits on the dock. *Advantage:* Party discovers valuable rations or crafting materials. | **Adverse Wind:** Gales blow smoke barriers inland. *Burden:* Local vermin or scouts immediately harass the landing line. |
| **XI Justice** | **Strict Fairness:** High officer oversees inspection with integrity. *Advantage:* Party legally retains a concealed or restricted weapon. | **Bureaucratic Frame:** Corrupt guard plants contraband. *Burden:* Party saddled with an arbitrary processing fine or labor debt. |
| **XII The Hanged Man** | **Patient Harbor:** Vessel navigates tricky shoals without incident. *Advantage:* Tactical observation reveals guard patrol blindspots. | **Reef Lock:** Hull scrapes sandbar; party forced to wade ashore. *Burden:* Wet gear and shivering exhaustion penalize agility. |
| **XIII Death** | **Shedding the Past:** Old warrants forgotten across the border. *Advantage:* Free exchange of one background trait or proficency. | **Fateful Portent:** A prominent official dies upon landing. *Burden:* Inquest locks down port; party placed on suspect roster. |
| **XIV Temperance** | **Pristine Rations:** Fresh casks perfectly sealed and treated. *Advantage:* High resistance to local waterborne toxins. | **Fouled Water:** First water supply opened is stagnant and sour. *Burden:* Minor nausea or chemical allergy inflicts persistent strain. |
| **XV The Devil** | **Smuggler's Pact:** Local underworld offers fair gear trades. *Advantage:* Start with 'Favored Customer' rank with black-market fences. | **Indentured Bond:** Coerced into an exploitative labor contract. *Burden:* Underworld brokers hold enforceable claims over party services. |
| **XVI The Tower** | **Beacon Guidance:** Signal towers pierce the storm cleanly. *Advantage:* Heroic catch of falling crane rigging earns worker respect. | **Structural Quake:** Pier timber or cliff stairway collapses. *Burden:* Minor injury and loss of one non-essential starting supply. |
| **XVII The Star** | **Guiding Peak:** Distant landmark shines clear under moonlight. *Advantage:* Visual clarity restores morale and navigational confidence. | **Blinding Miasma:** Dense fog extinguishes visual guidance. *Burden:* Must navigate treacherous terrain in near-total darkness. |
| **XVIII The Moon** | **Subterranean Grate:** Keen eyes notice a submerged iron grate. *Advantage:* Insight into subterranean utility corridors. | **Paranoid Whispers:** Echoing wind mimics distant threats or voices. *Burden:* Hallucinatory dread creates fear of open spaces or fire. |
| **XIX The Sun** | **Roaring Warmth:** Great hearths push back the biting marine chill. *Advantage:* Welcoming warmth makes local non-combatants friendly. | **Spill Fire:** Careless spark ignites dry tar barrels on wharf. *Burden:* Minor fire burns and charred clothing damage. |
| **XX Judgement** | **Public Pardon:** Old criminal record cleared due to critical labor. *Advantage:* Free modest lodgings secured in the outer settlement. | **Branded Outcast:** Arrival announced with full criminal recital. *Burden:* Local settlers avoid party; trade carries prejudice. |
| **XXI The World** | **Harmonic Landfall:** Environmental tensions in perfect equilibrium. *Advantage:* Discovery of a reliable compass or true-path map. | **Hostile Rejection:** Unsettled local climate refuses long rests. *Burden:* Environmental friction delays rest recovery for 24 hours. |

* **The High Priestess:** Grand Coastline Rite at peak potency / 'Hell' levels beneath cathedral groan; guards look pale.
* **The Empress:** Floral smoke clears lung fatigue (+5 temp HP) / Oppressive humidity clogs lungs (DC 11 Con save vs coughing).
* **The Devil:** Black Tide syndicate offers fair gear pricing / Immediate mining debt contract enforced upon disembarking.
* **The Star:** Matterhorn peak visible under clear moonlight / Blinding fog rolls in; lighthouse beam scattered.
