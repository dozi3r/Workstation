# MODULE SPARK-06: INDEPENDENT CONSPIRACY: THE PROMETHEAN LODGE

### 1. Conspiracy Arc Design

An independent modular conspiracy engine where the villains are elite alchemical freemasons obsessed with escaping the mortal reality loop, regardless of collateral damage.

* **The Core Philosophy:** The material world is a penal cage designed to trap divine sparks in flesh. The Lodge believes that destroying the metaphysical anchors of the island will free human consciousness into pure solar light.
* **The High Master:** A charismatic visionary who promises infinite clean energy, miraculous healing dynamos, and freedom from imperial taxes, secretly funneling millions of watts of power into a catastrophic planar tearing engine.
* **The Climax:** Climbing the electrified brass needle tower during a violent hurricane, fighting clockwork marble constructs while the Grand Master initiates the ritual to vaporize the island's firmament.
