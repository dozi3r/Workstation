# MODULE CWT-073: THE HARAPPAN SANITARY GRID AND SARASWATI DESICCATION

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Archaeological Grounding
Anchored in the mature urban phase of the Indus Valley Civilization (circa 2600–1900 BCE) along the great alluvial basins of the Indus and the now-extinct Ghaggar-Hakra river system (identified in archaeological hydrology and historical geography as the sacred Saraswati), this module operationalizes humanity's earliest and most sophisticated municipal sanitation and metric standardization engine. Spanning over one million square kilometers across major civic hubs—Mohenjo-Daro, Harappa, Dholavira, Rakhigarhi, and the tidal port of Lothal—this civilization rejected monumental palaces, dynastic tombs, and standing militarism in favor of an obsessive civic focus on public hygiene, hydraulic planning, and metric commerce.

Archaeological excavations and paleoclimatological surveys (spearheaded by Jonathan Mark Kenoyer, Gregory Possehl, and Rita Wright) document five foundational civil engineering systems:

1. **The World's First Closed Municipal Sewerage Grid**: Unlike the organic, refuse-choked alleys of contemporary Mesopotamia and Old Kingdom Egypt, Harappan cities were surveyed upon strict cardinal grid plans aligned to true north. Domestic residential dwellings, constructed around central courtyards, incorporated dedicated second-story and ground-floor terracotta bathing floors and private gravity latrines. Wastewater flushed through terracotta pipes embedded directly inside the exterior masonry walls, discharging down angled slipways into covered brick sewers running beneath street pavements. These street drains were lined with finely fitted fired bricks, roofed with removable limestone or terracotta slabs for municipal cleaning access, and equipped with subterranean settling sumps (*inspection traps*) that collected heavy fecal solids while permitting effluent to spill across corbelled overflow sluices outside the city perimeter.
2. **Standardized Metrology & The 1:2:4 Brick Ratio**: Harappan spatial planning rested upon strict metrological uniformity enforced across thousands of kilometers. All fired masonry adhered rigorously to the **1:2:4 dimensional ratio** (thickness: 7 cm, width: 14 cm, length: 28 cm; with monumental variations maintaining the identical proportions). This exact mathematical ratio enabled uniform Flemish bond corner-locking without cutting or trimming bricks on site. Economic exchange was strictly regulated by polished, cubical chert weights following a synchronized binary-decimal sequence ($1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 \times 16 = 160, 200, 320, 640$), calibrated down to 0.857 grams. This metrological integrity formed an inviolate trade currency facilitating long-distance maritime commerce through the Persian Gulf to Dilmun and Ur.
3. **The Great Bath and Bitumen Waterproofing**: Crowned atop the artificial mudbrick citadel platform of Mohenjo-Daro sat the **Great Bath** (12 meters north-south by 7 meters east-west, descending 2.4 meters deep via dual symmetrical brick staircases). Engineered for elite or communal civic purification ablutions, the pool’s inner face was constructed of edge-laid fired bricks bonded with gypsum mortar. Crucially, a **3-centimeter-thick continuous layer of natural bitumen (asphalt)** was sealed between the inner brick shell and the outer structural retaining wall, backed by compacted clay—establishing the ancient world’s premier hydraulic waterproofing membrane. Water was supplied from an adjacent high-capacity cylindrical brick well and evacuated through a tall corbelled brick tunnel tall enough for an adult worker to walk through upright.
4. **The Enigma of Absent Autocracy**: Across hundreds of surveyed settlements, excavations have revealed no royal palaces, no dynastic mausoleums, no inscriptions praising military conquests, and no depictions of armed conquest or captured slaves. Governance operated via collective municipal councils, merchant syndicates, and sanitary inspectors. Social cohesion was maintained not by the monarchical whip, but by ritual purity, metric standardization, and the universal civic amenity of clean water and unchoked drains.
5. **The Saraswati River Avulsion & 4.2 ka BP Desiccation**: Paleohydrological satellite tracking and geological drill cores prove that the Ghaggar-Hakra was once a perennial glacial river draining the high Himalayas. Around 1900 BCE, tectonic uplifts in the Siwalik hills and alluvial fan faulting diverted its primary glacial feeders: the Sutlej River was captured westward into the Indus drainage basin, while the Yamuna migrated eastward into the Ganges. Compounded by the global 4.2 ka BP aridification event that weakened the summer monsoon, the Ghaggar-Hakra dried into an ephemeral seasonal wadi. Without continuous glacial streamflow, the municipal sewer grid lost its flushing velocity; solid human waste choked the street sumps; water tables collapsed below well depths; waterborne epidemics swept the population; and the planned cities were peacefully abandoned as populations migrated eastward to form decentralized farming hamlets along the Ganges.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "Our standardized 1:2:4 brick ratios and covered street sewers guarantee eternal civic purity; no king rules us with an iron sword, and the sacred river will flush our drains and fill our Great Bath forever."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** Tectonic tremors upstream deflect the glacial headwaters into foreign valleys; the sacred riverbed dries to cracked clay dust; covered street drains choke on putrefying sewage, igniting epidemic typhoid while wealthy merchants barricade the few remaining 80-foot cylindrical aquifer wells.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Obsessive Municipal Standardization} + \text{Tectonic Glacial River Avulsion} = \text{Systemic Sanitary Sclerosis \& Gradual De-Urbanization}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** Geometric brick perfection and civic consensus cannot manufacture water. When the living river abandons the grid, the cleanest metropolis on earth becomes an unflushable, toxic brick tomb.

#### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1: The Establishment (Chief Metrological Surveyor of the Brick Guild):**  
  The ultimate bureaucrat of standardized civic hygiene. Armed with calibrated bronze measuring rods and cubical chert weights, the Surveyor inspects private courtyards to ensure every latrine drain maintains its required downward gradient and every repair brick matches the 1:2:4 ratio. Refusing to admit that tectonic avulsion has severed the river, the Surveyor insists the crisis is an administrative failure of silt dredging, fining citizens whose drain inspection traps overflow and sending forced work gangs into dry riverbeds to dig futile silt-retaining dikes.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (Eastward Exodus Patriarch):**  
  Venerable leader of the agricultural and pastoral clans whose zebu cattle graze the parched floodplains. Having watched the seasonal monsoon retreat for three consecutive years and well water turn saline, the Patriarch preaches total, immediate evacuation. He demands that families dismantle their timber roofs, pack their seed grain, and abandon the holy brick cities to march toward the humid, untamed jungle basins of the upper Yamuna and Ganges. He views the city's covered drains not as monuments of purity, but as diseased serpent holes poisoning their children.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (Deep-Well Water Oligarchs):**  
  The consortium of lapis-lazuli, carnelian, and marine-shell bead merchants who possess the deepest 80-foot cylindrical brick wells in the elevated citadel. As municipal street taps dry up, these oligarchs hire private mercenaries armed with copper-bladed axes to secure their courtyard wellheads. They sell muddy, brackish groundwater at extortionate prices, demanding standard 16-unit chert weights of unworked electrum, tin ingots, and dried barley for a single clay amphora of water, accelerating civic polarization and starvation.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (Dry Hakra Mud-Scavenger):**  
  A destitute potter residing in makeshift reed hovels along the cracked, desiccated riverbed outside the western city wall. Ostracized by municipal society for handling river mud without guild licenses, the Scavenger possesses irreplaceable empirical hydrology: by following subterranean moisture gradients in the gravel strata, they locate shallow seepages beneath the dry river boulders. The Scavenger understands that the city's choked sewers have transformed the lower town into a subterranean biohazard incubator that will soon kill every citizen who remains.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: River Desiccation & Sanitary Grid Failure
The environmental, sanitary, and structural viability of the settlement is tracked across a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Drain Silt Accumulation & Low Well Head / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The Ghaggar-Hakra river drops below its seasonal intake sill; street drains flow sluggishly; fine river silt settles in the main collector channels; municipal inspectors conduct emergency audits of private courtyard sumps.
  * *Mechanics:* A persistent stench of decomposing organic sludge hangs over the lower residential quarters. Navigating narrow brick streets strewn with dislodged limestone inspection covers requires a **DC 10 Athletics or Engineering check** to avoid stumbling into waste sumps or twisting an ankle. Negotiating access to municipal cisterns or purchasing fresh well water requires a **DC 10 Bureaucracy or Persuasion check**.
* **Level 2 (Sewer Backflow & Metrological Currency Friction / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Covered sewers choke on un-flushed solid waste; gray wastewater backs up into domestic courtyards; public bathhouses shutter; merchants reject common grain barter, demanding payment exclusively in certified cubical chert weights.
  * *Mechanics:* Street gutters overflow with stagnant, foul wastewater, transforming street corridors into difficult terrain. Characters consuming municipal water or failing to sterilize their gear must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution check** against dysentery (inflicting **2d8 poison damage** and imposing disadvantage on all physical checks for 24 hours). Conducting trade or currency transactions requires a **DC 13 Appraisal or Insight check** to detect rigged or shaved weights.
* **Level 3 (Typhoid Outbreak & Wellhead Blockade / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Stagnant sewage pools in the heat, breeding epidemic typhoid and cholera; the Great Bath stands dry, its bitumen lining baking and cracking; Deep-Well Oligarchs barricade their 80-foot wellheads with bronze-tipped pikes and hired muscle.
  * *Mechanics:* The lower town becomes a contagion zone. Characters breathing the ambient aerosolized sewer miasma or moving within 30 feet of an open inspection trap must make a **DC 16 Fortitude check** or contract virulent sewer fever (**4d10 poison/necrotic damage** and 1 level of Exhaustion). Forcing access through wellhead barricades or calming riotous mobs of parched citizens requires a **DC 16 Intimidation, Athletics, or Persuasion check**.
* **Level 4 (Complete River Avulsion & Desiccated Brick Tomb / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Upstream tectonic capture permanently redirects the Saraswati headwaters into the Indus and Yamuna; the riverbed becomes a blinding expanse of cracked silt and dust storms; all citadel wells draw dry mud; municipal authority dissolves completely.
  * *Mechanics:* Total systemic collapse. The settlement ceases to function as an urban node. Characters remaining within the city without independent magical or sealed water reserves must make a **DC 20 Survival check** every 6 hours; failure inflicts **8d12 dehydration and plague damage**. Panic riots and building collapses turn the grid into a lethal labyrinth. The node converts into an archaeological ghost settlement, losing all trade links.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Saraswati Glacial Avulsion Countdown (6 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ]       Municipal Sewer Sump Choke Meter (4 Ticks)
```

* **Saraswati Glacial Avulsion Countdown [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each operational watch or campaign turn that upstream tectonic tremors occur, seismic shifts shake the Siwalik hills, or satellite river channels divert additional headwater volume into the Yamuna or Indus basins.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* River velocity drops by half; the Great Bath can no longer be filled via gravity intake and sits half-empty.
    * *Tick 4:* Perennial flow ceases; the river fragments into isolated stagnant pools; fish die en masse, choking the mudflats with rot.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* Triggers **Dial Level 4: Complete River Avulsion & Desiccated Brick Tomb** immediately. The glacial riverbed completely desiccates. The node suffers permanent hydrological failure.
* **Municipal Sewer Sump Choke Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) whenever municipal dredging crews strike, go unpaid, or are reassigned to defensive fortifications, or when latrine inspection traps remain uncleaned for more than 48 hours.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Terracotta street drainage grates vent rancid sulfurous gas; lower wards report widespread nausea.
    * *Tick 2:* Untreated domestic blackwater erupts through courtyard bathing platforms, flooding residential ground floors.
    * *Tick 3:* Main arterial sewer conduit ruptures under solid waste pressure, creating a 20-foot sinkhole of toxic mire in the central bazaar.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* The citywide drainage grid suffers total hydrostatic lock. Untreated sewage submerges the lower streets, instantly elevating the hazard dial to **Dial Level 3: Typhoid Outbreak & Wellhead Blockade**.

### 4. Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Hydrology: Perennial-Glacial-River-Flow`: Uninterrupted alpine meltwater descending from the Himalayas to maintain continuous flushing velocity through street-level covered drains and replenish shallow alluvial aquifer wells.
  * `Resource: Kiln-Baked-Mudbrick-Supply`: Massive volume of kiln-fired mudbricks (adhering to the 1:2:4 ratio) and timber fuel required to line water channels, build waterproof retaining walls, and replace erosion-degraded sewer tiles.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Infrastructure: Municipal-Sanitary-Drain-Grid`: A fully covered, gravity-fed street sewer network with subterranean inspection traps and solid-waste sumps, eliminating urban disease vectors when properly flushed.
  * `Metrology: Standardized-Cubical-Chert-Weights`: Incorruptible metric measurement standards (calibrated binary/decimal cubes) that eliminate fraud, stabilize market prices, and unlock cross-regional mercantile trust.
  * `Civil: Waterproofed-Bitumen-Great-Bath`: Monumental civic ablution architecture featuring high-capacity brick cisterns, gypsum mortar joints, and multi-layered natural bitumen membranes for ritual purification and disease containment.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When `Hydrology: Perennial-Glacial-River-Flow` drops to zero, the module transitions into **De-Urbanized Agrarian Hamlet Fallback**:
    * Covered sewers choke with dry dust and refuse; private bathing platforms are abandoned.
    * The population strips cedar timbers and roof beams from the two-story brick townhouses, abandoning the planned street grid.
    * The civic community fractures into decentralized, rural farming hamlets scattered across seasonal monsoon tributaries.
    * Standardized chert weights and un-deciphered seal scripts fall out of use, replaced by localized bartering of barley, cattle, and coarse pottery.
    * Urban population capacity plummets by 90%, and the node loses its mercantile connectivity.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks (writing-generation)

* **Encounter 1: The Inspection Sump Dispute (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party arrives at the First Eastern Gate of the metropolis escorting a caravan of grain and bronze ingots, but is halted by an armed detail of Sanitary Inspectors accompanied by the Chief Metrological Surveyor. The inspectors demand that all beasts of burden be unharnessed and quarantined outside the brick perimeter for "foreign river-tick infestation and uncalibrated dung carriage." When a stressed pack-mule steps upon a fractured limestone drain cover, the stone snaps, releasing a geyser of fermented black wastewater across the ceremonial avenue. The Surveyor immediately fines the party 16 standardized chert weights of refined silver and orders their cargo impounded until the street drain is dredged and repaired with certified 1:2:4 fired bricks.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party expects to enter a golden utopian civilization celebrated for supreme order and peace, but is immediately ensnared in a predatory, dysentery-splattered bureaucratic shakedown in the middle of a toxic street spill.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The Chief Surveyor passionately delivers a pious speech regarding how Harappa's metric cleanliness preserves the cosmic balance without the bloody whips of foreign kings, while his own fingers twitch compulsively as he uses a bronze toothpick to scrape crusted, dried typhoid vomit from the hem of his bleached linen robe.
* **Encounter 2: The Bitumen Siphon in the Great Bath (Resource Dilemma)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Within the sacred colonnade of the Great Bath atop the citadel, the water level has plummeted to a foot of green, stagnant scum. A desperate crowd of lower-town citizens, armed with bronze scraping adzes and iron chisels, has invaded the empty basin to pry off chunks of the precious 3-centimeter bitumen waterproofing layer, intending to melt the asphalt down to caulk cracked water jars and seal sickrooms against rising sewer stench. The Bath Guardians draw copper daggers, ordering the party to defend the sacred monument with lethal force. Amid the standoff, a fever-wracked mother falls to her knees before the party, pleading for a single slab of tar to waterproof the leaking crib of her infant dying of cholera.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Defend the Bath:* The party aids the Guardians in repelling the crowd (facing **DC 13 Athletics/Combat** checks to disperse the mob without slaughter). This preserves the ancient world's hydraulic masterpiece and earns the high patronage of the merchant council, but condemns dozens of poor families to waterborne infection and marks the party as heartless mercenaries of the oligarchs.
    * *Permit the Desecration:* The party stays the Guardians' blades and allows the mothers to salvage the bitumen. This saves dozens of lives in the immediate crisis, but strips the Great Bath of its watertight seal forever. The pool dries out, gypsum mortar crumbles under the sun, and the Citadel Council brands the party as blasphemous vandals, placing bounties upon their heads.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The High Priest of the Bath solemnly proclaims that every drop of bathwater is consecrated to the celestial rain spirits, yet as he speaks, a party member notices a concealed terracotta siphon pipe running from the rear drain directly into private copper flagons hidden inside the priest's personal palanquin.
* **Encounter 3: The Broken 80-Foot Wellhead (The Severance Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* At the citadel’s deepest cylindrical brick well—an engineering wonder plunging 80 feet through alluvial gravel to the last clean aquifer vein—the heavy braided hemp winding cable has snapped under strain, dropping the only copper hoist-bucket to the sludge-choked bottom. A frantic mob of three hundred parched citizens storms the raised brick platform, screaming for water. The Deep-Well Oligarch who owns the well orders his crossbowmen to loose into the crowd, while the pressure of the rioters threatens to topple the ancient brick parapet into the open shaft, which would bury the water table under forty tons of rubble.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party is in desperate need of clean drinking water to cure an infected companion, but stepping onto the well platform thrusts them into the crossfire between desperate, dying commoners and paranoid oligarchs on the verge of destroying the city's last potable water source.
  * *Tactical Resolution:*
    * *The Shaft Descent:* A brave character can rappel down the dark, crumbling 80-foot cylindrical brick tube (**DC 16 Athletics or Acrobatics check**) to hook the fallen bucket and clear the silt blockage before suffocating on pooled carbon dioxide.
    * *The Wellhead Defense:* Alternatively, the party can attempt to quell the riot (**DC 16 Intimidation or Persuasion check**) and disarm the oligarch's trigger-happy guards before the parapet cracks.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* As the Oligarch frantically promises the party infinite gold if they secure the well, he quietly leans over and whispers the horrifying truth: the well's bottom contains less than twenty inches of muddy water. Whoever drinks today will haul up nothing but bone-dry gravel tomorrow morning.

### 6. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies

* **Recommended Node Placement:**
  * **Node 3 (Civil Hub / Riverine Alluvial Basin)**: Ideal placement as an advanced trading metropolis along a primary river artery where players expect high civilization, only to find the community teetering on the brink of ecological and sanitary collapse.
  * **Node 14 (Desiccated Frontier Sump / Ancient Hydraulic Monument)**: Placed as an archaeological ruin or a dying border settlement, where the party must navigate toxic subterranean sewer tunnels to uncover buried metrological relics and engineering plans.
* **Topological Synergies:**
  * **Macro Collapse Cascade**: Sockets directly downstream from `CWT-060` (The Systemic Interdependence Collapse Matrix), demonstrating how the severance of inter-regional bronze trade and grain logistics cascades into local municipal and hydraulic failure.
  * **Hydraulic Interlock**: Links logically to `CWT-063` (The Angkor Monsoon Canal and Silt Trap), contrasting the seasonal monsoonal flood management of tropical Southeast Asia with the fragile glacial river reliance of the Indus basin.
  * **Aquifer Extraction Networks**: Pairs seamlessly with `CWT-034` (Deep Well Aquifer Siphon and Solar Distillation Array), providing a technological upgrade path where characters introduce solar condensers to extract fresh water from the saline mud of the dried Hakra.
  * **Metrological Commerce**: Interacts with any trading league or banking node, allowing players who secure genuine Harappan cubical chert weights to gain advantage on market appraisals and cross-regional trade negotiations.
