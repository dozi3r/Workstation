# MODULE ESOT-03: THE SATURNINE MAINFRAME & THE LINEAR TIME-LOOP

### 1. Eschatological Architecture

This module codifies the eschatological operating system governing linear cause-and-effect, mortality cycles, and temporal recurrence across pointcrawl campaign networks.

* **The Saturnian Mainframe (Cronus / The Black Cube):**
  * At the absolute center of the island's subterranean core lies a monolithic, polished black basalt cube that hums at an infrasonic frequency of 8 Hz.
  * *Function:* It broadcasts the local reality field—enforcing linear cause-and-effect, gravitational density, and physical mortality. Without the Cube, physical matter would dissolve into raw astral light.
* **The Trap of Linear Time:**
  * The Cube acts as a temporal flywheel. When mortals die on the campaign network, their souls do not ascend to an afterlife; they are reeled back into the linear loop by the Saturnine gravitational field, recycled into new convict bodies, and forced to mine their own spiritual prison.

### 2. The Two Ancient Factions Fighting Over the Keyboard
1. **The Saturnian Traditionalists (The Black Cube Keepers):** Believe the Cube is holy divine order that protects mortals from dissolving into cosmic madness. They worship stasis, discipline, and the preservation of the law.
2. **The Promethean Firebringers (The Solar Lodge):** Believe the Cube is a parasitic prison cage built by an indifferent Demiurge. They seek to build high-energy electric arc cannons (`INFRA-02`, `ESOT-06`) to crack the basalt cube and burn reality open into pure solar freedom.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Temporal Distortion Zone:** Within 200 feet of the Black Cube, spells that alter time or haste roll with double potency, but physical gear accumulates 2 ticks on the Iron-Blight clock per hour.
