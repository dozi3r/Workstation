# MODULE ESOT-04: AQUABAH: THE HANGING CANYON CLIFF-CITY

### 1. Architectural Splendor

**Aquabah** is an exotic cliff-dwelling vertical metropolis bridging ancient canyon cave architecture with opulent, gilded mercantile pavilions cantilevered over crashing breakers.

* **Vertical Urban Stratification:**
  * Instead of expanding across horizontal land, the entire city is chiseled into a 1,000-foot vertical limestone canyon amphitheater directly overlooking the ocean breakers.
  * **The Upper Alcoves (The Sultanate):** Opulent sandstone palaces adorned with turquoise mosaic tiles, soaring copper minarets, and silk-draped balconies jutting out over empty air.
  * **The Mid-Tier Terraces (The Grand Souk):** Labyrinthine bazaar streets carved into cavern ledges, aromatic with burning frankincense, roasted cardamon, and exotic perfumes designed to mask the sulfur air.
  * **The Lower Water Gates:** Hydraulic counterweight elevators powered by ocean tidal flumes that hoist cargo, draft mules, and travelers straight up from sea-level smuggler docks.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Political Neutrality
* **The Sovereign Mirage:** Aquabah pays lip-service tribute to the Imperial Outpost, but maintains total autonomy through its monopoly on maritime trade, spice smuggling, and privateer alliances.
* **The Hanging Gardens:** Fed by mountain meltwater diverted through intricate copper aqueducts, lush hanging date palms and jasmine vines cascade down the vertical rock face.

### 3. Vague Game Encounters & Hazards
* **The 500-Foot Balcony Duel:** Fighting along Aquabah's narrow silk-hung bridges requires Agility checks (2d6); critical fails risk plummeting into the churning black surf below.
* **The Street Thieves Guild:** Highly agile acrobats armed with grappling hooks and silk parachutes who leap between cliff levels with ease.
