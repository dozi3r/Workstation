# MODULE ESOT-02: DIVINE COUNCIL THEORY AS SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION

### 1. Metaphysical Architecture

This module translates classical Near Eastern Divine Council Theory into a functional, structural cybernetic/esoteric cosmology for pointcrawl campaign networks.

* **The Core Cosmology:**
  * The gods do not rule by divine magic; they operate a literal **Celestial System Administration Panel**.
  * The Supreme Deity is the **Root User** who wrote the original operating protocols of reality.
  * Presiding under the Root User is the **Divine Council (The Watchers / Sons of God)**, an assembly of high-tier administrators assigned territorial jurisdiction over specific nations, biomes, and nodes.
* **The Territorial Allocation (Deuteronomy 32:8 Engine):**
  * Each of the 22 nodes on the campaign network is an assigned **Administrative Sector** overseen by a designated Councilor.
  * Local miracles, harvests, and pest immunities are simply energy quota allocations approved by that node's presiding administrator.

### 2. The Great Deception (System Illiteracy)
* The convict population lives in manufactured **System Illiteracy**. They believe lighting incense and kneeling before stone menhirs is a religious duty that pleases a moral deity.
* In reality, the prayer chant is a 20-second acoustic timing code, the kneeling grounds an electrical circuit, and the silver offering provides conductive metal to repair regional circuitry.

### 3. Rogue Councilors & The Interface Hack
* **The Crime of the Crown:** The Imperial Archpriests did not establish a holy church—they discovered the abandoned console terminal of a dormant Councilor and hacked the root interface.
* **The Soul Siphon:** By overwriting the sector's reincarnation protocol, they redirected departing mortal souls into private silver storage accumulators rather than returning them to the planetary core.
