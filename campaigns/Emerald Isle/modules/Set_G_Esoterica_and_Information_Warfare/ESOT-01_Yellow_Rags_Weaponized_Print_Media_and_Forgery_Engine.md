# MODULE ESOT-01: YELLOW RAGS: WEAPONIZED PRINT MEDIA & THE FORGERY ENGINE

### 1. Information Warfare System Architecture

Extracted from the companion SRD *Yellow Rags Mechanics & Execution*, this engine governs how localized, mass-produced disinformation sheets are manufactured to shift node alignment, mask resource theft, or tank a neighboring hub's stability.

* **Definition:** A "Yellow Rag" is an intentional forgery masquerading as an official proclamation, leaked diplomatic correspondence, or sensational whistle-blower broadsheet.
* **The Structural DNA (The 3 Mandatory Pillars):**
  1. **The Grievance Anchor (The Real Stressor):** A Yellow Rag cannot create fear out of thin air. It must latch onto a pre-existing, verifiable physical problem in the local node (e.g., rations cut in half, a well tasting of sulfur, or a guard squad being reassigned).
  2. **The Malicious Explanation:** It provides an emotionally satisfying, conspiratorial explanation (e.g., *"The rations were cut because the Gnomish artificers are secretly feeding our flour to their iron automatons"*).
  3. **The Panic Escalation:** A concrete, urgent call to action that forces a crisis before authorities can issue a retraction.

### 2. Player & NPC Forgery Crafting
* **Crafting a Rag:** Players or NPCs invest 50 Silver Links and 4 hours at a printing press. Roll a Craft/Forgery check (2d6):
  * **10+ (Masterwork Hoax):** Believed by 90% of the populace; reduces Node Stability by $-15$ SP; incites riots against the scapegoat.
  * **7–9 (Plausible Rumor):** Sparks widespread suspicion and work slowdowns; $-5$ SP; authorities struggle to contain it.
  * **6- (Clumsy Libel):** The forgery is spotted immediately; leads investigators directly back to the print shop.

### 3. The Debunker's Investigative Loop
* **For Investigative Parties:** When a rival faction floods the city with Yellow Rags, players must:
  1. Inspect the ink and watermarks to locate the clandestine press.
  2. Interrogate the typesetter to identify who paid for the print run.
  3. Solve the real underlying physical stressor (fixing the well or securing grain) to break the hoax's psychological grip on the public.
