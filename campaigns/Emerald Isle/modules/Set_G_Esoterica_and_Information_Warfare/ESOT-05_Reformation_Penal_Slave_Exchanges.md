# MODULE ESOT-05: THE "REFORMATION" PENAL SYSTEM & TRI-POINT DISCIPLINE TRIANGLE

### 1. Judicial Architecture

This module codifies the penal colony's disciplinary machinery, forced labor routes, and contractual prisoner exchange economy.

* **The Tri-Point Labor Circuit:**
  * Minor infractions (curfew violations, hoarding food, insolence) are punished with **The Circuit**.
  * Convicts are chained in iron coffins or tethered in teams of four to haul 500-lb granite blocks continuously in a brutal triangle between **The Lighthouse Breakwater**, **The 60-ft Cliff Walls**, and **The Central Outpost**.
  * Breaks the convict's physical spirit in 30 days while maintaining public fortification masonry.
* **The "Reformation" Sale (Inter-Faction Human Trafficking):**
  * Severe offenses (striking a guard, attempting escape, organizing a strike) are not punished with execution. On the campaign network, human bodies are too valuable to waste.
  * The convict is sentenced to **"Reformation"**—a euphemism for being sold on contract to rival or neutral factions (e.g., sold to the Troglodytes for oil-well dredging, to the Kobolds for deep drift mining, or to the Black Tide as galley rowers).
  * Generates massive revenue for corrupt colonial magistrates while ridding the outpost of charismatic troublemakers.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Player Hooks
* **The Prisoner Transfer Convoy:** A recurring random encounter or heist scenario where PCs can intercept an armored wagon hauling 10 "Reformed" prisoners before they reach the Kobold sulfur mines.
* **The Reformation Sentence:** When PCs are captured by the law, instead of a boring execution or dungeon stay, they are sentenced to Reformation and sold to a bizarre faction in another node, kicking off an escape adventure.
