# MODULE CWT-076: THE GREAT ZIMBABWE MORTARLESS GRANITE AND GOLD MONOPOLY

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Archaeological Grounding
Flourishing between the 11th and 15th centuries CE (c. 1100–1450 CE) across the southeastern highveld plateau between the Zambezi and Limpopo rivers, **Great Zimbabwe** (*Dzimba-dza-mabwe*, "Venerated Houses of Stone") was the imperial capital of a sprawling medieval Shona civilization. Encompassing over 1,780 acres and supporting an urban agglomeration of more than 18,000 inhabitants at its zenith, this metropolis served as the paramount trade and liturgical nexus linking the alluvial goldfields of the interior crystalline plateau to the maritime merchant empires of the Indian Ocean rim.

Archaeological excavations and architectural analyses (notably by David Beach, Peter Garlake, Innocent Pikirayi, and Thomas Huffman) demonstrate that the monument’s physical permanence and geopolitical dominance rested on three interrelated technical pillars:

1. **Mortarless Dry-Stone Masonry via Thermal Exfoliation**: The monumental walls of Great Zimbabwe contain an estimated **one million dressed granite blocks** erected without a single drop of mortar or lime cement. Masons exploited the geological sheet-fracturing (*exfoliation*) of local granite batholiths: by lighting controlled brushwood bonfires along flat rock surfaces and dousing the red-hot stone with cold well water, the thermal shock cleaved uniform, tabular slabs 10 to 20 centimeters thick. Masons dressed these slabs with spheroidal dolerite hammerstones into standardized rectangular ashlar blocks (*Q-style masonry*).
   * **Battered Slopes & Sinuous Curves**: Lacking binding mortar, structural stability against lateral earth pressure and seismic settling was achieved through engineering geometry. Walls were constructed with an inward slope (*batter*) of 5 to 10 degrees, utilizing gravity to constantly lock the inner courses against the core. Right-angled corners—vulnerable to shear fractures—were strictly forbidden; every enclosure, portal, and buttress curves in undulating, serpentine arcs that distribute gravitational load seamlessly.
   * **The Double-Chevron Frieze**: Along the upper northern rim of the Great Enclosure's perimeter wall runs a 70-meter continuous decorative frieze of double-chevron and herringbone stonework. In Shona cosmological semiotics, this represents the sacred lightning serpent (*Nyami Nyami* or the water python), symbolizing seasonal rainfall, dynastic fertility, and the spiritual mantle of the *Mambo* (paramount sovereign).
2. **The Great Enclosure (*Imba Huru*) and the Solid Conical Tower**: The largest dry-stone monument in sub-Saharan Africa, the Great Enclosure features a perimeter wall 252 meters in circumference, rising up to 11 meters (36 feet) high and 6 meters thick at the foundation. Within its concentric interior lies the enigmatic **Conical Tower**—a solid, 9-meter-tall tapered cylinder containing roughly 180 cubic meters of solid stone with zero interior voids. Representing a colossal stone facsimile of a Shona grain bin (*tsumo* or *dura*), the tower materialized the monarch's divine role as the universal grain distributor and drought-buffer, demonstrating that even if drought scourged the grasslands, the king’s stone silos stood permanent and full.
3. **The Indian Ocean Maritime Mercantile Siphon**: Great Zimbabwe sat at the geographic choke-point controlling trans-shipment between the interior gold-panning rivers (Runde, Save, and Mazowe basins) and the Swahili coastal port-city of **Sofala** (subordinate to the Sultanate of Kilwa Kisiwani). In exchange for raw alluvial gold dust, elephant ivory, and iron bloom, dhows navigated the monsoon winds from Arabia, Persia, and India, funneling high-status prestige goods into the valley. Stratigraphic layers have yielded tens of thousands of colored drawn-glass trade beads (*Zhizo* and *Khami* series), Syrian cut glass, Persian earthenware tiles, and shards of **Song, Yuan, and early Ming Dynasty celadon and blue-and-white porcelain**. This exotic trade wealth was monopolized by the royal court, distributed downward as gift-tribute to bind client sub-chiefs.
4. **Sacred Soapstone Birds of the Hill Complex**: Perched atop monolithic stone pillars in the eastern shrine of the precipitous Hill Complex (the royal ritual citadel 80 meters above the valley), eight masterfully carved **soapstone raptor monoliths** fused human and avian iconography. Embodying the bateleur eagle (*chapungu* or *hungwe*), these raptors were revered as spiritual messengers carrying ancestral prayers directly between the Mambo and the supreme deity, *Mwari*.
5. **Pastoral Carrying Capacity & The Inevitable Migration (~1450 CE)**: Great Zimbabwe was never conquered by foreign armies, sacked by raiders, or overthrown in violent revolution. Its decline was an ecological carrying-capacity crisis:
   * **Savanna Overgrazing**: Power and bridal alliances (*lobola*) were denominated in humped zebu cattle (*Sanga* cattle). The presence of over 18,000 cattle on the crystalline granite soils exhausted the nutritious plateau grasses (*sweetveld*), triggering topsoil erosion and cattle malnutrition.
   * **Firewood Deforestation**: Fueling granite-cracking quarry bonfires and heating 18,000 clay *daga* roundhouses stripped the surrounding miombo and mopane woodlands of firewood for a radius exceeding 30 leagues.
   * **Northern Trade Route Defection**: As southern alluvial gold reefs became exhausted, prospecting shifted northward to the Dande and Zambezi valleys. Prince Nyatsimba Mutota led a massive dynastic migration north to establish the **Mutapa Empire**, while another branch moved south to form the Butua Kingdom at Khami. Great Zimbabwe was peacefully evacuated; its mortarless walls were left intact as a silent ancestral sanctuary.

### 2. Thematic Premise, Syllogism & Dramatic Opposition

#### 2.1 Thematic Premise & Syllogism
* **The Stasis / The Lie**: *"Our mortarless granite walls curve like the coils of the sacred python; our cattle herds stretch across the horizon, and our gold buys the porcelain of the Son of Heaven. As long as our stone grain tower stands solid, the savanna will feed our children forever."*
* **The Crucible / Conflict**: The miombo woods are cut to the bedrock for granite-cracking fires; 18,000 cattle churn the pastures into dust storms, and prospectors find richer goldfields 200 miles north along the Zambezi River. Swahili dhow captains begin docking at northern river mouths, bypassing the Great Enclosure.
* **Dramatic Syllogism**:
  $$\text{Pastoral Overstocking} + \text{Caravan Route Northward Defection} = \text{Peaceful Civic Evacuation \& Inviolate Granite Reliquary}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis**: True power resides not in immovable stone monuments or stockpiled gold, but in living cattle pastures and the flow of trade routes. When ecological balance shatters, wisdom lies not in dying behind 11-meter walls, but in packing the ancestral eagles and following the grazing herds north.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Mambo Royal Granite Master):**  
  Paramount sovereign of the Great Enclosure, ruling from the secluded *Imba Huru* behind 11-meter batter-sloped granite curtains. Clothed in gold-beaded cotton and ivory bracelets, the Mambo commands the masonry guilds to dress new courses of chevron ashlar to reaffirm ancestral dominance. He treats every stray steer as sacred royal capital and insists that foreign trade dhows must pay the traditional 50% gold tariff at the valley gates, deaf to the reality that northern caravans are slipping past his toll stations.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Northern Mutapa Dynastic Splinter Prince):**  
  A charismatic royal splinter heir (embodying the historical Nyatsimba Mutota) who has established fortified palisades along the northern Dande plateau near the Zambezi River. Realizing that the highveld pastures are dead and southern gold reefs depleted, he invites Swahili dhow merchants directly to northern river moorings with slashed tariffs. He actively lures younger cattle herders away from Great Zimbabwe with promises of lush alluvial grazing along the Zambezi bottoms.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (The Sofala Swahili Dhow Factor):**  
  An urbane mercantile broker representing maritime syndicates from Kilwa and the Persian Gulf, residing in the clay *daga* compounds of the Lower Valley. Surrounded by crates of Chinese Longquan celadon, Indian carnelian beads, and Syrian glassware, the factor smiles deferentially before the Mambo while quietly liquidating his local storehouses. He uses fraudulent balance scales to cheat domestic gold panners and bribes northern caravans to redirect their finest elephant tusks directly to the coast.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (The Savanna Firewood Cutter & Soapstone Sculptor):**  
  A hereditary artisan-shrine keeper dwelling in the natural rock alcoves of the Hill Complex. Tasked with sculpting the sacred bateleur eagle monoliths from soft chlorite schist, they are also forced to organize woodcutters who must walk three days into the wilderness just to gather green brushwood for granite-splitting bonfires. Attuned to the cries of dying raptors and the cracked dry savanna mud, they prophesy that the eagles have already flown to the northern rivers and urge the common folk to prepare their travel baskets.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Highveld Carrying Capacity & Commercial Siphon
The ecological exhaustion, urban stress, and commercial viability of Great Zimbabwe are monitored along a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Granite Exfoliation Flaking & Daga Hut Mud Wash / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Miombo timber within a half-day's march is clear-cut; quarry fires must burn green scrub; unseasonal torrential downpours wash away outer layers of clay *daga* plaster from commoners' roundhouses.
  * *Mechanics:* Procuring camp firewood or draft fuel costs triple standard prices. Quarrying usable granite ashlar or repairing eroded *daga* walls requires a **DC 10 Athletics or Crafting check**; failure results in rock splinters or structural collapses inflicting minor cuts and bruises (1d4 bludgeoning/slashing damage). Fine granite dust blankets the lower valley, imposing disadvantage on scent-based tracking checks.
* **Level 2 (Firewood Fuel Shortage & Cattle Pasture Overgrazing / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Grasslands within 10 leagues are stripped down to red hardpan by 18,000 cattle; cattle ribs protrude; timber must be hauled from 20 leagues away; dust storms sweep through the narrow stone corridors.
  * *Mechanics:* Moving across the denuded plateau savanna requires a **DC 13 Survival check** each march watch; failure inflicts **2d8 bludgeoning/exhaustion damage** from choking red dust storms and heat prostration. Purchasing grain or provisions with unrefined alluvial gold dust requires a **DC 13 Appraisal or Insight check** to avoid being swindled by Swahili dhow factors manipulating balance weights.
* **Level 3 (Gold Trade Route Diversion & Daga Quarter Abandonment / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Swahili trade caravans bypass the valley for northern Zambezi camps; foreign prestige goods vanish; cattle murrain strikes stagnant holding pens, poisoning valley streams; commoners begin packing household goods.
  * *Mechanics:* Drinking unboiled water from valley drainage creeks requires a **DC 16 Fortitude check** against enteric murrain dysentery; failure inflicts **4d10 poison/necrotic damage** and incapacitating nausea for 24 hours. Attempting to negotiate with angry mobs or navigate deserted, crumbling *daga* slums requires a **DC 16 Streetwise, Intimidation, or Survival check** to avoid opportunistic ambushes by starving cattle raiders.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Valley Ecological Exhaustion & Capital Relocation / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The savanna carrying capacity is completely broken; all firewood within 30 leagues is exhausted; the royal Mambo council packs the ancestral soapstone raptors into ox-litters and leads a mass peaceful exodus north; 18,000 inhabitants disperse; the Great Enclosure is left completely abandoned.
  * *Mechanics:* Total civic and infrastructural dissolution. Commercial exchange drops to zero; the node permanently loses its trade settlement status. Characters stranded in the empty stone complex face a **DC 20 Survival check** every 12 hours; failure inflicts **8d12 famine, dehydration, and exposure damage**. The site transitions permanently into an uninhabited, sacred dry-stone reliquary.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```

```

* **Plateau Firewood & Pasture Exhaustion Clock [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each decade or extended operational phase that 18,000 cattle remain concentrated in the valley basin, or whenever massive thermal granite-quarrying projects consume remaining woodland reserves.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* Miombo woodcutters must trek 15 leagues into hostile neighboring lands; commoners burn dried cow dung for cooking fires, fouling the urban air.
    * *Tick 4:* Pasture grass failure triggers competitive cattle raids among tributary clans; milk yields drop by 75%; triggers **Dial Level 2**.
    * *Tick 5:* Deep erosion gullies cut across the valley floor, undermining exterior dry-stone enclosure perimeter footings.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* The highveld ecosystem collapses into sterile dust. Triggers **Dial Level 4: Catastrophic Capital Relocation**. The royal court peacefully abandons the site, migrating northward to the Zambezi basin.
* **Northern Trade Route Defection Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) whenever the Mambo increases gold tariffs, when northern Mutapa miners offer cheaper alluvial nuggets directly to coastal factors, or when player actions disrupt southern caravan security.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Swahili dhow captains reduce glass bead shipments to the Great Enclosure by 30%.
    * *Tick 2:* The northern splinter prince signs an exclusive trade pact with the Sultan of Kilwa's coastal emissaries; Chinese celadon imports to Great Zimbabwe cease entirely.
    * *Tick 3:* Coastal merchants quietly dismantle their valley compounds in the dead of night, slipping away with remaining ivory stocks; triggers **Dial Level 3**.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Total commercial severance. Caravan traffic hits 0; the trade monopoly is permanently transferred to the nascent Mutapa Empire.

### 4. Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Fauna: Highveld-Humped-Cattle-Herds`: Vast herds of *Sanga* humped cattle (minimum 10,000 to 18,000 head) to provide dairy subsistence, bridal exchange alliances (*lobola*), and ritual manure/meat that sustain the urban masonry labor corps.
  * `Material: Thermal-Exfoliated-Granite-Slabs`: Extensive geological batholiths of sheet-fracturing granite combined with continuous cordwood fuel to produce fire-quarried, rectangular ashlar blocks without mortar.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Structure: Mortarless-Curved-Granite-Great-Enclosure`: The largest dry-stone architectural complex in pre-colonial sub-Saharan Africa, featuring 11-meter battered curtain walls, the 9-meter solid Conical Tower, and labyrinthine parallel defense passages.
  * `Mineral: Sub-Saharan-Alluvial-Gold-Dust`: Hundreds of kilograms of pure alluvial gold dust washed from crystalline plateau streams, melted into crucibles, and cast into trade ingots for foreign export.
  * `Trade: Indian-Ocean-Dhow-Monopoly`: Chokepoint maritime trade access connecting interior African raw materials to the Swahili coast, importing thousands of glass beads, Syrian glassware, and Chinese Song/Ming dynasty celadon porcelain.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When highveld pasture fails and firewood is exhausted within 30 leagues, the module enters **Inviolate Ancestral Reliquary Mode**:
    * All commercial gold refinement, ivory exports, and foreign caravan traffic cease permanently.
    * The Mambo lineage, masonry guilds, and common herdsmen execute a peaceful, organized migration north to the Zambezi (founding Mutapa) and southwest to Khami (founding Butua).
    * No fires are lit, no walls are toppled, and no blood is spilled: the Great Enclosure, Hill Complex, and Conical Tower remain standing pristine, dry-stone monuments preserved untouched in the silent savanna for centuries.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks

* **Encounter 1: The Cattle Stampede in the Parallel Passage (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party arrives at the Great Enclosure seeking audience with the Mambo to negotiate gold concessions or deliver an ivory consignment. Escorted into the **Parallel Passage**—a claustrophobic, 1-meter-wide canyon trapped between the 11-meter outer wall and a 9-meter inner wall—they are met with terror: a dust storm has panicked a herd of 50 royal humped cattle inside the enclosure. The herd stampedes directly down the narrow stone corridor toward the party. Behind the beasts, royal spear-guards bar the exit with heavy iron *assegais*, shouting that letting a single royal cow escape onto the parched plains is punishable by execution.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* Expecting a dignified diplomatic reception in a serene imperial capital, the party is suddenly trapped in a stone death-funnel facing 1,000-pound horned animals with no room to maneuver.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The guard captain screams pious loyalty to the Mambo's sacred herd through the dust, but his hollow cheeks and ravenous gaze reveal the bitter truth: he is starving, and if a steer is trampled in the crush, he intends to butcher it in secret the moment the dust settles.
* **Encounter 2: The Fire-Cracking of the Batholith (Resource Dilemma)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* At the royal quarry dome adjacent to the valley, the Mambo's master mason has stacked the city’s last three wagon-loads of dry miombo timber against a granite outcrop. The mason prepares to ignite the bonfire and douse it with five hundred gallons of precious spring water to crack new ashlar blocks for the king's new decorative wall course. Simultaneously, a desperate council of valley elders and woodcutters surrounds the kiln with flint knives and digging sticks, begging the party to intervene: if this timber is burned, there will be no firewood to cook sorghum for three thousand children in the lower *daga* quarters during the coming cold winter winds.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Enforce the Royal Quarrying Edict:* Defend the masons against the elders. The granite shatters into flawless building blocks (**DC 13 Crafting/Engineering**), pleasing the Mambo and granting access to royal gold ingots, but triggers an immediate riot in the lower town, ticking the **Plateau Exhaustion Clock** by +1 and plunging the common quarters into starvation.
    * *Douse the Quarry Kiln & Distribute the Firewood:* Disarm the mason's guards and seize the timber for the elders. The commoners survive the cold season, but the Mambo brands the party sacrilegious traitors to the ancestral building works, dispatching forty royal archers armed with poison-tipped arrows to execute them on the quarry floor.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The master mason passionately argues that without magnificent stone walls, their civilization is indistinguishable from nomadic brush-dwellers; yet as he speaks, his own young apprentices cough violently from the acrid soot of green scrub they were forced to forage.
* **Encounter 3: The Secret Packing of the Soapstone Raptors (Severance Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* In the dead of night atop the sheer granite cliffs of the Hill Complex, the party stumbles upon the High Priest of Mwari and a detachment of veteran royal bodyguards secretly prying the sacred **soapstone bird monoliths** from their stone sockets. The monoliths are being wrapped in soft ox-hides and tied to pack-oxen for an emergency evacuation march north toward the Zambezi River. The 18,000 commoners sleeping in the valley below have not been told that their rulers and gods are abandoning them before the next dry season.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party seeks the high priest for ancestral divination or trade mediation, only to uncover that the royal regime has already collapsed from within and is quietly fleeing into the night.
  * *The Choice:*
    * *Raise the Alarm:* Shout to wake the lower valley quarters. A chaotic panic and civil clash erupts between royal guards and terrified citizens; the party must fight their way through crumbling *daga* streets as mobs scramble for remaining food stocks.
    * *Escort the Secret Migration:* Aid the priest in guiding the pack-oxen down hidden cliff pathways. The priest rewards the party with an uncut bar of refined gold, a carved soapstone ancestral relic, and safe conduct into the nascent northern Mutapa territory.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The priest presses his weathered hand against the cold soapstone feathers of the bateleur eagle, whispering that a true sovereign does not anchor his soul to stationary rocks, but follows the rains; yet behind his serene theology lies cold panic that commoner spears will pin him to the wall if his escape is discovered.
