# MODULE CWT-022: THE GLACIAL CREVASSE CABLE FUNICULAR

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Transit Affordance
Spanning an uncrossable eight-hundred-foot-deep chasm of moving blue glacial ice, this aerial cableway connects isolated alpine bastions to the high tundra. Two two-inch braided iron cables stretch three-quarters of a mile between towering masonry anchor pylons, carrying an enclosed oak-and-canvas gondola suspended above yawning seracs.

* **The Valley Anchor Pylon:** Anchored deep into solid granite with molten lead, housing counterweights made of stone-filled iron cages that maintain cable tension as temperatures drop.
* **The Suspended Cradle:** A reinforced passenger gondola fitted with kerosene heaters, brass observation slits, and dual mechanical pinch-brakes capable of biting into the guide cable in an emergency.
* **The Wind Lass Station:** Powered by twin draft mules or water dynamos, turning giant drum winches that haul the traction cable across the void.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The High Alpine Cable Directorate):** Severe, bearded mountaineers who maintain the pulleys. They refuse to launch the gondola during high winds without signed death waivers and triple passenger fares.
* **Main Opponent (The Frost-Bitten Mountain Ascetics):** Hermits dwelling in ice caves below who view the metal cables as a desecration of the mountain's sacred silence. They shoot flaming oil arrows at the hemp traction ropes.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Smuggler Aeronauts):** Daring alpine thieves who ride small wooden trollies hooked to the main cable at midnight, hauling un-taxed gems and forbidden codices past the border fortresses.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Crevasse Salvage Creepers):** Desperate mountaineers who rappel down the icy abyss beneath the cable line, scavenging the frozen remains of passengers from earlier disasters.

### 3. GM Edge Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Edge Placement:** Recommended on **Edge #8 ➔ #10** (Outer Alpine Redoubt to Stasis Core) or any alpine transit edge crossing hazardous high-elevation chasms.
* **Transit Velocity:** Reduces an otherwise impassable 3-day mountaineering trek to a 45-minute aerial crossing.
* **Standalone Fallback:** When mechanical winches freeze, manual hand-cranks allow agonizingly slow passage at double energy cost.
