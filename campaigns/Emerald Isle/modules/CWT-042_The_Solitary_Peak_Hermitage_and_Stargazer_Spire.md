# MODULE CWT-042: THE SOLITARY PEAK HERMITAGE & STARGAZER SPIRE

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Stellar Observatory
Perched upon the needle-sharp summit of the highest peak in the range, the Stargazer Spire pierces the clouds at ten thousand feet. Built from mortarless basalt blocks anchored into living granite with iron staples, it houses a rotating brass dome and a massive refracting telescope made with hand-ground optical quartz lenses. From this freezing aerie, a solitary vow-bound astronomer watches the slow dance of planets, recording ephemeris charts that guide shipping fleets and warn farming valleys of incoming multi-year droughts.

* **The Great Brass Astrolabe:** A twelve-foot geared mechanism driven by water-clepsydra escapements, calculating lunar declination and equinoctial precession.
* **The High Heliograph Platform:** A cantilevered balcony equipped with a silvered parabolic mirror that flashes Morse-like mirror codes to distant coastal signal towers.
* **The Ten Thousand Steps:** A harrowing spiral staircase hacked directly into the vertical cliff face, unprotected by handrails and glazed with perpetual rime ice.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Stargazer Hermit):** A vow-bound astronomer who communicates only through written chalk notes; he considers human wars trivial compared to the cosmic movements of the stars.
* **Main Opponent (Militant Border Dukes):** Ambitious generals demanding astrological calculations to determine the exact auspicious hour to launch surprise cavalry invasions.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Star-Catcher Heretics):** Rogue alchemists who seek to harness falling starfall debris to forge forbidden planar superweapons.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Breathless Stair-Ascetics):** Half-frozen pilgrims hauling up bags of rice and firewood on their backs, praying for a single horoscope reading from the hermit.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 05 (Mountain Watchtower & Signal Pylon)**, **Node 21 (Glacial Peak Apex)**, or any commanding alpine height.
