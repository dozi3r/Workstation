# MODULE CWT-024: THE HIGH SIERRA SWITCHBACK CARAVAN TRAIL

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Transit Affordance
Carved with cold chisels and black powder out of a sheer four-thousand-foot granite escarpment, this historic military and merchant caravan road zigzags up twenty-seven engineered switchbacks. Supported by massive dry-stacked cyclopean retaining walls, the road is wide enough for two laden pack mules or a narrow ox wagon to pass.

* **The Turnaround Benches:** Wide semi-circular stone platforms at each switchback corner, fortified with iron tie-rings and water troughs fed by melted snow flumes.
* **The Rock-Shed Gallerias:** Timber and slate roofs sloped at steep angles against the mountain wall, deflecting falling stones safely over the heads of passing travelers.
* **The High Saddle Gate:** A fortified gateway built at the pass summit, housing a small garrison, toll hearth, and signal beacon tower overlooking both sides of the range.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The High Road Warden Order):** Mountain rangers sworn to maintain the road and clear snowdrifts. They demand strict wheel-width compliance and collect road-repair taxes from passing wool merchants.
* **Main Opponent (The Sierra Crag Raiders):** Highland goat-herders and bowmen who strike from inaccessible ledges above, rolling rounded granite boulders down onto rich baggage trains.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Camelid Freight Monopoly):** Wealthy caravan masters who intentionally block switchback turnaround points with broken carts to delay competing merchants from reaching market first.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Stone-Masonry Penitents):** Disgraced convicts serving life sentences rebuilding retaining walls in biting winds, trading cold spring water for salt and tobacco.

### 3. GM Edge Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Edge Placement:** Recommended on **Edge #3 ➔ #8** or **Edge #7 ➔ #8** (connecting foothills or ascent shelves to high alpine redoubts).
* **Transit Velocity:** Dependable overland crossing in 1 travel watch; resistant to ordinary winter closures thanks to rock-shed gallerias.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Converts into a treacherous climbing scramble if masonry collapses.
