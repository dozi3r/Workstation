# MODULE CWT-023: THE SUNKEN CANAL OF THE WEEPING WILLOW

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Transit Affordance
A half-submerged artificial canal system excavated through centuries of peat bogs and cypress wetlands. Draped in trailing curtains of silver weeping moss and dense willow canopies, the canal is completely invisible from the air and surrounding ridgelines, providing a covert water transit corridor linking outer lowlands directly to independent river syndicates.

* **The Drowned Towpaths:** Stone masonry paths submerged twelve inches beneath tea-colored marsh water, allowing mules wearing leather mud-shoes to haul barges without creating ripples.
* **The Sluice Camouflage Weirs:** Hidden wicker and reed gates that hold just enough water to float shallow-draft punts while appearing to be impassable beaver dams to casual scouts.
* **The Willow Tunnels:** Continuous arched canopy corridors where willow branches have been woven together overhead, blocking direct sunlight and muffling sound.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Canal Revenue Bailiffs):** Patrol skiffs armed with lanterns and pole-axes. They demand proof of origin for all cargo, searching marsh barges for contraband saltpeter and tax-evading flour.
* **Main Opponent (The Moss-Strangler Marauders):** Swamp outlaws who know every false branch of the canal. They stretch weighted iron chains across the water at neck-height to sweep pilots from punts.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Peat-Smuggler Cartels):** Merchant syndicates using the canal to bypass coastal harbor customs, hiding barrels of fine silver ingots beneath pungent loads of wet peat moss.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Mud-Wader Families):** Indigenous wetland folk dwelling in stilt huts among the roots. They pilot pole-punts with uncanny silence and trade rare bog antidotes for dry salt.

### 3. GM Edge Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Edge Placement:** Recommended on **Edge #2 ➔ #5** or **Edge #5 ➔ #13** (connecting lowlands through wetlands to merchant guilds).
* **Stealth Affordance:** Travel along this edge cannot be tracked by overland patrols or aerial scouts.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Converts into an exhausting, leech-infested mud-hike if water levels drop.
