# MODULE CWT-011: THE IMPERIAL REGICIDE & TOTAL SUCCESSION WAR

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Climax Trigger
The supreme sovereign authority has been assassinated in the inner sanctum. The sovereign seal has vanished, and no clear heir holds uncontested claim. Within hours, regional garrisons divide into irreconcilable pretender factions, transforming trade highways into contested front lines and subjecting satellite nodes to ruthless requisitioning raids.

* **The Shattered Throne Room:** The marble seat of state sits drenched in dried blood and sealed behind iron grates, surrounded by competing heraldic banners.
* **The Succession Crucible:** Four pretenders claim the regency. Player actions (bribery, military escort, forgery, or tactical decapitation) determine which faction secures the sovereign hub.
* **The Climax Vector:** When the 8-tick Succession War Clock fills, full-scale siege warfare erupts across the entire network, destroying vulnerable Tier 3 infrastructure unless an undisputed sovereign mandate is forged.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Iron Regents Council):** Veteran paladins and supreme bailiffs holding the central fortress. They demand total military conscription, hang suspected conspirators without trial, and treat all neutral traders as enemy spies.
* **Main Opponent (The Pretender Coalition):** A league of wealthy provincial oligarchs and exiled nobles funding foreign mercenary pikemen to storm the capital and overturn royal monopolies.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Sovereign Siphon Guild):** Deep-cover spymasters who orchestrated the regicide, now operating auction blocks in hidden taverns to sell forged letters of marque, noble titles, and cryptographic state ciphers.
* **Tertiary Outcast (Deserter Peasant Brigades):** Conscripted levies and tenant farmers who abandoned the front line. Fortified behind ditch-and-palisade berms, they murder any noble officer who approaches their perimeter.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 11 (Theocratic High Sanctum)** or **Node 1 (Beachhead Bastion)**.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Governance: High-Feudal-Charter` and `Military: Heavy-Armored-Pikes`; yields `Governance: Sovereign-Regency-Mandate` which resets regional stability points or commands continental armies.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Operates as a fragmented garrison redoubt without needing active regional supply lines.
