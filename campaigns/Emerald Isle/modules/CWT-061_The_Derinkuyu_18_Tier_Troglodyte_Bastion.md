# MODULE CWT-061: THE DERINKUYU 18-TIER TROGLODYTE BASTION

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Geological Grounding
Carved into the Miocene ignimbrite and pyroclastic ash deposits of an arid volcanic plateau (analogous to the Cappadocia tablelands formed by ancient eruptions of Mount Erciyes and Mount Hasan), the **Derinkuyu 18-Tier Troglodyte Bastion** represents the apex of subterranean siege survival engineering. Descending roughly 85 meters (280 feet) into the earth across eighteen distinct architectural tiers, the city was engineered to shelter up to 20,000 citizens, their livestock herds, food reserves, and monastic institutions during catastrophic surface campaigns.

The engineering of the bastion rests on three foundational geotechnical realities:
1. **The Volcanic Tuff Geotechnical Matrix**: Freshly quarried tuff is soft enough to be hollowed with simple bronze or iron picks. Crucially, when exposed to circulating air, chemical oxidation and calcite crystallization harden the exposed rock face into a load-bearing shell. The troglodytes excavated vast vaulted halls, arched passages, and multi-tier galleries without requiring timber shoring, provided structural support pillars and barrel-vaulted ceiling profiles were preserved.
2. **The Tirhaz Monolithic Rolling Blast Doors**: Access between levels and transit corridors is compartmentalized by massive discoid stone doors (*tirhaz*). Each circular door measures between 1.0 and 1.5 meters in diameter, 30 to 50 centimeters in thickness, and weighs between 300 and 500 kilograms. Carved *in situ* within dedicated lateral wall recesses, each stone rests on an inclined rolling groove. Defenders inside lever the stone into position with cedar poles, wedging it against an exterior rock rebate. The disc cannot be pried or shoved inward from the outside. A 10-centimeter central aperture allows defenders to thrust lances, loose arrows, or observe attackers while sealed behind half a ton of volcanic stone.
3. **Pneumatic Convection & Decoupled Hydrology**: Atmospheric life support is maintained by an engineered pneumatic circulation system consisting of a primary 55-meter vertical ventilation trunk linked to more than 15,000 distributed micro-flues. Geothermal heat differentials and surface wind-shear across flue cowls create continuous stack-effect convection, drawing fresh air down into the 18th tier and discharging warm, stale air through secondary exhaust vents. At the base of the central shaft lies the 85-meter aquifer well. In an intentional anti-sabotage design, the drinking well is physically isolated from upper-tier access grates: invaders dumping carcasses, sulfur, or pitch into surface flues cannot poison the deep aquifer.

* **Upper Tiers (Tiers 1–2 / 0m to -12m)**: Animal holding stalls, haylofts, dung composting pits, and outer baffle gates. Confining horses, sheep, and oxen to these upper levels prevents livestock from navigating steep downward stairs while keeping animal odors and methane from fouling the lower residential atmosphere.
* **Intermediate Tiers (Tiers 3–8 / -12m to -40m)**: Communal refectories, rock-cut wine presses with foot-treading vats, linseed oil extraction presses (producing fuel for ceramic lamps), tiered sleeping quarters, and smoke-trapped kitchens venting into secondary soot flues.
* **Lower Deep-Refuge Tiers (Tiers 9–18 / -40m to -85m)**: Monastic cloisters, cruciform rock-hewn basilica, baptismal sumps, confessional vaults, high-security granaries containing parched barley and lentils, emergency air-pocket vaults, and the secure wellhead floor.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Presbyter-Architect of the Tuff):**  
  Governs the subterranean sanctuary with ascetic rigor and monastic mathematical discipline. Charged with preserving the cultural seed of the valley through multi-year surface sieges, the Presbyter-Architect treats air and water as strictly finite sacred currency. When surface alarms sound, they order the *tirhaz* blast stones levered shut on schedule, impassively barring the outer corridors against late-arriving surface refugees to guarantee the survival of those already sealed within.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Steppe Siege Khan):**  
  Commander of horse-archer tumens encamped across the arid basalt steppe above. Thwarted by the absence of visible walls, ramparts, or defenders, the Khan wages environmental warfare against the subterranean ghost-city. Khanate sappers dig counter-trenches to locate hidden flue cowls, ignite sulfur-soaked animal dung directly over intake chimneys, and divert spring runoff into fissures to drown the upper livestock tiers.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (Black-Market Flue Siphoners):**  
  A clandestine syndicate of master cellarers and miners operating from Tier 4 fermentation vaults. Refusing monastic rationing, they have chiseled unmapped vertical chimneys through the tuff to barter vintage amphorae, cured ham, and liturgical gold with corrupt raider outposts above. Their illicit flues create catastrophic pneumatic backdrafts, drawing surface sulfur smoke straight into the residential sleeping dormitories.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (Blind Sump Hermits):**  
  A cloistered order of hereditary well-tenders inhabiting the absolute floor of the bastion (-85m). Blinded by lifetime residence in the lightless aquifer sumps, their acute acoustic senses detect the faintest vibrations traveling through the volcanic ignimbrite. By pressing bronze acoustic bowls to the living bedrock, they track the precise heading, depth, and pick-rate of the Khan’s sappers hundreds of yards away, trading their early warnings for clean oil and consecrated salt.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Subterranean Atmospheric & Siege Integrity
The physical, atmospheric, and defensive status of the bastion is monitored along a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Dim Lamp Cloister & Acrid Draft / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Surface raid alert sounds; livestock herds are driven into Tiers 1–2; perimeter baffles close; dim linseed oil lamps flicker in narrow 4-foot passages.
  * *Mechanics:* Movement through stairwells is restricted to single file; lantern visibility is limited to 30 feet. Traversing vertical ladder-chutes without iron spikes requires a **DC 10 Athletics check**. Acrid soot and animal odor irritate mucous membranes, imposing disadvantage on scent-based Perception checks.
* **Level 2 (Tirhaz Blast Stone Lockdown & Air Stagnation / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Primary *tirhaz* blast stones are rolled into position; intermediate tiers (3–8) are compartmentalized; natural air convection drops by 40%.
  * *Mechanics:* Heavy air stagnation and carbon dioxide pooling. Characters performing strenuous physical exertion (sustained combat, running, heavy mining labor) must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution check** each hour or suffer **2d8 bludgeoning/strain damage** (or take 1 level of Exhaustion). Manually levering a seated 500-kg *tirhaz* blast stone open from the defensive side requires a **DC 13 Athletics check** with leverage poles.
* **Level 3 (Sulfur Flue Smoke & Upper Sump Flood / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Raider sappers ignite sulfur-and-dung fire pots directly above ventilation flues; diverted surface runoff floods Tier 1–2 manure sumps down into the intermediate staircases.
  * *Mechanics:* Dense yellowish sulfur dioxide smoke billows through the upper flues into living quarters. Characters caught without vinegar-dampened cloths must succeed on a **DC 16 Fortitude check** or suffer **4d10 poison/suffocation damage**, fall prone retching, and suffer blinded status for $1\text{d}4$ hours. Traversing sludge-soaked stairways requires a **DC 16 Reflex check** to avoid tumbling down rock chutes. Panicked civilian crowds create trampling hazards.
* **Level 4 (Atmospheric Collapse & Sapper Vault Breach / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Sapper powder-mines or hydraulic undermining collapse the 55-meter central ventilation trunk; air stops circulating below Tier 8; rockfalls wedge blast doors into their tracks.
  * *Mechanics:* Catastrophic anaerobic environment. Rooms rapidly deplete oxygen. Characters without sealed air bladders must make a **DC 20 Fortitude or Athletics check** each round; failure inflicts **8d12 crushing and suffocation damage** from structural cave-ins and pulmonary hypoxia. Unblocking a crushed corridor or punching an emergency air flue requires a **DC 20 Athletics or Mining check** before the deep-tier population suffocates.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Oxygen Depletion & Toxic Inversion Clock (6 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ]       Raider Sapper Counter-Mine Clock (4 Ticks)
```

* **Oxygen Depletion & Toxic Inversion Clock [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each round or operational watch that primary ventilation flues remain choked by sulfur smoke, rockfall rubble, or intentional water mud-backfill.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* Oil lamps flicker out across Tiers 5–8 due to low oxygen; inhabitants must navigate in pitch darkness.
    * *Tick 4:* Children and elders slip into carbon dioxide narcosis; work crews suffer halved movement speed and disadvantage on all physical tests.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* Triggers **Dial Level 4: Atmospheric Collapse** immediately. Convection fails, sealing Tiers 9–18 into anaerobic tombs unless surface flues are cleared within 2 campaign rounds.
* **Raider Sapper Counter-Mine Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) whenever the party fails to eliminate surface trench guards, ignores acoustic detection reports from the Blind Sump Hermits, or discharges loud concussive explosives in the upper levels.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Faint, rhythmic metallic tapping echoes through the stone walls of Tier 4.
    * *Tick 2:* Deep hairline fractures split the vaulted ceiling of the Tier 5 winery; plaster dust rains into fermentation vats.
    * *Tick 3:* Sulfur smoke and iron chisel tips punch through the wall of a residential dormitory; Khanate archers blind-fire through the breach.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Raider sappers detonate an iron-banded powder keg, blowing a 10-foot breach directly into the Tier 5 winery. Raider shock infantry and dismounted lancers pour through, instigating a close-quarters subterranean melee.

### 4. Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Geology: Soft-Volcanic-Tuff-Stratum`: Homogeneous ignimbrite and pyroclastic ash deposits capable of rapid pick-excavation and atmospheric oxidization hardening without structural timber framing.
  * `Air: Vertical-Convection-Airshafts`: A continuous 55-meter vertical flue conduit driving thermal stack-effect circulation through thousands of branch channels.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Topography: Subterranean-Refuge-Node`: Complete defensive subterranean redoubt capable of sheltering up to 20,000 citizens and their domestic livestock completely hidden from surface armies.
  * `Defense: Hermetic-Rolling-Door-Seals`: 300–500kg one-way stone discoid seals providing absolute compartmentalization against hostile penetration.
  * `Life-Support: Deep-Tuff-Aquifer-Well`: 85-meter bottom-tier water well tapping subterranean groundwater, structurally disconnected from surface runoff to prevent contamination and poisoning.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When `Air: Vertical-Convection-Airshafts` is severed or poisoned, the complex shifts into **Hermetic Anaerobic Lockdown**:
    * Lower tiers (Tiers 9–18) are sealed behind double *tirhaz* rolling stone doors.
    * The population enters draconian oxygen rationing: all open flames are extinguished, non-essential movement is forbidden, and up to 500 elite defenders survive up to 14 days on stored emergency vintage wine and parched pulses before asphyxiation.
    * Outside communication, economic trade, and surface sortie capacity drop to 0.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks

* **Encounter 1: The Rolling of the 500-Kilo Stone (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party arrives at the camouflaged volcanic fissure entrance herding a wagon of wounded surface farmers and refugees, with the Khan's steppe cavalry vanguard cresting the ridge behind them. Inside the entrance passage, the Presbyter-Architect's guard captain has already begun levering the 500-kg *tirhaz* blast disc into its stone groove, refusing to hold the door open for fear that raider lancers will force the threshold.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party reaches their destination sanctuary, only to find themselves trapped in the narrow 4-foot approach gorge facing an immovable convex stone wall that seals them into the raider kill zone.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The guard captain shouts theological doctrine through the 10-cm central firing hole, claiming that divine law demands the gate roll shut at the twelfth gong; through the hole, his eyes betray sheer terror that opening the door will expose an unbonded contraband wine cache stacked directly behind the stone.
* **Encounter 2: The Sulfur Flue Infiltration Dilemma (The Severance Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Yellow, acrid sulfur smoke pours down an unmapped Tier 6 vertical chimney, filling the sleeping dormitories where hundreds of families are trapped behind a stuck corridor door.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Ascend the Soot Flue:* A single combatant or scout can squeeze up the 2-foot vertical soot-chute to spear the raider fire-tenders on the open steppe (facing **DC 16 Fortitude** against heat exhaustion and confronting a fortified raider trench guard).
    * *Sacrifice the Wine Cistern:* The defenders can smash the seals on the ancient Tier 6 winery vats, flooding the lower flue with 4,000 gallons of aged vintage to form a liquid smoke-trap. This clears the air immediately, but destroys the community’s emergency hydration reserve for the coming dry season and triggers an armed mutiny by the Black-Market Flue Siphoners.
* **Encounter 3: The Blind Sump Acoustic Listening Post (Subterranean Infiltration)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Desperate to locate the Khan’s sapping trenches before an explosive breach occurs, the party descends to the -85m bottom floor beside the silent aquifer basin. A sightless elder of the Blind Sump Hermits places a beaten bronze bowl against the bedrock and allows the party to listen.
  * *Acoustic Discovery:* Through the bronze resonance, the party hears the rhythmic clinking of iron picks right beneath the Tier 5 chapel. The Hermit offers to guide the party through an abandoned 18-inch emergency drainage conduit directly into the sappers' tunnel behind their defensive wooden mantlets—provided the party retrieves a jar of consecrated lamp oil stolen by the Siphoners on Tier 4.

### 6. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies

* **Recommended Placement:**
  * **Node 12 (Hazard Enclave / Deep Subterranean Redoubt)**: Serves as a subterranean emergency sanctuary where surface factions can retreat during catastrophic planetary or military events.
  * **Node 18 (Isolated Highland Plateau / Hermit Redoubt)**: Placed along rugged high-elevation passes where the subterranean complex anchors local trade and hides regional populations from nomadic raids.
* **Topological Synergies:**
  * **Macro Network Integration**: Sockets into `CWT-060` (The Systemic Interdependence Collapse Matrix), fulfilling its downstream requirement for an autarkic refuge node when regional trading hubs collapse.
  * **Transit Integration**: Directly bridges to `CWT-020` (The Subterranean Minecart Gravity Incline) to form a high-speed underground evacuation corridor linking mountain redoubts to lowland valley aquifers.
  * **Martial Tension**: Pair with `CWT-015` (The Great Peasant Levee) or `CWT-011` (Imperial Regicide) to model a besieged bastion where external armies besiege royal claimants or insurgent commoners sheltering in the deep tiers.
