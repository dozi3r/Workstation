# MODULE CWT-040: THE SYLVAN SANCTUARY OF THE LOVERS

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Neutral Canopy
In a deep glen sheltered by mist-veiled granite bluffs stand the Twin Silver Oaks: two monumental trees whose massive boles have grown coiled around one another for seven centuries, their silver-grey bark fusing into a single living archway. By ancient law recognized across all cultures and factions, no steel weapon may be drawn within sight of their canopy. Here, bitter clan wars are settled not through slaughter, but through binding marriages, shared oaths, and the exchange of silver-bark covenant rings.

* **The Entwined Archway:** The focal point of the sanctuary, where opposing clan leaders kneel to drink spring water infused with sweet silver oak sap.
* **The Peace-Boning Pavilions:** Open-air timber pavilions where visiting warriors surrender their blades to the wardens, receiving woven willow rods in exchange.
* **The Spring of Temperance:** A limestone basin fed by natural snowmelt where disputed property deeds and hostage treaties are signed with indelible gall-ink.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Keepers of the Covenant):** Unarmed herbalists and white-robed arbitrators who enforce sanctuary law with stern moral authority and ritual banishment.
* **Main Opponent (The Vendetta Heirs):** Hot-headed younger sons whose fathers were slain in past wars; they view compromise as cowardly surrender and plot murder in the holy grove.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Dynastic Matchmakers):** Scheming matriarchs and oligarchs who manipulate sacred betrothals to monopolize trade routes, treating young lovers like cattle.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Branded Vow-Breakers):** Outlaws exiled to the bramble wastes outside the boundary stones for violating ancient pacts, praying for the oaks to burn so they can return.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 06 (Whispering Old Woods)**, **Node 12 (Highland Glen Border)**, or directly on any contested boundary edge between rival sovereign hubs.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Ecology: Sacred-Silver-Oak-Grove` and `Social: Willing-Treaty-Partners`; outputs `Governance: Binding-Peace-Covenant` (which halts military aggression across connected corridors) and `Social: Cross-Faction-Trust`.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Even without external visitors, the grove yields valuable restorative herbs and calming silver resin.
