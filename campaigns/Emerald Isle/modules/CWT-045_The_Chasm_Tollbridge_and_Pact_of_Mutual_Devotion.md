# MODULE CWT-045: THE CHASM TOLLBRIDGE & PACT OF MUTUAL DEVOTION

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Dual Bastion Span
Leaping across a sheer, cloud-filled abyss twelve hundred feet deep, the Chasm Bridge connects the northern mountains to the fertile southern plains. Built from interlocking blocks of dove-grey granite, the bridge is defended at each terminus by a fortified stone tower owned by one of two ancient rival clans: House Vane at the North Gate, and House Oln at the South Gate. Under the sacred Treaty of Devotion signed two centuries ago, each clan holds half of the mechanical gear-keys required to raise the central counter-weighted portcullises; neither house can open the bridge alone, turning mutual interdependence into the bedrock of regional trade.

* **The Great Granite Arch:** A single monumental catenary arch spanning four hundred feet of empty space, flanked by solid stone parapets built chest-high against fierce valley crosswinds.
* **The Dual Gear-Houses:** Twin windlasses requiring synchronized turns from both clan captains simultaneously to lift the spiked portcullis barriers.
* **The Under-Arch Cradle:** A maintenance platform suspended by chains eighty feet below the roadway, where masons inspect foundation voussoirs and scrape off frost.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Keepers of the Dual Gates):** Captains from Houses Vane and Oln who stand side-by-side at the toll booths, splitting every copper coin into paired iron boxes.
* **Main Opponent (The Gorge Free-Riders):** Daring canyon bandits using rope grapples and leather wings to swing beneath the span, hijacking valuable pack loads in mid-air.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Betraying Suitor):** A disinherited clan noble plotting to assassinate the bride of the upcoming dynastic marriage, intending to blame the other house and trigger total bridge seizure.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Abyss Dung-Sweepers):** Penniless wretches tethered by frayed hemp ropes to the guardrails, sweeping horse droppings and ice off the narrow roadway in freezing sixty-mile-per-hour winds.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Edge (04 - 05 Mountain Gap)**, **Edge (08 - 14 Canyon Transit)**, or between any two disconnected regional clusters.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Geography: Deep-Canyon-Chasm` and `Material: Hewn-Granite-Arch-Stones`; outputs `Transit: Fortified-Chasm-Bridge` (converts a severed or blocked edge into a high-throughput corridor) and `Social: Inter-Clan-Devotion-Pact`.
* **Standalone Fallback:** The secondary rope bridge beneath the main span remains passable for unladen travelers even when the main road is shuttered.
