# MODULE CWT-072: ANTIKYTHERA CLOCKWORK ASTRONOMICAL RELIQUARY

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Horological Engineering Grounding
Recovered from the tempest-battered shelf off the jagged Aegean islet of Antikythera, the **Antikythera Clockwork Astronomical Reliquary** represents the absolute pinnacle of ancient mechanical computation and astronomical science. Preserved within a cedar-framed bronze case measuring roughly 340 × 180 × 90 millimeters, the device is the earliest known mechanical analog computer. Dating to the 1st century BCE (circa 70–60 BCE, corresponding to a Roman grain-and-plunder merchant galley laden with looted Hellenistic bronze statues, Syrian glassware, and marble colossi bound from Rhodes or Pergamon toward Rome), it operationalizes complex mathematics into a physical, hand-cranked gearwork cosmos.

Rather than a static decorative dial, the mechanism contains **at least 37 precision bronze gears** cut from low-oxidation alpha-tin bronze sheets (average thickness 1.5–2.0 mm), featuring hand-divided triangular teeth with 60-degree angles and pitch radii as small as 1.5 millimeters.

The device's mechanical architecture is grounded in four revolutionary horological systems:
1. **The Pin-and-Slot Differential Mechanism**: The machine incorporates a mechanical differential turntable—a principle not reinvented in Europe until the 16th century CE. It mechanically subtracts the angular motion of the Sun from that of the Moon to compute the synodic lunar phase. To model the Moon's variable orbital velocity (what Keplerian orbital mechanics would later explain as an elliptical orbit with non-uniform velocity), the mechanism implements an eccentric **pin-and-slot epicyclic gear coupling** derived from Hipparchus’s lunar anomaly theory. A gear rotating on an offset axis drives a second gear via an eccentric pin sliding within an elongated radial slot; the pin accelerates the driven gear at lunar perigee and decelerates it at apogee, replicating lunar epicyclic acceleration with sub-degree accuracy.
2. **The Front Cosmic Dashboard & Planetary Train**:
   * **The Solar & Zodiac Calendar**: Features two concentric rings—an outer 360-degree Zodiac ring divided into twelve 30-degree constellations, and an inner 365-day Egyptian solar calendar ring with an adjustable pin-and-hole intercalary ring to accommodate leap days every four years.
   * **The Mechanical Lunar Phase Sphere**: A tiny, geared spherical bead, half-burnished bronze and half-blackened copper, rotates along its own internal axis as it revolves around the Zodiac circle, physically displaying the exact phase of the Moon.
   * **The Epicyclic Planetary Pointers**: Geared planetary trains projecting from the front arbor drive miniature pointers tracking the synodic stations, retrogrades, and celestial longitudes of the five known classical planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn).
3. **The Multi-Spiral Back Dials & Eclipse Parapegma**:
   * **The Metonic Dial (Upper Back)**: A 5-turn Archimedean spiral partitioned into 235 cells, tracking the 19-year Metonic cycle that synchronizes solar years with whole lunar months. Nested within are subsidiary dials for the 76-year Callippic cycle and the **Olympiad Dial**, a 4-year dial timing the sacred Panhellenic games (Olympia, Pythia, Nemea, Isthmia).
   * **The Saros Dial & Exeligmos (Lower Back)**: A 4-turn spiral partitioned into 223 cells, tracking the Saros eclipse periodicity. Over 240 engraved glyph symbols (*glyph indices*) in the spiral cells specify the exact celestial hour, color, wind direction, and astronomical magnitude of upcoming solar and lunar eclipses. A nested 3-part *Exeligmos Dial* calibrates the 54-year triple-Saros cycle, mechanically adding 8 hours per Saros interval to maintain diurnal precision.
4. **The Tragic Technological Chasm & Marine Encrustation**: When the Roman prize galley broke its spine upon the submerged reefs of Antikythera, the device plunged into an abyssal underwater trench at a depth of 45 meters. In the cold, high-salinity bottom currents, the delicate bronze sheets and microscopic triangular teeth became fused with calcium carbonate, shells, and corrosive copper carbonate (verdigris). The loss of this single physical repository of differential mathematics and precision bronze gearing severed the transmission of automated analog horology from human knowledge, creating a 1,500-year technological amnesia until Renaissance cathedral clocks re-emerged.

* **The Cedar Casing & Input Arbor**: An outer box crafted from seasoned Lebanese cedar, fitted with bronze hinge plates and engraved instructions (*parapegma* inscriptions) detailing solar risings and stellar conjunctions. A square iron input winding arbor on the right flank accepts a manual crank.
* **The Differential Turntable & Epicyclic Gear Cage**: A bronze turntable carrying epicyclic gears e3 and e4, revolving inside the gearwork housing to calculate lunar orbital anomalies.
* **The Saros Spiral Groove & Sliding Guide Pin**: A spiral track along the back plate fitted with a sprung bronze pointer pin that glides along the helical groove as the crank turns, navigating the spiral without jumping threads.
* **The Roman Plunder Hold**: The waterlogged, fractured oak cargo deck of the sunken 300-ton Roman merchant galley, surrounded by rolling 100-pound wine amphorae, toppled marble horses, and crushed bronze statuary.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** *"Mechanical gears are crude brute instruments fit only for grinding corn, hoisting cut granite, and pumping bilge sludge; the majestic dances of the wandering planets and the terrifying shadow of celestial eclipses belong exclusively to the arbitrary wrath of the gods."*
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** A violent Aegean gale drives a heavily loaded Roman plunder galley onto the razor-sharp submerged reefs of Antikythera; while hull planks split and freezing seawater inundates the hold, the Greek master-horologist and the Roman consular prize-master wage a lethal duel over the bronze clockwork reliquary as the vessel slides toward a 45-meter seabed trench.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Isolated Intellectual Mechanical Genius} + \text{Imperial Plunder Shipwreck Severance} = \text{Fifteen Centuries of Technological Amnesia}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** Theoretical scientific brilliance cannot survive without an institutional industrial ecosystem. When a masterwork of human intellect is treated merely as imperial loot rather than shared knowledge, a single maritime catastrophe can plunge human civilization into a millennium and a half of technological darkness.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Roman Consular Plunder Fleet Admiral — Proconsular Legate Lucius Licinius):**  
  A ruthless military commander and patrician looter returning from the sack of eastern Greek academies. Clad in gilded muscle cuirass and scarlet cloak, Licinius views the cedar-encased mechanism solely as an exotic mechanical toy to present to the Senate or Julius Caesar to secure a provincial governorship. Ignorant of mathematics, he treats the Greek artisans as enslaved chattels and the astronomical dials as curiosities, threatening instant crucifixion if any sailor damages the bronze casing during bailing.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Rhodes Astrological Clockmaker — Geminus of Rhodes):**  
  The brilliant, fanatical natural philosopher and horologist who spent three decades calculating gear ratios and hand-cutting the 37 bronze wheels. Captured alongside his creation, Geminus despises Roman martial barbarism. He understands that Rome values conquest over calculation, and is prepared to sabotage the differential pins, throw the gear assemblies overboard, or drown alongside his machine rather than let his sacred mechanical cosmos become a parlor trick for Roman oligarchs.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (The Coral-Encrusted Diver Guildmaster — Captain Symeon of Symi):**  
  The hardened master of a free-diving sponge fleet operating out of the Dodecanese. Intimately familiar with the treacherous depths off Antikythera, Symeon cares nothing for astronomy, imperial prestige, or philosophical treatises. He views the shipwreck solely as a resource bonanza: his divers use heavy stones and oiled air-bladders to descend into the abyss, wielding bronze cold-chisels to pry off gear arbors and melt down the low-oxidation tin-bronze into saleable metal ingots for Syrian weaponsmiths.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (The Heretical Differential Mathematician — Kallisto the Exiled):**  
  A former disciple of the Academy living as a cave-dwelling hermit among the limestone cliffs of Antikythera. Banished for preaching that the universe runs on mathematical clockwork rather than divine caprice, Kallisto is the only living person besides Geminus who can decipher the Saros spiral inscriptions. Looking out at the storm, Kallisto realizes that the mechanism’s back dial has just clicked into alignment with a rare glyph: a total solar eclipse that will plunge the entire reef into unnatural freezing darkness within sixty minutes.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Clockwork Precision & Shipwreck Abyss Dial
The physical integrity of the vessel, the mechanical functionality of the 37 bronze gears, and the countdown to catastrophic marine submersion are monitored along a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Creator Studio's Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Gear Tooth Click & Bronze Canker Stiffening / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The hand-crank is turned; internal bronze gears click with precision; sea spray wets the cedar casing; fine salt mist begins accelerating verdigris oxidation across exposed tooth teeth.
  * *Mechanics:* Operating the manual crown crank to calculate celestial ephemerides requires a **DC 10 Intelligence or Crafting (Clockwork) check**. A successful check aligns the solar, lunar, and planetary pointers, granting advantage on all celestial Navigation and Astrological divination checks for the next 24 hours. Navigating the rocking ship deck during preliminary swells requires a **DC 10 Balance (Acrobatics) check** to avoid dropping loose tools.
* **Level 2 (Pin-and-Slot Jam & Reef Gale Breakers / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Ferocious gale waves pitch the Roman galley into the breakers; saltwater floods the upper hold; dynamic torque causes the eccentric pin of the lunar anomaly gear to bind inside its slot; the differential turntable binds; hull strakes shudder.
  * *Mechanics:* Clearing the bound pin-and-slot mechanism without bending the delicate 1.5-mm bronze arbor requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) or Engineering check**. Failure shears a gear tooth, imposing disadvantage on all subsequent lunar predictions and locking the Olympiad dial. Navigating the galley across churning breakers requires a **DC 13 Vehicles (Water) check**; failure inflicts **2d8 bludgeoning damage** to crew members from snapping rigging and shifting ballast.
* **Level 3 (Hull Fracture & Differential Escapement Slip / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The galley impales its keel on a submerged limestone reef spur; oak planks shatter; knee-deep seawater and floating cargo flood the hold; 100-pound terracotta wine amphorae break loose and roll across the listing deck; the Saros eclipse pointer slips its spiral track.
  * *Mechanics:* High-velocity interior hazard. Characters within the cargo hold must succeed on a **DC 16 Reflex save** each round to avoid rolling amphorae and splintering ribs; failure inflicts **4d10 bludgeoning and crushing damage**, trapping the victim beneath shattered pottery and ballast stones. Rescuing the 30-pound cedar-encased mechanism from the surging bilge requires a **DC 16 Athletics or Mechanics check** before water reaches the central gear train.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic 45-Meter Plunge & Cosmic Eclipse Lock / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* The galley snaps along its transverse beams and slips off the reef shelf, plunging into the 45-meter abyssal seabed trench; simultaneously, the Saros dial reaches the terminal glyph, triggering a total solar eclipse that plunges the storm into icy, pitch-black daytime darkness.
  * *Mechanics:* Absolute aquatic disaster. Characters caught aboard the fracturing hull must succeed on a **DC 20 Survival or Athletics (Swimming) check**; failure inflicts **8d12 lethal crushing and drowning damage** from collapsing masts, swirling whirlpools, and rapid pressure descent. If the reliquary settles onto the 45-meter seabed floor without recovery, the module permanently enters its Fallback State: the differential mechanism fuses with calcium carbonate, and precision analog clockwork is permanently eradicated from the setting for 1,500 years.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Saros Total Eclipse Countdown Clock (6 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ]       Galley Hull Plunge to 45m Abyss Clock (4 Ticks)
```

* **Saros Total Eclipse Countdown Clock [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each round or operational phase that the manual input crank is wound forward toward the glyph indices, or as storm squalls build toward the celestial conjunction.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* The sky dims to an eerie copper twilight; temperature drops sharply; sea birds shriek and crash into the rigging in panic.
    * *Tick 4:* Baily's Beads appear as the solar crescent narrows to a sliver; shadow bands ripple across the foam; winds violently reverse direction.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* The black disk of the Moon completely covers the Sun. Totality plunges the reef and sea into pitch-black darkness, triggering **Dial Level 4: Cosmic Eclipse Lock**. Characters without darkvision suffer blinded status; panicked crew members abandon bailing stations to pray or mutiny.
* **Galley Hull Plunge to 45m Abyss Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) whenever the vessel strikes submerged reef spurs, when bilge pumps jam, or when the party fails an emergency ship repair or seamanship check.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Hull strakes spring loose; freezing seawater pours through the forward cargo deck.
    * *Tick 2:* Keel snaps with a deafening crack; steering oars shear away; the galley lists 30 degrees to port.
    * *Tick 3:* The aft section fills with water; stern slips off the reef and hangs suspended over the 45-meter abyssal shelf.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* The galley fractures completely in two. Both halves slide down the sheer underwater precipice into the 45-meter trench, dragging all unrescued cargo and crew into the benthic abyss.

### 4. Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Metal: Low-Oxidation-Fine-Bronze-Gears`: High-purity copper-tin alloy bronze (approx. 90% Cu, 10% Sn) cold-worked and hammered into uniform sheets capable of maintaining sharp 60-degree triangular teeth without brittleness or catastrophic oxidation.
  * `Mathematics: Hipparchian-Lunar-Calculus`: Advanced geometrical and trigonometric formulations of epicyclic motion, eccentricities, and synodic cycles (incorporating the 235-month Metonic, 223-month Saros, and Hipparchian lunar anomaly models).
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Relic: Differential-Bronze-Clockwork-Computer`: The complete 37-gear mechanical analog calculation engine, capable of modeling non-linear celestial mechanics, planetary stations, and mechanical time computation.
  * `Science: Saros-Eclipse-Predictor`: Automated predictive capacity for both solar and lunar eclipses decades in advance, recording celestial hours, magnitudes, and eclipse colors.
  * `Navigation: Precision-Zodiacal-Ephemeris`: Complete celestial ephemeris tracking the solar, lunar, and planetary longitudes, granting absolute navigational orientation across any open ocean or uncharted territory.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When the plunder galley sinks and the horologist drowns, the installation permanently enters **Lost Relic Marine Encrustation Fallback**:
    * The cedar case rots away; the 37 bronze gears fuse into a calcified, rock-like mass of calcium carbonate, copper hydroxychloride, and verdigris resting 45 meters beneath the Aegean Sea.
    * The science of differential gearing and precision horological calculation is completely erased from human civilization for **1,500 years**.
    * Recovering the mechanism from the 45-meter abyss requires specialized deep-diving apparatus or magic, imposing **DC 20 Athletics (Diving) checks** and risk of nitrogen narcosis. Restoring the fused lump without destroying the microscopic gear teeth requires multi-year chemical acid baths and a **DC 20 Engineering/Archeology check**.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Spatial Encounters

* **Encounter 1: The Turning of the Bronze Crank (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* In the violently pitching main cabin of the Roman plunder galley, the party discovers the cedar box. As a character touches the square bronze winding crown, Geminus of Rhodes lunges forward with a bronze cold-chisel, screaming that uninitiated hands will strip the gear arbors. Simultaneously, Proconsular Legate Lucius Licinius kicks the door open with drawn gladius, followed by four armored legionaries, shouting that the chest belongs to Rome and ordering the execution of anyone who touches the prize.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party discovers what appears to be an ornamental chest of loot, only to trigger an instant three-way lethal standoff inside a pitching, water-logged cabin while the gear train begins clicking on its own.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* Licinius shouts about the majesty of the Roman Senate and the monetary value of Greek art, but his eyes reveal superstitious dread of the clicking gears, which seem to turn with occult life. Geminus screams insults at the soldiers, but his trembling hands cradle the delicate differential cage to protect it from stray blade strikes.
  * *Tactical Mechanics:* De-escalating the standoff requires a **DC 13 Persuasion or Intimidation check**. Intercepting Geminus's chisel without damaging the casing requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check**. Every round of melee combat inside the pitching cabin risks bumping the crank, advancing the *Saros Eclipse Clock* by +1 tick.
* **Encounter 2: The Reef Breakers of Antikythera (Hull Crisis / Environmental Dilemma)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* With a bone-shattering shudder, the galley impales its forward timber hull on a jagged underwater reef off the Antikythera cliffs. Freezing seawater geysers through the hold floorboards; dozens of heavy 100-pound terracotta wine amphorae snap their hemp lashing and roll across the slanting deck like battering rams.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Option A (Save the 37-Gear Clockwork Reliquary):* The party can dedicate their actions to wrapping the mechanism in waterproof oiled fleece, clearing the pinned gear train, and hauling the 30-pound bronze box up the slick companionway ladder (requiring a **DC 16 Athletics or Mechanics check** while dodging rolling amphorae, **DC 16 Reflex** or take **4d10 crushing damage**). This secures the greatest technological artifact of the ancient world; however, while the party secures the relic, forty enslaved rowers chained to their benches in the flooding lower hold drown in the rising black bilge water.
    * *Option B (Smash the Benches & Free the Chained Rowers):* The party can use their weapons and prybars to smash the iron chains and save the forty drowning rowers (requiring **DC 13 Athletics or Weapon Attack checks** against the chains). All forty human lives are spared; however, during the chaotic rescue, an unsecured marble statue of Hercules topples across the cedar box, crushing the bronze gear train into mangled scrap and stripping thirty teeth from the differential turntable.
* **Encounter 3: The 45-Meter Abyssal Plunge (Climax Salvage Dilemma / Eclipse Meltdown)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The galley’s stern breaks free and hangs over the sheer edge of the continental shelf, beginning its final slide into the 45-meter seabed trench. Concurrently, the Saros clock expires: the total solar eclipse locks into place, extinguishing the sun and plunging the tempestuous reef into pitch-black midday midnight. In the screaming darkness, Captain Symeon’s sponge divers surface around the sinking stern with grappling hooks and harpoons, attempting to haul the chest away to their own craft while cutting the party’s mooring lines.
  * *Tactical Environment:* Pitch-black storm darkness; water churning over a sheer 45-meter drop; characters without light sources suffer blinded status. Free-diving sponge divers attack from the water using barbed trident poles and line-cutting knives.
  * *Resolution:* The party must secure a heavy hemp recovery line to the reliquary’s bronze iron-banded carrying ring, fight off the looters (**DC 13 Athletics/Combat**), and make a **DC 20 Athletics (Swimming) or Survival check** to swim clear of the sinking hull's suction before the 300-ton galley plunges into the 45-meter trench, burying the wreckage in benthic silt forever.

### 6. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies

* **Recommended Placement:**
  * **Node 19 / 20 (Deep Ocean Hazard Trench / Storm-Tossed Reef Shoal)**: Serves as an isolated maritime hazard node where high-tier parties must brave treacherous coastal navigation or deep-sea diving to recover legendary technological artifacts.
  * **Node 21 / 22 (Campaign Climax Nexus / Astronomical Horizon)**: Placed as the ultimate intellectual or occult objective required to predict world-ending cosmic alignments, plan planar jumps, or unlock lost ancient super-structures.
* **Topological Synergies:**
  * **The Thonis-Heracleion Liquefaction Port (`CWT-062`)**: Sockets directly downstream from maritime customs hubs. The plunder galley can be the very vessel that cleared customs at Thonis-Heracleion carrying looted temple reliquaries before striking the Antikythera reefs.
  * **Systemic Interdependence Collapse Matrix (`CWT-060`)**: Functions as the primary intellectual "Extinction Event" in the collapse matrix. Losing the reliquary represents the severance of precision metallurgy, scientific astronomy, and horological education, transitioning the campaign setting into a dark age.
