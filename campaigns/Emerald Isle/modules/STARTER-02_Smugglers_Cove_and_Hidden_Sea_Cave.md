# MODULE STARTER-02: SMUGGLERS' COVE & HIDDEN SEA CAVE

### 1. Architectural & Natural Layout
A secluded sea-arch cove concealed behind razor-sharp coastal reefs. At low tide, a natural cavern mouth opens into a dry limestone vault large enough to conceal three rowboats and twenty crates of cargo.

* **Tidal Gate:** Navigable only at half to low tide; rowing boats through at high tide requires a DC 13 Agility check to avoid crushing on reef spikes.
* **Hoisting Platform:** Roped timber cranes hoisted from an upper cliff ledge allow goods to be lifted directly to the plateau above, bypassing all road patrols.
* **Fence's Den:** A discreet cave chamber furnished with driftwood tables, oil lamps, and iron lockboxes where black-market merchants barter.

### 2. GM Placement & Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 20 (Smugglers' Cove)** or any secluded coastal node.
* **Synergies:** Siphons untaxed trade away from **Node 1 (Harbor Haven)**; buys stolen ore from **Node 17 (Abandoned Iron Mine)**.
