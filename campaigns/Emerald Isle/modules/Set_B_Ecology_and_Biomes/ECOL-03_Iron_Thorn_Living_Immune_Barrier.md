# MODULE ECOL-03: THE IRON-THORN LIVING IMMUNE BARRIER

### 1. Botanical Architecture

A living, sentient ecological defense barrier that acts as the primary "immune system" of pristine wilderness against colonial industrial expansion.

* **Iron-Thorn Vines:** Thick, fibrous black brambles interlaced with hardened lignified thorns sharp enough to pierce iron plate.
* **Ferrous Sensitivity:** The root network detects electromagnetic vibrations and oxidized iron. Any metal-bearing soldiers crossing the border cause the thicket to rapidly curl and constrict.
* **The Fey Court's Sylvan Law:** Guarded by Blink Dogs, Pixies, and Centaur Wardens who forbid the carrying of unsheathed iron into the sacred groves.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Ecological Defense
* **Buffer Zone:** Ideal for placement between an industrial node (e.g., Foundry or Mine) and an ancient nature sanctuary.
* **Memory Tithes:** The Fey Guardian allows passage only if travelers surrender a treasured personal memory or secret lore clue.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Entangling Thicket:** Armored travelers moving through the barrier must make an Athletics check (2d6). On a failure, they are pinned by living thorns and advance 1 tick on the Iron-Crush Clock.
