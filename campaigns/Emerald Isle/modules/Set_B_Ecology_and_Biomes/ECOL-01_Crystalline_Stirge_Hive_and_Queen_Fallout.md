# MODULE ECOL-01: CRYSTALLINE STIRGE HIVES & QUEEN FALLOUT

### 1. Biological Profile

The apex aerial pest vector of the campaign network. While colloquial settlers dismiss them as "giant mosquitoes," true stirges are silicon-based bio-predators.

* **Crystalline Anatomy:** Translucent razor-edged wings that generate high-frequency acoustic clicks; hollow quartz proboscis designed to drill through leather and flesh to siphon hemoglobin.
* **Thermal & Iron Scent Tracking:** Stirges do not track visually in darkness; they home in on body heat, open combustion, and the scent of oxidized blood-iron.
* **Subterranean Queens:** Massive, bloated 15-foot immobile Queens anchored deep inside limestone karst chimneys, producing thousands of larvae per month.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Ecological Synergy
* **Nesting Sockets:** Drops into vertical cliffs, abandoned mine shafts, or dark forest canopies.
* **Countermeasures:** Repelled only by silver particulate updrafts (`INFRA-03`), pitcher-plant mist (`INFRA-05`), or sulfur-infused Zim-Sharr torches.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics & Queen Fallout
* **Blood Drain Attack:** A stirge latches on contact; drains 1 point of Vitality per round until crushed or burned off.
* **The Queen Death Rule:** Killing a Hive Queen triggers an immediate, island-wide **Pheromone Surge**. For $1\text{d}4$ days, all stirge swarms on the island enter an uncoordinated, hyper-aggressive frenzy, attacking settlements regardless of smoke barriers.
