# MODULE ECOL-06: HIGH CANOPY REDWOODS & AMBER-RESIN TAPS

### 1. Arboreal Ecology

A colossal vertical forest ecosystem rising 500 feet into the clouds, completely bypassing the predator-infested ground floor.

* **500-Foot Sequoia Giants:** Massive ancient trunks with bark 2 feet thick, fire-resistant and immune to fungal decay.
* **Amber-Pitch Resin:** Rich, golden botanical resin tapped from tree trunks. Coating iron gear in boiled amber pitch grants **total immunity to the Iron-Blight Clock**.
* **Glider Communities:** Autonomous clans who navigate the upper canopies using bat-winged spider-silk gliding capes and counter-weighted rope trolleys.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Atmospheric Advantages
* **Stirge Resistance:** High canopy winds and continuous pitch-smoke cauldrons prevent stirges from swarming in the upper 300 feet.
* **Vertical Travel:** Ascending to the canopy requires rope-winch elevators or perilous ladder climbs; cutting elevator counterweights traps invaders on the forest floor.
