# MODULE ECOL-04: CARNIVOROUS PITCHER BOGS & GLANDULAR SLUGS

### 1. Wetland Ecology

A deeply carnivorous wetland biome where plant life actively hunts warm-blooded fauna and giant mollusks dredge acidic peat.

* **Carnivorous Pitcher Plants:** Gargantuan, ribbed fibrous pitchers rising 10 to 15 feet from stagnant water. Their rims secrete sweet, narcotic nectar that lures animals; interior walls are slick with downward-pointing hairs over 8 feet of concentrated digestive fluid.
* **Glandular Giant Slugs:** Massive, 20-foot amphibious mollusks that feed on decaying peat and dead stirge carcasses. Their trail of acidic slime melts boots and iron buckles, but their glandular secretions are the primary ingredient in anti-scent mist.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Resource Extraction
* **Synergy with Mist Poles (`INFRA-05`):** Harvesters dare the bogs to milk living pitcher glands for alchemical camouflage fluid.
* **Boardwalk Chokepoints:** Settlements build elevated wooden boardwalks to cross the bog; cutting boardwalk pillars drops pursuers straight into the waiting pitcher mouths.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Pitcher Grab:** Brushing against a pitcher rim triggers sensitive trigger hairs. Agility check (2d6) or slide into the vat, suffering acid trauma every round until hauled out by rope.
