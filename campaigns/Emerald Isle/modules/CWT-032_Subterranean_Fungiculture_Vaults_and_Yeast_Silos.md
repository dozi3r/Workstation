# MODULE CWT-032: SUBTERRANEAN FUNGICULTURE VAULTS & YEAST SILOS

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Lightless Harvest
Carved into deep limestone caverns insulated from surface droughts, freezes, and siege warfare, the Fungiculture Vaults provide an unbreakable caloric floor. Hot geothermal steam vents are piped across terraced beds of decomposed straw, mine manure, and woodchips. In this humid darkness, bioluminescent oyster mushrooms, iron-stem button caps, and fast-growing yeast mounds generate tons of dense protein without an ounce of sunlight.

* **The Steaming Shelf Tunnels:** Multi-tiered cavern chambers fitted with rot-resistant cedar racks where mycelial colonies fruit in continuous eight-day cycles.
* **The Yeast Fermentation Silos:** Giant copper-banded stone vats bubbling with sourdough and brewer's yeast, vented through water traps to harvest pure carbon dioxide for underground fire dampening.
* **The Slag-Compost Trench:** A subterranean processing pit where surface trash, kitchen waste, and draft-animal bedding are inoculated with ravenous saprophytic bacteria.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Spore-Wardens Guild):** A secretive subterranean guild wearing oiled leather masks and grease-rubbed smocks. They guard moisture meters with their lives and distribute dense mushroom bricks strictly by ration ticket.
* **Main Opponent (Surface Merchant Oligarchs):** Grain cartel lords whose extortionate overland flour monopolies collapse whenever the underground vaults pump cheap mushroom loaves onto the market.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Black-Caps Apothecaries):** Criminal distillers cultivating hallucinogenic purple mold varieties in deep abandoned stopes to brew intoxicating "Cave Gin" for black-market dens.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Blind Bed-Turners):** Lifelong subterranean laborers who work in pitch darkness, turning steaming compost beds with wooden forks by smell and feel alone.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 03 (Goblin Warrens / Clay Pits)**, **Node 18 (Deep Subterranean Caverns)**, or beneath any besieged castle or mountain citadel.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Moisture: Geothermal-Steam-Condensation` and `Organic: Compost-Mulch-Slag`; outputs `Food: Caloric-Fungal-Protein` (feeds garrisons indefinitely) and `Bio: Dry-Active-Yeast` (fuels bakeries, breweries, and alchemical labs).
* **Standalone Fallback:** When cut off from surface refuse, the beds switch to limestone moss cultivation, sustaining core spore genetics at subsistence levels.
