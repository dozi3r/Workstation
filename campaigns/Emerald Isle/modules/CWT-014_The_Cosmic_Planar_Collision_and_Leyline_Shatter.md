# MODULE CWT-014: THE COSMIC PLANAR COLLISION & LEYLINE SHATTER

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Climax Trigger
High atop the apex celestial crags, an experimental array of acoustic menhirs was tethered directly to the hydroelectric arc grid. The objective was instantaneous wireless telegraphy across the sea; the result was a puncture wound through the dimensional skin of the material plane. Now, an alien sky of two suns and violet rings bleeds into our atmosphere, warping local gravity and transmuting mundane materials into reality-distorting alloys.

* **The Leyline Apex Fracture:** A vertical tear in the sky two hundred yards tall, humming at 432 Hz, emitting pulses of warm crystalline light that bend shadows horizontally.
* **Non-Euclidean Topography:** Walking between two connected nodes no longer takes a predictable day of travel; distances expand or contract based on the emotional resonance of travelers.
* **The Climax Vector:** When the 8-tick Planar Incursion Clock fills, the physical laws of the campaign network are rewritten, blending fantasy medieval terrain with an alien crystalline cosmos.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The High Observatory Astrologers):** Magocrats who view the rift as humanity's stepping stone to godhood. They demand all regional energy output be diverted into the menhirs to widen the tear and harvest alien star-matter.
* **Main Opponent (The Geotectonic Wardens):** Earth-priests and conservative dwarven tunnelers who know that tearing the sky tears the planetary crust. They seek to detonate black-powder charges beneath the menhirs, burying the experiment in an avalanche.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Planar Scrapper Consortium):** Smugglers and artificers who venture into the perimeter of the tear to harvest floating zero-gravity ores, selling antigravity wagon axles to high bidders regardless of contamination.
* **Tertiary Outcast (Spatial Rift Mutants):** Peasants who were caught in early micro-shears whose shadows are detached from their bodies, speaking backwards prophecies and wandering the shifting passes.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 19 (High Celestial Spire)** or **Node 22 (Subterranean Core Key)**.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Esot: Resonant-Menhir-Array` (from `INFRA-07`) and `Power: Extreme-Arc-Current` (from `INFRA-02`); yields `Matter: Exotic-Planar-Alloys` needed to construct flying vessels or reality-anchoring armor.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Collapses into harmless magnetic dust if arc-current dynamos shut down.
