# MODULE CWT-043: THE COLOSSAL FOUNDRY OF STRENGTH & CRUCIBLE STEEL

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Titanic Metallurgy
Spanning a roaring mountain gorge whose churning waters drive three fifty-foot undershot waterwheels, the Colossal Foundry transforms raw sponge iron into the strongest structural steel known to modern engineering. Here, titanic mechanical drop-hammers weighing up to four thousand pounds pound glowing steel blooms day and night, their ground-shaking rhythm audible ten miles downriver. Giant clay crucibles heated by forced-air blast furnaces melt high-carbon alloys into flawless liquid steel for fortress gates and monumental bridges.

* **The Trip-Hammer Hall:** A subterranean colonnade of massive iron trip-hammers driven by cam-notched timber shafts, beating out scale and slag with deafening force.
* **The Blast Crucible Pits:** Sub-floor furnaces lined with refractory fireclay, where molten steel reaches two thousand degrees beneath swirling fluxes of crushed oyster shells and silica.
* **The Girders Casting Bed:** Sand molds cut into the stone floor fifty feet long, where molten alloy is poured to create one-piece structural bridge spans.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Syndicate of Foundry Masters):** Aristocratic metallurgical engineers who guard heat formulas in ciphered notebooks, demanding ruthless production quotas from every work gang.
* **Main Opponent (The Hammer-Floor Labor Union):** Sweat-drenched strikers who threaten to smash the water gates unless shift hours are capped to stop sleep-deprived workers falling into open slag pits.
* **Thematic Shadow (Imperial Munitions Contractors):** Corrupt brokers who dilute the crucible mix with cheap pig iron to pocket profits, knowingly shipping brittle sword blades to front-line levies.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Deaf Hammer-Lads):** Young apprentices who grease moving trip-hammer cams amidst ninety-decibel pounding, having lost all hearing before their fifteenth year.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 02 (River Waterfall & Watermill)**, **Node 07 (Iron Ore Mines & Slag Heap)**, or any heavy transit chokepoint requiring bridge components.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Material: Direct-Reduced-Iron` and `Power: Waterwheel-Hydraulic-Torque`; outputs `Material: High-Tensile-Crucible-Steel` and `Material: Heavy-Structural-Girders` (vital for heavy transit bridges like CWT-045).
* **Standalone Fallback:** Maintains basic repairs and wrought iron manufacture even when industrial water supplies drop.
