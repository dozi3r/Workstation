# MODULE CWT-060: THE SYSTEMIC INTERDEPENDENCE COLLAPSE MATRIX

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Macro-Systemic Vulnerability
Modelled on the synchronous systemic collapse of Late Bronze Age Mediterranean and Near Eastern palatial economies (~1177 BCE), this module simulates how hyper-specialized, highly optimized trade leagues shatter when long-distance logistics fail under synchronized stress.

In this civilizational architecture, sovereign power relies entirely on the precise metallurgical alchemy of bronze: combining local copper with rare, distant tin transported over thousands of leagues of precarious maritime corridors and mountain passes. No single realm controls both resources. Palatial administrations maintain massive redistributive storehouses, bureaucratic scribes recording taxes on clay tablets, and elite chariot regiments. When multi-year droughts or peripheral disruptions sever a single critical trade link, the hyper-optimized network cannot adapt. The domino effect is sudden and systemic: without tin, armories cannot cast weapons; without grain surpluses, palatial garrisons mutiny; displaced littoral raiders sever the remaining maritime passages; and populations abandon burning stone palaces to scatter into illiterate, self-sufficient highland subsistence.

* **The Palatial Megaron & Clay Tablet Archives:** The administrative epicenter where scribes catalog central grain silos, tax quotas, and bronze ingots. When debt ledgers burn, the institutional memory and legal legitimacy of the state vanish with them.
* **The Fragile Metallurgical Ratio (90/10 Dependency):** Regional smithies can cast copper tools, but high-tier martial weapons, chariot fittings, and structural hardware require tin. Severing `Trade: Maritime-Tin-Corridor` stalls advanced metallurgy across all downstream network nodes.
* **Cascading Systemic Contagion:** Unlike localized disasters that affect a single settlement, trade disruption in one node propagates exponential instability across every connected edge, converting commercial highways into refugee-choked hazard funnels.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Corner 1: The Establishment (Palatial Scribes & Central Treasury):**  
  High administrators, royal bailiffs, and palatial accountants clinging fanatically to centralized authority. Facing empty granaries and missing trade flotillas, their response is ruthless authoritarian extraction: melting down ancestral bronze temple bells into spearheads, requisitioning peasant seed grain under armed guard, and rigidly rationing moldy barley to mutinous garrison troops.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (Littoral Sea-Raider Coalition):**  
  Displaced maritime clans, coastal refugees, and desperate renegades whose own island homelands burned during earlier droughts. Convinced that the corrupt, hoarding palatial empires are parasitic tyrannies that deserve total annihilation, they coordinate amphibious fleet landings in longboats, systematically torching coastal ports, granaries, and customs towers.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (Autarkic Smuggler Syndicates):**  
  Mercenary merchant cartels and opportunistic hill brokers operating from fortified mountain redoubts. They hoard tin ingots, scrap bronze, knapped flint blades, and dried grain, running predatory black markets that bleed the palace dry. They actively accelerate imperial collapse to purchase mortgaged royal estates and bankrupt aristocratic titles for pennies on the pound.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (Highland Clay-Potters & Pastoral Nomads):**  
  Transhumant goat herders, terraced hill farmers, and rustic potters who never relied on palatial grain rations, Linear scribal script, or expensive bronze tools. Watching the coastal capitals burn with detached grim satisfaction, they possess the rugged, localized survival knowledge required to endure the coming civilizational Dark Age.

### 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: The Systemic Collapse Cascade
The operational stability of the trade network is governed by a 4-level systemic cascade dial strictly bound to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Tin Scarcity & Scribe Rations / Light Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Scheduled maritime tin convoys fail to arrive. Palatial armories substitute impure copper alloys or melt domestic scrap.
  * *Mechanics:* Cost of manufactured metal weapons, heavy armor, and specialized tools doubles. Purchasing or requisitioning palatial supplies requires a **DC 10 Diplomacy or Insight check**; failure inflicts minor bureaucratic friction, long queues, and 1 level of fatigue.
* **Level 2 (Interdependent Edge Severance / Moderate Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* One primary trade corridor connecting the node to the broader pointcrawl is severed or blockaded. Palace guards go unpaid; border outposts face food deficits.
  * *Mechanics:* Adjacent connected nodes suffer a -2 penalty to economic stability. Node-wide unrest triggers a **DC 13 Charisma or Leadership check** to prevent bread riots; failure inflicts **2d6 resource damage** (stockpile loss) or physical injury from rioters. Traversing coastal edges requires a **DC 13 Survival or Stealth check** to avoid desperate deserter ambushes.
* **Level 3 (Palatial Requisition & Bread Riots / Severe Severity Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* High scribes dispatch royal chariots into rural districts to violently confiscate sacred temple bronze idols and agricultural seed stores.
  * *Mechanics:* Open insurrection erupts throughout the urban center. Navigating barricaded avenues or protecting personal gear requires a **DC 16 Fortitude or Athletics check**; failure inflicts **4d6 bludgeoning/trampling damage** and lasting physical harm. All influence links outbound from this node degrade into severe raider corridors.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic De-Urbanization & Palatial Inferno / Lethal Climax Tier):**  
  * *Condition:* Scribes flee, garrison mutineers shatter the palace gates, and mobs torch the administrative archives. Centralized governance collapses entirely.
  * *Mechanics:* The node loses its Sovereign Hub status permanently. Escaping the collapsing stone megaron and city-wide inferno requires a **DC 20 Reflex or Agility check**; failure inflicts **8d10 lethal fire/structural crushing damage**. All downstream supply dependencies immediately fail and revert to standalone fallback mode.

#### 3.2 Segmented Threat Clocks
* **Regional Supply Cascade Clock [8 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each time a connected node's trade or resource link is severed, an artisan caravan is intercepted, or a seasonal grain harvest fails.
  * *Consequence when full:* Triggers Dial Level 4 immediately: the central palace is burned, royal scribal literacy is abandoned, and the entire regional sub-graph enters an illiterate, decentralized Dark Age.
* **Sea-Raider Littoral Incursion Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each campaign turn the maritime approach remains unpatrolled or harbor defense tariffs go unpaid.
  * *Consequence when full:* A fleet of littoral sea raiders lands in force, blockading the harbor terminal, burning coastal warehouses, and looting municipal granaries.

### 4. Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Trade: Maritime-Tin-Corridor` (Secure sea lane delivering raw cassiterite or refined tin bars)
  * `Mineral: Refined-Ingot-Supply` (High-purity smelted copper or metal bullion)
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Governance: Systemic-Interdependence-Matrix` (Graph-wide trade efficiency and diplomatic federation mandate)
  * `Tension: Trade-Cascade-Contagion` (Systemic vulnerability rules that propagate supply shocks across edges)
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When input dependencies are severed or blockaded, this module immediately degrades into **Autarkic Emergency Mode**:
    * All advanced bronze casting halts; node economic output drops by 75%.
    * Artisans and soldiers revert to cold-hammered native copper, bone, and knapped flint tools.
    * The urban population disperses into satellite farmsteads practicing subsistence horticulture and goat pastoralism.
    * Outbound commercial trade routes permanently dissolve into high-hazard wilderness paths subject to predatory raiding.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks

* **Encounter 1: The Melt-Down Requisition (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* As the party enters the palatial lower ward, royal bronze-bailiffs and soot-stained guards demand the immediate surrender of all foreign metal armor, bronze blades, and trade ingots for "the Royal Emergency Foundry." Simultaneously, a crowd of furious temple acolytes forms a physical barricade around the temple's sacred bronze bells, screaming curses at the bailiff.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party arrives seeking formal diplomatic hospitality and rest, but finds themselves immediately branded as illegal contraband hoarders facing weapon confiscation.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The chief bailiff reads the requisition decree with booming bureaucratic haughtiness, but his hands tremble uncontrollably, and his white linen tunic is scorched with soot from outlying estates already in flames.

* **Encounter 2: The Scribe's Clay Tablet Bonfire (The Severance Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Outside the royal archival vaults, a rioting mob of indebted farmers and mutinous soldiers prepares to burn centuries of clay tax ledgers and debt rolls. The elderly Chief Archivist, bleeding from a cobblestone strike, crawls toward the party, offering a pouch of rare raw tin beads if they help smuggle the dynastic census tablets through the secret water sally-port.
  * *Dilemma:* If the party protects the archivist and saves the archives, they preserve imperial legal memory and property deeds, but consign the starving citizenry to perpetual serfdom; if they assist the mob, the debt slate is wiped clean and thousands celebrate freedom, but regional trade contracts dissolve and civilizational literacy is extinguished.

* **Encounter 3: The Highland Smuggler's Redoubt (Shadow Bargain)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Trapped in the dry foothills between burning lowlands and sea-raider camps, the party is contacted by an agent of the Autarkic Smuggler Syndicate. The smugglers possess hidden mountain caches of flint spearheads, smoked goat meat, and mountain spring water, but demand payment in confiscated palace bronze seals or lethal escort to bypass a desperate deserter roadblock.

### 6. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies

* **Recommended Placement:**  
  * **Node 01 (Sovereign Harbor Bastion / Capital)** or **Node 13 (Market Cross Central Terminal)**.
  * Ideal for socketing into any elected Sovereign Hub with maritime coastal links or critical caravan junctures.
* **Topological Synergies:**  
  * Acts as the macro-governance engine for multi-node trade alliances. Interlocks seamlessly with `INFRA-02` (Crucible Smelter) or `STARTER-01` (River Watermill).
  * Injects systemic risk: when paired with famine triggers (`SPARK-01` Salt Famine or regional drought), it forces players to choose between defending fragile international commerce or embracing decentralized local survival.
