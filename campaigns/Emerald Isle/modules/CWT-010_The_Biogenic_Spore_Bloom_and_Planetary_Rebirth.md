# MODULE CWT-010: THE BIOGENIC SPORE BLOOM & PLANETARY REBIRTH

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Climax Trigger
Deep within the central tectonic fault, an ancient macro-fungal nervous system stirs. What local miners cataloged as luminescent lichen is an apex planetary super-organism entering its 500-year fruiting cycle. As it awakens, it secretes digestive enzymes that liquify mortar, granite, and iron, transmuting artificial bastions into rich organic loam.

* **The Spore Caldera:** A subterranean chasm filled with pulsing fungal pillars fifty feet thick, venting rhythmic plumes of golden spores through geothermal vents.
* **Cellulose Transformation:** Those who inhale the deep spores do not perish; their nervous systems intertwine with the fungal network, granting telepathic communion across pointcrawl corridors at the price of irreversible anatomical metamorphosis.
* **The Climax Vector:** When Threat Clock segments fill, the super-organism breaches surface settlements simultaneously, forcing players to choose between burning the ecosystem with chemical sulfur or surrendering civilization to post-human ecological symbiosis.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (Bacteriological Quarantine Garrison):** Military bailiffs and alchemists manning perimeter sulfur braziers. Their doctrine is absolute sterilization: execute infected laborers, burn contaminated grain silos, and seal the deep tunnels with quicklime.
* **Main Opponent (The Chloroplast Cult / Sylvan Prophets):** Fanatics who view the mycelium as the world's rightful sovereign. They wage guerrilla sabotage against quarantine pumps, seeking to crack the bedrock and accelerate the global spore harvest.
* **Thematic Shadow (Desperate Alchemical Syndicate):** Mercenary apothecaries harvesting raw spore pods to distill high-priced youth elixirs, recklessly smuggling samples across quarantined borders for oligarchic patrons.
* **Tertiary Outcast (Feral Myco-Hybrids):** Quarantined laborers whose flesh has half-turned to spongy bark. Exiled by the garrison and distrusted by the cult, they guard the subterranean water flumes and seek a painless equilibrium.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 18 (Deep Subterranean Caverns)** or any central subterranean transit chokepoint.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Ecology: Subterranean-Moist-Vaults` and `Bio: Concentrated-Blood-Alkaloids` (from hospital wards or battlefields); yields `Bio: Planetary-Symbiotic-Sap` which can cure lethal blights or dissolve sovereign walls.
* **Standalone Fallback:** If severed from outside supply lines, the organism enters an autotrophic dormancy, glowing faintly and digesting ambient mineral moisture without advancing its climax clock.
