# MODULE CWT-015: THE GREAT PEASANT LEVÉE & TOTAL CLASS INSURGENCY

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Climax Trigger
Years of extractive resource tariffs, grain requisitions to feed industrial smelters, and mandatory military conscription have reached their breaking point. Triggered by a harsh winter and a sudden salt tariff hike, tenant farmers, smelter stokers, and runaway convicts have banded together into an armed revolutionary commune. Wielding re-forged scythes and stolen mining powder, they march on the sovereign power centers to tear down the class order.

* **The Commoner's Crucible:** A fortified wagon-ring camp of twenty thousand rebelling families sprawling across the central lowlands, governed by delegates elected from every plow-team.
* **The Ledger Bonfires:** Insurgents prioritize capturing county archives and tax vaults, burning cadastral surveys, debt notes, and land titles to make return to feudal tenure legally impossible.
* **The Climax Vector:** When the 8-tick Insurgency Clock fills, the peasant army storms the sovereign hubs simultaneously. If they succeed, the 5 Sovereign Hub balance is dismantled and replaced by an egalitarian confederation of autonomous village soviets.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Landed Gentry & Manorial Bailiffs):** Hereditary lords who view the revolt as sub-human blasphemy. They refuse all dialogue, barricade themselves inside stone keeps, and dispatch cavalry patrols to execute any commoner carrying steel.
* **Main Opponent (The Leveler Tribunal / Red Banner Prophets):** Radical ideologues leading the peasant levies. They demand the total abolition of nobility, private land ownership, and debt, executing any compromising councilor on portable gallows.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Black Market Munitions Smugglers):** Corrupt garrison armorers who siphon gunpowder kegs and crossbows from sovereign armories to sell directly to both the rebels and the noble retinues at extortionate gold markups.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Moderate Village Elders & Clerks):** Rural mayors and parish priests trying desperately to negotiate land reform without triggering a slaughter that will leave the entire island starving by winter.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 2 (Frontier Lowlands)** or **Node 13 (Independent Guild Syndicate)**.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Social: Starvation-State-Crisis` (from severed supply lines or drought) and `Munitions: Black-Powder-Reserves` (from `INFRA-04`); yields `Labor: Mobilized-Mass-Levies` capable of digging massive earthworks or overcoming heavily armored garrisons through sheer numbers.
* **Standalone Fallback:** Converts into decentralized guerrilla resistance if heavy munitions are unavailable.
