# MODULE CWT-041: THE SCHOLASTIC SCRIPTURIUM & CODEX ARCHIVE

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Institutional Memory
Constructed from dense limestone ashlar with slit windows oriented to capture pure northern daylight, the Archive serves as the legal and theological bedrock of the province. Behind reinforced ironbound oak doors lie three stories of oak shelving holding over ten thousand bound folios: royal land grants, guild charters, astronomical logs, and judicial precedent registers. In the central scriptorium, forty monks labor in absolute silence, scraping, inking, and illuminating duplicates to ensure no single fire can erase civic civilization.

* **The Scriptorium Gallery:** High vaulted hall with sloped walnut reading desks, where master copyists transcribe legal charters in microscopic Gothic script.
* **The High Chained Vault:** A secure sub-level where ancestral treaties, original sovereign coronations, and banned alchemical treatises are secured with heavy brass padlocks and chain tethers.
* **The Seal-Casting Scriptorium:** Workshop where scribes verify wax formulations, counter-stamping deeds with intricate heraldic signets.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Archival Curators):** Monastic scholars who believe written law is the sole barrier between human dignity and animal slaughter; they refuse entry to anyone without proper letters of reference.
* **Main Opponent (The Revisionist Royal Inquisitors):** Crown jurists seeking to burn the original charters of free peasant towns to legitimize arbitrary emergency taxes.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Antiquarian Syndicate):** Cultivated black-market bibliophiles who bribe underpaid novices to razor priceless illuminated plates out of ancient bestiaries.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Ink-Stained Scriveners):** Indentured scribes whose eyesight has been destroyed by decades of candle-lit copying; they reside in windowless garrets, subsisting on stale bread and pea soup.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
* **Placement:** Recommended on **Node 01 (Sovereign Castle & Town Hall)**, **Node 11 (Monastery & Scriptorium)**, or within the capital administrative precinct.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:** Consumes `Material: Cured-Vellum-and-Ink` and `Governance: Monastic-Sanctuary-Charter`; outputs `Knowledge: Verified-Legal-Precedents` (resolves factional territorial disputes) and `Governance: Uncontested-Charter-Deeds`.
* **Standalone Fallback:** When severed from trade, the archive functions on local slate etchings, providing immutable regional law.
