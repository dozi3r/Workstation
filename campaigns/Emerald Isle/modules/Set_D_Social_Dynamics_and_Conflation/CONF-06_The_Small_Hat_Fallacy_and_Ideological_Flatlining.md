# MODULE CONF-06: THE VISIBLE-PROXY FALLACY & IDEOLOGICAL FLATLINING

### 1. Sociological Weaponry Architecture

The psychological vulnerabilities of illiterate, traumatized populations that allow agitators to manipulate public panic without presenting verifiable evidence.

* **The Visible-Proxy Fallacy:**
  * In a complex totalitarian hierarchy, the true architects remain completely invisible behind distant walls. The convict populace directs all its hatred toward the most visible, accessible symbol of authority or difference—the petty clerk with the tax ledger, the foreign teamster delivering salt, or the apprentice measuring well water.
  * Agitators exploit this by directing mob violence away from the Sovereign Hub's governor and toward innocent minority craftsmen or neutral frontier merchants.
* **Ideological Flatlining:**
  * When a population is stripped of education and kept in perpetual survival panic, their cognitive bandwidth collapses. Complex logistical and geopolitical issues are "flatlined" into binary superstitions:
    * *"The well ran dry because the Gnomes didn't pray to the Mountain."*
    * *"The stirges attacked because the new convicts brought a cursed coin."*

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Agitator Loops
* **The Forger's Wedge:** Players or NPCs can exploit these cognitive fallacies using **Yellow Rags ([`ESOT-01`](file:///c:/Users/Milo/Documents/Antigravity/Creator/modules/Set_G_Esoterica_and_Information_Warfare/ESOT-01_Yellow_Rags_Weaponized_Print_Media_and_Forgery_Engine.md))** to turn a hostile garrison town against its own supply officers.
