# MODULE CONF-04: ALLY-ASSISTED CONFLATION & HEGEMONY SMOTHERING TACTICS

### 1. Advanced Information Warfare Design

The sophisticated geopolitical maneuver wherein a dominant imperial power forms a strategic coalition with a traditional local faction to manufacture total compliance.

* **The Mechanism:** When the White Hegemon (Imperial Administration) rules alone, it is hated as a foreign conqueror. To bypass this resistance, it co-opts the Green Traditionalists (local druids or village elders) and the Blue Technocrats (engineers).
* **The Triangular Trap:**
  1. The Imperial Governor provides soldiers and silver subsidies.
  2. The Local Priest preaches that submitting to the Governor is an ancient sacred custom ordained by the local earth spirits.
  3. The Technocrat displays blueprints proving that any deviation from the governor's quota will cause catastrophic infrastructure failure.
* **The Result:** Anyone resisting imperial taxation is attacked simultaneously as an unpatriotic traitor (White), a blasphemous heretic (Green), and an illiterate fool (Blue).

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Investigation Hooks
* **Unraveling the Alliance:** Players can break this hegemony by driving wedges between the allies—e.g., proving to the Green priests that the Blue engineers are secretly dumping toxic arc-furnace slag into the sacred burial grove.
