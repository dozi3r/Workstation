# MODULE CONF-03: THE GREEN-BLUE CONFLATION (TRADITIONALIST STAGNATION)

### 1. Social Warfare Architecture

The cultural paralysis mechanism where high intellectual theory is combined with pastoral traditionalism to crush innovation and maintain permanent agrarian helplessness.

* **The Lie:** *"Building a Blue-tech pump isn't just inefficient; it's a sin against our ancestors and a lethal threat to the village soul. We must exile the 'thinkers' to keep the community pure."*
* **How It Operates:** Green provides the reverence for the past (our grandfathers died carrying water buckets by hand; to carry water any other way insults their memory); Blue provides the labyrinthine philosophical arguments (writing treaties proving that mechanical pulleys corrupt the metaphysical harmony of the leyline).
* **The Result:** The settlement deliberately suffers preventable famines and disease rather than adopting basic sanitation or irrigation machinery. Any youth who tries to build a windmill or repair an aqueduct is branded a demon-worshiper and cast into the wilderness.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & NPC Dialogue
* **Elder Council Dialogue:** *"Yes, the children cough from the well-rot. But their souls are clean. If we allow that gnomish artificer to install his copper filtration pipes, the spirits of the soil will turn from us forever. Break his tools and burn his notebooks."*
* **Gameplay Hook:** PCs arrive with an engineering solution to save a dying community, only to find the village elders trying to execute the only apprentice who knows how to read the blueprints.
