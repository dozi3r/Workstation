# MODULE CONF-01: THE WHITE-BLUE CONFLATION (THE DIVINE BUREAUCRACY)

### 1. Social Warfare Architecture

This module codifies the premier psychological weapon deployed by the Imperial Hegemony to pacify convict populations without needing to maintain large standing armies.

* **The Lie:** *"The system is both mathematically perfect and morally divine."*
* **How It Operates:** White provides the moral framework (convicts are spiritually diseased sinners repaying their debt to the Architect); Blue provides the technocratic justification (actuarial life-expectancy formulas prove that a 14-hour workday in the sulfur mines is mathematically required to sustain the colony's energy balance).
* **The Result:** The populace internalizes their own oppression. Protesting an unfair silver quota is treated not as a political strike, but as an irrational, unscientific heresy that endangers the life-support grid.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & NPC Dialogue
* **Archpriest / Magistrate Dialogue:** *"I do not whip you because I hate you, convict. The equations calculated by the Architect demand 400 bushels of lime by sundown, or the flame wall suffocates us all. To resist the quota is to murder your own children."*
* **Investigation Hook:** Players discovering the Archpriest's private journals find that 60% of the silver quota is diverted directly into private escape galleons rather than the public wall troughs.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Gaslighting Aura:** Any PC attempting to organize labor resistance in this node must make a Will check (2d6). On a failure, public opinion turns against them, branding them "reckless terrorists who want to extinguish the wall fire."
