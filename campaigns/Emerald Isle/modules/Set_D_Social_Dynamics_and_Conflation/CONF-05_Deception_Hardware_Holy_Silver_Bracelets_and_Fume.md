# MODULE CONF-05: DECEPTION HARDWARE: HOLY SILVER BRACELETS & THE SUSTAINER'S BREATH

### 1. Theological Obfuscation Architecture

The material devices through which the ruling church transforms high-tech penal control and soul harvesting into liturgical blessings.

* **The Silver Bracelets (Soul Anchors):**
  * Distributed to convicts upon arrival at the pier gallows.
  * *Public Lie:* "Wear this sacred band; its argent sheen turns aside the fevered proboscis of the flying pests and wicks away the mountain chill."
  * *Metaphysical Mechanism:* Pure refined silver is the sole soul-conductive element on the island (`ADR-04`). The bracelet is etched on the inside with microscopic runic circuits that attune to the wearer's life-force. When the wearer dies, their departing soul is caught in the conductive loop and beamed straight along subterranean silver grounding rods to the Imperial Siphon (`Node 22`).
* **The Holy Fume (The Sustainer's Breath):**
  * The 60-foot curtain of sparking white smoke encircling the stronghold.
  * Settlers gather daily to inhale the drifting smoke and sing hymns, unaware they are standing directly in the toxic exhaust of an industrial calcium carbide reaction trough (`INFRA-03`).

### 2. GM Socket Placement & Player Discovery
* **The Blacksmith's Chisel:** Removing a silver bracelet without church rites requires an Agility / Tools check (2d6). Failing damages the wearer's wrist and sounds an acoustic alarm resonant on 3.5 MHz.
* **The Looting Dilemma:** Dead convicts with bracelets provide immediate silver value (melted down into 5 silver links), but carry a heavy spiritual curse if taken without grounding tools.
