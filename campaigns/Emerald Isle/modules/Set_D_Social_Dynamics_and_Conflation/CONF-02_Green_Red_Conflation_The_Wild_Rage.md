# MODULE CONF-02: THE GREEN-RED CONFLATION (THE WILD RAGE)

### 1. Social Warfare Architecture

The psychological framework adopted by frontier marauders and radical sylvan warriors to justify extreme atrocity under the guise of ecological balance.

* **The Lie:** *"Nature is violent, and so are we. The forest doesn't weep when the wolf disembowels the deer; why should we show mercy to the colonial interlopers?"*
* **How It Operates:** Green provides the ideological justification (the island's sacred soil is being raped by foreign iron tools and chemical smokewalls); Red provides the unhinged emotional momentum (gleeful torture, burning captured supply wagons, flaying guards alive).
* **The Result:** Legitimate ecological grievances (e.g., stopping toxic runoff from poisoning fresh streams) are weaponized into indiscriminate slaughter of anyone who wears manufactured cloth or speaks colonial dialects.

### 2. GM Socket Placement & NPC Dialogue
* **Bandit Warlord / Shaman Dialogue:** *"You call us murderers? Look at the Axe Beak ripping the foal's throat out. Look at the stirge draining the lamb. Blood is the currency of the living earth! We are merely the teeth of the forest, chewing up the soft meat of your civilization."*
* **Moral Dilemma:** Players face a group fighting against an undeniably evil colonial strip-mine, but doing so by crucifying innocent convict miners along the tree branches.

### 3. Vague Game Mechanics
* **Intimidation Surge:** In combat against Green-Red zealots, PC morale checks suffer a -2 penalty due to the horrific howling and mutilated corpses displayed along the perimeter.
