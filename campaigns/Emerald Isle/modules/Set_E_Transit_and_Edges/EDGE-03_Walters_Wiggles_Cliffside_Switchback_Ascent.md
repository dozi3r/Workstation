# MODULE EDGE-03: WALTERS WIGGLES CLIFFSIDE SWITCHBACK ASCENT

### 1. Vertical Transit Architecture

The harrowing 1,400-step hand-hewn stone staircase that scales the colossal interior limestone escarpments.

* **"The Wiggles":** A dizzying series of 28 sharp hairpin switchbacks chiseled directly into the vertical cliff face by convict chain-gangs a century ago.
* **Guano Hazards:** Karst fissures directly above the stairs house bat and stirge colonies; steps are perpetually slick with damp, ammonia-scented guano.
* **Mule Incompatibility:** Pack animals larger than mountain goats refuse to ascend the upper switchbacks unless blindfolded and led by professional highland sherpas.

### 2. GM Edge Placement & Mechanics
* **Ascent Cost:** Climbing the stairs takes 2 Travel Turns and forces an Athletics check (2d6). Failing accumulates 1 tick on the Exhaustion Clock.
* **Aerial Ambush:** Stirges nesting in the rock face attack climbers while their hands are full grasping guide ropes.
