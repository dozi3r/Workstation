# MODULE EDGE-04: HIGH ARBOREAL SUSPENSION HIGHWAYS

### 1. Aerial Transit Architecture

A spiderweb network of braided hemp suspension spans, counterweighted rope trolleys, and bamboo walkways strung 400 feet above the forest floor.

* **High-Speed Glider Corridors:** Travelers equipped with bat-winged glider capes can unhook from platforms and glide silently down-gradient to downstream forest nodes in 10 minutes.
* **Trolley Freight Baskets:** Iron pulleys running along greased wire ropes carry barrels of amber pitch and dried game between redwood boughs.
* **Ground Evasion:** Bypasses all ground-floor predators (Dire Wolves, Boars, Pitcher plants) completely.

### 2. GM Edge Placement & Tactical Rules
* **Cutting the Ropes:** A single severed suspension anchor sends the entire 200-foot span crashing down into the jungle below, permanently severing the edge until repaired.
* **Aerial Archer Defense:** Glider sentries perched on redwood branch blinds fire recurve bows downward with total cover.
