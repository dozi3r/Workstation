# MODULE MOD_T3_THORBARDIN_LIFE_TREE_SEAL: THE GREAT NORTHGATE & LIFE TREE FOUNDATIONS OF THORBARDIN

## Granular Disposition Baseline ([1, 1, 2])

### 1. Executive Summary & Egri Premise
Monumental counterweighted stone plug sealing the subterranean dwarven kingdom since the Dwarfgate War, reopened only by the Helm of Grallen and Hammer of Kharas (`Q_WIN_01`).

- **Pipeline Tier:** Tier 3
- **Provides:** `hammer_of_kharas_authority`, `refugee_winter_sanctuary`
- **Requires:** `helm_of_grallen_relic`
- **Standalone Fallback:** Remains hermetically sealed on internal subterranean mushroom farms and aquifers.

### 2. 5D Sensory Pentagon
- **Physical Environment:** Hundred-foot granite counterbalance blocks and the cavern-spanning column of the Life Tree.
- **Behaviors:** Hylar guards inspecting ancient clan seals while Theiwar plot in the dark.
- **Relationships:** Flint Fireforge confronting his old kinship wounds to win sanctuary for human refugees.
- **Attitudes:** Xenophobic isolationism yielding to ancient honor.
- **Values:** Stone permanence and oaths.

### 3. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)

* **Thematic Shadow:** Legacy of Fistandantilus's Dwarfgate Betrayal
