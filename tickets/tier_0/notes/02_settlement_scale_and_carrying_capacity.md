# 02: Settlement Scales & Carrying Capacity in Pointcrawl Networks

## 1. Settlement Hierarchy

| Scale Tier | Population | Mundane Substratum Infrastructure | Pointcrawl Graph Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thorp** | 20–60 | 1 communal artesian well, perimeter gardens, 1 woodcutter / herbalist | Minor Waypoint / Hazard Edge Anchor |
| **Hamlet** | 60–250 | 3-field crop rotation, communal smithy, flour mill, ditch-and-palisade | Satellite Node (3 Sockets) |
| **Village** | 250–1,000 | Weekly barter square, dedicated watch bailiff, parish chapel, craft shops | Major Satellite / Secondary Trade Anchor |
| **Market Town** | 1,000–5,000 | Stone walls, guild halls, regional grain silos, formal magistrates | Buffer Sovereign / Regional Bastion |
| **Sovereign Hub**| 5,000–25,000+ | Monumental palaces, aqueducts, standing garrison, specialized academies | Sovereign Hub (4 Sockets) |

## 2. Supply Hop Dependencies
* **Thorps and Hamlets** are 100% self-sufficient for basic survival (Tier 0), but generate zero industrial surplus.
* **Sovereign Hubs** run a permanent calorie deficit:
  * A 10,000-person Hub requires at least **3 to 4 connected Satellite Nodes** dedicated to agriculture, fisheries, or timber.
  * If an enemy faction blockades or severs a single transit edge, the Sovereign Hub begins ticking its **Threat Clock** within 2 campaign turns.
