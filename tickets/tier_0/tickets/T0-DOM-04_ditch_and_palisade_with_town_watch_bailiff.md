## 1. Baseline Concept & Mundane Reality
Sharpened wooden stake palisade, earthen ditch embankment, heavy timber gate, and a roster of 12 local militia armed with bills and hunting bows.

---

## 2. Smooth Generic Mode ("Checkbox Through")
* Deters roving wild beasts, petty bandits, and unorganized raiders (CR 1/8 to 1/2).
* When "Enable Baseline Substratum: ON" is set, all settlement nodes receive this benefit implicitly with zero GM bookkeeping.

## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Vigilant): Gate barred at sundown; watch horn signals every 2 hours.
* Level 2 (Routine): Sleepy watchman at gate; occasional contraband smuggled in.
* Level 3 (Breached): Rotted palisade stakes; night raiders slip in through gaps.
* Level 4 (Overrun): Gatehouse torched; bailiff slain; settlement plundered.
