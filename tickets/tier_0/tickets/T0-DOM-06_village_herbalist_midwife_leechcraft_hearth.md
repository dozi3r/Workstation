# T0-DOM-06: Village Herbalist, Midwife & Leechcraft Hearth
*Domain: DOM-06 Health, Folk Medicine & Midwifery | Scale: All Settlement Scales*
*Status: ready_for_spec*

---

## 1. Baseline Concept & Mundane Reality
Knowledgeable elder gathering willow bark, yarrow, and wild honey to soothe childbed fevers, stitch farm wounds, and deliver infants.

---

## 2. Smooth Generic Mode ("Checkbox Through")
* Allows recovery from non-magical disease and mundane injuries during resting watches.
* When "Enable Baseline Substratum: ON" is set, all settlement nodes receive this benefit implicitly with zero GM bookkeeping.

---

## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Healing): Abundant dried herbs; clean bandages; fever broken safely.
* Level 2 (Coping): Basic poultices; wounds take 1d4 days to close.
* Level 3 (Infected): Rotting herbs; gangrene in open wounds; infant mortality spikes.
* Level 4 (Pestilence): Black tongue contagion spreads; herbalist dies; quarantine hut burned.
