## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Sanctified): Fresh wildflowers on altar; harvest feast; high solidarity.
* Level 2 (Quiet): Seasonal candles lit; minor squabbles resolved by village elder.
* Level 3 (Desecrated): Shrine vandalized; ominous omens; distrust between families.
* Level 4 (Cursed): Sacred icon weeps blood; paranoia turns to witch hunts and bloodletting.
