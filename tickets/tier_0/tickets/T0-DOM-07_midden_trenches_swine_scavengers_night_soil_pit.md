Dedicated community compost trench, pig pens consuming refuse, and lime-dusted night-soil pits keeping streets free of lethal cholera.

## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Clean): Lime applied weekly; swine pen contained; clean runnels.
* Level 2 (Normal): Standard animal smell; muddy ditches; flies in summer.
* Level 3 (Foul): Overfilled middens; foul runoff enters street puddles; dysentery.
* Level 4 (Epidemic): Night-soil floods wells; flies blacken the air; cholera plague.
