## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Solid): Dry thatched roofs, glazed windows, hearth flue drafts cleanly.
* Level 2 (Weathered): Minor roof leaks, drafty walls, soot on beams.
* Level 3 (Dilapidated): Collapsed timbers, leaking straw, hypothermia risk in rain.
* Level 4 (Blaze/Ruin): Fire sweeps through timber quarter; 50% left homeless in winter.
