## 1. Baseline Concept & Mundane Reality
Charcoal forge shaping plowshares, scythes, and horse shoes alongside a weekly open-air barter market trading butter, eggs, homespun cloth, and copper bits.

---

## 2. Smooth Generic Mode ("Checkbox Through")
* Supplies basic equipment repairs, draft animal shoeing, and mundane gear replenishment.
* When "Enable Baseline Substratum: ON" is set, all settlement nodes receive this benefit implicitly with zero GM bookkeeping.

## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Thriving): Traveling tinkers present; good iron tools; lively barter trade.
* Level 2 (Steady): Local smith busy shoeing plow-oxen; modest trade.
* Level 3 (Scarcity): Iron scraps exhausted; tools break without replacement; barter gridlock.
* Level 4 (Depleted): Forge cold; charcoal gone; economy collapses into pure desperation.
