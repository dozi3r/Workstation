# T0-DOM-01: Subsistence Agrarian Perimeter & Root Cellars
*Domain: DOM-01 Food & Caloric Subsistence | Scale: Hamlet / Village*
*Status: ready_for_spec*

---

## 1. Baseline Concept & Mundane Reality
Dryland crop rotation, cabbage patches, communal threshing floors, and subterranean root storage keeping 200–500 souls alive through winter.

---

## 2. Smooth Generic Mode ("Checkbox Through")
* Automatically satisfies baseline calorie needs for up to 500 inhabitants. Ambient stability +1.
* When "Enable Baseline Substratum: ON" is set, all settlement nodes receive this benefit implicitly with zero GM bookkeeping.

---

## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Bountiful): Full winter root cellars; lard and salt-pork surplus.
* Level 2 (Normal): Standard 2,000 kcal rations; porridge, bread, cabbage.
* Level 3 (Lean): Spring hunger gap; rations halved; wild greens foraging.
* Level 4 (Famine): Grain seed-corn eaten; draft beasts slaughtered; starvation checks.

### Resource Flow Contract
* **Provides:** `Food: Baseline-Subsistence-Rations`
* **Requires:** `Geography: Arable-Perimeter-Soil`

## 4. GM Adventure Hooks & Crises
* **Hook 1 (Local Tension):** A vital component breaks, forcing players to mediate an internal clan dispute or seek replacement parts from a neighboring satellite.
* **Hook 2 (Vulnerability to High-Concept Threat):** A Tier 1–5 macro faction targets this mundane system, turning a simple supply outage into an existential countdown.
