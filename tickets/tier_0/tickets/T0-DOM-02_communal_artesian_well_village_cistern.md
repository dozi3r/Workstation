# T0-DOM-02: Communal Artesian Well & Village Cistern
*Domain: DOM-02 Water & Hydrology | Scale: All Settlement Scales*
*Status: ready_for_spec*

---

## 1. Baseline Concept & Mundane Reality
Deep stone-lined artesian well with bucket windlass and communal rain collection cistern providing safe, year-round potable drinking water.

---

## 2. Smooth Generic Mode ("Checkbox Through")
* Prevents settlement water stress under non-catastrophic climate conditions.
* When "Enable Baseline Substratum: ON" is set, all settlement nodes receive this benefit implicitly with zero GM bookkeeping.

---

## 3. Granular Modularity Mode (Deep Authoring)

### 4-Level Vulnerability & Crisis Dial
* Level 1 (Abundant): Cold sweet water; cattle trough overflowing; stone wash house active.
* Level 2 (Normal): Standard well draw; 8 gallons daily per hearth.
* Level 3 (Brackish/Drought): Water table drops; bucket scrapes mud; cloudy mineral taste.
* Level 4 (Tainted/Dry): Well poisoned or dry; typhoid outbreak; population forced to migrate.

* **Provides:** `Water: Baseline-Potable-Well-Head`
* **Requires:** `Geography: Subterranean-Aquifer-Seep`
