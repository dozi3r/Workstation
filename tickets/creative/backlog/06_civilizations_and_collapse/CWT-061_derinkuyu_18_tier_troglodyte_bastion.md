## 1. Executive Summary & Historical Research Dossier

### Archaeological & Anthropological Grounding (Cappadocia Troglodyte Complexes)
Carved into the soft volcanic ash (tuff) deposits of Central Anatolia (Nevşehir Province, Turkey), Derinkuyu ("Deep Well") represents one of the most astonishing feats of subterranean defensive civil engineering in human history. Extending roughly 85 meters (280 feet) below the arid Anatolian plateau across at least 18 distinct subterranean levels, this subterranean city was engineered to shelter between 15,000 and 20,000 citizens along with their livestock, provisions, and sacred installations during catastrophic surface invasions.

Key archaeological realities of the Derinkuyu complex:
1. **Volcanic Tuff Geotechnical Matrix**: The geology consists of Miocene ignimbrites and pyroclastic tuff erupted from Mount Erciyes and Mount Hasan. The tuff is exceptionally soft and easily carved when freshly exposed to air with bronze or iron picks, but hardens into durable rock upon oxidization. This enabled rapid excavation without structural timber framing, provided vertical pillar load-bearing arches were maintained.
2. **The Tirhaz Rolling-Stone Blast Doors**: Access between levels was sealed by massive round discoid stone doors (*tirhaz*). Each stone measured 1 to 1.5 meters in diameter, 30 to 50 centimeters in thickness, and weighed between 300 and 500 kilograms. Critically, these stones were carved *in situ* within dedicated wall niches and could only be rolled into place and wedged from the *inside* using wooden levers. A central 10-centimeter hole allowed defenders to fire arrows, thrust spears, or observe attackers without opening the seal. To an invading surface force, each corridor presented an impassable, convex stone wall.
3. **Pneumatic & Hydraulic Infrastructure**: Derinkuyu maintained atmospheric integrity via more than 15,000 small flues branching from a primary 55-meter vertical central ventilation shaft. Natural convection maintained fresh air even on the 18th level. The main airshaft doubles as a water well reaching deep groundwater aquifers. In a brilliant anti-sabotage design, the bottom wells were physically disconnected from the upper and surface levels, preventing surface raiders from poisoning the city's drinking water by dumping venom, pitch, or carrion into surface grates.
4. **Zoned Ecological Organization**: Upper tiers (Levels 1–2) held livestock stables, animal pens, and manure sumps to minimize animal transit down narrow stairs and reduce odor infiltration. Intermediate levels (Levels 3–8) housed communal kitchens with soot-dispersal chimneys, refectories, wineries with stone treading vats, linseed oil presses for lamp fuel, and living dormitories. Lower levels (Levels 9–18) contained water cisterns, armories, a cruciform basilica, confessional cells, and emergency air-pocket vaults.

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "Surface raiders may scourge the Anatolian plains and burn our crops to ash; beneath the stone, behind our discoid blast doors, we are totally self-sufficient, invulnerable, and righteous forever."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** A prolonged, multi-month surface siege encampment by steppe horse-archers locates the surface intake chimneys; raiders ignite sulfur-soaked dung fires above the flues while diverted surface runoff threatens to flood the upper livestock tiers.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Subterranean Hermetic Isolationism} + \text{Surface Atmospheric Siege} = \text{Paranoiac Internal Asphyxiation or Desperate Vertical Counter-Sortie}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** A sanctuary built entirely underground is only as resilient as its connection to breathable air. Total physical isolation converts a bastion into a mass tomb unless surface footholds can be contested.

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1 (The Establishment / Presbyter-Architect of the Tuff):** Governs the subterranean tiers with monastic severity; demands absolute water rationing, orders the immediate rolling shut of *tirhaz* blast stones even if fleeing surface farmers are locked out, and interprets air thinning as a moral trial.
* **Corner 2 (The Zealot Opponent / Steppe Siege Khan):** Encamped on the sun-baked basalt plains above; frustrates at his inability to breach the stone doors, he commands sappers to dig counter-trenches and burns sulfur into airshafts to smoke the troglodytes into unconditional surrender.
* **Corner 3 (The Thematic Shadow / Black-Market Flue Siphoners):** Rogue cellarers who carved unmapped vertical chimneys through Level 4 to smuggle cured meats, church gold, and vintage amphorae to corrupt raider outposts, fatally leaking smoke into the lower dormitories.
* **Corner 4 (The Tertiary Outcast / Blind Sump Hermits):** Generational well-tenders living at the 85-meter bottom aquifer floor; having lost their sight to permanent darkness, their acoustic sensitivity detects the exact vibrations of raider sappers digging counter-tunnels hundreds of yards away.

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Subterranean Atmospheric & Siege Integrity
* **Level 1 (Dim Lamp Cloister & Acrid Draft / Light):**  
  * *Condition:* Surface raid alert sounded; livestock ushered into upper levels; dim olive-oil lamps flicker in narrow 4-foot tunnels.
  * *Mechanics:* Movement in single file; visibility limited to 30 feet. Navigating vertical ladder-chutes requires DC 10 Athletics. Minor smoke irritation imposes disadvantage on acute scent-based Perception.
* **Level 2 (Tirhaz Blast Stone Lockdown & Air Stagnation / Moderate):**  
  * *Condition:* *Tirhaz* round stones rolled into place; levels 3 through 8 sealed into compartmentalized vaults.
  * *Mechanics:* Heavy air stagnation; characters taking vigorous actions (combat, heavy labor) must pass DC 13 Constitution saves each hour or gain 1 level of Exhaustion from carbon dioxide buildup. Rolling a *tirhaz* stone open from inside requires DC 13 Athletics (or leverage levers).
* **Level 3 (Sulfur Flue Smoke & Upper Sump Flood / Severe):**  
  * *Condition:* Raiders light sulfur smoke bombs over primary flues; upper manure sumps overflow from diverted surface torrents.
  * *Mechanics:* Toxic yellowish sulfur smoke fills stairwells (DC 16 Fortitude or take 3d8 poison/suffocation damage and fall prone coughing). Upper stairwells slippery with slurry (DC 16 Reflex). Panicking crowds cause trampling hazards.
* **Level 4 (Atmospheric Collapse & Sapper Vault Breach / Lethal Climax):**  
  * *Condition:* Main ventilation trunk collapses from sapper explosives or counter-trenches; air ceases circulating below level 8; a *tirhaz* stone is wedged by rockfall.
  * *Mechanics:* Immediate suffocation hazard. Characters take 4d10 bludgeoning and 4d10 suffocation damage per turn without sealed breathing bladders. DC 20 Athletics/Mining to force open jammed stone doors or breach an emergency vertical crawl-flue before asphyxiation claims the lower population.

### 3.2 Threat Clocks
* **Subterranean Oxygen Depletion Clock [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks each turn primary ventilation flues remain choked by smoke, rubble, or intentional water flooding.
  * *Consequence when full:* Dial advances to Level 4; lower tiers become uninhabitable hypoxic kill zones.
* **Raider Sapper Counter-Mine Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks when party fails to eliminate surface trench guards or intercept acoustic digging signals.
  * *Consequence when full:* Raider sappers breach the Level 5 winery vault with black powder and iron pikes, initiating a room-by-room subterranean melee.
