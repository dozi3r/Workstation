## 1. Executive Summary & Historical Research Dossier

### Archaeological & Geological Grounding (Submerged Canopic Estuary & Soil Liquefaction)
Thonis-Heracleion (known to ancient Egyptians as *Thonis* and to the Greeks as *Heracleion*) was the mandatory gateway port to Egypt from the 8th century BCE until the rise of Alexandria (331 BCE). Straddling the mouth of the Canopic branch of the Nile in the Bay of Aboukir, the city was built upon an intricate, waterlogged archipelago of low-lying alluvial silt islands, crisscrossed by natural canals, wooden quays, stone retaining walls, and colossal religious complexes—most notably the monumental Grand Temple of Amun-Gereb.

Key archaeological and geological realities of the site (rediscovered in 2000 by Franck Goddio and the European Institute for Underwater Archaeology - IEASM):
1. **The Geotechnical Mechanism of Soil Liquefaction**: Thonis-Heracleion did not simply submerge due to gradual sea-level rise; it was violently swallowed by **soil liquefaction** and sudden geotechnical subsidence. The city was founded on water-saturated, high-plasticity clay and loose alluvial sand. When subjected to cyclical dynamic stress—specifically earthquakes along the Hellenic arc and massive flood crests from the Nile—pore water pressure in the sediment rose abruptly until it matched the overburden weight. In seconds, the shear strength of the ground dropped to zero: solid land liquefied into a fluid quicksand slurry.
2. **Structural Super-Load Collapse**: The Egyptians had constructed colossal granite colossi (exceeding 5 meters in height and weighing tens of tons), limestone temple pylons, and massive stone quayside embankments upon this precarious clay base. When liquefaction occurred during catastrophic seismic and flood events in the 2nd century BCE and 8th century CE, the immense downward bearing pressure caused the clay beneath the foundations to buckle and "pancake." Entire temple colonnades, royal treasuries, and administrative quays slipped bodily into the sea in catastrophic mud-slides.
3. **The Maritime Archaeological Graveyard**: Marine surveys across the 110-square-kilometer bay have revealed over **64 ancient shipwrecks**, 700 iron and stone anchors, thousands of gold coins, bronze weights, Greek and Phoenician trade ceramics, and colossal statues lying preserved in anaerobic underwater silt beneath 10 meters of water, frozen in the exact moments of their catastrophic descent.
4. **Customs Chokepoint Monopoly**: As the sole legal customs station for foreign ships entering Egypt, royal scribes inspected all cargo, levied mandatory 10% taxes on Greek silver, oil, and wine, and controlled the iron harbor booms that chained off the Canopic mouth. This created immense wealth, extreme administrative arrogance, and deadly quayside congestion.

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "Pharaoh's customs collectors sit upon eternal granite quays; every foreign galley pays homage in gold, and our sacred temples will anchor the mouth of the Nile forever against any sea."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** A violent offshore seismic tremor strikes during peak Nile inundation; the water-saturated clay foundations instantly liquefy, turning stone plazas into boiling quicksand while temple columns topple into the harbor, dragging tied-up merchant galleys to the sea floor.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Monumental Imperial Hubris on Alluvial Silt} + \text{Cyclic Seismic Soil Liquefaction} = \text{Instant Maritime Inundation \& Submerged Silt Graveyard}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** No quantity of granite masonry can resist the collapse of the earth beneath it. Commercial empires built on unstable ecological foundations are swallowed whole when the ground gives way.

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1 (The Establishment / Royal Customs Arch-Scribe):** Clings fanatically to imperial regulations, demanding full tax tariffs in gold bullion even as seawater erupts through his marble floorboards, refusing to unchain the harbor boom without stamped clearance tablets.
* **Corner 2 (The Zealot Opponent / Hellenic Galley League Captain):** Commander of trapped foreign merchant-mercenaries; viewing Egyptian tax laws as extortion, his crew prepares to ram the harbor chains, set fire to the customs wharves, and fight their way to open water before the island slides into the sea.
* **Corner 3 (The Thematic Shadow / Silt-Scavenger Diver Guild):** Native reed-divers and wreckers who secretly loosen harbor revetments and pilfer quay anchors, knowing that when the customs house plunges into the bay, they will monopolize the salvage of submerged royal bullion.
* **Corner 4 (The Tertiary Outcast / Canopic Papyrus-Raft Foragers):** Marginalized marsh-dwellers living on buoyant papyrus skiffs who warned for decades that the clay was rotting; they navigate the liquefying channels with ease, rescuing drowning aristocrats in exchange for sacred relics and freedom deeds.

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Soil Liquefaction & Estuarine Subsidence
* **Level 1 (Mud Slurry Seepage & Tilting Pavement / Light):**  
  * *Condition:* Minor foreshocks cause water to bubble up through stone flagstones; quays tilt at 5-degree angles.
  * *Mechanics:* Wet, shifting mud imposes DC 10 Acrobatics checks to sprint or run. Galley mooring lines groan under tension; DC 10 Insight to notice unnatural receding tides.
* **Level 2 (Cyclic Tremor & Saturated Soil Sieve / Moderate):**  
  * *Condition:* Ground vibrates steadily; sand boils burst from plaza cobblestones; harbor waters slosh violently over quayside docks.
  * *Mechanics:* Ground counts as difficult terrain. Characters wearing heavy armor suffer movement speed halved. DC 13 Athletics or Balance checks to stay upright; failed checks result in sinking to waist-level in mud (requiring an action and DC 13 Athletics to extract).
* **Level 3 (Soil Liquefaction Breach & Quayside Slip / Severe):**  
  * *Condition:* Soil shear strength drops to zero; granite storehouses and temple columns pancake bodily into churning water; massive sinkholes open across the waterfront.
  * *Mechanics:* Port segments slide into the water. DC 16 Reflex save to avoid being swept into a liquefaction whirlpool (takes 4d8 bludgeoning/drowning damage). Moored ships risk being crushed under falling masonry (DC 16 Vehicles/Piloting).
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Tsunami Surge & Total Inundation / Lethal Climax):**  
  * *Condition:* A 25-foot seismic tsunami crashes into the collapsed archipelago; the entire city slips 10 meters beneath the Mediterranean.
  * *Mechanics:* Absolute aquatic catastrophe. Characters caught on land or on sinking quays must succeed on a DC 20 Athletics (Swimming) or Survival check or suffer 8d12 crushing/drowning damage and be pinned beneath submerged granite blocks. The node permanently converts into a sunken underwater ruin.

### 3.2 Threat Clocks
* **Subsoil Liquefaction Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks each turn following seismic foreshocks or when heavy stone cargo is stockpiled on un-reinforced quays.
  * *Consequence when full:* Dial advances to Level 3; entire waterfront slips into the sea.
* **Canopic Tsunami Inundation Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks as ocean tides recede abnormally from the harbor basin following offshore tremors.
  * *Consequence when full:* Triggers Dial Level 4 catastrophic tsunami wave immediately.
