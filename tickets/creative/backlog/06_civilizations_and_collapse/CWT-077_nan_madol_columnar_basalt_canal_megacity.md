## 1. Executive Summary & Historical Research Dossier

### Archaeological Grounding (The Coral Reef Megacity of Pohnpei & The Saudeleur Dynasty)
Situated on the tidal coral reef flats off Temwen Island on the eastern coast of Pohnpei in the Federated States of Micronesia, Nan Madol ("The Spaces Between") was the ceremonial and political capital of the tyrannical Saudeleur Dynasty from roughly 1100 to 1628 CE. Often described as the "Venice of the Pacific," it represents one of the most enigmatic feats of maritime civil engineering on Earth.

Key archaeological realities established by modern surveys (William Ayres, Rufino Mauricio, and J. Stephen Athens):
1. **The 92 Artificial Islets**: Nan Madol was not built on land, but directly upon a living coral reef lagoon. Builders constructed **92 artificial, geometric stone islets** separated by a labyrinth of tidal canals. Retaining seawalls and islet borders were built up to 8 meters (25 feet) high to withstand Pacific typhoons and tidal surges, encompassing roughly 80 hectares of maritime infrastructure.
2. **The 750,000-Ton Columnar Basalt Logistics**: The building material was not quarried locally; it consisted of natural **prismatic columnar basalt**—massive volcanic rocks that fractured into five- and six-sided hexagonal "stone logs" as lava cooled. These monoliths, weighing between 5 and 50 metric tons and measuring up to 6 meters in length, were quarried from volcanic plugs on the opposite side of Pohnpei (such as Sokehs Rock). Masons transported an estimated **750,000 metric tons** of basalt columns across the ocean on large rafts or via complex buoyant log-rolling systems over open water.
3. **The "Log-Cabin" Masonry System**: Builders stacked the basalt columns in an alternating "header-and-stretcher" criss-cross pattern, exactly mimicking a log cabin. The hollow cores were then backfilled with tons of crushed coral rubble and massive boulders. This allowed seawater to surge through the porous foundations without blowing out the walls during heavy storm surges.
4. **Nandauwas and the Sacred Moray Eel Crypt**: The apex of the complex was the royal mortuary fortress of **Nandauwas**, featuring outer walls 8 meters high enclosing inner central crypts. In sacred saltwater chambers, the Saudeleur high priests kept and revered *Nan Samwohl*, a colossal sacred saltwater moray eel. During secret nocturnal ceremonies, priests fed cooked turtle fat to the eel, treating it as an oracle and spiritual conduit linking royal ancestors to the deep ocean.
5. **The Fatal Metabolic Vulnerability of Tyranny**: Nan Madol suffered from a fatal ecological design flaw: **the stone city had zero natural freshwater springs and zero arable soil**. The despotic Saudeleurs deliberately built their capital on the coral reef to isolate themselves from commoner revolts. Every single drop of drinking water and every basket of taro, breadfruit, and firewood had to be rafted daily from mainland Pohnpei by oppressed commoners under penalty of death.
6. **The Overthrow by Isokelekel (circa 1628 CE)**: Oral history and archaeological strata converge on the fall of the city: around 1628 CE, a semi-mythical warrior named **Isokelekel** led an invasion fleet of 333 seasoned warriors from Kosrae. Allying with starving, rebellious mainlanders, Isokelekel blockaded the lagoon channels, cutting off freshwater convoys to the islets. In the ensuing siege of Nandauwas, the last cruel Saudeleur king, *Saudemwohl*, was routed; according to legend, he fled into a river and transformed into a bluefish. Isokelekel established a decentralized system of district chiefs (*Nahnmwarki*). Nan Madol, with its lack of freshwater, was declared cursed (*tabu*) and permanently abandoned to the advancing mangroves.

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "We rule from black stone rafts upon the living reef; our ancestors command the sacred moray eels, and the mainlanders will paddle our fresh water and sweet taro across the lagoon for eternity."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** An allied Kosraean war-fleet and mainland canoe rebels blockade the lagoon channels, cutting off all freshwater convoys; the despotic elite in their basalt fortresses are trapped without a single drop of drinkable water while surrounded by an ocean of brine.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Despotic Offshore Theocracy Without Water Autarky} + \text{Mainland Canal Blockade} = \text{Dynastic Decapitation \& Mangrove Ghost Metropolis}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** An offshore fortress that cannot water itself is a magnificent prison. When the mainland refuses to row the water canoes, the black stone citadel starves in a moat of poison.

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1 (The Establishment / High Priest-King Saudeleur):** Holed up in the megalithic sanctuary of Nandauwas; demands turtle sacrifices and executes canoe captains who arrive with half-empty water gourds, trusting his 25-foot basalt walls to keep out the rebels.
* **Corner 2 (The Zealot Opponent / Kosraean Liberator Warlord Isokelekel):** Commands 333 elite canoe warriors armed with stingray-spine lances and shark-tooth swords; blockades the reef channels to starve the citadel, determined to annihilate the Saudeleur bloodline.
* **Corner 3 (The Thematic Shadow / Lagoon Smuggler Middlemen):** Corrupt canoe factors who secretly row freshwater gourds to thirsty Saudeleur guards by night in exchange for royal pearl-shell fishhooks and greenstone pendants, dragging out the siege for profit.
* **Corner 4 (The Tertiary Outcast / Nandauwas Crypt Eel-Tender):** Blind reef keeper who feeds the sacred moray eel in the mortuary chamber; knows the secret submerged tunnels beneath the coral foundations and warns that if the eel dies in stagnant water, the stone islets will sink into the reef.

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: The Tidal Lagoon & Dynastic Siege
* **Level 1 (Low Tide Mud & Canal Coral Snags / Light):**  
  * *Condition:* Low tide exposes sharp coral heads in the canals; green slime blankets submerged basalt blocks; canoes scrape bottom.
  * *Mechanics:* Shallow water movement speed halved without shallow-draft outrigger canoes. Wading through canals requires DC 10 Athletics to avoid razor-sharp coral cuts (1d4 slashing damage).
* **Level 2 (Freshwater Thirst & Basalt Log Slip / Moderate):**  
  * *Condition:* Water rations on the islets cut; guards suffer from heat exhaustion; climbing the un-mortared hexagonal basalt walls is treacherous.
  * *Mechanics:* Scaling basalt column walls requires DC 13 Athletics (failure triggers a slip dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage). Characters resting on the islets must consume 2 water units or gain 1 level of Exhaustion.
* **Level 3 (Canoe Blockade & Shark-Tooth Skirmish / Severe):**  
  * *Condition:* Rebel war-canoes ram each other in the narrow canals; flaming arrows ignite thatch roofs on islet pavilions; poisonous stingray-spine lances fly from walls.
  * *Mechanics:* Intense naval melee in 10-foot-wide canals. Ranged attacks suffer disadvantage from sea spray. Characters struck by stingray-spine weapons must pass a DC 16 Fortitude save or suffer 3d8 poison damage and agonizing paralysis.
* **Level 4 (The Fall of Nandauwas & Mangrove Strangulation / Lethal Climax):**  
  * *Condition:* The royal mortuary complex is breached; the sacred eel is slain; the Saudeleur dynasty falls; a catastrophic tropical typhoon slams the reef, breaking seawalls and submerging low islets.
  * *Mechanics:* Absolute environmental and dynastic collapse. Storm tides surge 15 feet high across the islets (DC 20 Swimming or Survival checks to avoid being crushed against basalt logs, taking 8d10 damage). The city loses all governance and becomes an abandoned tabu zone.

### 3.2 Threat Clocks
* **Freshwater Gourd Depletion Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks each turn the mainland water blockade intercepts a supply canoe or guards drink remaining cisterns.
  * *Consequence when full:* Islet defenders completely incapacitated by dehydration; gates fall open.
* **Isokelekel Liberation Fleet Advance [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks each turn rebel canoes breach a seawall gate or set fire to an outer islet bastion.
  * *Consequence when full:* Nandauwas crypt stormed; Saudeleur king deposed.

## 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Tabletop Sparks (writing-generation)

* **Scene 1: The Grounded Water Canoe in the Tidal Canal (Arrival Collision):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party navigates a narrow, 8-foot-wide canal between 20-foot basalt walls at falling tide, only to collide with a heavy wooden dugout canoe loaded with 40 clay water gourds that has run aground on a coral head; royal guards on the battlements drop 50-pound basalt stones into the canal, shouting that the party is deliberately sabotaging the king’s drinking supply.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party enters seeking safe harbor or diplomatic audience, but is trapped in a narrow saltwater kill-box under heavy stone fire from above.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The canoe captain screams that the water is sweet mountain rain for the king, while his crew frantically drinks from broken gourds knowing they will be executed before sundown anyway.
* **Scene 2: The Sacred Moray’s Crypt Pool (Resource Dilemma):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* In the sunken seawater vault of Nandauwas, the party corners a dying rebel scout who has jumped into the sacred feeding pool with a ceramic vial of deadly mangrove root poison, preparing to poison the colossal sacred moray eel to break the religious morale of the royal defenders.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* If the party stops the poisoning, they preserve the unique sacred ecological oracle of the Pacific and gain the trust of the high priests, but allow the tyrannical dynasty to continue torturing mainland captives; if they allow the poisoning, the sacred eel thrashes in agony, snapping its teeth and collapsing the stone ceiling into the pool.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The blind eel-tender chants soothing sea prayers into the dark water, stroking the 12-foot moray’s head while pretending not to notice the blood already clouding the pool.
* **Scene 3: The Breach of the Outer Seawall at High Tide (Severance Crisis):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* As high tide peaks and an incoming Pacific gale roars over the reef, Isokelekel’s 333 warriors ram their war-canoes directly into the outer seawall, dislodging a 5-ton hexagonal basalt column that creates a breach; water surges into the royal residential compound, threatening to wash thirty captive mainland children into the shark-infested lagoon.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party must choose between holding the breach against the invading shock-troops to save the drowning children from the rip-current, or fleeing through a secret submerged canal to recover the royal treasury of ancient greenstone adzes before the entire islet collapses into the surf.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The Kosraean warlord Isokelekel stands at the prow of his double-hulled canoe, pointing his shark-tooth spear at the palace and shouting that the stone sea-monsters of the Saudeleurs will drown in their own salt.
