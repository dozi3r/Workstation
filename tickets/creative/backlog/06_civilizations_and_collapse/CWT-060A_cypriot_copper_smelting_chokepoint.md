## 1. Executive Summary & Historical Research Dossier

### Archaeological & Industrial Grounding (Enkomi, Kition, and Troodos Mines)
In the Late Bronze Age, the island of Cyprus (ancient Alashiya) was the industrial crucible of the Mediterranean world. Complexes such as Enkomi and Kition housed immense multi-furnace smelting installations dedicated to transforming sulfidic chalcopyrite ore into standardized 30-kilogram "oxhide ingots" (cast with four projecting handles to allow a single porter or pack animal to carry them).

Key archaeological realities of ancient industrial copper production:
1. **The Slag Heaps of Antiquity**: Modern geological excavations across the Troodos foothills have identified ancient slag heaps totaling over **4 million metric tons** of industrial waste. Smelting required multi-stage roasting to drive off sulfur, followed by crucible smelting at over 1,100°C using clay blowpipes and pot-bellows.
2. **The Charcoal Deforestation Crisis**: The thermodynamic constraint was immense: producing **1 kilogram of refined copper required roughly 100 to 200 kilograms of charcoal**. This meant that sustaining peak palatial export required the clear-cutting of thousands of acres of ancient oak, pine, and cedar forests annually. By the end of the 13th century BCE, local timber reserves were severely depleted, forcing smelters to haul timber from increasingly remote highland escarpments.
3. **Arsenic and Sulfur Hazards**: Bronze Age smelters routinely breathed dense sulfur dioxide plumes and worked with arsenic-rich copper ores. Chronic exposure caused systemic peripheral neuropathy, respiratory necrosis, and early mortality among furnace laborers. Runoff from the slag heaps leached heavy metals into coastal aquifers.

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "The mountain has an inexhaustible vein of copper, and the highland forests are endless; the foundries will smoke and make us rich forever."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** Complete clear-cutting of the foothills causes catastrophic flash-floods and landslides that bury the access roads; charcoal supplies fail just as imperial tax galleys arrive demanding their mandatory copper tribute.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Ecological Resource Depletion} + \text{Inflexible Palatial Export Quotas} = \text{Industrial Catastrophe \& Labor Mutiny}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** Heavy manufacturing cannot outpace the carrying capacity of its fuel base. When the primary fuel is exhausted, the foundry turns on its own workforce.

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1 (The Establishment / Royal Smelting Overseer):** Enforces 24-hour bellow shifts under armed guard; demands furnace crews burn holy orchard trees and olive groves to meet the palace's ingot quota.
* **Corner 2 (The Zealot Opponent / Highland Charcoalers' League):** Mountain woodcutters who have blockaded the timber trails, demanding triple grain wages and refusing to burn down ancestral highland groves.
* **Corner 3 (The Thematic Shadow / Arsenical Counterfeiters):** Rogue metallurgists who spike brittle, low-grade copper with lethal concentrations of arsenic and lead to fake the density of pure royal ingots, pocketing the difference.
* **Corner 4 (The Tertiary Outcast / Crippled Slag-Scavengers):** Ex-bellows workers suffering from arsenic poisoning and respiratory damage living in toxic slag gullies; they know secret subterranean furnace bypasses and trade furnace intelligence for fresh, unpoisoned spring water.

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: The Blast-Furnace Ecological Tension
* **Level 1 (Sulfur Smog & Respiratory Cough / Light):**  
  * *Condition:* Continuous furnace roasting fills the valley with acrid yellowish haze.
  * *Mechanics:* Visibility reduced to 60 feet. Characters without damp cloth masks suffer DC 10 Constitution checks or coughing fits (disadvantage on Stealth).
* **Level 2 (Charcoal Scarcity & Furnace Throttling / Moderate):**  
  * *Condition:* Timber runs out; overseers mix green wood and scrub into crucibles, lowering smelting temperature.
  * *Mechanics:* Ingot production speed halved. Scraping and fluxing crucibles requires DC 13 Crafting/Athletics. Unrest among conscript crews increases; work accidents inflict 2d6 bludgeoning/burn damage on failed checks.
* **Level 3 (Arsenical Gas Surge & Timber Insurrection / Severe):**  
  * *Condition:* Rotten ore roasts release toxic arsenical vapors while highland charcoalers ambush timber convoys with bows and axes.
  * *Mechanics:* Toxic gas clouds drift across workyards (DC 16 Fortitude or take 3d6 poison damage and gain 1 level of Exhaustion). The foundry perimeter is contested by armed strikers.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Slag Breach & Molten Cascade / Lethal Climax):**  
  * *Condition:* Overpressurized blast crucibles rupture their stone retaining walls; a river of 1,200°C molten slag floods the settlement.
  * *Mechanics:* Immediate evacuation required. DC 20 Reflex save to escape collapsing stone workshops; contact with molten slag inflicts 8d10 lethal fire damage. The foundry is permanently destroyed.

### 3.2 Threat Clocks
* **Highland Deforestation Horizon [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks each production cycle that timber quotas are enforced without replanting or restraint.
  * *Consequence when full:* Local forests completely denuded; charcoal supply hits 0; foundries go cold and shutdown.
* **Crucible Slag Pressure Breach [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks whenever maintenance checks are skipped or green timber is burned to force speed.
  * *Consequence when full:* Triggers Dial Level 4 catastrophic molten wall breach.

## 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks (writing-generation)

* **Encounter 1: The Weight of the Oxhide (Arrival Collision):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party seeks to purchase raw metal or hire smiths, but royal guards seize their pack beasts to haul 300-pound oxhide ingots to the harbor under an emergency royal requisition.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party arrives with coin ready to trade, but discovers their transportation is confiscated for state cargo.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The overseer boasts of imperial wealth and glorious production, while behind him exhausted conscripts collapse into steaming puddles of slag water.
* **Encounter 2: The Timber Blockade at Charcoal Gap:**
  * *Dilemma:* Highland woodcutters hold a mountain gorge and offer the party un-taxed high-grade copper ingots if they assassinate or depose the brutal furnace overseer; the overseer promises royal knighthoods if the party breaks the strikers' blockade with fire.
