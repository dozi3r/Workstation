## 1. Executive Summary & Historical Research Dossier

### Archaeological & Environmental Grounding (Upano Valley & Llanos de Moxos)
For over a century, western anthropology and colonial historiography treated the Amazon Basin as a "pristine wilderness" or a "counterfeit paradise" (Betty Meggers' environmental determinism)—arguing that nutrient-poor tropical soils (*oxisols* and *ultisols*) could never support dense, sedentary, complex urban civilizations. In early 2024, groundbreaking high-resolution airborne LiDAR surveys published by Stéphen Rostain and colleagues in *Science*, combined with investigations in the Llanos de Moxos (Casarabe culture, Heiko Prümers et al., *Nature* 2022), permanently shattered this paradigm.

Key archaeological realities of ancient Amazonian urbanism:
1. **The Upano Valley Mega-Grid**: Dating from circa 500 BCE to 600 CE (with continued occupations up to 1200 CE), eastern Ecuador's Upano Valley was home to a dense, sprawling network of garden cities. LiDAR revealed more than **6,000 rectangular earthen platform mounds** (*tolas*), measuring up to 20 meters by 10 meters and 2 to 3 meters in height, meticulously arranged around sunken rectangular plazas across a 300-square-kilometer grid. Major central settlements (such as Kilamope and Kunguints) covered areas rivaling classic Maya ceremonial centers.
2. **The Sunken Causeway Highway System**: Settlements were interconnected by colossal, deeply engineered sunken roads and causeways. These transit corridors, extending straight for up to 10 to 25 kilometers, were excavated 2 to 3 meters below the natural ground surface with flanking lateral ditches that served as dual-purpose roads and wet-season canal highways, enabling high-speed canoe transit and rapid troop movement between mound clusters.
3. **The Alchemy of Anthropogenic *Terra Preta***: The ecological foundation of this civilization was not slash-and-burn shifting cultivation, but **deliberately engineered anthrosols (*Terra Preta de Índio*)**. Ancient communities manufactured vast deposits of deep, pitch-black, hyper-fertile soil by incorporating low-temperature biochar (smoldering charcoal), crushed ceramic sherds, bone meal, river silt, and fermented organic waste. This biochar matrix acted as a microscopic sponge, holding volatile nitrogen, phosphorus, and moisture, resisting tropical leaching, and self-regenerating micro-fungal networks for millennia.
4. **Domesticated Forest Landscapes**: The "jungle" surrounding these cities was an intensively cultivated, multi-canopy food forest: polycultures of peach palm (*Bactris gasipaes*), cacao, manioc, sweet potatoes, and Brazil nuts, interlocked with raised fields (*camellones*) and weir-trapped aquaculture ponds.
5. **The Mystery of Abandonment**: The Upano settlements experienced significant disruption around 600–800 CE, potentially accelerated by explosive tephra fallouts from the nearby active volcano Mount Sangay, coupled with regional sociopolitical reorganization. Rather than being destroyed by invaders, the settlements were vacated, allowing the fast-growing rainforest to drape over the earthworks until laser pulses penetrated the canopy 1,500 years later.

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "The rainforest is an untamed, primeval jungle that swallows human ambition; mortal men can only survive here as scattered, primitive nomads bending to nature's cruel whim."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** An extractive mercantile expedition arrives intending to strip timber and clear "virgin land" for pasture, only to discover their saws and fires are wrecking an ancient, highly engineered 300-square-kilometer garden metropolis defended by entrenched clan confederacies.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Extractive Exploitative Arrogance} + \text{Interconnected Anthropogenic Agro-Ecology} = \text{Devastating Ecological Entanglement or Humbling Integration}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** The Amazon is not an unpopulated wilderness, but a legacy garden created by inter-generational human engineering. Survival in the jungle requires learning to manufacture living soil and respect the forest canopy, not clear-cutting it into barren mud.

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Corner 1 (The Establishment / Tola Soil-Matriarch of Kilamope):** Oversees the sacred biochar smolder-pits and sunken causeways; commands thousands of clan garden-tenders to maintain the raised field ditches and treats the earthen platform grid as a living, ancestral organism.
* **Corner 2 (The Zealot Opponent / Extractive Cattle-Timber Conquistador):** Views the forest strictly as board-feet of hardwood and open grazing land; builds dams across ancestral aquaculture canals and burns living peach palm orchards to claim "empty" territory.
* **Corner 3 (The Thematic Shadow / Biochar Siphon-Poachers):** Rebellious younger clansmen who dig up ancient *Terra Preta* beds to sell as miracle fertilizer to highland traders, enriching themselves while permanently stripping the local valley mounds of their nutrient buffer.
* **Corner 4 (The Tertiary Outcast / Mount Sangay Ash-Nomad):** High-Andean volcanic forager who survived explosive eruptions of the nearby volcano; understands the geochemical balance between poisonous sulfur fallout and soil fertilization, warning that clearing the trees will bring down deadly mudslides.

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: The Living Agro-Urban Canopy
* **Level 1 (Sunken Causeway Silt & Swarm Pester / Light):**  
  * *Condition:* Tropical drizzle; sunken causeways fill with knee-deep water; biting midges and wasps swarm the fruit canopies.
  * *Mechanics:* Movement speed along sunken causeways reduced by 5 feet without canoe/raft. Mild insect venom imposes DC 10 Constitution checks to maintain concentration.
* **Level 2 (Biochar Pit Smolder & Flash Causeway Inundation / Moderate):**  
  * *Condition:* Dense smoke from underground biochar charcoal kilns fills low plazas; sudden tropical downpour turns sunken causeways into roaring drainage flumes.
  * *Mechanics:* Visibility in sunken causeways reduced to 20 feet. Navigating against the current requires DC 13 Athletics or Vehicles (Water). Sinking into soft biochar beds requires DC 13 Dexterity or Athletics to extract footwear without losing equipment.
* **Level 3 (Platform Mound Erosion & Trench Mudslide / Severe):**  
  * *Condition:* Retaining ditches fail after clear-cutting; earthen platform mounds begin to slump; flash floods wash away raised fields and aquaculture weirs.
  * *Mechanics:* Earthen slopes are unstable (DC 16 Balance check; failure triggers a 30-foot mudslide dealing 4d8 bludgeoning damage and pinning victims). Hostile clan skirmishers utilize blowguns and poison darts from hidden canopy blinds (DC 16 Fortitude or gain 1 level of Exhaustion and paralysis).
* **Level 4 (Sangay Ash Deluge & Canopy Meltdown / Lethal Climax):**  
  * *Condition:* Mount Sangay erupts; volcanic ash combines with torrential rain into heavy acidic lahar flows that choke causeways and crush earthen tolas under boiling mud.
  * *Mechanics:* Absolute environmental catastrophe. Acidic ash rain inflicts 2d6 acid damage per turn without sealed shelters. Characters caught in lahar mudflows must succeed on a DC 20 Athletics (Swimming) or Survival check or suffer 8d10 lethal bludgeoning/suffocation damage. The settlement is permanently buried beneath volcanic silt and reclaimed by the jungle.

### 3.2 Threat Clocks
* **Sangay Volcanic Tephra Accumulation [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks each turn volcanic tremors rattle the valley or tephra ash blankets the peach palm canopy.
  * *Consequence when full:* Ash weight collapses earthen tolas; rivers turn to boiling mud; triggers Dial Level 4.
* **Agroforestry Siltation & Biochar Leaching Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks when external invaders clear-cut trees or biochar pits are neglected during war.
  * *Consequence when full:* Terra Preta fertility permanently leaches away; crops rot in acidic soil; food supply collapses.

## 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Tabletop Sparks (writing-generation)

* **Scene 1: The Sunken Causeway Ambush (Arrival Collision):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The party advances down a straight, 15-foot-wide sunken earthen road expecting a clear trade highway, but collides with a barricade of thorny peach-palm trunks defended by clan blowgunners who demand tribute in metal tools; behind the party, floodgates are opened, sending a waist-deep torrent of water rushing down the sunken trench.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party enters seeking an ancient trade city, but finds themselves trapped in a 3-meter-deep canal trench filling with rushing muddy water and covered by poisoned dart fire from above.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The clan war-chief speaks politely of ancient hospitality laws while his warriors deliberately aim blowguns at the party’s pack animals, preparing to seize their metal gear.
* **Scene 2: The Biochar Smolder-Pit Sabotage (Resource Dilemma):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Rogue poachers have cracked open the primary biochar kiln beneath a central platform mound, attempting to bag high-grade *Terra Preta* to sell to foreign merchants, accidentally setting fire to the ancient wooden longhouse on top of the mound.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party has an opportunity to secure valuable ancient maps from the longhouse, but saving the scrolls means letting the underground biochar fire spread, which will consume the sacred root system and collapse the entire platform into a smoldering sinkhole.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The head poacher claims he is only liberating the dirt for hungry farmers outside the valley, while pocketing gold coins paid by an overseas colonial factor.
* **Scene 3: The Mudslide on the Sangay Platform (Severance Crisis):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Torrential rains and volcanic ash loosen the retaining walls of the High Tola of Kunguints; an 80-ton mudslide threatens to sweep away the seed-storage vaults and crush an evacuation fleet of wooden canoes tied in the canal below.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party must choose between using their magical or physical strength to shore up the wooden revetments to save the genetic heritage of the valley's domesticated crops, or cutting the canoe lines to save thirty wounded villagers who will otherwise be crushed under collapsing earthen masonry.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The Soil-Matriarch commands the party to save the seeds at all costs, declaring that human bodies can be reborn but dead seeds mean the extinction of an entire civilization.
