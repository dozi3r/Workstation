## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Cavernous mushroom farms cultivating high-protein puffballs and brewer's yeast on composted agricultural waste in complete darkness.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Farming the Under-Caverns + Mold Spore Contamination = High-Calorie Autonomy or Toxic Mycosis

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Vault Mycologists (Govern moisture balance and spore dusting schedules)
* **Main Opponent:** Black Spore Smugglers (Intentionally growing illegal narcotic hallucinogen varieties)
* **Thematic Shadow:** The Compost Cartel (Controlling access to animal manure and timber sawdust bedding)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Spore-Coughers (Workers who develop whistling lungs from decades in the vaults)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Mushroom Paste - Nutritious but bland grey porridge; daily rations secured (Light)
* Level 2: Surplus Loaves - Baked mushroom yeast bread; trade goods for export (Moderate)
* Level 3: Black Rot Infiltration - Slime mold destroys 60% of mushroom beds; food riots (Severe)
* Level 4: Poison Spore Explosion - Entire vault overtaken by lethal asphyxiating spore clouds (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Mycelial Rot Blight Clock [4 Ticks] - Mold rot spreads through interconnected compost beds.
* Underground Humidity Failure [4 Ticks] - Ventilation failure dries out spore beds.
