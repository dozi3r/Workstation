## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Compounding laboratory extracting medicinal alkaloids, antivenom serums, and surgical coagulants from marsh stirges and rare lichens.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Synthesizing Medicine from Lethal Parasites + Overdose Toxicity = Biological Survival or Chemical Madness

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Guild of Apothecary Chirurgeons (Monopolizes tincture formulas, rations medicine by rank)
* **Main Opponent:** Desperate Quack Salvers (Distilling counterfeit water to sell to desperate plague victims)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Military Bioweapon Researchers (Weaponizing pure stirge venom into paralyzing darts)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Venom-Milkers (Addicted laborers who let wild stirges bite their arms to milk them)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Basic Salves - Herbal compresses; cuts heal cleanly (Light)
* Level 2: Standard Antidotes - Rapid neutralization of moderate venom; trauma clotting (Moderate)
* Level 3: Serum Shortage - Tinctures spoiled by mold; infected wounds turn gangrenous (Severe)
* Level 4: Toxic Batch Epidemic - Contaminated batch of fever-draught kills 40 patients in agony (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Plague Serum Depletion Clock [4 Ticks] - Serum ampoules run out during active epidemic.
* Venom Extraction Bite Infection [4 Ticks] - Unsterilized needles spread parasitic stirge eggs.
