## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Massive stepped water terraces recycling tailrace wastewater into nutrient-rich rice paddy cultivation and carp aquaculture.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Intensive Agrarian Recycling + Chemical Runoff Threats = Abundant Caloric Food or Poisoned Harvest

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Water-Bailiff Clan (Controls sluices, calculates water shares by strict family lineage)
* **Main Opponent:** Upstream Industrial Refineries (Dump toxic carbide slag into irrigation flumes at night)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Grain Black-Marketeers (Hoarding dried rice bundles in dry caves during artificial droughts)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Fish-Dredge Untouchables (Wading through muck to clear dead carp and silt filters)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Subsistence Rations - Green shoots; daily bowl of algae protein and fish (Light)
* Level 2: Bountiful Harvest - Crisp vegetables, fat river carp, surplus trade dried rations (Moderate)
* Level 3: Algal Rot & Fertilizer Shortage - Leaves turn yellow; fish die off in droves (Severe)
* Level 4: Flume Slag Contamination - Toxic runoff poisons all crops; settlement famine (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Crop Blight Contamination Clock [4 Ticks] - Chemical runoff poisons aquaculture ponds.
* Silt Siphon Clog Clock [6 Ticks] - Silt buildup blocks gravity irrigation flumes.
