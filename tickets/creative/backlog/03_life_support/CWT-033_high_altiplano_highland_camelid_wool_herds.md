## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Nomadic pastoral grazing leagues across high alpine plateaus, producing warm wool textiles, dried jerky meat, and beast of burden mobility.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Nomadic Pastoral Freedom + Harsh Glacial Winters = Self-Sufficient Resilience or Herd Starvation

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Highland Clan Patriarchs (Brand animals, direct seasonal pasture migrations)
* **Main Opponent:** Lowland Conscription Officers (Attempting to requisition herds as army pack beasts)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Rustler Raiding Clans (Stealing prized breeding stallions during night snowstorms)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Herdless Drifters (Shepherds who lost their stock to blizzards, working for meager wool scraps)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Tough Jerky & Thick Wool - Coarse clothing; dried meat rations (Light)
* Level 2: Prime Shearings - Water-resistant felt cloaks; pack caravans available (Moderate)
* Level 3: Highland Rime Frost - Pastures freeze under ice; 40% of newborn calves perish (Severe)
* Level 4: Rinderpest Epizootic - Viral plague wipes out entire herds; famine blankets highland (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Highland Winter Blizzard Clock [4 Ticks] - Snow isolates pastures from lowland trade.
* Livestock Parasite Plague [4 Ticks] - Ticks cause fever and wool shed.
