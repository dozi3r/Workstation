## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Artesian pumping station driving pressurized deep-water through solar glass condensers, producing crystal-pure potable water for arid regions.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Harnessing Deep Geologic Water + Arid Water Monopolies = Regional Oasis or Hydro-Tyranny

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Water Monopoly Guild (Sells sealed glass carboys of potable water at steep tariffs)
* **Main Opponent:** Desert Nomad Well-Breakers (Demanding free open access to sacred ancestral aquifers)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Well-Salting Terrorists (Threatening to poison deep wells with brine if ransoms aren't paid)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Condenser Wipers (Blind laborers polishing silica glass panes under scorching sun)

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Metered Water - Daily gallon jug rationed per citizen; brackish taste (Light)
* Level 2: Pure Artesian Gush - Cold sweet water fills public fountains; orchards flourish (Moderate)
* Level 3: Deep Salinization - Salt brine seeps into pumps; water burns throats (Severe)
* Level 4: Aquifer Basin Depletion - Piston pumps grind dry dust; settlements collapse into dust (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Aquifer Pressure Drop Clock [4 Ticks] - Water table drops below intake valves.
* Solar Mirror Shatter Clock [4 Ticks] - Hailstorms crack glass distillation panes.
