## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Towering stone arch bridge spanning a 1,000-foot chasm, co-owned by two ancestral clan families bound by an unbreakable mutual devotion pact.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Mutual Interdependent Trust + Generational Greed = Secure Continental Crossing or Plunge into the Abyss

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Dual Clan Keepers (Both clans hold one key to the portcullis; neither can cross alone)
* **Main Opponent:** Chasm Bandits (Use climbing ropes to bypass gates and rob travelers in mid-span)
* **Thematic Shadow:** The Seditious Suitor (Scheming to break the inter-clan engagement to seize sole toll rights)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Abyss Sweepers (Poor souls who clean horse dung off the narrow roadway in high winds)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Steady Crossing - Guards from both clans nod respectfully; 10-minute safe transit (Light)
* Level 2: High Wind Escort - Roped caravans; wagons cross safely; steady toll revenue (Moderate)
* Level 3: Sunder Dispute - One clan refuses to unlock its portcullis; traffic backed up for miles (Severe)
* Level 4: Arch Keystones Blasted - Dynamite detonated in center arch; bridge collapses into chasm (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Clan Marriage Betrothal Clock [4 Ticks] - Impending wedding seals 50-year bridge alliance.
* Chasm Arch Structural Creep [6 Ticks] - Mortar washing out from foundation piers.
