## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Ancient sacred grove where conflicting factions swear binding blood treaties beneath the intertwined roots of twin silver oaks.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Radical Empathy & Reconciliation + Vengeful Pride = Lasting Covenant or Bloody Betrayal

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Grove Preservers (Priests sanctifying marriages and cross-faction alliances)
* **Main Opponent:** Bitter Feud Vow-Holders (Families refusing to forgive past blood debts)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Cynical Political Arrangers (Forcing young lovers into loveless treaty marriages)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Oath-Breakers (Branded exiles living in the briars outside the grove boundary)

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Whispering Canopies - Peaceful sanctuary; weapons must be peace-bound (Light)
* Level 2: Harmonious Pact - Inter-marriage festival; trade tariffs lifted between rival clans (Moderate)
* Level 3: Feud Spark - Secret assassination at the grove altar; swords drawn (Severe)
* Level 4: The Severed Oak - Sacred twin trees chopped down; total blood feud erupts (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Blood Feud Retaliation Clock [4 Ticks] - Bitter heirs plan to violate treaty sanctuary.
* Sacred Oak Wilt Clock [6 Ticks] - Parasitic gall blight threatens ancient trees.
