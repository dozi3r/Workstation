## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Harnessing Titanic Mechanical Force + Brutal Labor Endurance = Architectural Triumph or Crushed Under the Hammer

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Master Smiths Syndicate (Regulate hammer cadences and steel temper metallurgy)
* **Main Opponent:** Exhausted Forge Strikers (Demand shorter shifts to prevent workers falling into molten vats)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Military Armaments Contractors (Bribing inspectors to pass brittle low-grade armor)

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Measured Pounding - Regular hammer thuds; steady production of iron billets (Light)
* Level 2: Master Castings - Flawless spring steel plates; bridge girders shipped downstream (Moderate)
* Level 3: Crucible Slag Spill - Molten vat tips; liquid fire floods the casting floor (Severe)
* Level 4: Cam-Shaft Shatter Explosion - Giant waterwheel shaft snaps under load, destroying the foundry (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Foundry Slag Vat Overheat [4 Ticks] - Crucible temperature exceeds refractory brick tolerance.
