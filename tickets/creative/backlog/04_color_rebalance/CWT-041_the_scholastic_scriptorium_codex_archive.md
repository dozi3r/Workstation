## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Fortified monastic library preserving ancestral legal charters, astronomical logs, and philosophical encyclopedias against historical revisionism.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Preserving Institutional Truth + Inconvenient Historical Crimes = Enduring Civic Law or Tyrannical Censorship

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Archival Curators (Strict monastic scholars controlling access to reading rooms)
* **Main Opponent:** Revisionist Royal Inquisitors (Demanding the burning of inconvenient historical treaties)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Book-Thief Antiquarians (Stealing rare spell folios and deeds to sell to private collectors)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Scraped Scribes (Copyists blinded by candle smoke living in monastic basements)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Quiet Scriptorium - Scratch of quills; smell of vellum and gall ink (Light)
* Level 2: Authoritative Judgments - Disputed deeds authenticated; legal stability restored (Moderate)
* Level 3: Inquisitorial Redaction - Censors lock down aisles; manuscripts seized and blacked out (Severe)
* Level 4: The Archive Fire - Arsonists torch the library; five centuries of recorded law reduced to ash (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Archival Book Burn Countdown [4 Ticks] - Inquisitors prepare sulfur torches for forbidden codices.
* Vellum Mold Rot Clock [6 Ticks] - Humidity threatens ancient calfskin manuscripts.
