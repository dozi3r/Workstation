# TICKET CWT-080: THE INDENTURED CARAVAN DESPERATION LEAP AND TRUCK-SYSTEM FRACTURE
*Category: 02_transit_edges | Pipeline Tier: 4 | Target Set: Set F | Status: Shipped*

---

## 1. Narrative Premise & Central Dramatic Argument
*Authority: `writing-discovery` (Mazin / Egri)*

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "Debt peonage is a fair, lawful, voluntary contract; the Company provides security, shelter, and grain in a savage frontier; work hard and you will purchase your freedom."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** An exhausted, inebriated miner (Jael Moros) leaps beneath the iron wheels of an 8-ton ore freight wain on a muddy switchback. He survives with a crushed pelvis. Trapped and bleeding in the road ruts, his boot spills a folded company commissary ledger showing that 26 years of continuous labor have left him owing 487 Silver Links, with his 8-year-old daughter already mortgaged to the sorting flumes.
* **Egri's Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Compulsory Company Store Monopsony} + \text{Desperate Bodily Martyrdom} = \text{Systemic Labor Revolt and Supply Chokepoint Fracture}$$
* **The Thematic Truth / Synthesis:** An industrial supply chain engineered on indentured human desperation is an unshielded powder keg; a single broken body can halt the wheels of sovereign empire.

---

## 2. 4-Corner Opposition Web
*Authority: `writing-discovery` (Truby)*

* **1. Establishment / Hegemony — Factor Vance Callow (The Iron Tally):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* Contracts are sacred; the ore wains must run on schedule regardless of blood on the road.
  * *Leverage:* Corporate legal authority, 12 arbalest guards, monopoly on grain and medical supplies.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Cold administrative blindness; incapable of recognizing human despair until the torches touch his storehouses.
* **2. Main Opponent / Challenger — Mara "Red-Sieve" Thorne (The Clandestine Hammer):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* The truck system must be destroyed with fire; Jael's martyrdom is the match that will ignite the general strike.
  * *Leverage:* 80 radicalized pit miners, stolen mining blasting powder, knowledge of ventilation flues.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Fanatical indifference to collateral damage; willing to freeze downstream cities to win the strike.
* **3. Thematic Shadow / Profiteer — "Gimlet" Jock (The Scrip Usurer):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* The system is crooked, so profit off the misery. Buys worker scrip at 25% value in exchange for watered gin.
  * *Leverage:* The only wayside shanty tavern; acts as a paid informant for both the company and the miners.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Total cowardice; vulnerable to extortion and exposed when violence erupts.
* **4. Tertiary Outcast / Victim — Jael Moros (The Maimed Collateral):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* Save his daughter from the sorting flumes; believed death under company wheels would void the debt lien.
  * *Leverage:* Unimpeachable moral indictment; his bloodstained ledger is a smoking gun that can shatter company legitimacy.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Physical agony, delirium from silicosis and grain alcohol; dying without immediate surgery.

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics
*Authority: `adventure_framework_srd.md`*

### 3.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* **Level 1 (Dormant / Routine Monopsony):** Miners subsist on tin company scrip; debts compound quietly; caravans depart with military precision (Light Tier, DC 10 / 1d6 damage).
* **Level 2 (Standard / Intercepted Martyrdom):** Jael's desperate leap halts the caravan in the mud; guards deploy truncheons; strike rumors emerge (Moderate Tier, DC 13 / 2d8 damage).
* **Level 3 (Overdriven / Chokepoint Picket):** Miners seize the crane switchback; teamsters held hostage; company imports mercenary strikebreakers with arbalests (Severe Tier, DC 16 / 4d10 damage).
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic / General Insurgency):** Full armed rebellion; ore hoppers dynamited; company store sacked; transit corridor severed; downstream hub faces blackout (Lethal Tier, DC 20 / 8d12 damage).

### 3.2 Threat Clocks
* **Caravan Stoppage Clock [4 Ticks] (Tactical):** Advances on argument or delay. At 4/4, elite company dragoon cavalry arrive to scour the road.
* **Labor Insurgency Clock [6 Ticks] (Social):** Advances on public beatings or discovery of union leaflets. At 6/6, 200 pit miners take up sledgehammers and dynamite.
* **Downstream Smelter Starvation Clock [4 Ticks] (Macro):** Advances each Campaign Turn the corridor is blocked. At 4/4, downstream blast furnaces freeze solid.

## 5. Scene Calculus & Encounter Sparks
*Authority: `writing-generation`*

* **Encounter 1: The Wet Crunch Under the Rim (The Arrival Collision):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Overseers attempting to drag Jael off the road in 120 seconds vs. Jael screaming for someone to read his ledger.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* Routine travel instantly pivots to an active moral and systemic crisis.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* Overseers claim they are giving medical aid while surreptitiously searching Jael's boots for union propaganda.
* **Encounter 2: The Weigh-Scale Interrogation (The Moral Crucible):**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Factor Vance tallying demurrage charges on Jael's dying body vs. the party trying to stabilize him.
  * *Dilemma:* Pay 487 Silver to free him (which causes the company to hike prices on all other miners), fight the guards (severing the regional supply corridor), or walk away (condemning his daughter to the flumes).
