## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Narrow basalt column road traversing bubbling volcanic mud lakes and steaming sulfur vents, bridging two sovereign territories.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Crossing the Geothermal Hell + Acid Scalding Hazards = Crucial Tactical Shortcut or Boiled Alive in Mud

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Sulfur Ford Masters (Sell wet wool respirators and guide sticks for high coin)
* **Main Opponent:** Fire Drake Cultists (Lure caravans into boiling mud geysers as sacred sacrifices)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Sulfur Smugglers (Gathering raw yellow crystals without guild licenses)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Scalded Leapers (Desperate guides with acid-scarred feet living in volcanic huts)

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Choking Stench - Yellow fumes irritate eyes; wet cloths required (Light)
* Level 2: Scalding Steam Vents - Random jets of boiling water; DC 14 evasion (Moderate)
* Level 3: Magma Crust Cracking - Basalt stones sink into red-hot slurry; DC 17 agility (Severe)
* Level 4: Caldera Hydrothermal Blast - Mud lake explodes in superheated steam blast (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Sulfur Inhalation Asphyxiation [4 Ticks] - Caustic gas burns lung tissue.
* Basalt Column Thermal Fracture [4 Ticks] - Heat causes stone bridge columns to shatter.
