# CWT-024: The High Sierra Switchback Caravan Trail
*Category: 02_transit_edges | Pipeline Tier: 2 | Target Set: Set E*
*status: shipped*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Paved stone switchback climbing 8,000 feet through jagged volcanic passes, fortified with imperial toll keeps and avalanche deflectors.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Imperial Logistical Ambition + Mountain Scree Instability = Secure Arterial Commerce or Swept Off the Precipice

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** Imperial Toll Wardens (Collect high tariffs and enforce strict pack-mule weight limits)
* **Main Opponent:** Highland Guerrillas (Roll boulders down onto toll fortresses from unscalable crags)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Contraband Muleteers (Using treacherous scree bypasses to evade taxes)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Avalanche Breakers (Convicts chained to watch posts to blast snowpacks with gunpowder)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Thin Air & Steep Incline - Labored breathing; slow mule pace (Light)
* Level 2: Scree Slide - Loose gravel under hooves; DC 13 animal handling check (Moderate)
* Level 3: Roaring Avalanche - Thousand tons of snow and slate sweeps the road (Severe)
* Level 4: Shelf Shear Collapse - Half a mile of road shears off into the canyon abyss (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Altitude Sickness Clock [4 Ticks] - Oxygen deprivation induces blinding headaches.
* Avalanche Snowpack Tension [6 Ticks] - Gunpowder echoes or loud horns trigger slide.
