## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
High-speed navigable white-water transit corridor dropping 30 miles in 3 hours through roaring granite flumes.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Reckless High-Speed Transit + Raging Hydraulic Rapids = Rapid Mobility or Splintered Keels

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The River Pilot Guild (Monopolizes navigation charts and charges hefty toll fees)
* **Main Opponent:** River Pirates & Wreckers (Lure barges into razor shoals with false signal lanterns)
* **Thematic Shadow:** The Corrupt Lockmaster (Deliberately stalls gates until merchants pay double tariffs)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Barrel-Raft Outlaws (Running contraband down flumes on unsteerable pine skiffs)

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Calm Pool - Slow poling; 12 hours transit (Light)
* Level 2: Roaring Flume - High-speed rush; 3 hours transit with skilled pilot (Moderate)
* Level 3: White-Water Torrent - Spray blinds crew; DC 16 steering check or hull damage (Severe)
* Level 4: Lock Collapse & Hydraulic Whirlpool - Sluice gate shatters; barges pulverized (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Hull Integrity Shatter Clock [4 Ticks] - Scraping granite teeth tears oak planks.
* River Pirate Ambush Clock [4 Ticks] - Smoke signals summon war canoes.
