# CWT-023: The Sunken Canal of the Weeping Willow

*status: shipped*

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Murky, sunken canal lined with ancient weeping willows and marsh pyres, connecting fenland hamlets via silent flatbottom punts.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Navigating Ancient Taboo Waters + Swallowing Fen Miasma = Silent Infiltration or Drowning in the Black Silt

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Fenland Punt Guild (Family clans knowing secret channels through the reeds)
* **Main Opponent:** Leech-Poachers & Bog Bandits (Setting submerged stake traps to sink passing skiffs)
* **Thematic Shadow:** The Willow Morticians (Transporting plague dead by water to avoid quarantine checkpoints)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** The Mud-Larkers (Outcast diving orphans retrieving drowned relics from canal mud)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Mist and Stagnant Water - Heavy silence; biting insects (Light)
* Level 2: Root Entanglement - Willow roots foul rudders; DC 13 navigation check (Moderate)
* Level 3: Miasmic Fever Fog - Marsh gas causes delirium; DC 16 poison save (Severe)
* Level 4: Submerged Bog-Ghoul Swarm - Dead rising from canal bottom to drag boats under (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Swamp Fever Infection Clock [4 Ticks] - Contaminated water causes burning ague.
* Weeping Root Choke Clock [6 Ticks] - Rapid willow growth blocks the waterway.
