## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Braided steel aerial gondolas spanning a two-mile void over bottomless blue ice crevasses and frost winds.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Daring High-Altitude Engineering + Unforgiving Glacial Storms = Bypassed Peril or Frozen Plummet

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Cableway Wardens (Monastic order maintaining the iron pulleys and wind-breaks)
* **Main Opponent:** Frost Reavers (Cut anchor cables to isolate mountaintop citadels for plundering)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Gondola Ticket Speculators (Extorting stranded refugees fleeing low-land wars)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Crevasse Hermits (Living in frozen caves beneath the cables, salvaging fallen cargo)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Gentle Sway - High altitude breeze; stunning icy panorama (Light)
* Level 2: Rime Gale - Frost coats steel cables; gondolas freeze in mid-span (Moderate)
* Level 3: Blinding Blizzard - Cables vibrate violently; DC 16 hypothermia checks (Severe)
* Level 4: Main Cable Shearing - Steel strands snap one by one; gondola dangling by a thread (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Frostbite Exposure Clock [4 Ticks] - Sub-zero winds freeze extremities.
* Cable Rime Ice Weight Clock [6 Ticks] - Ice accumulation threatens cable tensile limit.
