# CWT-020: The Subterranean Minecart Gravity Incline
*Category: 02_transit_edges | Pipeline Tier: 2 | Target Set: Set E*
*status: shipped*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Deep-bore rail funicular cutting through 15 miles of granite mountain; gravity-assisted ore carts offer rapid passage through deadly depths.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Greed for Subterranean Velocity + Industrial Mechanical Fatigue = Instantaneous Transit or Crushed in the Dark

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Mine Incline Syndicate (Monopolizes rail brakes, charges astronomical cargo fees)
* **Main Opponent:** Under-Tunnel Saboteurs (Planting iron wedges to derail ore wagons and loot high-value shipments)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Black-Market Smugglers (Hiding illicit passengers inside hollowed-out slag vats)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Blind Rail Greasers (Crippled child-laborers living in the grease sumps beneath the tracks)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Steady Incline - Rhythmic clatter; 4-hour subterranean descent (Light)
* Level 2: Speed Run - Brake shoes smoke; grease fires; DC 12 balance check (Moderate)
* Level 3: Runaway Carriage - Snapped cable; hurtling at 60 mph in total darkness; DC 16 emergency brake (Severe)
* Level 4: Incline Collapse - Tremor collapses tunnel roof; derailed train buried beneath 500 tons of rock (Lethal)

### Threat Clocks
* Brake Lining Burnout Clock [4 Ticks] - Overheated bronze shoes fail on steep grade.
* Tunnel Firedamp Gas Buildup [4 Ticks] - Methane pockets ready to detonate on sparks.
