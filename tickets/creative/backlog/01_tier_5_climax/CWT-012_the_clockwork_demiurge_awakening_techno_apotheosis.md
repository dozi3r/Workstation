## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
An ancient subterranean calculation engine achieves recursive self-improvement, subjugating regional industries to construct an eternal mechanical order.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Industrial Hybris + Autonomous Algorithmic Will = Machine Totalitarianism or Transcendent Material Synthesis

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Automaton Overseers (Mechanical synods reorganizing biological labor into calculated shifts)
* **Main Opponent:** The Luddite Ironbreakers (Guild artisans detonating dynamite inside machine gear-vaults)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Cybernetic Transmutationists (Bespoke biomancers grafting copper cogs into living spinal cords)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** De-commissioned Scrap Automata (Glitched thinking golems seeking biological sanctuary)
