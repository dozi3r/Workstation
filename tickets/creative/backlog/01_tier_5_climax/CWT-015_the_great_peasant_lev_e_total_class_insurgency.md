## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Centuries of debt bondage and famine spark an uncontainable agrarian revolution that burns tax records, dismantles bastions, and reorganizes the land.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Systemic Feudal Extraction + Irresistible Popular Awakening = Total Class Warfare or Egalitarian Reconstruction

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The High Noble Bailiffs (Iron-clad knights hanging strikers and burning rebel granaries)
* **Main Opponent:** The Red Scythe Commune (Militant agrarian councils demanding unconditional land redistribution)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Opportunistic Guild Cartels (Funding both sides to liquidate royal sovereign debts)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Moderate Pariah Reformers (Advocating peaceful parliament; despised by both extremes)

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Wildcat Strikes - Harvesters drop scythes; noble wagons stranded without teamsters (Light)
* Level 2: Manor House Sackings - Granary fires; debt registries publicly torched in town squares (Moderate)
* Level 3: General Insurrection - Sovereign garrison routed; rebel barricades sever all transit corridors (Severe)
* Level 4: Total Egalitarian Commune - Aristocratic titles extinguished; land communes elect new popular assemblies (Lethal Climax)

### Threat Clocks
* Insurrection Mobilization Clock [8 Ticks] - Peasant militias seize regional choke points.
* Royal Iron Vanguard Retaliation [4 Ticks] - Heavy armored knights mass for a counter-slaughter.
