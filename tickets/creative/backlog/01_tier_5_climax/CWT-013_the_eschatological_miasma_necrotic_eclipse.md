# CWT-013: The Eschatological Miasma & Necrotic Eclipse

*status: shipped*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
A perpetual obsidian eclipse shrouds the sky as an ancient necrotic miasma seeps from the hollow mantle, raising the collective dead of every era.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Unatoned Generational Slaughter + Necrotic Inversion = Universal Extinction or Ancestral Reconciliation

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Ash Pyre Inquisition (Burning all dead, razing cemeteries with molten sulfur)
* **Main Opponent:** The Eclipse Revenant Host (Legion of resurrected ancestors claiming their ancient lands)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Necro-Chemical Profiteers (Distilling corpse fat into dark illumination fluids)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Half-Turned Twilight Wanderers (Living individuals whose blood has curdled black)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Dimming Sunlight - Pale violet noon; shadows stretch unnaturally (Light)
* Level 2: Whispering Fog - Ground mist rises; auditory hallucinations of deceased kin (Moderate)
* Level 3: Midnight Inversion - The sun extinguishes; ancient tombs burst open across all hills (Severe)
* Level 4: Eternal Necrotic Dominion - The living become an endangered minority in a continent of ancestors (Lethal Climax)

### Threat Clocks
* Total Solar Eclipse Countdown [8 Ticks] - Darkness hardens into an impenetrable celestial canopy.
* Ancestral Memory Plague Clock [4 Ticks] - Living minds overwritten by spirits of past warriors.
