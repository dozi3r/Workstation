## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
The Sovereign monarch is assassinated by unknown conspirators, shattering regional law into a multi-faction civil war of scorched-earth purges.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Autocratic Tyranny + Decapitation Strike = Total Civil Anarchy or Reconstructed Constitutional Compact

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** The Iron Regents Council (Military junta enforcing martial law, summary executions)
* **Main Opponent:** The Pretender Coalition (Band of rival dukes hiring mercenary armies to burn rival estates)
* **Thematic Shadow:** The Sovereign Siphon Guild (Shadow assassins auctioning off state secrets and sovereign seals)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Deserter Peasant Brigades (Ex-soldiers forming commune militias to resist conscription)

---

## 3. Injected Tabletop Mechanics (adventure_framework_srd.md)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Mourning Curfew - Black flags; gates shuttered; identity checks (Light)
* Level 2: Factional Skirmishes - Street fighting between rival house guards; bread rationing (Moderate)
* Level 3: Siege Artillery Deployment - Trebuchets and mortars level municipal quarters (Severe)
* Level 4: Total Civil Conflagration - Palaces burn, all legal titles voided, sovereign succession resolved by blood (Lethal Climax)

### Threat Clocks
* Succession War Bloodshed Clock [8 Ticks] - Factional warfare spreads to rural satellites.
* Imperial Regicide Investigation Clock [4 Ticks] - Assassins prepare secondary strikes against heirs.
