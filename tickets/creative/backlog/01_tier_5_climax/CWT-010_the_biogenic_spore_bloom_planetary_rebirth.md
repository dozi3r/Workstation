## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Subterranean fungal super-organism breaches bedrock, dissolving masonry into fertile loam and offering transcendent post-human symbiosis.

---

## 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism (writing-discovery)
> **Dramatic Syllogism:**
> Entrenched Anthropocentrism + Spore Mutation Cascade = Extinction or Post-Human Symbiosis

### Truby 4-Corner Opposition Web
* **Establishment:** Bacteriological Quarantine Garrison (Burns spores with sulfur, executes infected)
* **Main Opponent:** The Chloroplast Cult / Sylvan Prophets (Breaches quarantine silos to accelerate planetary bloom)
* **Thematic Shadow:** Desperate Alchemists (Harvesting raw spores for youth serums, causing bio-weapon outbreaks)
* **Tertiary Outcast:** Feral Myco-Hybrids (Living symbiotes who perceive the mycelium as ancestral memory)

### 4-Level Dynamic Dial
* Level 1: Fungal Mold - Spoils rations, rots boots (Light)
* Level 2: Airborne Hallucinogen - Shared dreams; lung coughing checks (Moderate)
* Level 3: Root-Breach Surge - Masonry cracks, iron corrodes, spore clouds blind (Severe)
* Level 4: Planetary Mycelial Rebirth - Masonry dissolves into fertile loam; organic hive mind awakens (Lethal Climax)

### Threat Clocks

* Myco-Lung Petrification [4 Ticks] - Inhaled spore infection converts muscle to vegetative fiber.
