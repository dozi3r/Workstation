## Overview: The Iron Fist of the Dark Queen

The military machine that overran Ansalon was not a faceless horde of monsters; it was directed by a brilliant, ruthless, and fractured high command of warlords, renegades, dark clerics, death knights, and opportunistic sellswords. United only by their ambition and terror of the Five-Headed Dragon [[Takhisis]], the Dragon Highlords embodied the catastrophic axiom of evil: *it inevitably turns upon itself in a frenzy of paranoia and treachery.*

This master dossier synthesizes the definitive psychological profiles, multi-era evolutions, combat statistics, and autonomous GM playbooks for the 11 dark commanders who orchestrated the War of the Lance.

## 1. [[Kitiara Uth Matar]] (The Blue Lady / Dragon Highlord of the Blue Wing)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 3, C: 4] | CVF: Adhocracy / Market (Predatory Individualism)
Class/Role Progression:
  - Soulforge: Fighter 3 / Rogue 2 (Mercenary Sellsword / Half-Sister)
  - Brothers in Arms: Mercenary Captain 6 (Vanguard Commander / Imperial Scout)
  - Autumn Twilight: Dragon Highlord Aspirant 8 (Dark Emissary of Sanction)
  - Winter Night: Dragon Highlord 10+ (Commander of the Blue Wing / Aerial Slayer)
  - Spring Dawning: Supreme Warlord 12+ (Mistress of Dargaard Keep / Consort of Soth)
```

### 1.1 Backstory & Etymology
- **Daughter of the Disgraced Knight:** Born to Gregor uth Matar, a disgraced Solamnic knight who abandoned his family to become a mercenary brigand, and Rosamun, who subsequently remarried woodcutter Gilon Majere and birthed twins Raistlin and Caramon. Kitiara inherited her father’s swordsmanship, cold calculation, and utter contempt for the Measure.
- **Naming Etymology & Jeff Grubb's Playtest Note:** In *The Annotated Chronicles* (Book p. 39), Jeff Grubb notes that Kitiara was named after his wife, game designer Kate Novak—wryly adding, *"and there the similarity between the two ends. Really!"*

### 1.2 Psychological Anatomy
- **Primal Wound (Shard of Glass):** Childhood abandonment by her mercenary father, whom she idolized; growing up in squalor in Solace, forced to parent her sickly, strange half-brother Raistlin and her slow-witted half-brother Caramon; enduring the claustrophobic piety of village life.
- **The Lie She Believes:** *"Mercy is a fatal disease; physical dominance and fear are the only true currencies of reality; only those who conquer without pity have the right to live."*
- **The Want (External Goal):** To conquer Solamnia and Palanthas, crush the Solamnic Order, supplant Emperor Ariakas as supreme ruler of Krynn, and force Tanis Half-Elven to kneel at her side as her lover-consort.
- **The Need (Internal Moral Truth):** To acknowledge that her ruthless pursuit of absolute power has made her a disposable pawn of Takhisis, and that in murdering the love she shared with Tanis, she has destroyed her own soul.

### 1.3 Sol Stein's Bifocal Dialogue Profile
- **Focal Spoken Voice:** Husky, low, teasing, vibrant contralto; quick, mocking, throaty laugh; signature crooked smile that curls only one side of her mouth; switches effortlessly between warm, predatory sexual charm and ice-cold military commands.
- **Unspoken Subtext Agenda:** Constant psychological testing of everyone in her presence; calculating how to seduce, intimidate, or execute rivals; maintaining total dominance over powerful men (Tanis, Sturm, Soth, Ariakas).
- **Psychological Leverage Points:** Her lingering, possessive obsession with Tanis Half-Elven; her fierce professional pride in her undefeated aerial record with Skie; any insult regarding her illegitimate heritage.

### 1.4 Evolution Across All 5 Books
- ***The Soulforge*:** The rebellious mercenary maiden in Solace; trains Caramon in heavy swordsmanship; mocks Raistlin's physical weakness while secretly admiring his ruthless intellect; takes young Sturm Brightblade as her brief lover during a journey north (conceiving their secret son, Steel Brightblade); abandons Solace to seek her fortune in the mercenary armies.
- ***Brothers in Arms*:** Emerges as a rising cavalry captain in the imperial vanguard; navigates the early Dragonarmy command circles; secures connections with the dark clerics of Sanction; marks her brothers' progress in Baron Ivor's army from afar.
- ***Dragons of Autumn Twilight*:** Operates in the shadows; absent from the reunion at the Inn of the Last Home (leaving a mysterious note for Tanis); rises rapidly through the Dragonarmy hierarchy by executing incompetent superiors and earning the personal favor of [[Emperor Ariakas]] and Takhisis.
- ***Dragons of Winter Night*:** Appears as the supreme commander of the Blue Dragonarmy, mounted atop the ancient blue wyrm [[Skie]] (Khellendros); encounters Tanis in Flotsam, seducing him into her bed and manipulating his torn heart; leads the aerial siege of the [[High Clerist's Tower]]; slays her former lover [[Sturm Brightblade]] in single combat atop the battlements, recognizing his broken armor with cold, tragic melancholy.
- ***Dragons of Spring Dawning*:** Forges a dark alliance with the death knight [[Lord Soth]], promising him the pure soul of Laurana; orchestrates the capture of Laurana through a forged letter; rides to the Grand Council in Neraka; plans to assassinate Emperor Ariakas; after Tanis slays Ariakas, she offers Tanis the Crown of Power; when Tanis flees with Laurana, she watches them escape, her crooked smile acknowledging the final defeat of her heart.

### 1.5 Combat Tactics & Signature Gear
- **Tactical Doctrine:** The most lethal aerial shock commander on Krynn. Specializes in mounted dragon combat, diving lance charges, sweeping lightning attacks, and tactical ambushes. On foot, she is a ferocious master of the bastard sword and throwing daggers.
- **Mount:** [[Skie]] (Khellendros), colossal ancient blue dragon; bonded as equal tactical partners.
- **Signature Gear:** Full dragon-scale plate armor tinted cobalt blue; custom dragonrider saddle; +3 magical broadsword of keen sharpness; masterwork Solamnic-style steel throwing daggers.

### 1.6 Autonomous Boss / Antagonist Playbook
- **DM Directives:** Kitiara is terrifying because she is charismatic, brilliant, and completely devoid of guilt. When interacting with player characters (especially Tanis), she leans close, touches their arm, laughs at their high morals, and offers them power, wealth, and safety.
- **Combat Flow:** Never fights on foot if Skie is airborne. Uses dive-and-climb maneuvers, strafing lightning breath across battlefields, and dismounting only to personally execute opposing commanders.

## 2. [[Emperor Ariakas]] (Supreme Commander of the Dragonarmies)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 1, C: 1] | CVF: Pure Hierarchy (Imperial Totalitarianism)
Class/Role: Fighter 15 / Cleric of Takhisis 10 (Emperor of Neraka / Favored of the Queen)
```

### 2.1 Backstory & Ascent to Power
- **The Mercenary Emperor:** A massive, brutal human warrior of exceptional tactical brilliance and iron will. Rose from the squalor of cutthroat sellsword companies through unhesitating murder and political cunning. Personally chosen by [[Takhisis]] to organize the disparate goblin tribes, mercenary battalions, and chromatic dragons into the five great Dragonarmies, crowning himself Emperor of the Dragon Empire at Neraka.

### 2.2 Psychological Anatomy
- **Primal Wound (Shard of Glass):** Born into peasant obscurity; constant, gnawing paranoia that every general beneath him is plotting to poison his goblet or plunge a dagger between his shoulder blades.
- **The Lie He Believes:** *"The Dark Queen rewards absolute strength and terror alone; with the Crown of Power and my Five-Fold Shield, I am an invincible living god who will rule Krynn forever."*
- **The Want (External Goal):** To crush the Whitestone Alliance, capture the Everman [[Berem]], execute rival Highlords (especially Kitiara), and stand beside Takhisis as her earthly monarch.
- **The Need (Internal Moral Truth):** To realize that the Queen of Darkness views all mortals—even her greatest champions—as disposable kindling to be consumed and discarded.

### 2.3 Sol Stein's Bifocal Dialogue Profile
- **Focal Spoken Voice:** Thunderous, resonant, imperious bass; dripping with lazy, supreme arrogance; speaks in imperial decrees; laughs heartily at the agony of underlings.
- **Unspoken Subtext Agenda:** Hyper-vigilant scanning of every shadow, weapon, and facial twitch in the throne room; assessing which of his Highlords must be purged next.
- **Psychological Leverage Points:** Feeding his monstrous vanity; stoking his paranoia regarding Kitiara's secret alliances; challenging his personal martial supremacy.

### 2.4 Combat Statistics & Signature Relics
- **Tactical Profile:** A walking fortress of divine and martial destruction. Wields a colossal two-handed greatsword engulfed in unholy black-red flame.
- **The Crown of Power:** Massive golden circlet studded with dark dragon gems; enhances psychic domination, grants telepathic command over draconian battalions, and amplifies magical wards.
- **The Five-Fold Elemental Shield:** Divine aura bestowed directly by Takhisis. Creates an absolute invulnerable barrier that absorbs 100% of all physical strikes and mortal spells. The shield *cannot be breached from the outside*—it can only be dismantled by high sorcery from within the dark circle (as executed by Raistlin Majere in Neraka).

### 2.5 Autonomous Boss Playbook
- **DM Directives:** Ariakas should radiate overwhelming, suffocating dread. In his presence, lower-level PCs suffer moral panic. He sits casually on his dragon throne, stroking his jeweled sword, watching challengers break their weapons against his magical shield before crushing them with a single blow.

## 3. [[Highlord Verminaard]] of Neros (The Scourge of Pax Tharkas)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 1, C: 1] | CVF: Hierarchy (Theocratic Fanaticism)
Class/Role: Cleric of Takhisis 8 / Dark War-Priest (Dragon Highlord of the Red Wing)
```

### 3.1 Backstory & Personality
- **The Theocratic Butcher:** Commander of the Red Dragonarmy operating in Abanasinia and Pax Tharkas. A towering, brooding zealot clad in heavy plate armor and an ornate full-face steel helm that completely obscures his features. Completely devoted to [[Takhisis]], believing himself to be her chosen hand of divine vengeance upon an unworthy world.

### 3.2 Psychological Anatomy
- **Primal Wound (Shard of Glass):** A lifetime of bitter, burning resentment and spiritual fanaticism; absolute terror of failing the Dark Queen, knowing that her punishment for failure is eternal Abyssal agony.
- **The Lie He Believes:** *"The ancient gods of light abandoned Krynn because mortals are weak and corrupt; only Takhisis offers true order through total subjugation."*
- **The Want:** To crush the refugee rebellion, break the will of the Qualinesti elves, recover the Disks of Mishakal, and sacrifice the companions in the name of the Queen.
- **The Need:** To realize that the Dark Queen’s promises are hollow illusions, and that his unholy power will evaporate the moment true light confronts him.

### 3.3 Sol Stein's Bifocal Dialogue Profile
- **Focal Spoken Voice:** Hollow, metallic, booming voice echoing from behind his iron visor; preaches dark sermons laced with apocalyptic doom; quotes unholy texts of Takhisis.
- **Unspoken Subtext Agenda:** Frantic dread of disappointing Ariakas; simmering madness induced by the telepathic whispers of the Dark Queen.
- **Psychological Leverage Points:** Confronting him with true divine magic (Goldmoon); challenging his personal martial honor; threatening his red dragon mount [[Pyros]] (Ember).

### 3.4 Combat Tactics & Signature Gear
- **Tactical Profile:** Devastating frontline war-cleric. Casts high-circle divine banes (*Blindness*, *Bestow Curse*, *Hold Person*, *Flame Strike*). Mounted atop [[Pyros]], a colossal, cunning ancient red wyrm.
- **Nightbringer:** His signature weapon; an enchanted heavy steel morningstar crafted of midnight-black iron. Strikes with unholy force, inflicting permanent blindness on victims who fail their saving throws.

### 3.5 Autonomous Boss Playbook
- **Encounter Design (Pax Tharkas Throne Room):** Verminaard fights with terrifying synergy alongside Pyros. He opens combat by casting *Hold Person* on frontline fighters while Pyros strafes the hall with breath weapon attacks. Verminaard must be engaged simultaneously in melee and through divine counter-magic (Goldmoon) to shatter his dark conviction.

## 4. [[Lord Soth]] (The Knight of the Black Rose)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 1, C: 1] | CVF: Pure Hierarchy (Ancient Cursed Chivalry)
Class/Role: Death Knight (CR 19 / Ancient Solamnic Grand Master of Dargaard Keep)
```

### 4.1 Backstory & The Cataclysm Curse
- **The Fall of Castle Dargaard:** Formerly Sir Loren Soth, Lord of the Rose, the most celebrated Knight of Solamnia in the province of Knightlund. Fallen from grace when he rescued the Silvanesti elven maiden Isolde from ogres, brought her to Dargaard Keep, and murdered his wife Lady Gladria to marry her. When Isolde bore him a son, the Knighthood brought him to trial for murder. Soth escaped to Dargaard Keep, defying his brother knights.
- **The Unforgivable Sin:** Guided by the gods, Soth was given a sacred quest to ride to Istar and slay the mad Kingpriest, preventing the Cataclysm. Along the road, he encountered a company of mocking elven maidens who falsely whispered that Isolde was in the arms of another knight. Consumed by insane, jealous pride, Soth turned his warhorse around and rode back to Dargaard to confront her. As the fiery mountain struck Krynn, Isolde cursed him with her dying breath as Dargaard Keep burned: Soth stood by and watched his infant son perish in the flames. He was transformed into an immortal **Death Knight**, condemned to spend eternity reliving his crimes.

### 4.2 Psychological Anatomy
- **Primal Wound (Shard of Glass):** The eternal, agonizing wailing of the 13 elven banshees who sing the tale of his treason outside his bedchamber every night; knowing that he had the power to save Krynn from the Cataclysm and threw it away for petty jealousy.
- **The Lie He Believes:** *"Honor is an illusion; I feel neither pain nor pity; I am beyond salvation, and only the aesthetic perfection of claiming Laurana's soul will bring quiet to my ruined halls."*
- **The Want (External Goal):** To claim the soul of [[Laurana]], transforming her into an undead elven banshee queen to rule beside him in Dargaard Keep forever.
- **The Need (Internal Moral Truth):** The unobtainable peace of true oblivion; the bitter admission that no stolen soul can ever quiet the wailing song of his murdered wife.

### 4.3 Sol Stein's Bifocal Dialogue Profile
- **Focal Spoken Voice:** A hollow, resonant, chilling sepulchral whisper emerging from an empty visor holding only two blazing points of orange fire; speaks with exquisite, archaic Solamnic courtly chivalry; never shouts; polite, unyielding, and terrifying.
- **Unspoken Subtext Agenda:** Cold, absolute certainty in his lethal dominance; a deep aristocratic scorn for dishonorable trickery; smoldering, unquenchable self-loathing.
- **Psychological Leverage Points:** He *cannot break a sworn knightly oath or given word*; mentioning the death of his infant son causes a momentary shudder of metaphysical paralysis; challenging him to single combat appeals to his ancient knightly code.

### 4.4 Combat Profile & Death Knight Magic
- **Tactical Profile:** The most terrifying ground boss on Krynn (18/00 Strength, AC -4 / 24, 75% Magic Resistance). Surrounded by a permanent aura of unnatural frost and magical fear.
- **Signature Powers:**
  - *Hellfire Fireball:* 20d6 necrotic-fire blast ignoring standard fire resistance.
  - *Power Word Kill:* Slays any mortal with under 100 hit points instantly with a single spoken word.
  - *Withering Touch:* Permanent necrotic decay that rots flesh and breaks bone.
  - *Command Undead:* Commands skeletal warriors and an escort of 13 wailing elven banshees.

### 4.5 Autonomous Boss Playbook
- **DM Golden Rule:** *Lord Soth cannot be killed by conventional party weapons.* If the party attempts to fight him directly in open combat, Soth systematically dismantles them. He must be engaged through diplomacy, knightly codes of honor, or narrative leverage (e.g., confronting him with the memory of his sins).

## 5. [[Highlord Feal-Thas]] (The Dark Elf of Icewall)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 3, C: 1] | CVF: Hierarchy / Adhocracy (Cast-Out Magus)
Class/Role: Dark Elf Wizard 9 / Dragon Highlord of the White Wing
```

### 5.1 Backstory & Personality
- **The Cast-Out of Silvanesti:** A renegade Silvanesti dark-elf (*Cast-Out*) wizard who embraced dark arcane arts and was exiled from the ancestral forests. Journeyed south to the frozen wastes of the Icewall glacier, offering his services to the Dark Queen. Promoted to Dragon Highlord of the White Wing, ruling from the sub-zero battlements of [[Icewall Castle]].
- **The Aesthetic of Cold:** Refined, arrogant, and ruthless. Clad in heavy white furs lined with beaten silver. Despises his crude minion troops (thanoi walrus-men and baaz draconians); values only his pack of intelligent winter wolves and his bonded white dragon [[Sleet]].

### 5.2 Tactical Profile & The Dragon Orb
- **Combat Magic:** Master of cold evocation and illusion (*Ice Storm*, *Cone of Cold*, *Wall of Ice*, *Mirror Image*). Guards the [[Dragon Orb of Icewall]] within a frozen sub-zero sanctuary chamber.
- **Encounter Directives:** Feal-Thas uses his winter wolves as frontline harriers while he snipes from icy balconies, casting *Cone of Cold* into tight clusters before ordering Sleet to collapse the ice ceiling onto the party.

## 6. [[Highlord Toede]] (Fewmaster Toede / The Opportunist)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 3, E: 3, C: 4] | CVF: Pure Adhocracy (Chaotic Sycophancy)
Class/Role: Hobgoblin Rogue 4 / Fighter 4 (Fewmaster -> Highlord of Flotsam)
```

### 6.1 Backstory & Comedy of Treachery
- **The Climbing Vermin:** A bloated, cowardly, greasy-skinned hobgoblin officer who begins the war as a lowly "Fewmaster" in Abanasinia, hunting the Blue Crystal Staff. Through extraordinary bureaucratic survival instincts, flattering superiors, throwing underlings under charging wagons, and claiming credit for others' victories, Toede miraculously climbs the ranks to become the Dragon Highlord of the White Wing (post-Feal-Thas) and ruler of the port of Flotsam.
- **Tabletop Persona:** Margaret Weis and Tracy Hickman designed Toede as the dark comic relief of the Dragonarmies—groveling before Ariakas and Kitiara, but tyrannical, pompous, and vicious when dealing with helpless prisoners or peasants.

### 6.2 Tactical Profile & Fate
- **Combat:** Rides a monstrous, foul-tempered war-boar; wields a poisoned shortsword and hand crossbow. Flees combat the moment his bodyguards are slain.
- **Canonical Death:** Met his poetic, humiliating end in the Kendirwood prior to the Grand Council in Neraka, when he was ambushed, captured, and slain by a kender hunting party led by Kronin Thistleknot.

## 7. [[Highlord Salah-Khan]] (The Warlord of the Green Wing)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 2, E: 2, C: 3] | CVF: Market / Clan (Nomadic Warlord)
Class/Role: Fighter 10 / Khurish Horselord (Dragon Highlord of the Green Wing)
```

### 7.1 Backstory & Pragmatic Alliance
- **The Khurish General:** Supreme warlord of the nomadic horse tribes of Khur. Unlike the religious zealots or cursed knights of the Dragonarmies, Salah-Khan is a pragmatic, cold-blooded military tactician who allied with the Dragon Empire purely to ensure the survival and territorial dominance of his people.
- **Tactical Doctrine:** Master of light cavalry maneuver warfare, desert ambushes, and mounted archery. Commands the Green Dragonarmy with disciplined restraint, despising unnecessary cruelty that wastes valuable horses, steel, or trade supplies.

## 8. [[Bakaris]] (The Arrogant Noble of the Blue Wing)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 3, C: 4] | CVF: Hierarchy / Adhocracy (Aristocratic Predator)
Class/Role: Fighter 8 / Cavalier (Second-in-Command of the Blue Wing)
```

### 8.1 Persona & The Flotsam Captor
- **The Vain Peacock:** A handsome, aristocratic human nobleman who serves as Kitiara’s second-in-command in the Blue Dragonarmy. Arrogant, cruel, and deeply resentful of Tanis Half-Elven.
- **The Downfall:** Captures Laurana after her betrayal; attempts to sexually assault her in his tent during the march to Neraka; thwarted and killed when Laurana fights back with ferocious desperation, shattering his vanity before taking his life.

## 9. [[Gakhan]] (The Sivak Assassin of Neraka)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 4, C: 1] | CVF: Hierarchy (Infiltrator Assassin)
Class/Role: Sivak Draconian Master Assassin (CR 7)
```

### 9.1 Persona & Shape-Shifting Horror
- **Kitiara's Shadow:** A massive, nine-foot-tall Sivak draconian who serves as Kitiara’s personal spymaster and executioner. Uses his shape-shifting ability (transforming into the likeness of humanoids he slays) to infiltrate refugee camps, murder sentries, and track the Companions from Flotsam to Neraka.
- **The Death in Neraka:** Met a brutal end in the dungeons of Neraka when he attempted to capture Berem the Everman—Berem's supernatural un-death strength manifested, smashing Gakhan's skull to paste against the dungeon wall.

## 10. [[Commander Kholos]] (The Butcher of Bloodwatch)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 1, E: 1, C: 4] | CVF: Hierarchy / Adhocracy (Mercenary Tyrant)
Class/Role: Half-Goblin Fighter 8 (Fortress Commander from *Brothers in Arms*)
```

### 10.1 Narrative Role
- **The Enemy in the West:** The ruthless half-goblin commander who held the fortress of Bloodwatch against Baron Ivor's mercenary army in *Brothers in Arms*. A cunning, sadistic military engineer who employed trench traps, boiling pitch, and hobgoblin shock troops to slaughter advancing companies. Served as the dark anvil upon which the young Majere twins forged their tactical battlefield instincts.

## 11. [[Eben Shatterstone]] (The Traitor of Pax Tharkas)

```
Sociological Profile: [S: 4, E: 4, C: 4] | CVF: Market (Greedy Opportunist)
Class/Role: Fighter 4 / Rogue 3 (Double Agent / Sellsword)
```

### 11.1 The Judas of the Refugees
- **The Charming Traitor:** A dashing, charismatic human sellsword who attaches himself to the refugee column during the flight from Pax Tharkas. Claims to be a heroic freedom fighter, but is secretly on Highlord Verminaard's payroll, leaving trail markers, stealing supplies, and attempting to deliver the refugee leaders into Dragonarmy ambushes.
- **Canonical End:** Met a fittingly cowardly death during the battle of Pax Tharkas when a collapsing stone ceiling crushed him beneath tons of falling masonry as he scrambled to scoop up dropped gold coins.
