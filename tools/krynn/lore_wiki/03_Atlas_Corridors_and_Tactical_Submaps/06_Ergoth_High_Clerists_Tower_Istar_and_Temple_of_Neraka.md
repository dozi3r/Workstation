# 06: Ergoth, High Clerist's Tower, Istar and Temple of Neraka
*The Dragon-Trap Funnels, Sunken Spires, Aerial Citadels & The Five-Spired Climax of Darkness (Spring 352 AC)*

---

## 1. Operational Overview & Setting Horizon

This volume represents the strategic and tactical culmination of the **War of the Lance** (Spring 352 AC), synthesizing the climax of *Dragons of Winter Night* and the entirety of *Dragons of Spring Dawning* with the legendary AD&D modules:
- **[[DL7_Dragons_of_Light]] (Jeff Grubb, 1985):** The discovery of the [[Tomb of Huma]] in [[Southern Ergoth]], the reforging of the [[Dragonlances]] at the ancient forge of Reorx, and the allied convocation at the [[Whitestone Glade]] on [[Sancrist Isle]].
- **[[DL8_Dragons_of_War]] (Tracy & Laura Hickman, 1984):** The siege of the **High Clerist's Tower**, the activation of the Dragon Orb, and Sturm Brightblade's solitary stand against Kitiara and Skie.
- **[[DL12_Dragons_of_Faith]] (Harold Johnson & Bruce Nesmith, 1986):** The naval voyage across the [[Blood Sea of Istar]], the descent into the **Sunken City of Istar**, and the aerial boarding of the **Flying Citadels**.
- **[[DL14_Dragons_of_Triumph]] (Douglas Niles & Michael Dobson, 1986):** The final infiltration of the **Temple of Darkness at Neraka**, the assassination of Emperor Ariakas, the sacrifice of [[Berem the Everman]], and the collapse of the Dark Queen’s five-spired cathedral.

### 5D Sensory Pentagon: The Tomb of Huma
- **Physical Environment:** A hidden mountain amphitheater bathed in eternal, golden sunlight despite the impenetrable wall of silver fog encircling Foghaven; singing glacial streams of pure water; ancient weeping willows dropping golden leaves upon pure white marble crypts; air scented with mountain pine, wild honeycomb, and clean ozone.
- **Behaviors:** Knights, elves, and dwarves instinctively lowering weapons; kneeling reverently in deep mountain moss; speaking in hushed, church-like whispers.
- **Relationships:** Transcendent spiritual peace; ancient racial enmities dissolve in the presence of true chivalric holiness.
- **Attitudes:** Awe, humility, renewed moral resolve; the conviction that the gods have not abandoned Krynn.
- **Values:** Absolute chivalric self-sacrifice; truth; fidelity to the Light; the sacred law of the Oath and the Measure.

#### Tactical Key: The Ancient Forge & Crypt
- **E1. The Dragon Graveyard:** Titanic skeletons of silver and gold dragons from the Third Dragon War lie in peaceful repose among beds of white mountain lilies.
- **E2. The Forge of the Dragonlance:** An ancient temple forge of Reorx carved from solid granite. Houses the divine anvil, tongs, and bellows required to temper pure dragonmetal. Here, [[Theros Ironfeld]] bonds with the [[Silver Arm of Ergoth]] to forge the true [[Dragonlances]].
- **E3. The Crypt of Huma:** A plain white marble sarcophagus in the mountain’s heart. Upon the lid lies the original [[Mounted Dragonlance]] wielded by Lord Huma, gleaming as brightly as the day it was forged, untouched by dust or tarnish.

### Sancrist Isle: Mount Nevermind & The Whitestone Glade
- **Mount Nevermind:** A hollow volcanic peak housing fifty thousand tinker gnomes. Vertical transit is operated by erratic gnomish pneumatic lifts, corkscrew elevators, and catapult-launch chutes.
- **The Whitestone Glade:** An ancient mountain amphitheater surrounding the **Whitestone**, a massive, mirror-smooth boulder that fell from the heavens in the Age of Dreams. Here, the fractured factions of Ansalon forge the **Allied Council of the Whitestone** after Sturm and Laurana shatter the corrupted Dragon Orb of Silvanesti upon its surface.

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: The Whitestone Glade
- **Physical Environment:** An ancient mountain clearing ringed by colossal, moss-clad oaks; in the center stands the blindingly white, unweathered meteorite; crisp winter air smelling of frosted oak leaves and snow; the metallic glint of hundreds of ceremonial breastplates.
- **Behaviors:** Armored knights, silk-robed elven princes, and bearded dwarven elders arguing in shouting circles; hands hovering white-knuckled over sword hilts; scribes struggling to record diplomatic insults.
- **Relationships:** Razor-sharp diplomatic friction; factions eyeing each other with bitter suspicion; the threat of civil war simmering beneath aristocratic etiquette.
- **Attitudes:** Desperation masked by pedigree pride; everyone wants the war won, but no kingdom is willing to surrender its military sovereignty.
- **Values:** Territorial autonomy, knightly rank, ancestral honor, ideological purity.

### 5D Sensory Pentagon: The High Clerist's Tower
- **Physical Environment:** Octagonal granite walls sweeping upward like the petals of a colossal stone rose; bitter mountain gales carrying stinging sleet and the sulfurous stench of dragon breath; the thunderous roar of blue dragons circling in storm clouds above; cold flagstones slick with frozen blood and slush.
- **Behaviors:** Knights standing vigil in freezing rain; sharpening lance tips with rhythmic rasping strokes; checking counterweight catapult ropes; whispering prayers to Kiri-Jolith.
- **Relationships:** Fractured command; Derek Crownguard’s fanatical Rose Knights sneer at Sturm Brightblade’s stoic Crown guards; Laurana and Flint desperately organizing archery lines; common footmen looking to Sturm as their true captain.
- **Attitudes:** Grim, defiant resolve; the realization that no relief army is coming from Palanthas and that they represent the final barrier protecting the plains.
- **Values:** Chivalric duty, tactical precision, holding the parapet until the last breath.

### Architectural Breakdown & Tactical Zones

#### Zone 1: The Three Concentric Petal Walls
- **Design:** Arranged in an overlapping three-leaf clover (*petal*) configuration. Walls are 40 feet high, angled outward at 15 degrees to deflect catapult boulders.
- **Funnel Choke:** Breaching an outer gate forces the enemy into interior kill-zones flanked by arrow slits on three sides.

#### Zone 2: The Upper Battlements (Sturm's Stand)
- **Dimensions:** The narrow stone parapet crowning the central keep, 120 feet above the courtyard. Spanned by open stone arches without roofs.
- **The Historic Stand:** Here [[Sturm Brightblade]] stood alone against [[Kitiara Uth Matar]] and the blue wyrm [[Skie (Khellendros)]], sacrificing his life in single combat to buy time for Laurana to activate the subterranean Dragon Orb.

#### Zone 3: The Subterranean Dragon Orb Vault
- **Dimensions:** A lead-lined octagonal crypt deep in the mountain bedrock, accessible only through a hidden shaft behind the Clerist’s high altar (DC 18 Intelligence (Investigation)).
- **The Pedestal:** At its center stands a carved obsidian pillar holding the **Dragon Orb of the High Clerist's Tower**. Activating the orb requires a DC 16 Charisma saving throw to avoid psychic possession. When sounded, all chromatic dragons within 10 miles are seized by homicidal frenzy and compelled to dive directly into the tower’s courtyard.

#### Zone 4: The Three Curved Dragon-Funnels & The Kill Chamber
- **The Funnel Chutes:** Three smoothly polished, circular stone tunnels (25 feet in diameter) slope downward from the courtyard into the mountain roots.
- **The Aerodynamic Trap:** The tunnels are curved and narrow; a diving dragon cannot spread its wings to brake or turn around, and is forced down the smooth chute at terminal velocity.
- **The Lance-Kill Chamber:** At the base of the chutes lies the **Kill Chamber**. Reinforced iron stanchions bolted into the floor hold braced [[Footman's Dragonlances]] angled upward. As a dragon rockets out of the chute under uncontrollable momentum, it impales itself upon the lances, dying instantly under its own weight.

## 4. Strategic Regional Centers of Spring Dawning

### 4.1 Kalaman (The Northern Bastion)
- **Coordinates & Role:** Major port city on the Bay of Vingaard; operational headquarters of the [[Whitestone Army]]; staging ground for the liberation of Northern Ansalon.
- **5D Sensory Pentagon:**
  - *Physical Environment:* Cyclopean granite sea walls crusted with dried salt and barnacles; round bastions overlooking deep-water docks; muddy streets paved with river cobbles slick with melting spring slush; smell of woodsmoke, roasting mutton, damp horsehair, and pitch from the drydocks.
  - *Behaviors:* Squires sharpening longswords on whetstones; elven fletchers testing bowstrings under canvas awnings; dockworkers hauling grain into military warehouses; sentries scanning the gray horizon for draconic silhouettes.
  - *Relationships:* Friction between merchant guilds counting coin losses and stern Solamnic knights demanding emergency grain requisitions; elven refugees outside the east gate receiving wary glances from burghers.
  - *Attitudes:* War-weariness mixed with fragile hope inspired by [[Laurana]] (The Golden General); lingering dread that the Dragonarmies will deploy their dreaded Flying Citadels.
  - *Values:* Communal defense, civil order, the Measure’s hospitality code, economic recovery.

#### Tactical Zones of Kalaman
1. **The Harbor Bastion:** Fortified breakwater with twin ballista towers and heavy iron harbor chains designed to prevent aquatic ramming.
2. **The High Citadel of Caleb:** Thick limestone fortress housing the allied war council, tactical map rooms, and deep stone cellars converted into arms caches.
3. **The Castle Gatehouse:** Double portcullises with murder holes, overhead oil chutes, and an elevated archer gallery commanding the approach avenue.
4. **The Lower Refugium:** Sprawling tent encampments sheltering thousands of human, elven, and dwarven refugees fleeing the Khalkist advance.

---

### 4.2 Palanthas (The City of Seven Rings)
- **Coordinates & Role:** Ancient metropolis on the Bay of Branchala; seat of the Senate of Patricians and home to the [[Great Library of Palanthas]].
- **5D Sensory Pentagon:**
  - *Physical Environment:* Sweeping concentric semicircles of dazzling white marble palaces, polished silver domes, and terraced gardens; clean sea breezes carrying scents of blooming almond blossoms and salted plum wine; absolute absence of mud, filth, or siege scars—save for the circular dead-zone of midnight darkness at the city's heart: the [[Tower of High Sorcery of Palanthas]] and the rotting [[Shoikan Grove]].
  - *Behaviors:* Patricians strolling in silk robes trailing delicate perfumes; street musicians playing lutes in public plazas; scribes hurrying along shaded colonnades; kender tourists being politely escorted out of jeweler shops by silver-armored guards.
  - *Relationships:* Aristocrats treating the War of the Lance as a vulgar rumor; Senate patricians condescending to Solamnic knights; scholars of the Great Library maintaining total, indifferent silence toward civic politics.
  - *Attitudes:* Smug cultural elitism; deep-seated fear of magic; aesthetic perfectionism masking political cowardice.
  - *Values:* Architectural harmony, civic peace, aristocratic etiquette, encyclopedic preservation of history.

### 4.3 Dargaard Keep (The Withered Rose)
- **Coordinates & Role:** Solitary black mountain fortress perched on the northern spur of the Dargaard Mountains; ancestral stronghold of [[Lord Soth]]; cursed redoubt of skeletal warriors and elven banshees.
- **5D Sensory Pentagon:**
  - *Physical Environment:* Fire-blackened stone towers jutting into the moonlight like the petrified petals of a dead, rotted rose; jagged mountain winds howling through broken arrow slits; freezing chill that coats stone walls in frost; heavy stench of stale tomb-air, sulfur, dried blood, and burning grease from ancient dropped chandeliers.
  - *Behaviors:* Silent skeletal sentinels standing at attention on high parapets; shadowy elven banshees drifting across ceilings, their pale forms dissolving into mist; Lord Soth sitting immobile upon his granite throne for days without moving a gauntlet.
  - *Relationships:* Total, horrific subservience of the undead host to Soth's will; Kitiara walking among the ghosts with mocking swagger, drinking Soth's wine while concealing internal revulsion.
  - *Attitudes:* Timeless despair, melancholic torment, aristocratic pride cursed to endure beyond the grave.
  - *Values:* Knightly honor twisted into eternal damnation; punishment of unfaithfulness; the absolute preservation of a knight's word.

#### Tactical Zones of Dargaard Keep
1. **The Moat of Cinders:** Deep dry chasm bridged by a single narrow arch of vitrified black stone, commanded by ballista emplacements crewed by skeletal warriors.
2. **The Shattered Entry Hall:** Massive vaulted foyer where the great Cataclysmic chandelier fell, crushing Soth's wife and infant child; the flagstones remain forever scorched by unholy fire.
3. **The Banqueting Hall of the Damned:** Long oak table set with corroded silver plate and rotted food that never decays further; 13 elven banshees gather along the high minstrel gallery each midnight to sing the song of Soth's betrayal.
4. **The High Watch of Soth:** Solitary balcony overlooking the mountain pass where Soth keeps vigil, gazing toward Neraka and the rising dragon flights.

### 5.1 Sunken Istar (The Sea-Floor Capital)
- **Depth & Hazard:** 800 feet below the surface of the [[Blood Sea of Istar]]; zero ambient light; crushing oceanic pressure; crimson water thick with red silt churned by the Maelstrom.
- **Atmosphere & Magic:** The Dimernesti maintain magical air-domes over key palaces using ancient water-breathing enchantments and light-emitting bioluminescent coral globes.
- **5D Sensory Pentagon:**
  - *Physical Environment:* Crushing pelagic gloom tinted blood-red by perpetually suspended volcanic silt; cracked white-marble colonnades of the Kingpriest's drowned metropolis draped in giant swaying kelp; the sudden, startling transition through a shimmering membrane into Zebulah's warm, dry air-dome smelling of pipeleaf, old parchment, and spiced tea.
  - *Behaviors:* Dimernesti sea-elves gliding silently on giant seahorse mounts with bronze tridents poised; Zebulah fussing over terrestrial teacups while oblivious to the continental war above; Apoletta brewing the silver *Draught of Forgetting* in pearl vats.
  - *Relationships:* Protective, sorrowful custodianship; the Dimernesti shelter shipwrecked surface-dwellers only on the condition that their memories be wiped before release, while Zebulah clings to human companionship out of centuries of academic loneliness.
  - *Attitudes:* Melancholic detachment from the surface world; haunted reverence for the crater where the gods hurled down the fiery mountain.
  - *Values:* Secrecy of the sea-elf haven, preservation of pre-Cataclysm relics, and caution around the bleeding tectonic seam of the Foundation Stone.

### 5.2 Godshome (The Bowl of the Gods)
- **Location:** Hidden highland caldera in the Khalkist Mountains, accessible only through a razor-sharp mountain gorge.
- **5D Sensory Pentagon:**
  - *Physical Environment:* A perfect natural amphitheater of towering black basalt cliffs encircling a silent valley; air crisp, thin, and smelling of pure mountain ozone and crushed pine needles; complete absence of wind or bird calls; at the center sits a vast, circular pool of mirrored black obsidian, reflecting the heavens with supernatural clarity.
  - *Behaviors:* Pilgrims stepping forward in hushed awe; whispers echoing like silver bells off the basalt walls; the dying feeling a sudden cessation of physical pain.
  - *Relationships:* Mortals standing in the direct presence of the Divine; Paladine shedding his bumbling mortal disguise as Fizban to tenderly comfort his faithful companion [[Flint Fireforge]].
  - *Attitudes:* Sacred serenity, redemption, acceptance of mortality, transcendent peace.
  - *Values:* Faith, spiritual humility, divine justice, the eternal continuity of the soul.

#### Tactical Points of Interest
1. **The Golden Span:** A natural rock arch spanning the canyon entrance; metallic dragons land here to deposit pilgrims before returning to the skies.
2. **The Circle of Basalt Monoliths:** 21 towering stone pillars ringing the obsidian pool, each carved with the sacred constellation of one of the true gods of Krynn.
3. **The Mirror of Eternity:** The central black rock pool. Its surface is perfectly smooth and solid; characters of pure heart can walk upon its surface as if it were polished glass. Walking to the center allows one to step directly into the celestial halls of Paladine and Reorx.

### 5.3 The Flying Citadel (Aerial Bastion)
- **Nature:** A colossal granite castle torn from its earthly foundations in the mountains around Sanction, floating upon enchanted storm clouds driven by dark elemental sorcery.
- **Propulsion & Mechanics:** Powered by a subterranean heartstone bound to a captured earth elemental and maintained by black-robed mages in the lower steering vault.
- **5D Sensory Pentagon:**
  - *Physical Environment:* Jagged shelves of torn mountain bedrock jutting downward into empty sky, shedding loose gravel and roots into the clouds; freezing high-altitude gales howling across crenellated parapets; a sickening low-frequency magical hum vibrating through the soles of boots from the glowing green levitation orb in the steering vault.
  - *Behaviors:* Black-robed mages chanting in exhausted four-hour shifts around the Arcane Steering Helm; red dragons dropping onto the upper flight deck with bone-jarring impacts that make the entire floating island list five degrees; draconians tossing garbage and executed prisoners from the underside iron gibbets.
  - *Relationships:** Brittle operational dependence between arrogant Black Robe navigators and hot-tempered Dragonarmy flight commanders who resent relying on wizards to keep their fortress airborne.
  - *Attitudes:** Godlike aerial invulnerability; terrifying certainty that no terrestrial wall or moat can stop a castle that drops from the stratosphere.
  - *Values:* Unbroken concentration at the steering orb, clear signal flares through the storm shroud, and total obedience to the Highlord's flight plan.

## 6. The Temple of Darkness at Neraka (5-Level Climax Megadungeon)

The **Temple of Darkness** is the corrupted heart of the Dragon Highlord empire, erected within the volcanic caldera of [[Neraka]]. Constructed from black volcanic glass, dark granite, and crimson quartz veins, the cathedral features five soaring spires twisted into the shape of a five-headed dragon.

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: The Temple of Darkness at Neraka
- **Physical Environment:** Glinting, razor-edged walls of black volcanic obsidian veined with pulsing blood-red quartz; skies choked with sulfurous yellow ash that never lets true daylight reach the valley floor; the overwhelming stench of three thousand sweating mercenaries, dragon dung, burning black tallow, and metallic blood inside the cavernous Hall of the Highlords.
- **Behaviors:** Dragon Highlords of the Five Wings parading onto their elevated dais platforms in clashing colors while their bodyguards finger sword-hilts; black-robed wizards and dark clerics bowing toward the Shimmering Planar Curtain as the five chromatic heads of Takhisis press against the veil; Gakhan's torturers working in the volcanic basalt dungeons above the forgotten Foundation Stone.
- **Relationships:** Predatory feudal cannibalism held together only by terror of Emperor Ariakas's Five-Fold Shield and the imminent physical manifestation of the Dark Queen; every Highlord (Kitiara, Salah-Khan, Lucien, Toede) actively plotting to assassinate the person sitting one tier above them.
- **Attitudes:** Apocalyptic arrogance at the moment of total victory—blind to the truth that evil inevitably turns upon itself when a single pebble (Berem the Everman) jams the cosmic wheel.
- **Values:** The Law of the Crown of Power (*"Whoever wears the crown, rules"*), survival through betrayal, and tribute to the Five-Headed Dragon.

### Tabletop Infiltration & Combat Dynamics

- **Level 2, Council Amphitheater:** Tiered seating is packed with Dragonarmy commanders. Initiating combat draws 4d6 Baaz warriors per round unless a riot breaks out (triggered when Ariakas falls and Highlords battle over the [[Crown of Power]]).
- **Level 3, The Shield of Ariakas:** Ariakas is protected by the *Five-Fold Prismatic Shield of Takhisis* (immune to all damage and spells until Raistlin Majere subtly nullifies the ward from the shadows).
- **Level 4, The Cocoon of Night:** Freeing Laurana requires casting *Dispel Magic* (DC 18) or severing the strands with an artifact blade (*Brightblade* or *Dragonlance*). Touching the strands inflicts 3d8 necrotic damage and forces a DC 16 Constitution saving throw against paralysis.
- **Level 5, The Foundation Stone Chamber:** Dimensional turbulence fluctuates gravity (DC 14 Strength (Athletics) to move toward the column). When [[Berem the Everman]] hurls himself into the emerald socket, the stone seals the gate, triggering a catastrophic 6d10 radiant explosion that consumes all chromatic dragons and causes the five spires of the temple to collapse into the abyss.
