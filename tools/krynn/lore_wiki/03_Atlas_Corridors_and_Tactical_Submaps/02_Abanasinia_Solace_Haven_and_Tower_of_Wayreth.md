# 02: Abanasinia, Solace, Haven and Tower of Wayreth
*Tactical Dungeon Submaps, Architectural Profiles & The Testing Crucibles of High Sorcery*

---

## 1. Regional Overview & Historical Horizon

The region of **Abanasinia** serves as the cradle of the War of the Lance and the proving ground of its most tragic champions. Nestled between the northern shores of the [[New Sea]] and the jagged wall of the [[Kharolis Mountains]], Abanasinia spans rolling cultivated plains, ancient forests of titanic [[Vallenwood Trees]], and the desolate swamps around the sunken ruins of [[Xak Tsaroth]].

This dossier tracks the spatial progression of the region across two defining eras:
1. **The Crucible of Youth (326–346 AC):** As depicted in *The Soulforge*, following the childhood of [[Raistlin Majere]] and [[Caramon Majere]] in [[Solace]], their education under Master Theobald, their exposure to the corrupt Seeker cult of Belzor in [[City of Haven|Haven]], and Raistlin’s fateful Test within the shifting perimeter of the [[Forest of Wayreth]].
2. **The Autumn Invasion (351 AC):** As depicted in *Dragons of Autumn Twilight*, when the [[Red Wing Dragonarmy]] under [[Highlord Verminaard]] sweeps across Abanasinia, burning the plains of [[Que-Shu]] and enslaving the treetop populace of Solace.

## 2. Macro Locations & 5D Sensory Pentagons

### 2.1 The Free Settlement of Solace & The Inn of the Last Home
- **Macro Coordinates:** Centered around the deep alpine waters of [[Crystalmir Lake]], perched within the canopy of colossal [[Vallenwood Trees]] forty to sixty feet above the forest floor.
- **Ecological Architecture (The Living Wood):** Following the Cataclysm, refugees fled predatory goblin bands and roving highwaymen by building upward into the limbs of the ancient vallenwoods. Structures are engineered with flexible wicker suspension systems, notched interlocking timbers, and slotted joints that expand and flex with the tree’s growth without driving metal iron spikes into the living heartwood.
- **Key Sub-Sites:**
  - *[[The Inn of the Last Home]]:* Built into the crook of a massive vallenwood forty feet above the road. Centered around a polished living-wood counter, leaded green-tinted windowpanes overlooking the lake, and the kitchen hearth where [[Otik Sandath]] prepares his famous spiced fried potatoes.
  - *The Village Commons & Aerial Boardwalks:* Sinuous suspension bridges made of woven hemp rope and split-cedar planks linking treetop dwellings. Pulley lifts and counterweighted baskets haul cargo, firewood, and passengers from ground level.
  - *The Majere Family Ground-Hovel:* A damp, two-room timber cabin nestled in the root-shadows below the canopy, perpetually smelling of dry rot, sour fever tisanes, and wood shavings where Raistlin, Caramon, and their mother [[Rosamun Majere]] lived in poverty.
  - *Sorrowgrave (Solace Cemetery):* A shaded hollow east of the town where mossy burial cairns rest beneath planted vallenwood saplings.

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: Solace
- **Physical Environment:** Rough-barked vallenwood trunks six arm-spans in girth; swaying rope bridges creaking rhythmically in the autumn wind; dapple of amber sunlight through bronze leaves; the scent of woodsmoke, roasting ham broth, and cold lake water.
- **Behaviors:** Townspeople hauling baskets of dried squash up pulley ropes before the sundown horn; housewives shaking homespun wool blankets over balustrades; patrons descending rope ladders with ale-wobbly footing under the watchful eye of bouncers.
- **Relationships:** Tight-knit refugee kinship tempered by polite distance from anyone displaying madness, witch-signs, or sorcery; strained hospitality toward haughty Seeker tax collectors from Haven.
- **Attitudes:** Pragmatic, live-and-let-live frontier independence; deep weariness of religious fanaticism and knightly proclamations.
- **Values:** Honest manual craftsmanship, hot food, cold dwarf ale, and staying out of the affairs of high lords and wizards.

---

### 2.2 Master Theobald's School for Mages
- **Macro Coordinates:** Five miles west of Solace, hidden within a dense, lightless copse of tangled black scrub-oak.
- **Architecture & Layout:** A squat, windowless single-story bunker built of roughly dressed limestone blocks. Heavy iron-reinforced oak doors. Heated by a choked stone hearth that fills the windowless classroom with stagnant, nauseating woodsmoke.
- **Sub-Sites:**
  - *The Common Classroom:* Rows of splintered wooden benches where apprentices sit scratching slates with chalk; Master Theobald's elevated lectern holding chained, locked beginner primers.
  - *The Master's Scriptorium:* Private inner study behind a heavy wool curtain where Theobald keeps his locked mahogany desk, spellbooks, and disciplinary birch switches.
  - *The Apprentice Dormitory:* Cold earthen-floored annex holding damp straw pallets, drafty stone walls, and scurrying cellar mice.

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: Theobald's Academy
- **Physical Environment:** Stifling, eye-watering woodsmoke hanging in stagnant strata; the acrid tang of cheap tallow grease, animal glue, and wet slate; chalk dust coating skin, clothes, and tongues.
- **Behaviors:** Master Theobald pacing the stone floor with a heavy birch rod, striking the palms of boys who mispronounce archaic Magius syllables; wealthy students like [[Liam]] flicking ink pellets at Raistlin’s patched tunic; desperate, silent memorization under timed hourglass drills.
- **Relationships:** Institutional cruelty and academic bullying; the mediocre master resenting the destitute, sickly boy whose intellect outstrips his own.
- **Attitudes:** Petty pedagogical tyranny; reverence for rote repetition over creative magical comprehension.
- **Values:** Strict compliance, unquestioning submission to authority, and timely payment of tuition in gold coins.

---

### 2.3 The City of Haven
- **Macro Coordinates:** Western plains of Abanasinia; the primary commercial metropolis and spiritual seat of the [[Seeker Theocracy of Haven|Seeker Theocracy]], encircled by crumbling sandstone curtain walls.
- **Key Sub-Sites:**
  - *The Bargate & Grand Bazaar:* Teeming maze of canvas canopies, livestock pens, peddler wagons, and cutpurses.
  - *The Daft Gnome Alehouse:* Low-ceilinged tavern favored by mountain dwarves, tinkers, and sellswords; reeking of stale spirits, pipeweed, and mutton tallow.
  - *The Seeker High Quarter:* Paved plazas, algae-choked marble fountains, and walled limestone estates belonging to the governing aldermen and high priests.
  - *The Haven Lockup & City Barn:* Former timber stables converted into a drafty municipal prison; dirt floors slick with horse manure, vomit, and infested with bold sewer rats.

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: Haven
- **Physical Environment:** Blinding, dusty sunlight reflecting off whitewashed sandstone facades; road grit grinding between teeth; open sewage gutters and tanning vats mingling with sweet temple incense.
- **Behaviors:** Sky-blue-robed Seeker acolytes shouting apocalyptic street sermons while shaking brass collection bowls; kender picking cell padlocks while guards doze over dice cups; merchants aggressively haggling over shaved copper bits.
- **Relationships:** Transactional, paranoid, and volatile; common citizens glance over shoulders for church informants; city constables openly solicit bribes.
- **Attitudes:** Cynical opportunism draped in theatrical righteousness; swiftness to ignite into bloodthirsty mob violence against accused "witches" or heretics.
- **Values:** Civic ostentation, material steel wealth, public religious conformity, and the theatrical illusion of divine favor.

### 2.4 The Forest of Wayreth (The Living Hedge)
- **Macro Coordinates:** Surrounding the [[Tower of High Sorcery of Wayreth]] across the southwestern frontier of Abanasinia; a shifting, sentient topological labyrinth.
- **Non-Euclidean Spatial Mechanics:**
  - The forest exists partially out of phase with the physical geometry of Krynn.
  - Magnetic and magical compass needles spin erratically.
  - Paths loop back upon themselves; distances expand or contract based on the traveler's moral resolve.
  - Living oak and black-thorn trees physically glide across mossy loam in dense fog to bar retreat.
  - *Access Protocol:* The forest only permits passage to those summoned by the Conclave of Wizards or those carrying an Archmage's token (such as [[Antimodes]]'s white stone or [[Par-Salian]]'s invitation). Uninvited intruders walk in circles for weeks before emerging starving at the perimeter where they first entered.

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: Forest of Wayreth
- **Physical Environment:** Muted, unearthly silver twilight that never shifts between dawn and dusk; dense, chilling mist clinging to boots and knees; utter absence of wind, bird calls, or insect clicks; the muffled crunch of dead moss underfoot.
- **Behaviors:** Travelers raising drawn swords at phantom silhouettes only to strike solid bark; companions walking side-by-side suddenly finding themselves separated by dozens of yards of impenetrable briars.
- **Relationships:** The sentient forest acts as an active psychic extension of the Conclave, probing the subconscious mind of travelers and manifesting their worst nightmares.
- **Attitudes:** Ancient, predatory indifference; a sacred testing barrier designed to winnow out the weak-willed and the unworthy.
- **Values:** Arcane secrecy, moral resolve, and absolute defense of the mysteries of High Sorcery.

## 3. Tactical Submap: The Temple of Belzor & Mechanical Crypts (Haven)

The **Temple of Belzor** represents the apex of the Seeker fraud. Erected atop an ancient hillside in Haven, the temple houses a sophisticated mechanical theater engineered to simulate the living avatar of a giant subterranean spider-god.

### Room-by-Room Key

#### Room 1: The Grand Rotunda (Public Amphitheater)
- **Dimensions:** 80-foot circular auditorium with concentric tiers of sandstone benches accommodating 400 worshippers.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Dim, cavernous gloom illuminated only by four tall iron braziers burning sweet, opiated incense (*Dream-Lotus* gum; creatures remaining longer than 3 rounds must make a DC 11 Constitution save or suffer disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks).
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The rotunda floor is packed with kneeling, weeping citizens during services. Movement across the floor is treated as **difficult terrain**. The acoustic design magnifies whispers: any shouted combat or spellcasting alerts the complex instantly.

#### Room 2: The Blue Robe Processional Aisles
- **Features:** Twin vaulted hallways flanking the rotunda, curtained with indigo silk tapestries embroidered with golden spiders crushing wizards.
- **Guard Details:** 6 Seeker Temple Guards armed with lead-weighted truncheons (1d6+2 bludgeoning) and heavy crossbows. Their standing order is to violently pacify anyone attempting to approach the raised altar dais.

#### Room 3: The Flaming Altar of Belzor
- **Dimensions:** Raised black marble platform 15 feet above the rotunda floor, reached by steep white limestone stairs.
- **The Altar:** A massive slab of polished obsidian carved with intricate web motifs.
- **The Mechanical Trapdoor:** A concealed 8-foot-wide trapdoor sits directly behind the altar (DC 16 Wisdom (Perception) or DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) to detect the seams). A counterweighted gear winch allows the iron-and-papier-mâché spider automaton of Belzor to rise dramatically through clouds of theatrical sulfur smoke.
- **The Flamethrower Flues:** Two concealed copper nozzle pipes flank the altar. Operated via floor treadles in Room 4, they spray pressurized liquid naphtha across flint strikers, generating 20-foot gouts of blinding orange fire (3d6 fire damage, DC 13 Dexterity save for half).

#### Room 4: The Lower Crypt & Mechanical Gantry
- **Dimensions:** 40x50 foot damp stone cellar located directly beneath the raised altar.
- **Atmosphere:** Blistering heat radiating from a coke-fired iron boiler; pervasive stench of whale oil, valve lubricant, and unburned sulfur; the clanking rhythm of counterweight pulleys and hiss of escaping steam.
- **Tactical Hazards:**
  - *Slick Grease Flagstones:* Floors near the gantry are coated in machine grease. Moving more than half speed requires a DC 13 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check or the character slips and falls prone.
  - *High-Pressure Steam Pipes:* Striking or rupturing any copper pipe (AC 14, 15 HP) releases a 15-foot cone of scalding steam dealing 3d6 fire damage.

#### Room 5: The Bellows & Lens Chamber (The Acoustic Engine)
- **Dimensions:** 25x30 foot low-ceilinged vault.
- **The Voice of Belzor:** Houses a monstrous double-chambered leather bellows pumped by four sweating acolyte slaves. The bellows force air through resonant bronze horn pipes that terminate beneath the rotunda floor, producing the bone-rattling, subterranean rumble interpreted by the congregation as the voice of the spider-god.
- **Optical Projectors:** Polished concave brass parabolic reflectors direct lantern light through red stained-glass apertures in the ceiling, casting the illusion of two glowing demonic eyes hovering above the altar.

#### Room 6: Judith's Private Scriptorium
- **Dimensions:** 20x20 foot vaulted chamber appointed with plush eastern carpets, cedar-lined chests, and silk divans.
- **Loot & Forensic Evidence:**
  - Ledgers documenting extortionate church tithes and bribe payments to Haven aldermen.
  - Apothecary bottles containing belladonna, sleeping drafts, and *Spider-Bile* poisons.
  - Stolen silver chalices and pre-Cataclysm church plate melted down into crude bullion.
- **Historical Murder Scene:** The site where [[Kitiara Uth Matar]] ambushes [[Judith]], slitting her throat from behind and leaving her braided bone-handled hunting knife embedded in the bloodstained desk.

#### Room 7: The Mountain Escape Flue
- **Dimensions:** A rough-hewn, unmapped crawlway chiseled into the natural limestone bedrock behind the lower crypt.
- **Navigation:** Slopes downward for 600 yards before terminating in a heavily concealed brush-covered fissure half a mile into the rocky foothills behind Haven.

## 4. Tactical Submap: The Tower of Wayreth & The Chambers of the Test

The **Tower of High Sorcery at Wayreth** is one of only two towers surviving the destruction of the Kingpriest's inquisitions. Constructed from two spires—one of white translucent alabaster, the other of pitch-black basalt—that merge into a central gray stone citadel, the Tower exists at the intersection of time, space, and planar magic.

Here, every mage of Krynn who advances past the rank of apprentice must undergo the **Test of High Sorcery**: a lethal, customized crucible designed to judge skill, willpower, resource management, and moral alignment.

### Level-by-Level & Room-by-Room Key

#### 5D Sensory Pentagon: The Tower of Wayreth & Testing Chambers
- **Physical Environment:** Absolute acoustic stillness inside Seamless black obsidian and milk-white alabaster spires; the subtle high-voltage vibration of raw leyline magic causing tooth enamel to buzz; faint aromas of dried mountain lavender and burning frankincense in the upper Conclave dome giving way to choking sulfur, marsh methane, and copper blood-mist in the subterranean testing chambers below.
- **Behaviors:** Archmages of the Three Orders speaking in measured, formal cadences over the liquid-mercury Scrying Pool while watching candidates bleed in the labyrinth below; apprentices trembling through 24-hour fasts before stepping through the unsealable Rune-Iron Gate.
- **Relationships:** Transcendent guild solidarity across Good (White), Neutral (Red), and Evil (Black) robes—united in ruthless defense of the Magic itself, even when sacrificing favorite pupils on the altar of the Test.
- **Attitudes:** Clinical, unsentimental stewardship; the conviction that an untested wizard is a rabid beast who must either master their ambition or die in the crucible.
- **Values:** Sovereignty of the Three Moons, survival of the arcane craft, and absolute submission to the Conclave's judgment.

#### Level 1: Upper Chamber of the Three Robes (The Conclave)
- **Dimensions:** 100-foot-diameter circular domed hall crowning the central gray citadel where the black and white spires merge.
- **Thrones of the Conclave:**
  - *White Throne:* Carved of flawless milk-white alabaster; seat of [[Par-Salian]], Head of the Conclave.
  - *Red Throne:* Polished reddish-amber rosewood; seat of [[Justarius]].
  - *Black Throne:* Raw, porous black basalt; seat of [[Ladonna]].
- **The Scrying Pool of Wayreth:** A shallow, lip-less basin of liquid mercury ten paces across at the room’s center. Reflects scenes across Ansalon and projects the live astral perspective of the candidate navigating the subterranean Test below.
- **Sensory Reality:** Absolute acoustic stillness; the subtle high-voltage vibration of raw leyline magic causing tooth enamel to buzz; faint aromas of dried mountain lavender and burning frankincense.

#### Level 2: The Aspirant’s Waiting Vault & The Rune-Iron Gate
- **Dimensions:** Octagonal chamber of bare gray granite. Furnished with a straw mattress on a wooden frame, a clay washbasin, and a rough oak writing desk. No windows.
- **The Rite of Solitude:** The candidate spends their final 24 hours here fasting, meditating, and preparing spell components.
- **The Rune-Iron Gate:** A massive portal of black iron inlaid with silver runes of the Language of Magius:
  > *"He Who Enters Here Yields Up His Life to the Magic."*
- **Locking Invariant:** Once the gate swings shut behind an aspirant, it cannot be unsealed from within by any mortal or magical force. The candidate emerges either through the recovery sanctum as an initiated wizard of the Robes, or as a corpse transported to the Tower crypts.

#### Chamber 1: The Maze of Blind Steps (The Exhaustion Trial)
- **Environment:** Complete, lightless labyrinth of uneven flagstones, razor-edged shale drops, and 5-foot-wide stone ledges spanning fathomless chasms.
- **Arcane Light Suppression:** Standard magical illumination (*Light*, *Dancing Lights*) cast here burns with a sickly, dying purple flame that reveals only a 5-foot radius.
- **Tactical Dynamics:**
  - Shifting localized gravity wells force DC 14 Constitution saving throws to maintain balance and avoid nausea.
  - Auditory phantasms (footsteps, weeping children, mocking laughter) require DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) checks to ignore; failing causes the candidate to reflexively waste a spell slot on empty shadows.

#### Chamber 2: Hall of the Dark Elf Ambush (The Tactical Combat Trial)
- **Dimensions:** 60x80 foot pillared hall evoking the ruined courtyard of an ancient pre-Cataclysm elven manor. Fluted marble columns, cracked flagstones, and collapsed porticos provide half and three-quarters cover.
- **The Threat:** Three **Dark Elf Renegade Sorcerers**, one of whom manifests the sneering visage of Raistlin's childhood tormentor, [[Liam]].
- **Encounter Dynamics:**
  - Liam and his warlocks attack from elevated column bases using *Web*, *Magic Missile*, and *Fireball*.
  - *Tactical Imperative:* Forces the candidate to track spellcaster component casting, maintain counterspell positioning, and conserve high-level slots while taking fire under heavy cover.

#### Chamber 3: The Cistern of the Under-Goblins (The Resource Attrition Trial)
- **Environment:** Half-submerged subterranean cavern filled with knee-deep stagnant marsh water reeking of decaying reeds and sulfur. Slippery submerged stepping stones.
- **The Threat:** A pack of 12 **Under-Goblins** armed with barbed bone spears and weighted throwing nets, commanded by a goblin witch-doctor using minor hexes and blinding powders.
- **Environmental Hazard (Marsh Methane):** The stagnant water traps volatile pockets of swamp gas. Casting any spell producing open fire (*Burning Hands*, *Flaming Sphere*, *Fireball*) detonates the chamber’s atmosphere, dealing 3d6 fire damage to all creatures within a 20-foot radius (DC 13 Dexterity save for half). Hand-to-hand combat is equally lethal due to the candidate’s physical exhaustion.

#### Chamber 4: The Soulforge Threshold (Fistandantilus's Faustian Sanctum)
- **Dimensions:** A circular, windowless stone crypt holding a single scarred oak table illuminated by a solitary, flickering tallow candle.
- **Sensory Reality:** Bone-deep, supernatural cold that freezes breath into jagged ice crystals; the terrifying scent of thousand-year-old tomb dust, embalming spices, and dried myrrh.
- **The Encounter:**
  - The rotting, spectral shade of [[Fistandantilus]] sits waiting in black vestments, his skeletal fingers resting upon ancient leather-bound grimoires.
  - The upper trapdoor of the chamber is sealed with impervious eldritch wards. The candidate is physically broken, wounded, and depleted of spell slots.
  - *The Faustian Bargain:* Fistandantilus offers the candidate the ancient knowledge and arcane energy needed to survive the lethal trials ahead in exchange for a metaphysical anchor in the mortal’s living soul—a bond through which the ancient arch-mage intends to engineer his own rebirth.

#### Chamber 5: The Cavern of Dragonflame (The Crucible of Annihilation)
- **Dimensions:** Colossal underground magma vault spanned by a narrow 5-foot-wide natural stone bridge over a boiling lake of molten basalt.
- **The Encounter:** An immense Red Dragon wyrmling (an illusory construct engineered by Par-Salian possessing quasi-real fire breath).
- **The Climax of the Soulforge:**
  - The initiate's spell slots are completely exhausted. The dragon unleashes a lethal cone of roaring fire.
  - The candidate must choose between surrender, suicide, or tapping into the dark pact made in Chamber 4.
  - When Raistlin channels Fistandantilus's ancient power to shield himself, the feedback blast shatters his physical respiratory system, turning his flesh into metallic golden brass and searing his lungs with perpetual, bloody coughing spasms.

#### Chamber 6: The Hall of the Fratricide (The Mirror of the Soul)
- **Dimensions:** A long, vaulted marble corridor lined with polished silver mirrors and torch sconces.
- **The Illusion:**
  - A phantasm of [[Caramon Majere]] rushes down the hall to "rescue" the battered initiate.
  - When Fistandantilus’s spectral head rises from the floor to bar their path, the illusory Caramon produces a rod of amber and a rabbit pelt, rubs them together with a grin, and effortlessly casts twin lightning bolts that shatter the arch-mage's shade.
  - Caramon turns cheerfully to Raistlin: *"Don't worry, Raist. Magic can still be your little specialty."*
- **The Breaking Point:** Confronted with the unbearable psychological humiliation of his muscular, simple-minded brother effortlessly outshining him even in the sacred art of magic, Raistlin screams: *"I have no brother!"* and unleashes a lethal jet of magical fire, incinerating Caramon's phantasm into smoldering ash.

#### Level 3: The Alabaster Infirmary & Recovery Vault
- **Dimensions:** A serene, sunlit recovery rotunda high within the White Alabaster Spire of Wayreth. White silk draperies, aromatic lavender oils, and tall arched windows overlooking the silver and red moons.
- **The Physical Toll:** Here the candidate awakens to face the permanent physical price of the Test:
  - Raistlin's hair bleached snow-white.
  - Skin transformed into a polished brass sheen.
  - Lungs damaged, requiring perpetual herbal tisanes to stave off choking coughing fits.
  - Eyes cursed by Par-Salian with the golden hourglass pupils of Raelana—condemning Raistlin to perceive the constant, accelerated decay of all living things.
- **Presentation of the Staff:** Par-Salian presents the initiate with their official Robes of the Order and the legendary [[Staff of Magius]], along with the herbal recipe for red tea.
