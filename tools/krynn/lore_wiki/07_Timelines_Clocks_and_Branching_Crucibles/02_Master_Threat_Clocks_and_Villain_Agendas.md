## 1. Executive Summary: The Threat Clock Engine

In traditional static campaigns, villain forces wait passively in dungeons for player characters to arrive. On Krynn during the War of the Lance, **the villains operate on aggressive, proactive operational agendas**. 

Threat Clocks serve three vital campaign functions:
1. **Dynamic World State:** Time is a precious resource. Every Long Rest, detour, or failed stealth check directly advances enemy preparations.
2. **Telegraphed Danger:** Players can observe clock ticks advancing through environmental changes, refugee reports, and military scout dispatches.
3. **Severance States & Counter-Play:** Every clock provides clear mechanical conditions under which players can freeze, delay, or permanently break the enemy's progress.

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  THE THREE THREAT CLOCK TIERS                                      |
+--------------------+-------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
| Clock Tier         | Duration & Scale        | Operational Theater & Scope                         |
+--------------------+-------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
| 4-TICK TACTICAL    | Minutes to Hours        | In-Dungeon Alerts, Ambushes, Ritual Castings, Scapes |
| 6-TICK OPERATIONAL | Days to Weeks           | Regional Sieges, Troop Movements, City Betrayals    |
| 8-TICK STRATEGIC   | Weeks to Months         | Continental Conquest, Planar Ingress, Dragon Oaths  |
+--------------------+-------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
```

## 2. Strategic 8-Tick Macro Clocks (Continental War & Planar Ingress)

---

### Macro Clock 1: Takhisis's Planar Ingress (The Neraka Portal)
*Description:* The Dark Queen’s five chromatic heads push through the shattered planar barrier in the subterranean vaults beneath the Temple of Neraka.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |  [================> PORTAL FULLY OPEN]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** The foundation altar of Istar is consecrated in Neraka; dark clerics sacrifice 100 captives.
- **Tick 2:** Berem the Everman is located in the northern provinces; hunter squads mobilized.
- **Tick 3:** First chromatic dragon head (Black) materializes in the temple smoke.
- **Tick 4:** Red and Blue heads breach the planar boundary; volcanic earthquakes shake the Khalkists.
- **Tick 5:** Green head manifests; psychic terror waves radiate across 500 leagues, causing mass insanity.
- **Tick 6:** White head breaches; the sky over Neraka turns into a perpetual black vortex.
- **Tick 7:** All five heads materialize in the mortal sky; Emperor Ariakas receives the direct avatar blessing.
- **Tick 8 (CLIMAX):** Takhisis steps bodily onto Krynn. The material plane falls under eternal night; Good gods permanently banished from mortal interaction.
- **Advancement Triggers:** Highlord council victories; execution of major allied heroes; passage of 30 calendar days without allied disruption.
- **Severance Intervention:** Berem impales himself into the empty foundation socket; Jasla’s spirit seals the gate; death of Emperor Ariakas.

---

### Macro Clock 2: The Five Dragonarmies: Total Conquest of Ansalon
*Description:* The coordinated sweep of the Red, Blue, Green, Black, and White Dragonarmies across Ansalon.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |  [================> ANSALON FULLY ENSLAVED]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Eastern Ansalon (Nordmaar, Estwilde, Balifor) annexed under Dragon Highlord control.
- **Tick 2:** Red Wing crosses the Plains of Abanasinia; Solace, Gateway, and Que-Shu burned.
- **Tick 3:** Fall of Pax Tharkas and occupation of the southern mountain passes.
- **Tick 4:** Blue Wing naval air raid destroys Tarsis; Southern plains annexed.
- **Tick 5:** Silvanesti forest corrupted by Cyan Bloodbane; Elven resistance shattered.
- **Tick 6:** Kalaman falls to Kitiara’s Blue Wing; northern Solamnia encircled.
- **Tick 7:** Solamnic Knighthood fractured at the High Clerist’s Tower; Palanthas besieged.
- **Tick 8 (CLIMAX):** Sancrist, Thorbardin, and Ergoth fall; all civilized races forced into slave mines.
- **Advancement Triggers:** Fall of regional fortresses; party failure in diplomatic parleys.
- **Severance Intervention:** Liberation of Pax Tharkas; destruction of the Blue Wing at High Clerist's Tower; mobilization of the Good Dragons.

### Macro Clock 3: The Draconian Bio-Corruption Swarm
*Description:* The industrial egg-corruption production line beneath the Sanction volcano converting stolen metallic dragon eggs into draconian infantry.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |  [================> DRACONIAN SWARM DEPLOYED]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** First brass eggs corrupted in Temple of Luerkhisis; Baaz infantry cohort enters service.
- **Tick 2:** Copper eggs corrupted; Kapak assassin squads deployed for covert infiltration.
- **Tick 3:** Bronze eggs corrupted; Bozak spell-officers assume battlefield tactical command.
- **Tick 4:** Silver eggs corrupted; Sivak shape-shifters infiltrate human royal courts.
- **Tick 5:** Gold eggs corrupted; Aurak archmages take command of regional army wings.
- **Tick 6:** Sanction production doubles; secondary vats established in Pax Tharkas and Neraka.
- **Tick 7:** First 50,000-man all-draconian legion deploys to the northern front.
- **Tick 8 (CLIMAX):** The next generation of Good Metallic Dragons is completely eradicated; draconians replace mortal levies as the permanent dominant race.
- **Advancement Triggers:** Capture of additional metallic nests; failure to sabotage Sanction vats.
- **Severance Intervention:** Sabotage of Temple of Luerkhisis vats; Good Dragons discover truth.

## 3. Operational 6-Tick Theater Clocks (Regional Campaigns)

---

### Operational Clock 1: Verminaard’s Iron Grasp (Abanasinia & Pax Tharkas)
*Description:* Highlord Verminaard consolidates the Red Wing across the Haven-Solace corridor and operationalizes the iron mines of Pax Tharkas.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |  [===========> ABANASINIA PURGED & FORTIFIED]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Seekers of Haven sign formal capitulation; Toede’s goblin cavalry patrols roads.
- **Tick 2:** Que-Shu and Plains tribes rounded up; resistance fighters publicly crucified in Solace.
- **Tick 3:** 800 civilian refugees transported in iron cages to Pax Tharkas lower mines.
- **Tick 4:** High-grade iron ore mined and smelted into Dragonarmy weapons; blast furnaces online.
- **Tick 5:** Red dragon Ember begins aerial patrols scorching any mountain passes leading to Qualinesti.
- **Tick 6 (CONSEQUENCE):** Pax Tharkas becomes an impregnable forward fortress; all human resistance in Abanasinia exterminated.
- **Severance Intervention:** Infiltrating through Sla-Mori; instigating slave revolt; slaying Verminaard.

---

### Operational Clock 2: Feal-Thas’s Glacial Expansion (Southern Ice Reaches)
*Description:* Highlord Feal-Thas weaponizes the Icewall Dragon Orb and mobilizes the Thanoi (Walrus-men) to conquer Southern Ergoth.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |  [===========> SOUTHERN STRAITS FROZEN & SEALED]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** White dragon Sleet sinks the southern elven refugee flotilla.
- **Tick 2:** Icewall Castle defenses reinforced; winter wolf packs hunt the Ice Folk.
- **Tick 3:** Feal-Thas attunes to the Icewall Dragon Orb, mastering telepathic summons.
- **Tick 4:** Glacial freezing spell cast, locking the southern straits into impassable ice sheets.
- **Tick 5:** Thanoi strike legions land on Southern Ergoth, cutting off Kagonesti supply routes.
- **Tick 6 (CONSEQUENCE):** Southern Ergoth falls; Tomb of Huma sealed; dragonlance forging prevented.
- **Severance Intervention:** Slaying Feal-Thas; recovering the Icewall Dragon Orb.

---

### Operational Clock 3: Cyan Bloodbane’s Dream-Trap (Silvanesti Forest)
*Description:* The green dragon Cyan Bloodbane warps King Lorac’s mind through the Silvanesti Dragon Orb, transforming the ancient elven forest into a rotting nightmare.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |  [===========> SILVANESTI PERMANENTLY ACCURSED]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** King Lorac looks into the Orb; the psychic feedback loop snaps his waking mind.
- **Tick 2:** The river Thon-Thalas turns to foul gray slime; trees begin weeping warm blood.
- **Tick 3:** Spectral Minions and undead elven warriors awaken, slaughtering retreating elves.
- **Tick 4:** The Tower of the Stars is cocooned in thorny, necrotic brambles.
- **Tick 5:** Cyan Bloodbane takes permanent physical residence atop the royal palace.
- **Tick 6 (CONSEQUENCE):** King Lorac dies; Silvanesti becomes an irrecoverable necrotic death-swamp.
- **Severance Intervention:** Entering Lorac's dream; striking Cyan Bloodbane with Wyrmslayer; removing the Orb from Lorac's hands.

### Operational Clock 4: Kitiara’s Northern Blitz (The Fall of Solamnia)
*Description:* Dragon Highlord Kitiara Uth Matar leads the Blue Wing in a lightning strike to seize Kalaman and Palanthas.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |  [===========> SOLAMNIA TOTALLY CRUSHED]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Derek Crownguard’s faction falls for Kitiara’s false retreat at the High Clerist's Tower.
- **Tick 2:** Derek leads his cavalry into the fatal valley ambush; Solamnic knights routed.
- **Tick 3:** Sturm Brightblade isolated on the tower parapets; Blue dragons assault the walls.
- **Tick 4:** High Clerist's Tower falls; Kitiara claims the mountain pass into Palanthas.
- **Tick 5:** First Flying Citadel arrives over Kalaman, dropping siege boulders on city gates.
- **Tick 6 (CONSEQUENCE):** Palanthas sacked; Solamnic Knighthood permanently extinguished.
- **Severance Intervention:** Activating the Dragon Orb in the Tower cellar; Sturm holding the wall; Laurana’s tactical leadership.

### Operational Clock 5: Lord Soth’s Undead March (The Harvest of Dargaard)
*Description:* Lord Soth rides from Dargaard Keep with his 13 banshees and skeletal cavalry to claim souls for his eternal court.

```
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |  [===========> NIGHTFALL MARCH OF DARGAARD]
+---+---+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Soth leaves the iron gates of Dargaard Keep at twilight on his fiery nightmare.
- **Tick 2:** Outlying villages along the Vingaard River fall silent; inhabitants found dead without a wound.
- **Tick 3:** Soth corners the Solamnic rear guard; casting *Power Word Kill* on commanders.
- **Tick 4:** Soth’s 13 banshees unleash their death wails over allied encampments.
- **Tick 5:** Soth’s skeletal retinue breaches the gates of Kalaman’s eastern citadel.
- **Tick 6 (CONSEQUENCE):** Laurana’s soul is claimed; she is transformed into a banshee queen.
- **Severance Intervention:** Presenting true Solamnic honor; appealing to Soth’s ancient knightly shame; banishing his banshees with consecrated holy ground.

## 4. Tactical 4-Tick Scene Clocks (Immediate Encounters & Dungeons)

---

### Tactical Clock 1: Toede’s Ambush Patrols (Solace Basin)
```
+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |  [====> FULL AMBUSH SPRUNG]
+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** A snapping twig or loud spell alerts the goblin scouts in the brush.
- **Tick 2:** A goblin horn sounds from the ridge; three Baaz draconians draw broadswords.
- **Tick 3:** Fewmaster Toede arrives on his shaggy pony with ten goblin archers nocking arrows.
- **Tick 4 (AMBUSH):** The party is surrounded, arrows fire with surprise, and net traps drop.
- **Advancement:** Disadvantage on Stealth checks; lingering in open terrain for more than 10 minutes.
- **Severance:** Slaying the horn-blower before he sounds the alert; setting an illusory false trail.

---

### Tactical Clock 2: Khisanth’s Slumber & Alarm (Sunken Xak Tsaroth)
```
+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |  [====> DRAGON BREATH STRIKE]
+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Water splashes loudly in the sunken temple; the black dragon's yellow eye snaps open.
- **Tick 2:** Khisanth uncoils from her treasure hoard; acid begins dripping from her black fangs.
- **Tick 3:** Khisanth slips into the murky water, swimming silently behind the party's position.
- **Tick 4 (ATTACK):** Acid breath weapon erupts from the darkness ($8d8$ acid damage, DC 16 Dex save).
- **Advancement:** Metal armor clanking in the swamp; carrying torches within 100 feet of the lair.
- **Severance:** Bupu guiding party through sewer chutes; casting *Silence* on the party's boots.

---

### Tactical Clock 3: High Clerist's Tower Dragon-Trap Loading
```
+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |  [====> TRAP PRIMED & ARMED]
+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Lower winch cables engaged; ground-level iron portcullises raised 15 feet.
- **Tick 2:** Mounted dragonlances locked into downward pivot slots along the stone murder holes.
- **Tick 3:** Dragon Orb in the cellar begins its telepathic humming, drawing dragons downward.
- **Tick 4 (FIRE!):** Blue dragons dive into the tunnel; portcullises drop, lances impale simultaneously.
- **Advancement:** Successful DC 14 Athletics checks by defenders working the winch wheels.
- **Severance:** Draconian saboteurs severing the counterweight chains; damage to the control winch.

---

### Tactical Clock 4: Berem the Everman’s Flight
```
+---+---+---+---+
| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |  [====> BEREM ESCAPES INTO NIGHT]
+---+---+---+---+
```
- **Tick 1:** Berem overhears someone mention Neraka or the Green Gemstone; his eyes widen in terror.
- **Tick 2:** Berem slips his bonds or steps away from the camp fire while sentries are distracted.
- **Tick 3:** Berem sprints into the rocky ravines of the Khalkist Mountains at double movement speed.
- **Tick 4 (LOST):** Berem is captured by a roving Sivak patrol or falls into an untraceable chasm.
- **Advancement:** Harsh interrogation checks; shouting; leaving him unguarded during a long rest.
- **Severance:** Caramon or Tanis speaking to him with genuine gentleness; mentioning his sister Jasla.
