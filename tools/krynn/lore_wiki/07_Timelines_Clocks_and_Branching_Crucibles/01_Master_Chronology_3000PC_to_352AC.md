## 1. Executive Summary: The Four Ages of Krynn

The historical trajectory of Ansalon is marked by civilizational peaks shattered by cataclysmic resets. Time on Krynn is divided into four cardinal epochs:
1. **The Age of Dreams (approx. 3000 PC – 1000 PC):** Creation by the High God, the release of the Graygem of Gargath, the First and Second Dragon Wars, the Third Dragon War, the forging of the first Dragonlances, and [[Huma Dragonbane]]'s sacrifice to banish Takhisis.
2. **The Age of Might (1000 PC – 1 PC):** The founding of the Solamnic Knighthood, the rise of the Empire of Ergoth, the ascendancy of the Holy Empire of Istar, the moral degeneration of the Kingpriest, and the Thirteen Omens.
3. **The Cataclysm (0 AC):** The 19th of Chislmont, 1 PC / New Year 0 AC: The Fiery Mountain strikes Istar, drowning the east, forming the Blood Sea and New Sea, and severing the mortal realm from the gods.
4. **The Age of Despair (1 AC – 352 AC):** Three centuries of famine, plague, banditry, and false Seeker cults, culminating in the *Raistlin Preludes* (326–347 AC) and the *War of the Lance* (351–352 AC).
