# MODULE CWT-051: OPAL ISLAND MODULE DECONSTRUCTION & SCENARIO PRIMITIVES

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Deconstructive Method
Opal Island represents a densely realized 48-module campaign setting built around an industrial-alchemical pointcrawl graph. While exceptionally rich, hardcoded proper nouns and specific geographical relationships created a barrier to utilizing the engine for external settings. This module defines the architectural deconstruction methodology: extracting the underlying dramatic bones, hazard equations, and supply chain algebra from Opal Island and cataloging them as universal "Scenario Primitives."

* **The Extraction Pipeline:** A 3-step abstraction process that lifts (1) the Egri dramatic syllogism, (2) the Truby 4-corner tension web, and (3) the 4-level hazard dial from proprietary setting lore into universal design tokens.
* **The Semantic Token Dictionary:** Converting setting-specific trade goods (e.g. *Opal Island Vitriol*) into system-level resource types (`Acid: High-Purity-Vitriol`, `Bio: Fungal-Toxin-Extract`).
* **The Universes Beyond Ingestion Seam:** Establishing standard import interfaces so outside IPs (fantasy, sci-fi, cyberpunk, gothic) can map directly onto the authoring engine's five tiers without rewriting underlying balance algorithms.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)
* **Establishment (The Canonical Campaign Purists):** Designers who value the dense, interconnected specificity of the original 48-module Opal Island ecosystem and resist generalizing its lore.
* **Main Opponent (The Strict Abstract Modularists):** Systems engineers who advocate for zero worldbuilding lore, demanding purely mathematical nodes, abstract resource tags, and clinical mechanics.
* **Thematic Shadow (The Premature Pruners):** Careless editors who strip rich dramatic texture and micro-tensions during abstraction, reducing vivid tabletop fiction to sterile spreadsheets.
* **Tertiary Outcast (The Universal Scenario Primitives):** Elegant, reusable narrative blueprints that belong to no single campaign setting, ready to be deployed across any universe.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies
