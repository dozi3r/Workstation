Visual Grammar & Graph Legend
Corridor & Conduit Links
2-Way Mutual Transit (Blue/Green solid line): Open highway or pass establishing bidirectional travel between nodes.
1-Way Directional Transit (Amber line with arrow): Downriver flume or one-way pass. Flow moves in destination direction.
Hegemonic Conduit (Purple dashed line): Ideological vassalage, tithe flow, or leyline tether projecting regional influence.
Selected Corridor (Rose thick line): Active corridor under inspection in the property grid.
Node Tiers & Status Rings
Primary Settlement (Standard POI): Major city, township, or fortified regional haven.
Frontier Outpost / Hazard (Diamond/Hazard): Outpost, extraction mine, or wilderness hazard location.
🌀 Nested Portal (Pulsing Ring): Node connected to a deeper sub-map pointcrawl. Double-click to drill down!

🏛️ Clan (Ivory): Bastion, communal covenant, collective defense, sacred traditions.
⚙️ Hierarchy (Blue): Syndicate, industrial dynamo, legal order, craft monopolies.
🗡️ Market (Black/Red): Corsair freehold, privateers, cutthroat trade, underworld brokers.
🌿 Adhocracy (Green): Adaptor commune, nomadic mobility, druidic and ecological resilience.
