🛡️ DIAL S: SOLIDARITY & DEVIANCE Active: Level 4

Measures how strictly internal orthodoxy is enforced versus personal liberty and dissent tolerance.

1: Panoptic Conformity ✓ ACTIVE

Zero deviance allowed. Inquisitorial thought-policing, ruthless purges, or literal hive minds. Dissenters are eradicated.

2: Structured Collectivism ✓ ACTIVE

Duty-bound tradition. Explicit social castes, oaths of fealty, and guild hierarchies. Group welfare over personal ambition.

3: Pluralistic Association ✓ ACTIVE

Tolerant civil society. Multiple competing guilds, philosophical debate, freedom of assembly, and civic negotiation.

4: Atomized Autonomy ✓ ACTIVE

Radical individualism. Sovereign persons or transient cells. No master, no dogma; bonds are informal and temporary.

🚪 DIAL E: BOUNDARY PERMEABILITY Active: Level 2

Measures openness to foreign travelers, immigrant labor, outside news, and cross-border commerce.

1: Fortress Isolationism ✓ ACTIVE

Quarantined perimeter. Xenophobic violence, sealed gates, lethal interdiction. Trespassers shot on sight.

2: Regulated Checkpoints ✓ ACTIVE

Passports, visas, bonded escorts, and crushing trade tariffs. Monitored toll-gates with exhaustive searches.

3: Commercial Open Port ✓ ACTIVE

Free trade haven. Open docks, merchant envoys, immigrant labor, and pragmatic diplomacy with foreign flags.

4: Boundary Dissolution ✓ ACTIVE

Wild porous frontier. Cosmopolitan mixing, unpoliced wilderness trails, rogue sanctuaries without border guards.

👑 DIAL C: CENTRALIZATION & COMMAND Active: Level 2

Measures hierarchical autocracy versus cellular decentralization and decapitation vulnerability.

1: Monolithic Autocracy ✓ ACTIVE

Singular absolute ruler (monarch, archon, god-computer). Orders are absolute; strike the head and the entire system freezes.

2: Oligarchic Directorate ✓ ACTIVE

Executive council, high magistrate tribunal, or merchant board. Bureaucratic stability with vulnerability to infighting.

3: Federal Guild Polyarchy ✓ ACTIVE

Distributed institutional power. Checks and balances between wardmasters, regional burghers, and senates.

4: Decapitation-Proof Mosaic ✓ ACTIVE

Autonomous cellular network. No central palace or command bunker; independent cells operate seamlessly via shared culture.
