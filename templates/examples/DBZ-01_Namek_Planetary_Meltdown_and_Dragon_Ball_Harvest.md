# MODULE DBZ-01: THE NAMEK PLANETARY MELTDOWN & DRAGON BALL HARVEST

### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Core Premise
On the alien world of Planet Namek, three suns illuminate a dying cradle of ancient biological miracles. Emerald seas lap against towering plateaus crowned with blue *Ajissa* trees, beneath which a peaceful race of slug-like humanoids tend soil and meditate. But this tranquility has been converted into a high-lethal war theater by the arrival of the galactic tyrant Frieza and his armored subjugation army, hunting the seven planetary Dragon Balls to attain biological immortality.

The pointcrawl network consists of scattered agrarian villages situated on elevated plateaus, separated by vast tracts of open ocean and subterranean coral caverns. Traveling between nodes requires high-speed flight across open skies—an action that instantly alerts military sensor networks unless travelers master the subtle art of energy suppression. As the tyrant's frustration mounts, a direct energy blast pierces the planetary mantle, initiating a catastrophic 8-segment countdown to total planetary vaporization.

* **The Physical Anchor (The Core Rupture & Guru's Spire):** At one end stands the sky-scraping stone pinnacle of Grand Elder Guru; at the other, a gaping, incandescent magma wound where the planet's core vents superheated plasma into the upper atmosphere.
* **The Dynamic Engine at Stake:** The module governs a high-tension cat-and-mouse hunt. Characters must locate the seven distinct Dragon Balls, protect or communicate with the surviving Namekian elders who hold the sacred activation passphrase, and summon the celestial dragon Porunga before the planet fragments into orbital debris.
* **The Escalation Trigger:** Any player character who triggers a **Level 3 Output Surge** (such as firing an unsuppressed energy beam or unleashing full martial aura) instantly advances the *Scouter Convergence* clock by +1 tick, drawing elite hunters to their coordinates.

### 2. Dramatic Opposition Web (4-Corner Matrix)

* **1. Establishment / Hegemony (The Frieza Planetary Trade Army):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* Total galactic domination, ruthless resource liquidation, and the attainment of absolute physical immortality for Lord Frieza.
  * *Leverage:* Interplanetary dreadnoughts, automated scouter communication grids, overwhelming biological power (Frieza's four modular transformation tiers), elite specialized death squads (The Ginyu Force).
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Absolute terror-based command structure; subordinates conceal failures to avoid execution; arrogance that blinds commanders to rapid enemy power growth.

* **2. Main Opponent / Challenger (The Saiyan Vanguard & Earth Guerrillas):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* Shatter Frieza's empire, avenge slaughtered homeworlds, protect Earth's revived allies, and push personal biological limits to achieve legendary mythic states (*Super Saiyan*).
  * *Leverage:* High-gravity training regimens, rapid physical recovery surges (Zenkai), modular portable Dragon Radar technology, mastery of concealed internal ki.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Compulsive martial pride; obsession with fair single-combat duels; willingness to endanger entire planets to fight opponents at peak power.

* **3. Thematic Shadow / Profiteer (Rogue Mercenary Scavengers & Deserter Cells):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* Hijack abandoned spacepods, steal a single Dragon Ball during the chaos to ransom back to planetary brokers, and flee into deep space before the core detonates.
  * *Leverage:* Counter-scouter jamming frequencies, black-market healing tank canisters, complete disregard for imperial or civilian lives.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Cowardice when confronting true planetary heavyweights; easily routed by intimidation.

* **4. Tertiary Outcast / Victim (The Indigenous Namekian Agrarian Survivors):**
  * *Worldview & Goal:* Preserve the sacred Ajissa seedlings, protect the dying Grand Elder Guru, and hide the mystical dragon relics from all off-world conquerors.
  * *Leverage:* Ancient Namekian healing arts, telepathic bond with the planet's spirit, exclusive knowledge of the Namekian tongue needed to grant wishes.
  * *Blind Spot / Fatal Flaw:* Strict pacifist vows (outside of the rare Warrior Clan); physical fragility against planetary bombardment; refusal to tell lies.

### 3. GM Placement & Tactical Synergies

* **Recommended Placement:** Placed on **Node 22 (Equilibrium Key / Panopticon)** as an endgame **Tier 5 Climax Engine**, or on an isolated island chain within the pointcrawl perimeter.
* **Supply Chain Interlocks:**
  * *Consumes:* `Authority: Namekian-Elder-Concession` (the spoken passphrase) and `Power: Super-Ki-Aura-Surge`.
  * *Feeds:* Yields `Climax: Porunga-Three-Wish-Invocation` (capable of resurrecting entire slaughtered nodes, restoring depleted mineral biomes, or teleporting populations off-world).
* **Arrival / Disembark Integration:**
  * *Upright Boon (Wheel of Fortune):* Intact Healing Tank Pod—the landing group discovers an undamaged mobile rejuvenation chamber, immediately restoring all hit points, stamina, and removing persistent curses or blights.
  * *Reversed Debt (The Tower):* Crushed Spacecraft Hull—the party's landing vessel suffers a retro-thruster malfunction upon entering Namek's dense atmosphere, crashing into an emerald reef and destroying off-world escape communications.
* **Standalone Fallback:** If played as a standalone one-shot without a wider campaign map, the scenario functions as a survival sprint: the characters start with 3 Dragon Balls on a sinking island with 5 ticks remaining on the *Namek Core Meltdown* clock, and must infiltrate Frieza's grounded flagship to steal a functioning sub-light launch pod before the horizon collapses.
