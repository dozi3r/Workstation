# Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TIMBER` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Axemen and crosscut sawyers fell ancient oak and beech trees in deep temperate forests, trimming branches and bucking trunks into transportable lengths. Teams of oxen drag the massive hardwood logs along corduroy skid roads toward river loading banks or staging yards. This arduous extractive labor supplies both raw structural timber and essential firewood for the region. The required input of Livestock: Draft-Animals supplies the oxen, and the outputs are Timber: Hardwood-Logs and Fuel: Wood-Fuel.

The camp works a winter cut and a spring drive: trees fall and are hauled over frozen or watered roads to rollways, then ride the thaw downstream. Each firm stamps an end mark on its logs, and altering a mark is a crime. Tops, limbs and coppice poles are split and corded as fuel.
- **Rollway:** the bank stack where logs wait for the thaw.
- **Skid Road:** log-and-ice track the oxen follow.
- **Wannigan:** the cook raft that trails the spring drive.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[+2, -2, -1]` — **Rebel** (`Adhocracy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Dangerous frontier conditions, physical isolation, and seasonal nomadism breed rugged, independent-minded loggers who despise bureaucracy and rely strictly on crew solidarity.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Hardwood-Logs** (`Commodity`): Massive, unpeeled oak and beech logs stamped with the camp mark.
- **Felling Axe** (`Tool`): A balanced, razor-sharp broad axe fitted with a stout hickory handle.
- **Corded Wood-Fuel** (`Commodity`): Split tops and poles stacked by the cord, sold to kilns, furnaces and boiling houses.
- **Peavey Hook** (`Tool`): A pivoting-hook pole the drivers use to roll logs and pry out a key log.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Skid Road Glaze (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, dry dirt roads stall hauling sleds completely. At `8–10`, frozen winter ice allows effortless log transport, but a warm spell turns the glaze to slush and sleds slide into the ditch.
- **Quest Hook — *The Logjam Gorge***: When thousands of harvested logs jam a swollen river gorge after a flash thaw, Kaelen must lead a dangerous dynamite and cant-hook operation before the rising water washes away the entire winter cut; Anselm Corvane gives the crew three days before the whole jam gives way.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Hauls with `Livestock: Draft-Animals` (oxen) from the **Pasture Ranch (`FARM-CATTLE-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Floats `Timber: Hardwood-Logs` to the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**, the **Bark Peeling Camp (`EXT-TANNIN-01`)** and the **Seasoning Timber Pond & Sawpit (`IND-SHIP-01`)**. Sells `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` to many trades, chiefly the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)**, the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)**, the **Salt Boiling House & Granary (`IND-SALT-02`)**, the **Floor Malting Kiln (`IND-BREW-01`)**, the **Hop Garden and Oast Kiln (`FARM-HOPS-01`)**, the **Cinnabar Mine and Retort Furnaces (`MINE-MERCURY-01`)**, the **Copper Alembic Distillery (`IND-VINE-03`)**, the **Civic Bakehouse (`IND-WHEAT-03`)**, and other trades such as the tar kiln, paper vat, soapboiler, alum and saltpeter works.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The thaw. Whoever holds the gorge and the flash dam decides when the winter's cut reaches the mill.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Widowmaker Limb:** A dead branch breaks loose as a tree falls ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage to anyone under the crown).
* **Log Roll:** A stacked rollway shifts under a man working the hook ($3\text{d}6$ crushing damage, often fatal).
* **Jam Break:** A key log gives way while drivers stand on it ($2\text{d}6$ damage and a fall into icy water).
