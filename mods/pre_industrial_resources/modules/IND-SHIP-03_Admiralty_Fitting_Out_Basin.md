# Admiralty Fitting-Out Basin (`IND-SHIP-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SHIP` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The admiralty fitting-out basin represents the pinnacle of pre-industrial maritime manufacturing. Careened hulls receive towering pine masts, intricate hemp-cordage standing rigging, canvas sails, and heavy cast-bronze deck fittings and ordnance. Specialized riggers, carpenters, and naval inspectors coordinate the final outfitting before commissioning the ocean-going caravel for service.

Fitting out happens afloat at the quay, and a great ship may take most of a year. Masts rest in a mast pond and are lifted in from the mast house; the rigging house stores cordage, blocks and sails. The resident commissioner runs the yard for a central board that controls funds and commissions, while the master shipwright serves as his deputy.
- **Mast House:** where spars are shaped and stored before stepping.
- **Rigging House:** store for cordage, blocks and sails.
- **Quay Berth:** where the hull lies afloat for fitting out.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Vessel Sea-Readiness` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, incomplete rigging and loose ordnance render the ship unseaworthy and prone to dismasting. At `8–10`, flawless outfitting produces a magnificent flagship ready for global voyage, though every inspection and delay eats funds and the board grows impatient.
- **Quest Hook — *The Unstepped Mast***: Resident Commissioner Leofric Wrayburn threatens to withhold commissioning funds unless the rigging crew steps the mainmast before the board's inspection at dawn. Master Rigger Hendrik Vosmaer says the lower mast is too green to carry the strain, and Agent Tobias Reck's bronze fittings are still on the quay.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Fits out the `Naval: Careened-Hull` from the **Drydock Slipway & Caulking Yard (`IND-SHIP-02`)**; sets up `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` and bends on `Naval: Canvas-Sails`, both from the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)**; mounts `Alloy: Cast-Bronze` from the **Bell & Artillery Bronze Foundry (`IND-BRONZE-01`)**; and steps `Timber: Mast-Spars` from the **Boreal Conifer Logging & Turpentine Camp (`EXT-PINE-01`)**.

* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The commission. Without the board's funds and sign-off the hull stays at the quay.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Falling Spar:** A mast or yard drops from the tackle during stepping ($3\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Aloft in the Rigging:** A rigger slips on tarred cordage high above the deck ($2\text{d}6$ falling damage).
* **Loose Ordnance:** A bronze gun breaks free of its lashings on the quay or at sea ($2\text{d}6$ crushing damage).
