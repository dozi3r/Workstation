# Riverbank Clay Pit (`EXT-CLAY-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CLAY` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A terraced alluvial excavation site along a meandering river floodplain features spade-dug pits, silt-settling pools, and wooden cart loading ramps. Laborers excavate dense, cohesive alluvial clay strata, washing away pebble impurities and piling the plastic sediment for weathering. This direct earth-extraction process harvests raw, unrefined mineral clay for downstream ceramic and masonry production. The pit has no inputs of its own: its only output is `Mineral: Raw-Clay`, bought by brickyards, kilns and furnace builders alike.

Clay is dug by hand with mattocks and spades, carried or carted to the settling pools, and cleaned of stones and roots. The kilns set the pace: a firing is only as good as the clay in the pit, and the pit is only as good as its drainage. Brick and pottery clays differ, so the diggers sort the face by colour and feel into the fine plastic seams and the coarser sandy seams, and the carters load each for a different buyer.
- **Terrace Faces:** stepped cut walls of clay, shaved back in turns so no face hangs over the diggers.
- **Settling Pools:** shallow basins where washed clay slurry loses its sand and grit.
- **Drainage Trench:** the cut that carries seep water from the pit floor to the river.
- **Cart Ramp:** timber-floored slope where clay is loaded for the kiln road.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Pit Drainage & Seep` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, groundwater floods the pit, turning excavations into impassable mud bogs. At `8-10`, dry pit walls fracture and trigger sudden cave-ins.
- **Quest Hook - *The Mud Slide***: Heavy rains cause a clay wall collapse in Garret's pit, trapping a cart and burying the drainage trench. Garret and Lyra must rally workers to dig out the sluice before the entire pit fills with stagnant floodwater, while Idris Rowe demands to know who cut the bank, and the river will crest in three days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. This is a raw-earth extraction site that needs no tagged inputs, only labor, tools, dry weather and a working drainage trench.
* **Output Transmission:** Carts `Mineral: Raw-Clay` to the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)** for bricks and vessels, to the **Copper Smelting Furnace (`IND-COPPER-02`)** for furnace lining and tuyeres, to the **Bell & Artillery Bronze Foundry (`IND-BRONZE-01`)** for molds and crucibles, and to the **Climbing Dragon Kiln (`IND-PORC-02`)** for fine ceramic body.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The kiln road. Every kiln and furnace in the region lives on the clay that rolls down it.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Wall Collapse:** A clay face drops without warning on the diggers below ($3\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and the victims are pinned until dug out).
* **Pit Mud:** Wading the flooded floor leaves a digger stuck in cold clay that sucks off boots and stiffens limbs ($1\text{d}6$ cold damage per hour and a penalty to movement).
* **Flood Crest:** A river rise fills the settling pools and trench in minutes, sweeping carts and tools toward the channel ($2\text{d}6$ damage to anyone caught on the floor).
