# Drydock Slipway & Caulking Yard (`IND-SHIP-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SHIP` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The drydock slipway is a massive stone-lined tidal basin where shipwrights assemble seasoned ship timbers over sturdy keel blocks, fastening them with forged-iron bolts and trunnels. Once the structural hull skeleton is planked, caulkers hammer oakum into every seam and seal the exterior hull with boiling pine-pitch to create a watertight, floating vessel. The required inputs of Naval: Seasoned-Ship-Timber, Textile: Oakum-and-Tow, Chem: Pine-Pitch, and Metal: Forged-Iron yield Naval: Careened-Hull.

The master shipwright sets out the hull shape in the mould loft and keeps his design knowledge close. Gangs work by specialty, one on the keel, others on frames and planking. Caulkers drive tar-soaked oakum into wedge-shaped seams with mallet and caulking iron, putty the hull seams and pay deck seams with melted pitch. Cramped, awkward positions make the work hard on the body.
- **Keel Blocks:** the timber bed on which the hull is raised.
- **Pitch Kettle:** where pine-pitch is melted for paying the seams.
- **Mould Loft:** where the hull lines are drawn full size.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Hull Watertight Integrity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, faulty caulking causes severe leaks upon launching, risking immediate sinking. At `8–10`, flawless construction ensures absolute buoyancy and structural rigidity, but every seam is checked twice and the hull takes weeks longer to leave the slip.
- **Quest Hook — *The Leaky Seam***: Caulker Bramble Finch discovers a major seawater leak along the garboard strake during the initial drydock flood test, requiring immediate emergency pitching. Master Shipwright Oakhaven wants the flaw hidden before the tide tomorrow, and Clerk Perrin Haldane has the stores tally that shows who skimped on the oakum.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Raises the hull from `Naval: Seasoned-Ship-Timber` produced by the **Seasoning Timber Pond & Sawpit (`IND-SHIP-01`)**; drives in `Textile: Oakum-and-Tow` from the **Hemp Braking & Hackling Shed (`IND-HEMP-02`)**; pays the seams with `Chem: Pine-Pitch` from the **Tar Kiln & Pitch Boilery (`IND-PINE-02`)**; and fastens with `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Floats `Naval: Careened-Hull` to the **Admiralty Fitting-Out Basin (`IND-SHIP-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The tide. A hull must be sealed and launched on the spring tide or wait a month.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Pitch Fire:** Boiling pitch spills into shavings and spreads along the slip ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and the hull is at risk).
* **Falling Timber:** A frame slips from its tackle above the keel blocks ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Cramped Seams:** Hours bent beneath the hull take their toll (a level of fatigue until the gang rests).
