# Pasture Ranch (`FARM-CATTLE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CATTLE` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Open rolling grasslands enclosed by post-and-rail fencing and supplemented by winter fodder barns form the foundation of pastoral cattle production. Herders manage rotational grazing patterns across meadows to prevent over-exhaustion of turf while monitoring herd health and breeding cycles. This agrarian cycle extracts living biological units—beef cattle, powerful draft oxen, and the horses and mules bred alongside them as draft stock—from natural forage.

Fat stock is walked to market by drovers, ten to fifteen miles a day so the beasts keep their weight, shod with iron for the road and watered often. Dealers buy part-way and pass cattle on at autumn fairs. The sale of cattle and draft animals to mines, mills and loggers pays for the winter fodder.
- **Fold and Fence Line:** post-and-rail enclosures and a stock pond.
- **Fodder Barn:** hay for the winter months.
- **Droving Yard:** where herds are made up for the road.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Pasture Carrying Capacity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, overgrazing turns pastures to mud, starving the stock. At `8–10`, understocking allows brush encroachment and parasite infestation.
- **Quest Hook — *The Broken Fence Line***: Sally discovers a downed section of boundary fence allowing prime breeding bulls to intermingle with wild scrub cattle. Silas must coordinate an immediate roundup before disease or genetic dilution ruins the herd, and Drover Tam Ridley needs the herd on the road within three days or the dealer buys elsewhere.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The ranch lives on its own pasture, so it has no `requires` tags.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Livestock: Cattle` to the **Civic Abattoir (`IND-CATTLE-02`)**, and sends `Livestock: Draft-Animals` (oxen, horses and mules) to the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**, the **Stone Olive Press (`IND-OLIVE-02`)**, the **Stamping Mill & Patio Amalgamation Floor (`IND-SILVER-02`)** and the **Galena Deep-Vein Mine (`MINE-SILVER-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The winter hay. Without it the draft teams of half the valley stand idle.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Stampede:** A spooked herd bolts through the fold ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage to anyone caught in its path).
* **Murrain:** Sickness spreads through the herd, and every beast in the fold must be culled or isolated.
* **Kick and Gore:** Handling a half-broken ox or mule risks a kick or goring ($1\text{d}8$ damage).
