# Glassblower's Furnace (`IND-GLASS-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-GLASS` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Inside a roaring dome-topped furnace, `Mineral: Silica-Sand`, `Chem: Potash`, `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal`, `Chem: Slaked-Lime`, `Metal: Forged-Iron` blowpipes and `Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels` yield blown `Commodity: Glassware`. Workers frit roughly two parts sand to one part ash below melting for a day and a night, then melt and fine the batch inside covered crucibles of imported refractory clay.

Led by the gaffer, the shop gathers glowing glass on iron blowpipes, shapes it on the marver and with metal shears, reheats at the glory hole, and cools each piece slowly in the annealing lehr. Furnaces devour wood by the tonne, forcing many glasshouses to relocate once nearby coppices are cut bare.
- **Melting Furnace:** Domed oven housing covered refractory clay crucibles.
- **Glory Hole & Marver:** Reheating port and rolling slab for shaping gobs.
- **Annealing Lehr:** Slow-cooling tunnel preventing thermal shatter.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[3, 4, 3]` - **Passionate Creator** (`Adhocracy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Glassblowing requires instantaneous artistic timing, intense thermal focus, and fluid teamwork under extreme heat, fostering high creative autonomy and craft pride.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Glassware** (`Commodity`): Blown flasks, vials, and window panes of varying clarity.
- **Iron Blowpipe** (`Tool`): Five-foot hollow steel tube tipped with a heat-resistant mouthpiece.
- **Refractory Crucible** (`Tool`): Covered clay melting pot that holds the batch and is slowly eaten by the glass.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Crucible Heat` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, the batch chills and crystallizes prematurely on the pipe. At `8-10`, the crucible cracks, spilling molten glass into the firebox.
- **Quest Hook - *The Thermal Shock***: When a sudden draft cracks the primary melting pot mid-shift, Piero must salvage the remaining molten batch before it solidifies in the firebox; Tilo Brenner has only a few hours to bank the fire before the cracked pot spreads the burn to the roof.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Melts `Mineral: Silica-Sand` from the **Silica Sand Dredging Pit (`EXT-GLASS-01`)** with `Chem: Potash` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)**; fires the furnace with `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**; stabilizes the batch with `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)**; works with `Metal: Forged-Iron` blowpipes and shears from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**; and holds the melt in `Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels` from the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Commodity: Glassware` to the **Spagyric Apothecary & Quarantine Lazaretto (`IND-APOTH-01`)**, the **Assay Furnace & Goldsmith Bourse (`IND-GOLD-02`)** and the **Crown & Flint Lens-Grinding Lathe (`IND-OPTIC-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The fuel. A glasshouse that cannot feed its furnace cannot work.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Furnace Burns:** Gathering at the open furnace mouth scorches the face and arms ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Molten Spill:** A cracked crucible floods the firebox with glass ($3\text{d}6$ fire damage to anyone nearby).
* **Glass Splinters:** Thermal shock bursts a vessel in a worker's hands ($1\text{d}6$ slashing damage).
