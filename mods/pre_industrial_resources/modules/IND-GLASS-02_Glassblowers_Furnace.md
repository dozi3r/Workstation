# Glassblower's Furnace (`IND-GLASS-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-GLASS` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A massive, dome-topped brick furnace roars continuously, fueled by forced-draft charcoal chambers that melt silica sand, potash flux, and lime into a glowing, viscous batch of molten glass. Craftsmen dip long iron blowpipes into the crucibles, gathering the glowing gob and shaping it through synchronized lung inflation, rolling blocks, and dexterous metal shears. The required inputs of Mineral: Silica-Sand, Chem: Potash, Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal, Chem: Slaked-Lime, Metal: Forged-Iron and Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels yield finished Commodity: Glassware.

A batch of about two parts sand to one of ash is fritted below melting for a day and a night, then melted in covered crucibles of imported refractory clay and fined before work begins. The shop, led by the gaffer, gathers glass on the pipe, rolls it on the marver, reheats in the glory hole and carries finished pieces to the lehr to cool slowly. Furnaces eat wood by the tonne, which is why many glasshouses move when the woodland runs out.
- **Melting Furnace:** covered crucibles in a dome.
- **Glory Hole:** reheating opening.
- **Lehr:** annealing oven where the glass cools over hours.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Crucible Heat` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, the batch chills and crystallizes prematurely on the pipe. At `8-10`, the crucible cracks, spilling molten glass into the firebox.
- **Quest Hook - *The Thermal Shock***: When a sudden draft cracks the primary melting pot mid-shift, Lucian must salvage the remaining molten batch before it solidifies in the firebox; Morag Fenn has only a few hours to bank the fire before the cracked pot spreads the burn to the roof.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Melts `Mineral: Silica-Sand` from the **Silica Sand Dredging Pit (`EXT-GLASS-01`)** with `Chem: Potash` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)**; fires the furnace with `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**; stabilizes the batch with `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)**; works with `Metal: Forged-Iron` blowpipes and shears from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**; and holds the melt in `Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels` from the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Commodity: Glassware` to the **Spagyric Apothecary & Quarantine Lazaretto (`IND-APOTH-01`)**, the **Assay Furnace & Goldsmith Bourse (`IND-GOLD-02`)** and the **Crown & Flint Lens-Grinding Lathe (`IND-OPTIC-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The fuel. A glasshouse that cannot feed its furnace cannot work.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Furnace Burns:** Gathering at the open furnace mouth scorches the face and arms ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Molten Spill:** A cracked crucible floods the firebox with glass ($3\text{d}6$ fire damage to anyone nearby).
* **Glass Splinters:** Thermal shock bursts a vessel in a worker's hands ($1\text{d}6$ slashing damage).
