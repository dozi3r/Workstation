# Tallow Chandlery (`IND-CANDLE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CANDLE` (Stage 1: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A warm, smoky workshop filled with hanging wooden dipping frames where braided flax wicks are repeatedly immersed in vats of rendered animal tallow and allowed to cool between dips, building up solid cylindrical wax tapers destined for domestic and industrial illumination.

The best tallow is mutton and beef fat blended; plain beef is second and pig fat is cheap but foul. The chandler buys it from local butchers, and a guild searcher can walk in at any hour to hunt inferior goods. Tallow candles must be thick and need constant trimming, but miners and poor households buy them, while the rich pay for beeswax. Tapered metal moulds turn out half a dozen at a time.
- **Dipping Frames:** racks that lower a dozen wicks into the kettle together.
- **Kettle Hearth:** keeps the fat just liquid.
- **Cooling Loft:** where dipped tapers hang to set between dips.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Dipper Frame** (`Tool`): Multi-wick wooden rack used to suspend flax strings during successive wax baths.
- **Taper Mould** (`Tool`): Tapered metal tube set with a central wick thread, casting half a dozen candles at a time.
- **Rendered Fat Cake** (`Commodity`): Block of clarified beef and mutton fat waiting to be melted into the kettle.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Tallow Melting Temperature` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, cold fat clumps unevenly and ruins wick adhesion. At `8–10`, overheating boils off volatile fats and creates foul-smelling smoke, and a spark can set the kettle alight.
- **Quest Hook — *The Rancid Vat***: When a shipment of spoiled slaughterhouse fat contaminates the main melting kettle, Martha must source clean rendering stock before evening work begins, while Dilys Zouch hovers in the doorway and the guild's next search is due within the week.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Melts `Animal: Tallow` from the **Civic Abattoir (`IND-CATTLE-02`)** and braids wicks from `Textile: Flax-Fiber` supplied by the **Retting & Scutching Basin (`IND-FLAX-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Light: Tallow-Candles` to the **Galena Deep-Vein Mine (`MINE-SILVER-01`)**, where miners burn them underground.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The butchers' fat. Whoever gets the best tallow decides who has light.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Kettle Fire:** Overheated fat ignites and spreads across the loft ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage to anyone within reach, and the stock burns).
* **Tallow Smoke:** Long hours over a smoking kettle leave a cough and streaming eyes (disadvantage on fine work until rested).
* **Scalding Dip:** A slipped frame splashes hot fat ($1\text{d}6$ damage and the burn scars).
