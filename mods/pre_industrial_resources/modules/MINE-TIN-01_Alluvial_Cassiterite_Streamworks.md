# Alluvial Cassiterite Streamworks (`MINE-TIN-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TIN` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The streamworks harness diverted upland headwaters to wash tin-bearing detritus across tiered wooden leats and rough fern-matted riffles. Manual labor using shovels and diverting gates feeds heavy alluvial gravels into the current, washing away sterile muds while dense black tin oxide (cassiterite) settles behind the wooden stops. The requires input of Timber: Mine-Props constructs the diversion flumes and sluice boxes, transforming raw streambed sediment into concentrated Ore: Tin-Ore.

Tinners hold free rights to search and work ground under stannary law, and every parcel of ore must be weighed and taken on to be proved. Heavier stone-bound tin is stamped and rewashed, and old tailings (causalty) are piled and reworked after six or seven years.
- **Leat:** the cut that carries the head of water to the works.
- **Tally Board:** where each parcel of black tin is recorded and taxed.
- **Tailing Bank:** piled waste kept for a later wash.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[1, -1, 2]` — **Bureaucratic Guild** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: The repetitive, physically grueling labor governed by strict water rights and tenement leases breeds a rigid, rule-bound adherence to guild oversight and supervisory control.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Cassiterite Gravel** (`Commodity`): Heavy, water-worn pebbles of tin dioxide dredged directly from the riverbed.
- **Wooden Staking Riffle** (`Tool`): Grooved oak boards placed in the sluice run to catch dense heavy metals.
- **Black Tin Sack** (`Commodity`): Washed and dried concentrate, weighed and sealed for the smelter.
- **Prop-Timbered Flume** (`Tool`): A diversion flume framed with mine-props to carry the leat across the gravel.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Water Head Pressure` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, drought chokes the sluices and halts production. At `8–10`, torrential floods wash away the wooden flumes and scatter tailings.
- **Quest Hook — *The Stannary Contraband***: Bailiff Trevithick discovers high-grade cassiterite missing from the tally boards, directly implicating Margery Penrose and threatening a violent camp strike; Cador Pascoe has until the next proving day to produce the missing weight or lose his bound ground.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Frames its flumes and shaft pits with `Timber: Mine-Props` from the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Ore: Tin-Ore` to the **Tin Blowing House (`IND-TIN-02`)** for smelting and proving.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The leat. Whoever holds the head of water decides which works can wash.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Flume Collapse:** A prop-timbered flume gives way under a spate ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and a soaking in cold water).
* **Gravel Bank Slide:** A cut face of gravel slumps on a working washer ($3\text{d}6$ crushing damage).
* **Cold Standing Water:** Hours knee-deep in icy leat water bring a lasting chill and wasting fever (cumulative penalty to physical rolls).
