# Retting Trough & Stamping Mill (`IND-PAPER-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PAPER` (Stage 1: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
This water-powered processing works features stone retting troughs and heavy timber stamping hammers driven by an overshot waterwheel. Plant fibers, rags, and straw are soaked in lime-infused water to break down lignins, then mechanically macerated by falling pestles into a uniform, fibrous slurry known as rag pulp. The required inputs of Textile: Linen-Cloth, Material: Rice-Straw and Chem: Slaked-Lime become Material: Rag-Pulp.

Rags are sorted by women and girls into three to six grades, cut to pieces under two inches and fermented in a cemented basin for five to six weeks, the work stopped when the heap is too hot to hold a hand in. Lime speeds the rot but weakens the fibre, so some districts regulated its use. Stamper troughs, typically six, are worked by hammers striking about forty blows a minute: three to break, two to beat and one to brush out, with washing throughout. Mills stand away from town walls because of noise and smell.
- **Retting Basin:** cemented sides, uncemented floor.
- **Stamper Battery:** troughs of wooden hammers on a camshaft.
- **Rag Loft:** where sorted rags are stored by grade.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Maceration Time` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, coarse, lumpy pulp produces weak, uneven paper sheets. At `8–10`, over-beating turns fibers to mush, clogging drainage screens.
- **Quest Hook — *The Broken Axle***: The main wooden drive shaft splinters under heavy load, halting pulp production just as a major municipal book contract is due. Miller Hobb Miller needs a replacement beam felled and fitted in a week while Anselm Pike offers to supply cheap rags to keep the troughs full, and Grete Voss warns that most are rotten.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Takes worn `Textile: Linen-Cloth` from the **Handloom Linen Weaver (`IND-FLAX-03`)**; supplements it with `Material: Rice-Straw` from the **Irrigated Rice Paddy (`FARM-RICE-01`)**; and soaks the heaps with `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Delivers `Material: Rag-Pulp` to the **Paper Vat & Screw-Press Loft (`IND-PAPER-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The rag supply. A mill without rags stands still however well the wheel turns.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Stamper Hammer:** A hand or arm caught under a falling pestle is crushed ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Rot Fumes:** Working in the heated fermenting shed breathes mould and gas ($1\text{d}4$ damage and a lasting cough).
* **Wheel Race:** Slipping into the millrace or under the wheel drowns or maims ($2\text{d}6$ damage).
