# Filature Reeling Basin (`IND-SILK-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SILK` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The filature reeling basin is a steaming workshop equipped with rows of copper water cauldrons heated by wood-fired brick stoves. Workers plunge harvested silk cocoons into boiling water to dissolve the sericin gum, gently brushing the basins to locate filament ends before combining and reeling multiple strands onto wooden spools. Silk-cocoon and wood-fuel inputs are transformed into strong, continuous reeled-silk-thread. The required inputs of Animal: Silk-Cocoons, Fuel: Wood-Fuel, and Metal: Forged-Copper yield Textile: Reeled-Silk-Thread.

Each cocoon yields one strand, and several are combined into one thread; pulled too fast it breaks and too slow it tangles. The cocoons are killed and the gum is softened in hot water, then the skeins are cleaned and passed on for twisting into tram (light twist, for weft) or organzine (heavier twist, for warp). Silk needs warmth and humidity to work well, so the hall is kept heated.
- **Copper Basin:** hot water pan for softening cocoons.
- **Brick Stoves:** wood-fired heat beneath each basin.
- **Reeling Frames:** wooden reels that gather the skeins.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Water Temperature Variance` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, water simmers perfectly, allowing smooth filament unwinding. At `8–10`, boiling surges dissolve cocoons too fast, snapping threads constantly.
- **Quest Hook — *The Boiling Surge***: Stoker Baator overheats the primary cauldron, threatening to snap the delicate silk filaments currently being reeled by Master Reeler Xiulan. Factor Silvano Greco will refuse the lot at dawn unless the skeins come out even.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Boils and reels `Animal: Silk-Cocoons` from the **Mulberry Orchard & Silkworm Nursery (`FARM-SILK-01`)**; stokes the stoves with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; and heats the water in basins of `Metal: Forged-Copper` from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Textile: Reeled-Silk-Thread` to the **Drawloom Brocade Atelier (`IND-SILK-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The fire. If the stoves fail, the thread fails.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Scalding Basin:** A reeler's hands slip into the boiling water ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage and a loss of dexterity for a week).
* **Steam Fog:** Hours in the hot, humid hall wear the reelers down (a level of fatigue each shift).
* **Stove Fire:** A stray spark leaps from the brick stoves to the stacked fuel ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
