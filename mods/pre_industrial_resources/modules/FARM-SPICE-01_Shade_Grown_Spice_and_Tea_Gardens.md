# Shade-Grown Spice & Tea Gardens (`FARM-SPICE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SPICE` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Laid out beneath a protective forest canopy of taller timber trees, these terraced hillside plantations cultivate delicate tea bushes and spice vines in rich, humid soil. Agricultural laborers hand-pick tender leaves and aromatic pods at precise dawn hours, preserving essential volatile oils before the tropical sun burns off the dew. The harvested biomass is sorted and bundled for immediate transport to curing sheds.

Spice plots are often parcelled out by a trading company, which buys the whole crop at a fixed low price. Pepper vines climb rough-barked trees; spikes are picked as the first fruit reddens; clove buds are picked when bright red; nutmeg takes weeks in the sun. Much of the picking is done by bonded, enslaved or convict hands, and growers who know the crop are prized for it.
- **Terraces:** stepped hillside beds under thinned canopy.
- **Picking Paths:** dawn routes the crews walk each day.
- **Drying Floors:** sun-swept clay floors by the sorting shed.
- **Quota Shed:** where the buyer's clerk weighs and tallies the crop.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Canopy Density (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, scorching sun wilts tender leaves and ruins flavor profiles. At `8–10`, excessive shade breeds fungal blights and rots root systems.
- **Quest Hook — *The Fungal Blight***: A mysterious white mold begins spreading across the lower tea terraces, threatening the harvest before Tressa can implement quarantine measures. Plot-Holder Casimir Dyk wants the sick rows picked anyway to meet his quota, Anwar Sele knows which terraces to burn, and the blight reaches the upper rows within a week of damp weather.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy

* **Output Transmission:** Carries `Crop: Raw-Spices-and-Tea` in bamboo baskets to the **Firing Woks & Curing Sheds (`IND-SPICE-02`)**.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Slippery Terraces:** A fall on wet steps under a heavy basket ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a lost day's picking).
* **Harvest Fever:** Damp, mosquito-heavy dawns sicken the crews (a cumulative penalty to endurance rolls until rest).
* **Overseer's Lash:** Crews driven past quota by a nervous plot-holder break down or flee into the hills ($1\text{d}4$ damage and a lost crew).
