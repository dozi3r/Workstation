# Highland Sheep Station (`FARM-SHEEP-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SHEEP` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Rugged stone fold enclosures and turf-roofed shelters perched on wind-swept upland pastures graze resilient flocks on coarse grasses. Shepherds manage breeding, predator defense, and seasonal shearing cycles using specialized hand shears. The station transforms upland forage, seasonal livestock care, and manual shearing labor into raw greasy fleece and meat.

Flocks move to high summer pasture from late spring and come down in autumn, and shepherds live at the summer shieling while they are up. Ewes are shorn once a year, usually before lambing. After shearing a classer sorts each fleece into fleece, broken fleece, bellies and locks; the best grade sells, and the rest is spun at home.
- **Stone Fold:** walled pen for night penning and shearing.
- **Summer Shieling:** hill hut where shepherds live with the flock.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Pasture Grazing Pressure` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, under-grazing allows coarse brush to choke out nutritious pasture grass. At `8–10`, overgrazing strips the hillsides bare, causing severe soil erosion during spring thaws.
- **Quest Hook — *The Predator Incursion***: A pack of wolves breaches the fold perimeter of Flockmaster Callum MacLeod, requiring Morag Fenn and her fellow herders to track the marauders across the ridge before the breeding ewes scatter. Drover Ysolt Brandreth must leave for the autumn fair in five days, and the flock will not be sorted by Dunstan Reeve unless enough ewes are brought back alive.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. Upland grass and hereditary grazing rights supply this site; it is the root of the sheep chain.
* **Output Transmission:** Delivers `Animal: Raw-Fleece` to the **Fulling Mill & Scouring Vat (`IND-SHEEP-02`)**; `Food: Mutton` is an end product sold at the autumn fair.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The drove road. Tolls and closed gates between hill and mill decide whether the fleece arrives on time.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Winter Blizzard:** A storm on the high pasture buries the flock and strands the shepherds ($1\text{d}6$ cold damage per night in the open).
* **Wolf Pack:** Predators attack the fold at night ($1\text{d}8$ damage to anyone caught outside).
* **Shearing Cuts:** A hurried shearer nicks the animal and the wound festers, spoiling the fleece grade.
