# Rum Distillery and Excise Harbor (`AGRI-SUGAR-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SUGAR` (Stage 3: `commercial`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Stationed at the interface of fresh river water and ocean trade winds, the rum distillery converts refinery waste byproducts into the primary maritime trade currency of the realm.

Rum is a by-product of the sugar mill's forced labor, made from molasses and boiling-house scummings fermented in cisterns and run through pot stills. The spirit is proven by the old gunpowder test (about 57% ABV), is a naval ration and a trade currency, and is shadowed by tax: the realm's duties on molasses and spirit are widely evaded, and enforcement is uneven.

* **Dunder & Skimmings Cisterns:** Sunken stone vats where cane skimmings and seasoned dunder broth ferment into esters, creating rich molasses wash.
* **Gooseneck Copper Pot Stills:** Twin 600-gallon copper stills equipped with worm condensers coiled inside wooden cooling flumes. Distills rich alcoholic vapors into crystal spirit.
* **Bonded Rum Wharf & Cooperage:** Heavily guarded bonded warehouse with oak cask aging racks, barrel testing gauges, and rolling ramps to cargo sloops.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Navy-Strength Rum Cask** (`Commodity`): An oak cask of spirit proven by gunpowder test, the estate's chief export.
- **Gooseneck Pot Still** (`Tool`): Copper still with a worm condenser in a cooling flume, run in two passes from low wines.
- **Excise Seal and Gauging Rod** (`Relic`): The collector's tools for stamping and measuring every cask, whose absence turns a cargo into contraband.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Spirit Proof` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, weak low wines fetch little and spoil quickly in clay jugs. At `8-10`, overproof spirit is volatile and a stillhead deflagration can engulf the warehouse.
- **Quest Hook - *The Unstamped Casks***: Captain Orlaith Vance offers the party a share if they help move forty unstamped casks past Magistrate Hector Finch before the next tide, while Bram Ottley warns that the Level 3 run is too volatile to load. The excise cutter is due in three days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes black molasses (`Commodity: Cane-Molasses`) from the **Cane Crushing Mill and Boiling House (`AGRI-SUGAR-02`)**, barrels of `Timber: Cooperage-Casks` from the **Master Cooperage (`IND-TIMBER-03`)**, and `Metal: Forged-Copper` from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)** for the stills and worms.
* **Output Transmission:** Exports `Commodity: High-Proof-Rum` across regional sea lanes and satisfies military tavern rations; it is an end product with no module consumer.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Volatile Alcohol Vapors:** Enclosed distillery areas require dousing lanterns; sparks ignite explosive fireball flashes ($3\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Bonded Warehouse Guard Dogs:** Patrol mastiffs trained to tackle intruders attempting nighttime barrel theft.
* **Excise Boarding Party:** A revenue cutter's boarding party seizes unstamped casks and impounds the sloop on the spot.
