# Steeping Vat & Oxidation Beating Ponds (`IND-INDIGO-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-INDIGO` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Constructed from lined stone vats and shallow terraced oxidation basins, this facility ferments harvested indigo leaves in standing water to release indican, which is then hydrolyzed into indoxyl. Workers beat the alkaline solution with wooden paddles to aerate the liquor, triggering precipitation into dense indigo sediment. Slaked lime is added to stabilize the reaction, transforming raw agricultural biomass into concentrated indigo paste. The required inputs of Crop: Indigo-Leaves and Chem: Slaked-Lime become Dye: Indigo-Paste.

Leaves steep for roughly half a day to a day, and the liquor is drawn down into the beating vat to be paddled for half an hour or so. Once the sediment settles the clear water is let off, the sludge is drained in cloth bags, pressed into brick-sized molds, dried and cut into squares. The works are kept away from houses because of the stench.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Slaked-Lime** (`Reagent`): Caustic alkaline powder used to precipitate and settle dye molecules.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Aeration Rigor` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, incomplete oxidation produces weak, greyish dye. At `8–10`, aggressive beating causes foaming losses and structural vat strain.
- **Quest Hook — *The Spoiled Fermentation***: A sudden temperature drop stalls the steeping vats, threatening the entire seasonal dye yield unless Kaelen can secure emergency hot water barrels. Paddle-Hand Sula Thorne's crew will lose their harvest wages if the batch fails, and Buyer Percival Ashdown's ship sails in a week with or without the cakes.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Steeps fresh `Crop: Indigo-Leaves` from the **Indigofera Plantation (`FARM-INDIGO-01`)** and settles the liquor with `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends pressed `Dye: Indigo-Paste` to the **Calico Weaving & Print Works (`IND-COTTON-03`)** and the **Drawloom Brocade Atelier (`IND-SILK-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The beating vat. A stalled or fouled batch cannot be rushed, and the season's dye is made in a few weeks.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Vat Fumes:** Working the steeping and beating vats through a long shift brings headache and sickness ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a lingering weakness).
* **Caustic Lime:** Lime dust or slurry splashes burn skin and eyes ($1\text{d}4$ damage and blindness for an hour).
* **Slippery Rim:** A hand who falls from the vat edge into the liquor is soaked and half drowned ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
