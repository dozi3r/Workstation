# Tidal Salt Evaporation Pans (`EXT-SALT-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SALT` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Pre-industrial coastal evaporation pans harness solar radiation and tidal influx to concentrate seawater into dense brine-slurry. Laborers construct earthen dykes and shallow wooden lock-gates along salt marshes, managing sluices according to spring and neap cycles. Concentrated brine is channeled into crystallization basins where solar evaporation precipitates sodium chloride, which is harvested daily.

Tide-fed channels fill reservoirs, and the water is passed through a series of ever-shallower dams until only an inch or two remains, when it crystallizes. Marsh families hold their plots by long custom, and the harvest follows the sun. Whatever salt leaves the marsh must be delivered to the state granary and its guards, who search carts for unreported loads.
- **Sluice Bulkhead:** timber gate wall that admits the tide.
- **Reservoir Dams:** stepped shallow basins of beaten clay.
- **Crystallizing Pans:** the last shallow beds where the rakers work.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 2, 2]` — **Bureaucratic Operator** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Tidal evaporation requires rigid scheduling, precise tidal calculations, and standardized basin maintenance, fostering a strict administrative hierarchy among salt-boilers.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Concentrated Brine Slurry** (`Commodity`): Highly saline seawater concentrate drawn from primary evaporation pans for boiling.
- **Wooden Sluice Gate** (`Tool`): Hand-operated cedar gate used to control tidal influx into evaporation terraces.
- **Wooden Salt Rake** (`Tool`): Long-handled rake that draws crystals to the pan edge without gouging the clay floor.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Solar Evaporation Efficiency` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, unseasonal cloud cover and persistent drizzle dilute the pans, halting crystallization. At `8–10`, intense heat waves accelerate output but risk salt-crust contamination from wind-blown silt.
- **Quest Hook — *The Breached Sluice***: Sluice-Master Jethro Marwick needs urgent timber repairs after spring tides shatter the main bulkhead, threatening the entire seasonal salt harvest. Granary Searcher Odo Pellam will seal the marsh if the harvest slips past the salt duty date, and Marsh Widow Hester Quillon offers to hide the party's timber on her plots for a share of the salt; the next spring tide is in four days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. Tide and sun supply this site; it is the root of the salt chain.
* **Output Transmission:** Carries `Commodity: Brine-Slurry` by barrel to the **Salt Boiling House & Granary (`IND-SALT-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The sluice bulkhead. Whoever holds the gate decides how much brine the whole chain gets.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Salt-Crust Burns:** Long days kneeling on hot crystal crust blister hands and feet ($1\text{d}4$ damage and a penalty to fine work until healed).
* **Breached Dyke:** A bank failing in a spring tide sweeps workers into the flooded marsh ($2\text{d}6$ drowning or crushing damage).
* **Marsh Fever:** Standing water and biting insects bring fever to crews working the low dams.
