# Wine Press & Fermenting Cellar (`IND-VINE-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-VINE` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Subterranean stone-vaulted cellars maintain cool, stable temperatures ideal for fermentation. Laborers tread grapes in stone vats before drawing the must into wooden casks where natural yeasts convert sugars into alcohol, with over-oxygenated batches turning naturally to sharp vinegar. The facility transforms raw wine grapes and cooperage casks into aged vintage wine and acidic preservative vinegar.

Each vintage follows a set rhythm. Treaders push the floating cap of skins down daily during the one-to-two-week primary ferment, the juice is pressed off (free-run juice is finer than press wine), and the wine is racked off its lees into clean casks. Sulfur is burned to keep spoilage at bay, and bungs are watched for air.
- **Treading Vats:** Stone troughs where the cap is trodden each day.
- **Press Floor:** A beam or screw press for the last, coarser juice.
- **Cask Vault:** Rows of oak casks stamped with the cooper's mark, topped up and racked on a schedule.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 2, 3]` — **Cellar Master** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Precision in fermentation timing and strict environmental hygiene govern the transformation of volatile organic must into stable vintage stock.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Cellar Temperature` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, freezing winter drafts halt fermentation prematurely, leaving sickly sweet must. At `8–10`, summer heat spikes accelerate bacterial growth, turning entire reserves into sour vinegar.
- **Quest Hook — *The Spiked Cask***: A rogue heat wave threatens the main aging stock under Odo Ferrand, prompting Hubert Maillard to haul cold spring water through the cellars to cool the barrels before the prized vintage spoils entirely. Corvin Aldous arrives in three days to gauge and buy, and Tamsin Ardley's crew will not tread another vat until the cellar is cooled.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy

* **Output Transmission:** Delivers `Food: Vintage-Wine` to the **Copper Alembic Distillery (`IND-VINE-03`)** and `Chem: Wine-Vinegar` to the **Spagyric Apothecary & Quarantine Lazaretto (`IND-APOTH-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** A cellar without casks cannot hold a vintage; the cooper's bench decides how much wine survives to spirits.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Cellar Gas:** Fermenting vats release heavy gas that pools in the low vault; a worker overcome in the cellar suffers $1\text{d}6$ damage per round until dragged out.
* **Press Beam Slip:** A loaded press beam can kick back on the crew ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
