# Movable-Type Printshop & Bindery (`IND-PAPER-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PAPER` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Filled with wooden type cases, hand-cranked screw presses, and sewing benches, this workshop sets cast lead alloy glyphs into composing sticks. Typographers lock pages into heavy iron chases, ink the forms with lampblack-and-linseed ink on leather dabbers, and press laid paper sheets against them before master binders sew and bind the signatures in leather covers. The required inputs of Commodity: Laid-Paper, Material: Cured-Leather, Metal: Smelted-Lead, Chem: Lampblack and Oil: Linseed-Oil become Luxury: Printed-Codices.

Ink is boiled outside: linseed oil is heated until its vapours catch fire, snuffed and cooled into varnish, then lampblack is ground in by hand until it is stiff as molasses. A fire in the boiling shed is a real danger, and ink making later became a trade of its own. Type is lead mixed with tin and antimony, cast in hand moulds, and a type case holds hundreds of boxes. Pressmen take turns: one beats the forme with sheepskin ink balls stuffed with wool, the other works the bar and pulls the sheet. A press could pull some 1,500 to 3,600 sheets in a working day. Sheets are dampened, printed, dried, folded into signatures and sewn.
- **Ink Shed:** open fire for varnish.
- **Case Room:** type cases and composing sticks.
- **Press:** oak screw press with bed and platen.
- **Bindery:** sewing frames and leather covers.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Ink Viscosity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, runny ink bleeds and smudges text beyond legibility. At `8–10`, tacky ink rips the paper surface during impression release.
- **Quest Hook — *The Upside-Down Galleys***: An exhausted apprentice drops a complete forme of type for the new civil code, scattering thousands of lead characters across the floor. Alistair Thorne and Nola Drake must reset it overnight before Canon Ambrose Whitlow arrives to inspect the proofs at dawn.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Prints on `Commodity: Laid-Paper` from the **Paper Vat & Screw-Press Loft (`IND-PAPER-02`)**; binds in `Material: Cured-Leather` from the **Tanning Yards (`IND-CATTLE-03`)**; casts type from `Metal: Smelted-Lead` from the **Lead Smelting Bole & Plumbery (`IND-LEAD-01`)**; grinds ink from `Chem: Lampblack` (soot from burning pine tar and resin) supplied by the **Tar Kiln & Pitch Boilery (`IND-PINE-02`)**; and boils the varnish from `Oil: Linseed-Oil` supplied by the **Retting & Scutching Basin (`IND-FLAX-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Luxury: Printed-Codices` to scholars, courts and merchants; it is an end product with no consumer module.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** Ink. A shop without lampblack or linseed oil cannot print a sheet.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Varnish Fire:** Boiling linseed oil flares in the ink shed ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and the shed burns).
* **Lead Fumes:** Handling hot type metal and sorting lead for years sickens the compositors ($1\text{d}4$ damage per season).
* **Seizure:** Unlicensed text found in the forme brings officers to confiscate type and stock.
