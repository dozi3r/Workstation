# Terraced Olive Grove (`FARM-OLIVE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-OLIVE` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Dry stone agricultural terraces carved into arid hillsides capture sparse winter rainfall and retain thin, rocky limestone soils suited for resilient drupes. Human labor involves seasonal pruning, hand-harvesting with beating poles and baskets, and maintaining retaining walls. The grove transforms raw sunlight, winter moisture, and manual toil into fresh raw olives (`Crop: Olives`) destined for milling.

Calcareous limestone slopes suit the tree, and one pruned with care can bear for centuries. Harvest starts in October: crews shake or strip branches into sacks tied at the waist, or saw whole branches in steep country. The grove yields roughly four to five pounds of oil per tree a year. Fruit must reach the press within a day, since delay raises acidity. Frost below about minus ten degrees can kill even old trees, which may regrow from the root.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Freshly Picked Olives** (`Commodity`): Bitter, oil-rich drupes harvested at peak maturity for same-day pressing.
- **Pruning Hook** (`Tool`): A curved steel blade on a wooden pole used for trimming fruitless branches.
- **Harvest Sacks and Baskets** (`Tool`): Waist sacks and woven baskets for stripping and carrying fruit without bruising it.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Soil Retention` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, catastrophic winter flash floods collapse terrace walls. At `8–10`, deep root systems stabilize hillsides and maximize yields, but the heavy crop must be picked and carried to the press within a day or acidity rises.
- **Quest Hook — *The Collapsed Wall***: A torrential autumn cloudburst breaches the lower terrace wall of Grove Overseer Kosta Vassos, threatening to wash away ancient trees unless Eleni Maris and her crew rebuild the stonework before nightfall. Stavros Daskal says the old trees have only until the next storm before their roots are bared.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The grove works its own limestone terraces by hand and needs no input from another module.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Crop: Olives` to the **Stone Olive Press (`IND-OLIVE-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The mule track to the press. Fruit that waits past a day loses its value.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Terrace Collapse:** A wall gives way under a laden picker ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and a buried crew).
* **Ladder Fall:** Picking from a rotten ladder on a steep slope ($1\text{d}6$ falling damage).
* **Bad Weather:** Gales or long rains during harvest ruin a day and bruise fruit.
