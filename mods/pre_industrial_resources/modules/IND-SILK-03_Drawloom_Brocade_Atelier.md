# Drawloom Brocade Atelier (`IND-SILK-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SILK` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The drawloom brocade atelier is an elite, quiet workshop dominated by massive complex drawlooms operated by paired weavers and draw-boys. Artisans interlace reeled silk threads with dyed-indigo backgrounds and beaten gold-bullion thread to create heavy, patterned imperial textiles. Reeled silk thread, indigo paste, and gold bullion inputs are synthesized into luxurious silk-brocade bolts for nobility and export. The required inputs of Textile: Reeled-Silk-Thread, Dye: Indigo-Paste, and Metal: Gold-Bullion yield Luxury: Silk-Brocade.

Brocade is woven with an extra supplementary weft that stands out like embroidery, and each color needs its own shuttle. The weaver sits at the cloth while the draw-boy pulls the figure harness that lifts each warp thread. Cities that held silk privileges guarded them with guilds and chartered monopolies.
- **Drawloom:** loom with a figure harness for patterned cloth.
- **Pattern Room:** locked store of cards and cord sequences.
- **Gold Store:** weighed reels of gold thread.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[1, 2, 4]` — **Elite Guild** (`Hierarchy`).

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Harness Cord Tangling` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, draw-cords lift smoothly for clean patterning. At `8–10`, tangled cords jam the loom, ruining expensive gold-thread inlays.
- **Quest Hook — *The Tangled Draw-Cord***: Draw-Boy Kavi accidentally snaps a primary pattern cord, threatening to ruin a rush commission of imperial silk-brocade. Guild Syndic Ambrose Tavernier arrives for inspection within two days, and Master Weaver Balam needs the cord re-strung without the pattern leaving the room.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Weaves `Textile: Reeled-Silk-Thread` from the **Filature Reeling Basin (`IND-SILK-02`)**; dyes the ground with `Dye: Indigo-Paste` from the **Steeping Vat & Oxidation Beating Ponds (`IND-INDIGO-02`)**; and inlays `Metal: Gold-Bullion` from the **Assay Furnace & Goldsmith Bourse (`IND-GOLD-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Produces `Luxury: Silk-Brocade`, an end product for patrons and export with no consumer in the chain.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The pattern room. Whoever copies the figure undercuts the guild.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Snapped Cord:** A heavy harness cord whips back across the loom ($1\text{d}6$ damage and the pattern is lost).
* **Eye Strain:** Long hours over fine gold thread dim the weaver's sight (a penalty to fine work until rested).
* **Workshop Fire:** A candle ignites silk waste and lint in the closed hall ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
