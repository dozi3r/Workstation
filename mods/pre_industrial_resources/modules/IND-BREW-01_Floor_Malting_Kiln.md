# Floor Malting Kiln (`IND-BREW-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-BREW` (Stage 1: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Inside a vaulted masonry hall, `Crop: Raw-Wheat` and `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` are converted into saccharified `Food: Malted-Grain` for the city's brewers. Grain is steeped in stone cisterns through two or three wet-and-dry cycles until it holds roughly 40 to 45 percent water, then spread thin across cool stone germination floors.

Because sprouting beds generate their own heat and can burn, turners rake them by hand day and night to keep the grain loose and cool. Once sprouted, green malt moves to a closed wood-fired kiln for a cool drying followed by a warmer cure before the dried rootlets (culm) are rubbed off. Every steep is gauged and logged for the malt duty.
- **Steeping Cistern:** Stone tank where dry grain absorbs water.
- **Germination Floor:** Cool stone hall where beds sprout.
- **Kiln:** Closed hearth beneath a perforated drying floor.
- **Rake and Turning Shovel:** Hand tools of the floor gang.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - Thermal Draft Control (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, kiln temperatures drop, resulting in damp, under-modified grain. At `8-10`, excessive draft scorches the malt bed, turning batches bitter and useless.
- **Quest Hook - *The Scorched Sprout Emergency***: A sudden damp snap threatens the germination floor while a corrupted batch of wood-fuel sends thick soot over the malting beds. Ulrich Hesse must enlist Brigid Oakes to hand-sort the viable grain before guild inspectors condemn the entire seasonal malt yield, and Gauger Piers Holloway will not let the duty on the spoiled steep be forgiven.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Steeps `Crop: Raw-Wheat` from the **Open-Field Wheat Farm (`FARM-WHEAT-01`)** and fires the kiln with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Food: Malted-Grain` to the **Copper Mash-Tun & Civic Brewery (`IND-BREW-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The wood fuel. A damp or short fuel delivery decides whether the kiln cures malt or merely dries it.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Bed Heat:** A thick sprouting bed left unraked heats and rots; the sweating grain breeds mould and sickens those who breathe it ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a fever).
* **Kiln Fire:** A scorched floor or loose sparks can set the malt and the timber loft alight ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Wet Floor:** The slick stone floor and steeping cistern cause falls and drownings ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
