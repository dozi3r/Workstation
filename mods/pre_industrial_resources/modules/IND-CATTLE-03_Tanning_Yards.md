# Tanning Yards (`IND-CATTLE-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CATTLE` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A sprawling complex of sunken wooden vats, open-air drying frames, and scraping sheds forms the municipal tanning yards downwind from residential districts. Workers soak raw hides in lime baths to strip hair, scrape away subcutaneous tissue, and steep the pelts in organic tannin infusions until the collagen crosslinks into durable leather. This chemical transformation turns putrid animal skins into pliable, water-resistant cured leather.

Hides are salted, soaked, limed, scraped, bated and then laid down in pits of rising strength, ending about six weeks in the strongest liquor; a full tanning may take a year or more. Scraped flesh is sold to glue and soap boilers. A 1796 tanyard sale counted 43 tan pits, six lime pits and two bark mills on about an acre and a half. The currier finishes the leather with tallow and oil dubbin.
- **Lime Pits:** loosen hair.
- **Tan Pits:** bark liquor of rising strength.
- **Drying Lofts:** slow, covered drying.

- **Sun-Drying Racks** (`Tool`): Wooden frames where treated leather is stretched and oiled to retain suppleness.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Vat Liquor Strength` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, weak tannin liquor fails to penetrate hides, leaving them prone to rotting. At `8–10`, overly caustic baths burn and disintegrate valuable pelts.
- **Quest Hook — *The Rotted Vat Batch***: Corin discovers that a main soaking vat has lost its tannin potency due to a leaking seam, ruining half a season's hide delivery. He and Maren must rush emergency bark extract from Bark Factor Lambert Quill to salvage the remaining pelts before guild inspectors arrive in three days.
