# Nitrary Compost Beds & Leaching House (`EXT-SALTPETER-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-POWDER` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Constructed over wide, walled earthen terraces, decomposing organic refuse, lime, and animal wastes are layered in long ridges to foster nitrifying bacteria. The resulting calcareous earth is shoveled into large wooden leaching vats where boiling water extracts crude potassium nitrate liquor, which is subsequently crystallized through wood-fueled evaporation. Potash lye leached from wood ash is stirred into the liquor to turn its calcium nitrate into potassium nitrate before the boil.

A ridge is built roughly four feet high, six wide and fifteen long from dung, mortar or ash, soil and straw, then kept moist with stable liquor and turned often. After about a year it is leached; the finished crystals are redissolved, filtered and recrystallized in shallow vats until clean. Where the beds run short, warranted diggers scrape nitrous earth from cellars and stable floors.
- **Compost Ridges:** the year-long beds under turning and watering.
- **Leaching Vats:** oak tubs with straw and gravel false bottoms.
- **Boiling House:** wood-fired pans that crystallize the liquor.
- **Ash Store:** potash lye supply for the conversion.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Calcareous Earth** (`Commodity`): Earthen scrape rich in organic nitrates harvested from stable floors.
- **Leaching Vat** (`Tool`): Heavy oak tub fitted with straw and gravel false bottoms for brine filtration.
- **Refined Saltpeter Crystals** (`Commodity`): Clean white needles recrystallized in shallow vats, packed dry for the powder mill.
- **Potash Lye** (`Reagent`): Strong ash liquor that turns calcium nitrate in the brine into potassium nitrate.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Compost Aeration Rate` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, heaps go anaerobic and sour, yielding zero saltpeter. At `8–10`, excessive turning dries the beds and halts microbial activity, and the leachers burn extra wood on weak liquor.
- **Quest Hook — *The Fouled Basin***: When a batch of leaching liquor is contaminated with organic grease, Master Barnaby must root out negligence before the gunpowder works halt supply. Leacher's Mate Garrow is blamed first, and Warrant Digger Osric Pell offers the party coin to see the fault land on the vat crew instead of his dirty cellar earth; the powder mill's cart is due in four days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Converts the brine with `Chem: Potash` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)**, stokes the boiling pans with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**, and layers the ridges with `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Casks of `Chem: Refined-Saltpeter` go to the **Water-Powered Corning & Powder Mill (`IND-POWDER-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The potash. Without lye the brine stays bitter and damp, so whoever holds the ash carts holds the nitrary.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Boiling Pans:** A slip at the scalding evaporation pans burns badly ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Ammonia Reek:** Turning and leaching ripe beds in still air sickens the lungs and eyes (disadvantage on checks for an hour; $1\text{d}4$ damage per hour in closed sheds).
* **Beds Slump:** A wall of wet nitrous earth gives way onto a digger ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and buried until dug out).
* **Spoiled Liquor Fire:** Dry crystal dust near the boiling house hearth can flash ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
