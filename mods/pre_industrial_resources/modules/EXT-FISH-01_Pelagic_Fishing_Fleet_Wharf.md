# Pelagic Fishing Fleet Wharf (`EXT-FISH-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FISH` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The pelagic fishing wharf is the home port for drift-netters, busses, and open handline skiffs working coastal shoals. Using `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` for nets and rigging alongside `Timber: Sawn-Lumber` for hulls and stages, crews bring home `Food: Fresh-Catch` to waiting cleaning tables.

Boats shoot long drift nets at dusk, hung from floats so herring gill themselves in the mesh, and haul through the night. The season opens after midsummer and runs three or four voyages before autumn gales close the sea. Larger busses gib, salt, and barrel at sea while tenders ferry barrels, salt, and coin out to the fleet. Hemp nets rot quickly in salt water, so the wharf's loft never stops splicing and tarring.
- **Drift Nets & Net Loft:** Tarred hemp gillnets hauled at night and mended on the loft frames.
- **Fish-Handling Pier & Gutting Tables:** Timber stage where baskets land and catch is sorted before salting.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[3, 1, 4]` — **Communal Cooperative** (`Clan`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Pelagic fishing relies on tight-knit maritime crews sharing extreme physical risks, fostering profound internal solidarity and mutual trust.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Freshly Landed Pelagic Catch** (`Commodity`): Silver-scaled herring and mackerel hauled straight from coastal drift nets, awaiting gutting and salting.
- **Open Fishing Skiff** (`Tool`): Lightweight clinker-built open wooden boat, rowed or sailed, used for coastal netting and handlining.
- **Hemp Drift Net** (`Tool`): Floated and weighted gillnet of tarred hemp, hung across the path of the shoal and hauled at night.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Shoal Abundance` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, overfishing and shifting thermal currents leave the nets empty. At `8–10`, glut catches overwhelm wharf offloading capacity and spoil on the docks.
- **Quest Hook — *The Torn Trawl***: Skipper Barnaby Drake urgently needs advanced net repairs after a deep-water snag shreds half the fleet's primary trawling gear right before a major run of mackerel. Corinna Carne says the hemp is short, Osric Holm has no coin for more, and the shoal moves on in three days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Rigs and nets the fleet with `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` from the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)**, and builds piers, hulls and gutting tables from `Timber: Sawn-Lumber` supplied by the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Lands `Food: Fresh-Catch` and passes it straight to the **Salting Stages & Smokehouse (`IND-FISH-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The hours between landing and salting. Fresh fish keep little more than a day, so whoever delays the carts decides what is lost.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Autumn Gale:** A squall catches a loaded boat on the shoals ($2\text{d}6$ damage to the crew and a chance the boat founders).
* **Fouled Net Line:** A hand caught in a running net or hawser is dragged overboard or crushed against the rail ($1\text{d}8$ damage).
* **Brine Cuts:** Knife wounds on the gutting tables fester when soaked in salt and slime, imposing a cumulative penalty to grip until treated.
