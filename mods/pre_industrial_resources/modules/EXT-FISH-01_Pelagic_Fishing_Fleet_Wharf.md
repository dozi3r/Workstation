# Pelagic Fishing Fleet Wharf (`EXT-FISH-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FISH` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The pelagic fishing wharf serves as the bustling home port for deep-water trawlers and handline skiffs and open boats. Using heavy hemp-cordage rigging and timber-built fish-handling piers, local crews launch daily expeditions into coastal shoals. Returning vessels offload massive quantities of fresh-catch directly to waiting cleaning tables. The required inputs of Naval: Hemp-Cordage and Timber: Sawn-Lumber keep boats and nets sound, yielding Food: Fresh-Catch.

The working boats shoot long drift nets at dusk, hung from floats and weighted along the foot so the herring gill themselves in the mesh, and haul them through the night. The season opens only after midsummer, when the fish are fit to eat, and runs three or four voyages before autumn weather closes in. Big busses gib, salt and barrel at sea, while supply vessels ferry barrels, salt and coin out to the fleet and carry full casks home. Hemp nets rot unless they are tended, so the wharf never stops mending.
- **Drift Nets:** floated and weighted hemp gillnets, hauled at night.
- **Fish-Handling Pier:** timber stage where baskets come ashore.
- **Gutting Tables:** where catch is sorted before it moves to the salting stages.
- **Net Loft:** where hemp line is spliced, mended and tarred.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Shoal Abundance` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, overfishing and shifting thermal currents leave the nets empty. At `8–10`, glut catches overwhelm wharf offloading capacity and spoil on the docks.
- **Quest Hook — *The Torn Trawl***: Skipper Barnaby Drake urgently needs advanced net repairs after a deep-water snag shreds half the fleet's primary trawling gear right before a major run of mackerel. Corinna Vane says the hemp is short, Osric Holm has no coin for more, and the shoal moves on in three days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Rigs and nets the fleet with `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` from the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)**, and builds piers, hulls and gutting tables from `Timber: Sawn-Lumber` supplied by the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Lands `Food: Fresh-Catch` and passes it straight to the **Salting Stages & Smokehouse (`IND-FISH-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The hours between landing and salting. Fresh fish keep little more than a day, so whoever delays the carts decides what is lost.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Autumn Gale:** A squall catches a loaded boat on the shoals ($2\text{d}6$ damage to the crew and a chance the boat founders).
* **Fouled Net Line:** A hand caught in a running net or hawser is dragged overboard or crushed against the rail ($1\text{d}8$ damage).
* **Brine Cuts:** Knife wounds on the gutting tables fester when soaked in salt and slime, imposing a cumulative penalty to grip until treated.
