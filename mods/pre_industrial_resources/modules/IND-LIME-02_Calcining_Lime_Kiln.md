# Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-LIME` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Limestone blocks are stacked in a stout, refractory-lined masonry shaft kiln over a roaring wood or charcoal fire, driven by natural draught from a low-slung flue. Intense dry heat decomposes the calcium carbonate into quicklime, which workers carefully rake out and quench with water in slaking troughs to yield smooth, reactive slaked lime putty for construction. The required `Stone: Cut-Limestone` and `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` are burned to yield `Chem: Slaked-Lime`.

The stone is broken to lumps of 20 to 60 mm, fines are rejected, and the lumps are stacked in dome-shaped layers with fuel over the eye. A batch is loaded in a day, fired for about three, cooled for two and drawn on the last, so the gang rotates through the week; early kilns burned half a tonne or more of coal per tonne of lime. Burn quality is judged by trial and error. Putty is matured in the trough, and kept under water it keeps indefinitely.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Draft Intensity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, smothered fires produce uncalcined core stones. At `8–10`, thermal runaway vitrifies and ruins the kiln lining, and dead-burned lime is left that will not slake.
- **Quest Hook — *The Fused Draft***: When wet limestone clogs the primary flue, Everard must prevent the clogged shaft from cracking its masonry lining before the whole kiln has to be rebuilt. Maud Sibley and Piers Calloway have three days to clear the flue before the weekly mortar order is missed.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy

* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The fuel cart. A week without charcoal or coal idles every lime-hungry trade downstream.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Quicklime Burns:** Dust or splash on moist skin, eyes or lungs causes caustic burns ($1\text{d}6$ damage and risk of blindness).
* **Slaking Heat:** A trough quenched too fast boils and spits ($2\text{d}6$ scalding damage and nearby straw may catch fire).
* **Kiln Collapse:** A cracked lining or clogged flue bursts the shaft ($3\text{d}6$ damage to anyone at the eye).
