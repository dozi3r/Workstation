# Cane Crushing Mill and Boiling House (`AGRI-SUGAR-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SUGAR` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The crushing mill and boiling house forms the industrial core of sugar manufacture, extracting sticky juice between iron rollers and evaporating it across a cascading furnace train.

In crop time the mill and boiling house run through the night in shifts, because cut cane spoils within a day or two. The work is done largely by enslaved hands, and the skilled posts of boiler and skimmer carry real status and real danger. Syrup is poured into inverted conical clay pots and drains molasses for about thirty days.

* **Three-Roller Iron Mill:** Heavy grooved rollers driven by an undershot waterwheel or mule sweep. Stalks fed into the nip are crushed under immense mechanical pressure, expelling foamy green sap.
* **The Jamaica Boiling Train:** Five descending copper cauldrons built over a flue-fired firebrick furnace. The juice is clarified with quicklime, skimmed of scum, and ladled down the train until it granulates in the strike pan.
* **Curing Hogshead Loft:** Open-beamed timber loft where conical clay pots drain black molasses from the crystalline sugar loaves.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Molasses Vat** (`Commodity`): Black drainings from the curing pots, sold on or sent to the still house.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Boiling Battery` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, a gentle simmer yields only pale thin syrup and idle copper. At `8-10`, a high-Brix boil scorches, and a boilover or caramel fire can maim the shift and ruin the batch.
- **Quest Hook - *The Night Shift Boilover***: Kasia Vorn warns that the Level 3 strike is scorching because the fuel is short, Halsted Brann refuses to let the shift stand down, and Adaeze Morrow's crew has three nights before the next hogshead count. If the party cannot clear the flue or find fuel, the train will boil over.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Roller Nip Trapping:** Touching the moving rollers risks dragging loose clothing or limbs into the gears ($2\text{d}10$ crushing damage).
* **Scalding Sugar Slurry:** Spilling boiling syrup causes agonizing thermal burns ($3\text{d}6$ fire damage) that adhere to skin like pitch.
* **Crop-Time Fatigue:** Night shifts run past exhaustion; a worker who falters at the rollers or the coppers risks a crushing or scalding accident.
