# Placer Sluice & Quartz Crush Mill (`MINE-GOLD-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-GOLD` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The gold extraction works combine water sluices for alluvial gravels with cam-driven drop-stamp quartz mills powered by overshot wheels. Prospectors shovel pay dirt or crushed quartz through riffled wooden sluices, consuming Timber: Mine-Props for flumes, shafts, and stamp frames to yield Ore: Gold-Dust.

Claims hold only while worked, with miners' meetings arbitrating disputes. Hand-fed rockers wash a few yards daily while longer sluices and Long Toms handle bulk gravel before weekly cleanup. Diggers also sink shafts into deep leads, though plain sluices lose fine flour gold: mercury works sell only to silver patios, forcing camps seeking quicksilver onto the black market.
- **Rocker and Sluice Run:** Baffled wooden boxes lined with riffles to trap heavy gold dust.
- **Stamp Battery:** Cam-lifted iron shoes pulverizing hard gold-bearing quartz ore.
- **Water Ditch:** The claim's contested lifeline feeding every sluice and wheel.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[3, 4, 4]` - **Independent Prospectors** (`Adhocracy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: The speculative, high-reward nature of gold panning and quartz crushing attracts fiercely independent, opportunistic prospectors resistant to central authority.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Quartz Ore Richness` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, barren rock yields negligible dust, bankrupting the mill. At `8-10`, rich pocket strikes yield heavy flakes and nuggets, and a rush of newcomers crowds the ground and tempts claim-jumpers.
- **Quest Hook - *The Jumped Claim***: A rival prospector syndicate fences off Silas's main water ditch, threatening armed violence over water rights right before the seasonal cleanup; Godwin Pennywell will call the miners' meeting in three days, and Silas must bring witnesses.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Shores flumes, shafts and stamp frames with `Timber: Mine-Props` from the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Carries `Ore: Gold-Dust` to the **Assay Furnace & Goldsmith Bourse (`IND-GOLD-02`)** for parting and stamping.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The water ditch. Whoever controls the water decides which claims can wash.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Shaft Cave-In:** Unpropped deep workings collapse on diggers ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and buried).
* **Stamp Battery:** A hand caught under a falling stamp is crushed ($2\text{d}6$ damage and the hand is lost).
* **Claim Violence:** Disputes at the claim stake turn into pistol or knife fights ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
