# Civic Abattoir (`IND-CATTLE-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CATTLE` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A stone-paved, channeled drainage building situated near a swift river comprises slaughter pens, hoisting gantries, and boiling cauldrons. Guild butchers process incoming cattle swiftly, harvesting edible meat, stripping raw hides for tanneries, and rendering fat into tallow. This sanitary civic facility converts living livestock into perishable food stores and vital industrial raw materials. Bones stripped of meat are boiled clean and calcined in a kiln into white bone ash, sold on to refiners for lining their cupel hearths.

Town rules say offal goes to the appointed pits or stream, not the street, yet the law is often evaded. Hides are salted at once, flesh scraps sold on to glue and soap boilers, and tallow rendered in the vats. Fat goes to chandlers and soap boilers, who each bid for it.
- **Slaughter Hall:** channeled paving with hoisting gantry.
- **Rendering Vats:** boiling the fat from the bones and trimmings.
- **Bone Kiln:** calcines boiled bone to white ash.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

- **Sociological Rationale**: Dealing with biohazardous waste and strict municipal health ordinances, the abattoir enforces strict sanitary protocols and rigid batch accountability.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Hoisting Gantry and Tackle** (`Tool`): Heavy wooden pulley rig used to suspend carcasses for efficient bleeding and skinning.
- **Rendering Vats** (`Tool`): Large cast-iron cauldrons for melting animal fat into usable industrial tallow.
- **Bone Ash** (`Commodity`): Fine white powder from calcined cattle bones, tamped into cupel hearths for assaying and silver refining.
- **Salted Hide** (`Commodity`): Raw hide cured in salt, bundled for the tanyard.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Sanitation and Drainage` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, bloodclogged drains attract vermin and threaten city health inspectors. At `8–10`, over-processing overwhelms the boiling vats, creating foul stenches that spark neighborhood riots.
- **Quest Hook — *The Blocked Sluice***: Waste clogs the abattoir's primary river sluice just as a massive cattle drive arrives from the ranches. Barnaby and Jessamyn must clear the organic blockage before civic magistrates suspend their operating charter, and Beadle Edda Marsh is already on her way to inspect it.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Slaughters `Livestock: Cattle` driven in from the **Pasture Ranch (`FARM-CATTLE-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Animal: Raw-Hides` to the **Tanning Yards (`IND-CATTLE-03`)**; sends `Animal: Tallow` to the **Cane Crushing Mill and Boiling House (`AGRI-SUGAR-02`)**, the **River Barge Slipway & Canal Lock (`IND-BARGE-01`)**, the **Bell & Artillery Bronze Foundry (`IND-BRONZE-01`)**, the **Tallow Chandlery (`IND-CANDLE-01`)** and the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**; sends `Material: Bone-Ash` to the **Cupellation Hearth & Sovereign Mint (`IND-SILVER-03`)** for lining its hearths; and sells `Food: Raw-Beef` as an end product.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The river sluice. If it blocks, the whole abattoir stops.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Slaughter Floor:** A slipped carcass or a kicking beast crushes ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Rendering Vat:** Spilled boiling fat scalds ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage) and can ignite.
* **Carrion Fever:** Working the offal pits without care brings sickness (disadvantage on checks until cured).
