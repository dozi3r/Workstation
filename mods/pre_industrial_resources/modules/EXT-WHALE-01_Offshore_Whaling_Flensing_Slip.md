# Offshore Whaling Flensing Slip (`EXT-WHALE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-WHALE` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The offshore whaling flensing slip is an inclined stone ramp jutting into tidal waters, equipped with heavy capstans, hemp-cordage blocks, and forged-iron flensing spades. When leviathans are towed ashore by whaling crews, workers winch the massive carcasses onto the slip to strip away thick layers of blubber and harvest flexible baleen plates. It runs on `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` and `Metal: Forged-Iron` and yields `Animal: Whale-Blubber` and `Material: Baleen`.

A watchman in a headland tower signals the town by fire, drum or flag, and light boats go out; a struck whale tires and is towed in by a larger boat of ten. Blubber is cut in strips with long-handled spades from a cutting stage, and baleen is pulled from the gum, scrubbed with sand and water, polished and bundled.
- **Lookout Tower:** Stone watch above the harbor, manned all season.
- **Cutting Stage & Slip:** Planks over the shallows where the carcass is turned and stripped.
- **Capstan Frame:** Hemp tackle and iron blocks that turn the carcass.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Raw Whale Blubber** (`Commodity`): Thick, fat-rich adipose tissue cut in strips for oil rendering at the tryworks.
- **Flensing Spade** (`Tool`): Long-handled razor-sharp iron blade used to slice thick cetacean hide and blubber.
- **Bundled Baleen** (`Commodity`): Polished flexible whalebone plates, washed and tied for the trade in stays and ribs.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Carcass Recovery Rate` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, stormy surf makes beaching impossible, causing carcasses to rot offshore. At `8–10`, a glut of whales overruns the slip, leading to putrid waste buildup and public health hazards.
- **Quest Hook — *The Broken Capstan***: Caleb Stark faces a race against the rising tide when the main iron winch snaps while hauling a massive right whale onto the slip. Nora Kincaid has one spare iron pin, Idony Vael's rival boat is already tugging at the carcass, and the tide turns in three hours.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Sliding Carcass:** A rolling carcass on the cutting stage can crush or drag a flenser into the surf ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Rope Snap:** A parted hemp or iron tackle line whips across the slip ($1\text{d}8$ slashing damage).
