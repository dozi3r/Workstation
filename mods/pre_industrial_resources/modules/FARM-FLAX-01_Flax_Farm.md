# Flax Farm (`FARM-FLAX-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FLAX` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Flax requires damp, fertile bottomlands where blue-flowered stalks grow tall without branching. Rather than cutting, village gangs pull ripe plants entirely by the roots to preserve full fiber length, stooking sheaves to dry. With no upstream inputs, this cultivation yields fibrous `Crop: Raw-Flax` for industrial retting.

Ground is plowed in winter and raked fine before oily seed is sown thick in April; the crop ripens in roughly a hundred days without weeding. When leaves yellow and seed browns, a few fair days decide the year: stalks left to brown in the field degrade, while ripened seed bolls ride to the rippling comb at the retting basin.
- **Bottomland Field & Drying Ground:** Deep, finely harrowed loam paired with stooking grounds for pulled sheaves.
- **Seed Store:** Loft holding ripened seed bolls for next April's sowing.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Harvest Timing (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, premature harvesting yields weak, underdeveloped fibers. At `8–10`, precise yellowing triggers maximum tensile strength, but a delay of days lets the stalks brown and the fibre degrade.
- **Quest Hook — *The Premature Storm***: An unseasonal autumn squall threatens to flatten the ripe flax fields, forcing Agnes Rudd to rally reluctant villagers for frantic round-the-clock pulling before stalks rot in the mud. Tobias Garrow insists the landlord's share is pulled first; the squall arrives in two days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The farm grows from its own saved seed and bottomland and `requires` no upstream module.
* **Output Transmission:** Carries bundled sheaves of `Crop: Raw-Flax` to the **Retting & Scutching Basin (`IND-FLAX-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The pulling window. A few fair days decide the grade of the whole year's cloth.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Back Strain:** Days of bent pulling cripple the gang ($1\text{d}4$ damage and a penalty to labor checks until rested).
* **Mud Squall:** A storm turns the bottomland to mire and carts stick fast, ruining sheaves left in the field.
* **Seed Moisture:** Damp seed in the loft molds and ruins next year's sowing.
