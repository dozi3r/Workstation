# Flax Farm (`FARM-FLAX-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FLAX` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Flax cultivation demands damp, fertile bottomlands where delicate blue-flowered stalks can grow tall without branching. Laborers painstakingly pull the mature plants up entirely by the roots rather than cutting them to preserve maximum fiber length, bundling them into sheaves for drying. This delicate agricultural extraction yields fibrous raw flax ready for industrial retting.

The ground is plowed in winter and early spring and raked fine, then the oily seed is sown broadcast in April, thick so that stalks do not branch. The crop needs no weeding and ripens in about a hundred days. When the leaves yellow and the seed browns, village gangs pull it by the root and spread it to dry, so a few days of fair weather decide the year. Fiber flax that turns brown in the field degrades, while seed bolls are left on the stalks for the rippling comb at the retting basin.
- **Bottomland Field:** deep loam, plowed, harrowed and raked fine.
- **Drying Ground:** where pulled stalks are spread and then stooked.
- **Seed Store:** loft where ripened bolls are kept for next year's sowing.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Harvest Timing (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, premature harvesting yields weak, underdeveloped fibers. At `8–10`, precise yellowing triggers maximum tensile strength, but a delay of days lets the stalks brown and the fibre degrade.
- **Quest Hook — *The Premature Storm***: An unseasonal autumn squall threatens to flatten the ripe flax fields, forcing Lyra to rally reluctant villagers for frantic round-the-clock pulling before stalks rot in the mud. Tobias Garrow insists the landlord's share is pulled first; the squall arrives in two days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The farm grows from its own saved seed and bottomland and `requires` no upstream module.
* **Output Transmission:** Carries bundled sheaves of `Crop: Raw-Flax` to the **Retting & Scutching Basin (`IND-FLAX-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The pulling window. A few fair days decide the grade of the whole year's cloth.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Back Strain:** Days of bent pulling cripple the gang ($1\text{d}4$ damage and a penalty to labor checks until rested).
* **Mud Squall:** A storm turns the bottomland to mire and carts stick fast, ruining sheaves left in the field.
* **Seed Moisture:** Damp seed in the loft molds and ruins next year's sowing.
