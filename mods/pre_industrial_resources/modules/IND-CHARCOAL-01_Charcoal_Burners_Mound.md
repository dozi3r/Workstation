# Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CHARCOAL` (Stage 1: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A series of conical earth-covered timber stacks smolder deep in forest clearings, managed by experienced colliers who carefully regulate oxygen flow to pyrolyze cordwood into high-grade charcoal. Workers stack split logs vertically around a central chimney stake, seal the mound with turf and loam, and ignite the core. This controlled, oxygen-starved thermal conversion transforms raw wood fuel into dense, high-temperature charcoal and byproduct potash.

A collier lives in a hut from spring to autumn and tends eight or nine mounds with one or two helpers. A pit "comes to foot" in ten to fourteen days, and night watches never stop. A single furnace eats thousands of cords of wood a year. Ashes from the burns are leached in vats and the lye boiled to potash for soapmakers and glassmakers.
- **Hearth:** level cleared ground thirty to forty feet across.
- **Chimney Pole:** central flue in the stack.
- **Leaching Vat:** where ash lye is drawn and boiled down.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Mound Oxygenation` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, smothered fires produce uncarbonized white wood. At `8–10`, blowouts rupture the earth seal, incinerating the entire batch.
- **Quest Hook — *The Midnight Blowout***: A sudden gale force wind tears the loam seal off Brenna's largest charcoal mound at midnight. She must rouse Jory and nearby workers to patch the glowing breach before the wind completely consumes the winter fuel store, and Furnace Clerk Ambrose Teague's wagon comes at dawn.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Mound Collapse:** Treading a hollowed pile drops a worker into glowing coals ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Smoke and Fumes:** Working the mounds or the charcoal pit without relief brings choking and dizziness (disadvantage on checks until rested).
* **Wildfire:** A blown ember sets the forest alight, threatening the camp and the standing timber.
