# Water-Powered Gristmill (`IND-WHEAT-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-WHEAT` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A rushing stream drives a massive timber undershot waterwheel, transferring kinetic rotational energy through iron-shod axle shafts to heavy vertical millstones. Raw wheat grain is fed steadily from overhead wooden hoppers down between the grinding surfaces, where shearing friction pulverizes the kernels into fine meal before bolting screens sift out the coarse bran. This mechanical mill transforms bulky agricultural harvests into refined flour. The mill takes `Crop: Raw-Wheat` and iron fittings (`Metal: Forged-Iron`) and yields `Food: Wheat-Flour`.

Water is led along a leat from the pond and metered by a sluice gate. Grain drops from bins through the hopper into a shaken trough that feeds the turning runner stone above a fixed bed stone; the miller sets the gap between them (tentering) to choose the fineness, and flour leaves through grooves at the rim. The miller is paid by a toll taken from every grist, and the mill itself was often built and owned by the lord.
- **Leat and Sluice:** channel and gate that govern the wheel.
- **Stone Floor:** paired runner and bed stones.
- **Grain Loft:** bins feeding the hopper.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Stone Clearance (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, stones grind too close, sparking fire and scorching flour. At `8–10`, optimal clearance yields premium silky flour, but the stones wear fast and the lord demands a higher toll on the finer yield.
- **Quest Hook — *The Cracked Runner Stone***: When the main granite millstone fractures during peak harvest grinding, Silas must broker with local miners for emergency steel reinforcement before restless farmers riot. Mill-Reeve Hugh Aldecott suspects Silas of hiding toll grain and threatens to take the lease; the party has until the next market day before the village carries its grain to a rival mill.

- **Threat Clock — *Damp Grain Mold Clock [4 Ticks]***: Wet rot ruins winter grain stores if sluice humidity rises.
- **Threat Clock — *Mill Dam Wear Clock [6 Ticks]***: Floodwater vibrations weaken the heavy timber weir and stone sluice gates.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Grinds `Crop: Raw-Wheat` from the **Open-Field Wheat Farm (`FARM-WHEAT-01`)**; fits its axle bands, gudgeons and picks with `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Food: Wheat-Flour` to the **Civic Bakehouse (`IND-WHEAT-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The leat and sluice. Whoever holds the water decides whether the stones turn.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Drive Gears:** A hand or sleeve caught in the wheel-driven gears is mangled ($2\text{d}6$ damage and the limb is lost).
* **Scorched Stones:** Stones run too close or dry throw sparks into the dusty loft ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and a spreading blaze).
* **Flood at the Sluice:** A swollen leat sweeps a worker off the wheel pit ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a drowning risk).
