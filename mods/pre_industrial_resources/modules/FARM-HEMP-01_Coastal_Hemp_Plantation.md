# Coastal Hemp Plantation (`FARM-HEMP-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-HEMP` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Coastal hemp plantations cultivate deep-rooted *Cannabis sativa* in fertile alluvial floodplains near tidal estuaries. Field laborers sow dense seedbeds that grow rapidly into tall, fibrous stalks. Once mature, workers harvest the crops by hand, binding them into bundles and submerging them in retting ponds to loosen the inner bast fibers from the woody core.

The crop is sown in spring and matures in three to four months; it is cut toward the end of flowering. Bundles lie in a pond for a few days to a few weeks, until they feel slimy and the woody core springs away from the fibre. Leave them too long and the fibre is ruined. Navies buy most of the crop, so the estate lives by its contracts.
- **Seedbeds:** Dense fields on the floodplain.
- **Retting Ponds:** Earthen pits filled by creek sluices; the stench carries.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Cut Hemp Stalks** (`Commodity`): Tall, fibrous plant stems harvested for maritime cordage and textile production.
- **Retting Pit** (`Tool`): Earthen pool where harvested stalks undergo water-rotting to separate fibers.
- **Retted Bundle** (`Commodity`): A tied sheaf lifted from the pond when the bast feels slimy and the core springs away.
- **Creek Sluice Gate** (`Tool`): Timber gate that lets tidal creek water into the ponds and holds it there.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Fiber Tenacity & Growth` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, soil salinity spikes stunt growth, yielding brittle, weak fibers. At `8–10`, ideal conditions produce massive bumper crops that overwhelm local retting capacity.
- **Quest Hook — *The Stagnant Sluice***: Piran Tregear finds the main irrigation channel blocked by silt, threatening to dry out the vital retting ponds before harvest processing begins. Factor Odalric Penhale arrives with the navy's tonnage due in two weeks, and Fieldreeve Mabyn Cobb needs the channel cleared before the autumn rains rot the stalks standing in the field.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. Hemp is sown directly into floodplain seedbeds and needs no supply from another module; the estate relies on tidal creek water and hand labor alone.
* **Output Transmission:** Carts retted bundles of `Crop: Raw-Hemp-Stalks` to the **Hemp Braking & Hackling Shed (`IND-HEMP-02`)**, where the fibre is broken and hackled.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The retting ponds. When the sluices silt up or the creek is fouled, the whole hemp chain stops at its first stage.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Retting Pond Stench:** Working the ponds in warm weather brings sickness and fever to the crew ($1\text{d}6$ damage and fatigue until a day's rest).
* **Soft Pond Banks:** A bank gives way under a laden cart or a tired retter and drops them into foul water ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a risk of infection).
* **Fouled Creek:** Pond water let into the creek sickens downstream villagers and livestock, and the plantation's name suffers.
