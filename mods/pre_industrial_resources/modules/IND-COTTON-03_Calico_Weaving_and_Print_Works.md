# Calico Weaving & Print Works (`IND-COTTON-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-COTTON` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The calico weaving and print works is a bustling urban workshop lined with pit looms, wooden block-printing tables, and steaming vats of mordant and dye. Weavers interlace spun cotton yarn into plain-cloth fabric, which is subsequently stamped with intricate geometric patterns using carved wooden blocks soaked in alum mordant and indigo paste. Spun yarn, indigo dye, and alum inputs are synthesized into vibrant, colorfast dyed-calico fabrics. The works also burns Fuel: Wood-Fuel under the mordant cauldrons.

Grey cloth is scoured, sun-dried and treated before printing. Printers press carved blocks of mordant or mud-resist onto it, dip it in indigo and let it breathe in the air, and then beat the dried paste off on the river stones. Buyers favor different grounds and designs, so the print tables change patterns by order.
- **Pit Looms:** weavers sit with their legs in a floor pit.
- **Print Tables:** teak or sheesham blocks pressed onto stretched cloth.
- **Washing Stones:** where cloth is soaked and beaten clean.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Mordant Bleed` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, alum holds lines crisp and sharp. At `8-10`, contaminated mordant bleeds across threads, blurring printed patterns into muddy smears.
- **Quest Hook - *The Blurry Block***: Master Printer Veda Sharma discovers that a batch of adulterated alum is causing dye bleed across the imperial export bolts. Export Bolt Inspector Anselm Rowe will reject the whole consignment at the end of the week unless she and Vat Keeper Kasim Rangrez can prove which sack was cut.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Weaves `Textile: Spun-Cotton-Yarn` from the **Roller Gin & Spinning House (`IND-COTTON-02`)**; dips cloth in `Dye: Indigo-Paste` from the **Steeping Vat & Oxidation Beating Ponds (`IND-INDIGO-02`)**; fixes the pattern with `Chem: Mordant-Alum` from the **Alunite Quarry & Leaching Vats (`EXT-ALUM-01`)**; and heats the cauldrons with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** `Textile: Dyed-Calico` is an end product, sold in bolts to merchant companies.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The alum sack. Weak or cut mordant ruins every bolt on the table.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Scalding Vat:** A tipped mordant cauldron scalds those near it ($2\text{d}6$ damage).
* **Dye-Stained Hands:** Hours in the vats stain and crack the skin, and a rash may follow (-1 to fine work).
* **Loom Damp:** A pit that floods in the rains leaves weavers with fever and joint pain ($1\text{d}4$ damage over a week).
