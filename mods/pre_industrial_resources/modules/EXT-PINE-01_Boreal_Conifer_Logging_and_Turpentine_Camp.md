# Boreal Conifer Logging & Turpentine Camp (`EXT-PINE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PINE` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Spread across dense, cold-climate evergreen forests, logging crews fell straight-grained old-growth pines for structural spars while simultaneously scarring living trunks with V-shaped 'cat faces' to bleed amber resin into hanging terracotta cups. Raw oleoresin and trimmed timber are hauled by sled to frozen riverbanks for spring thaw transport. The camp needs no inputs, and its two outputs are Timber: Mast-Spars for the shipyards and Resin: Raw-Pine-Resin for the tar kiln.

A tapper cuts each face with a hack down to the cambium, adding a new streak about eight inches long above the last until the scar stands over thirty inches tall, while the cup or box ledge below catches the sap. Mast timber is cut from a single straight trunk, then squared and trimmed by the mastmakers. A crown woods surveyor strikes three axe blows, a broad arrow, on reserved trees, and a factor advances tools and goods against the barrels delivered.
- **Cat-Face Rows:** tapped pines with hanging cups and a ledge for the sap.
- **Log Deck:** squared spars piled beside the sled road to wait for the thaw.
- **Landing:** the riverbank where timber and barrels are rafted out.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Raw Pine Resin** (`Commodity`): Viscous, aromatic amber sap harvested from scarred conifer trunks.
- **Broad Axe** (`Tool`): Heavy felling and squaring axe balanced for precision notching in sub-zero weather.
- **Mast Spar** (`Commodity`): A straight squared pine trunk cut for a ship's mast, rafted to the yards at the spring thaw.
- **Tapping Hack** (`Tool`): Curved blade that strips bark to the cambium and cuts each new streak above the last.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Bark Scarring Depth` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, shallow cuts yield meager resin flow. At `8-10`, deep slashing girdles and kills the pine trees prematurely, and a dead stand loses the camp its future spar timber.
- **Quest Hook - *The Frozen Haul***: When an early thaw threatens to strand the winter log decks before river floating, Torstein must mobilize every hand to raft the timber overnight. Woods Surveyor Halvard Brenn is on the road with his marking axe and the landing will be unusable in four days; Factor Saskia Orwell wants her barrels on the first raft, and Elenora Frost's crew has not been paid.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The camp has no `requires` tags and works from standing forest, its own axes and the winter sled road.
* **Output Transmission:** Floats `Timber: Mast-Spars` to the **Seasoning Timber Pond & Sawpit (`IND-SHIP-01`)** and the **Admiralty Fitting-Out Basin (`IND-SHIP-03`)**, and carries barrels of `Resin: Raw-Pine-Resin` to the **Tar Kiln & Pitch Boilery (`IND-PINE-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The river landing. Whoever holds the thaw-time rafting decides which yards get masts.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Felling Accident:** A pine kicks back off the stump or a hung limb falls on the crew ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Winter Exposure:** Tapping and hauling in deep cold without shelter or fire causes frostbite ($1\text{d}6$ damage per hour and numb hands that penalize fine work).
* **Resin Fire:** A spark in the cup rows or resin barrels flashes into a sticky fire ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage and burning resin clings to clothing).
