# Alunite Quarry & Leaching Vats (`EXT-ALUM-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-ALUM` (Stage 1: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Volcanic alunite rock is quarried from arid hillsides, broken into manageable fragments, and roasted in specialized kilns to drive off sulfur gases. The calcined rock is then steeped in masonry leaching tanks filled with water, and the clear liquor is drawn off to lead boiling pans, where it is boiled down and left to precipitate pure alum crystals upon cooling for use as an essential textile dyeing mordant. This sentence also covers the `Chem: Mordant-Alum` output and the `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` the kilns and boiling pans devour.

The works is a monopoly holding: a distant treasury takes direct control, a factor sells the alum to dyers and clothiers, and a workers' village grows beside the pit. Liquor settles in pits and is drawn off in troughs or sealed casks to the boiling house. The site needs abundant wood and a nearby port, and the fires strip the surrounding hills.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

- **Sociological Rationale**: Chemical refining demands strict temperature control and careful crystallization timing, fostering a methodical and disciplined workforce.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Masonry Leaching Tank** (`Tool`): Mortared stone tank where calcined alunite rock is steeped in water; the liquor then goes to lead boiling pans.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Roasting Heat` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, under-roasted alunite fails to leach into soluble alum. At `8-10`, excessive heat decomposes the mineral into useless alumina and floods the compound with sulfur gas.
- **Quest Hook - *The Sulfur Leak***: When a cracked roasting kiln releases choking sulfur dioxide across the compound, Cosimo must seal the breach before local authorities shut down the operation. Nell Coverdale is overcome, Factor Ludovic Carrow's audit arrives within the week, and the party has four ticks of gas to act.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes cartloads of `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)** to keep the roasting kilns and boiling pans burning.
* **Output Transmission:** Dispatches casks of `Chem: Mordant-Alum` to the **Calico Weaving & Print Works (`IND-COTTON-03`)**, the **Paper Vat & Screw-Press Loft (`IND-PAPER-02`)** and the **Master Draper's Guildhall (`IND-SHEEP-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** Whoever controls the alum casks controls the dye vats of every textile guild that depends on the works.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Sulfur Fumes:** Standing near a roasting kiln at Level 3 or higher without wet cloth over the face inflicts choking damage ($1\text{d}6$ per hour) and clouds the lungs.
* **Boiling Liquor Scald:** Hot alum liquor in the vats and troughs scalds anyone who falls in ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
