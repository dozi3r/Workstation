# Mulberry Orchard & Silkworm Nursery (`FARM-SILK-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SILK` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The mulberry orchard and silkworm nursery features rows of pruned Morus alba trees surrounding a climate-controlled bamboo rearing shed. Sericulture attendants meticulously harvest tender mulberry leaves to feed voracious silkworm larvae through multiple instars until they spin protective cocoons. Unprocessed inputs are zero, while harvested silk-cocoons are sorted by quality and delivered to filature reeling basins.

The larvae moult several times, and after the fourth moult they climb twigs to spin for two or three days. About 2,500 worms are needed for a pound of raw silk and each cocoon holds roughly 1,000 yards of filament. Sheds are dusted with slaked lime to hold back fungus, and the cocoons are killed with heat before the moth can break them.
- **Mulberry Grove:** leaf for the larvae.
- **Rearing Racks:** trays of larvae in the shed.
- **Spinning Frames:** twigs where the worms spin cocoons.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Spun Silk Cocoons** (`Commodity`): Oval proteinaceous casings spun by Bombyx mori silkworms, rich in continuous raw filament.
- **Bamboo Rearing Tray** (`Tool`): Woven slatted tray used to house silkworm caterpillars during their feeding stages.
- **Slaked Lime Sack** (`Reagent`): Powder dusted over trays and racks to discourage fungal growth.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Nursery Humidity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, warm, dry air keeps silkworms active and healthy. At `8–10`, damp stagnant air breeds fungal muscardine, killing entire trays of caterpillars overnight.
- **Quest Hook — *The Fungal Blight***: Matron Mei-Ling alerts Pruner Lao Chen that damp mold is spreading across the upper bamboo rearing racks. Cocoon Broker Jun-Ho Baek will pay only for clean cocoons, and the crop must be sorted and spun before the damp spreads in three days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The nursery lives on its own mulberry leaf and egg stock, with no `requires` tags from other modules.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Animal: Silk-Cocoons` to the **Filature Reeling Basin (`IND-SILK-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The leaf and the weather. A damp week or a failed flush can wipe out a season.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Fungal Spores:** Handling infected racks fouls hands and clothing and spreads the blight to clean trays ($1\text{d}4$ poison damage to the careless and the infection spreads).
* **Tree Fall:** A heavy bough gives way under a picker ($1\text{d}6$ falling damage).
* **Stifling Heat:** Killing cocoons in the heated shed overwhelms workers in poor air ($1\text{d}4$ fire damage and a level of fatigue).
