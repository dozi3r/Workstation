# Bark Crushing Mill (`IND-TANNIN-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TANNIN` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A rustic timber-framed water- or animal-powered pounder mill utilizes heavy stone stamps or iron-shod wooden beams to pulverize coarse oak and hemlock tree bark into a fibrous powder. Workers shovel raw bark into troughs where rhythmic crushing breaks down cellular structures, preparing the material for hot-water leaching. This mechanical transformation turns raw, bulky forest litter into concentrated tanning extract essential for hide preservation.

Bark mills were driven by water, horses or oxen, and the work chopped, ground, riddled (sifted) and pounded; powdering lets water draw out the tannin much more easily. Dry bark waits in a bark house until the mill is run, and the iron fittings on stamps, bands and cams come from the smith.
- **Bark House:** Dry store of bundles awaiting the stamps.
- **Stamp Troughs & Riddle:** Where bark is pounded and sifted into powder.
- **Leach Vats:** Wooden tanks where the powder steeps in hot water.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Stamp Pressure & Moisture` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, bark is insufficiently crushed, lowering tannin yield. At `8–10`, damp bark jams the stamps, splintering the wooden drive cams.
- **Quest Hook — *The Splintered Cam***: Master Alistair discovers a cracked wooden drive cam just as a large shipment of oak bark arrives. He enlists Giles and local adventurers to source seasoned heartwood before the milling schedule stalls entirely, while Factor Benedek Rowle threatens to take his contract elsewhere within the week.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes `Commodity: Tree-Bark` from the **Bark Peeling Camp (`EXT-TANNIN-01`)**, `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)** to heat the leaching water, and `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)** for iron shoes, bands, cam fittings and bearings.
* **Output Transmission:** Delivers `Chem: Tannin` to the **Tanning Yards (`IND-CATTLE-03`)**, whose tanners steep hides in bark liquor, and to the **Pelt Scraping & Furrier's Guild (`IND-FURS-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The drive cam and its iron fittings; one broken part stops all grinding.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Stamp Crush:** Reaching into the trough while the stamps fall maims a hand or arm ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Bark Dust:** Long hours at the troughs fill the lungs with fine powder, causing coughing and a penalty to endurance.
* **Scalding Liquor:** Hot leach vats splash and burn ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
