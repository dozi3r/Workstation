# Open-Cast Iron Ore Pit (`MINE-IRON-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-IRON` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The iron quarry is a terraced amphitheater cut into red bedrock, exposing veins of hematite and magnetite ore. It provides `Ore: Raw-Iron-Ore` and the co-product `Mineral: Emery-and-Rouge`, and needs `Timber: Mine-Props` to hold workings open.

Miners work in two-man teams or contracted partnerships, hauling ore up in baskets by winch while spoil refills old pits; groundwater and frost shut the lower benches.
* **Terraced Extraction Benches:** Step-cut benches where drill-and-wedge crews split multi-ton ore slabs.
* **Timber Shoring & Sump Flumes:** Heavy headframes and gravity flumes draining seepage into settling ponds.
* **Gravity Sled Runways:** Inclined timber chutes sliding ore crates to wagon staging squares.
* **Rouge & Emery Grinding Floor:** Soft hematite washed into polishing rouge and dark emery crushed into coarse grit, sold separately to lens and metal polishers at far higher rates per pound.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 2, 4]` - **Partnership Pit** (`Hierarchy / Clan`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: The pit boss and the furnace's ore contract set the quota from above, while crews bound by family, partnership and guild bond look after their own and bargain as a gang over every ton.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Raw Hematite Ore** (`Commodity`): Heavy red ore in basket and cart lots, weighed at the pit head and carried to the furnace under contract.
- **Sieved Emery and Washed Rouge** (`Commodity`): Sacks of coarse dark emery grit and fine red rouge powder, the co-product that polishers pay well for.
- **Sledge and Wedge Set** (`Tool`): The quarryman's drill steels, wedges and sledge for splitting slabs from the bench.
- **Pit Winch and Ore Basket** (`Tool`): The hand winch and baskets that lift ore from the deeper pits.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Ore Output` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, the crews are down to surface scrabbling of low-grade bog gravel and the furnace starves. At `8-10`, deep-trench blasting doubles the haul, but the benches are undercut and a slope slide can bury the lower workings.
- **Quest Hook - *The Docked Weight***: Hallam Rusk's partnership has dug a full month of ore, and the furnace clerk has docked it for dirt and short weight so that it will not pay. Quarrymaster Grim Torvald needs the tonnage to earn his crew's bond, Bera Iron-Grip warns the south bench props will not outlast the first rains, and Tamsin Redwash offers the party a share of her rouge sales if they get the ore weighed fairly before the partnership walks off the pit.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Requires constant timber shoring from `Timber: Mine-Props`, cut and delivered by the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships bulk raw ore (`Ore: Raw-Iron-Ore`) down-valley to the **Charcoal Bloomery and Blast Furnace (`MINE-IRON-02`)**. The co-product `Mineral: Emery-and-Rouge` goes out as sorted abrasive grit to polishing trades such as the **Crown & Flint Lens-Grinding Lathe (`IND-OPTIC-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The cart track is the only way ore reaches the furnace; a washed-out bridge or a road toll stops the whole chain.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Falling Rock Slides:** Working on lower terraces without lookouts risks crushing rockfalls ($2\text{d}8$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Hematite Dust Inhalation:** Dry days produce choking mineral dust clouds; prolonged labor requires dampened bandanas or inflicts lung fatigue.
* **Flooded Pits:** A deep pit can fill with groundwater faster than the siphons clear it, trapping the diggers below.
