# Open-Cast Iron Ore Pit (`MINE-IRON-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-IRON` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The iron quarry is an immense terraced amphitheater carved into red mineral bedrock, exposing thick veins of dense hematite and magnetite ore. It feeds the furnace downstream and, as a side trade, the polishers' grit that other crafts buy. It provides `Ore: Raw-Iron-Ore` and the co-product `Mineral: Emery-and-Rouge`, and it needs `Timber: Mine-Props` to hold the workings open.

Miners work in two-man teams, some on the furnace's wage list and others in partnerships that sell ore at a contracted price. Where the ore lies in pits rather than benches, each pit is dug a few men across and as deep as the ground allows, the ore is hauled up in baskets by winch, and the spoil of the next pit refills the last. Carts run ore to the furnace along a cart track, and the work follows the weather: groundwater and frost shut the lower benches.
* **Terraced Extraction Benches:** Step-cut stone benches where miners drill holes with sledge and chisel, wedging out multi-ton slabs of dark red ore.
* **Timber Shoring & Sump Flumes:** Heavy timber headframes and gravity flumes that pump out seepage groundwater into perimeter settlement settling ponds.
* **Gravity Sled Runways:** Inclined timber chutes that slide loaded ore crates down from high extraction benches directly to wagon staging squares.
* **Rouge & Emery Grinding Floor:** Soft earthy red hematite is washed and settled into fine polishing rouge, while dark emery rock found along the magnetite seams is crushed and sieved into coarse grit for lens and metal polishers. The floor is a separate small trade from the ore haul, sold by the sack at a far higher price per pound.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Sieved Emery and Washed Rouge** (`Commodity`): Sacks of coarse dark emery grit and fine red rouge powder, the co-product that polishers pay well for.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Ore Output` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, the crews are down to surface scrabbling of low-grade bog gravel and the furnace starves. At `8-10`, deep-trench blasting doubles the haul, but the benches are undercut and a slope slide can bury the lower workings.
- **Quest Hook - *The Docked Weight***: Hallam Rusk's partnership has dug a full month of ore, and the furnace clerk has docked it for dirt and short weight so that it will not pay. Foreman Grim Torvald needs the tonnage to earn his crew's bond, Bera Iron-Grip warns the south bench props will not outlast the first rains, and Tamsin Redwash offers the party a share of her rouge sales if they get the ore weighed fairly before the partnership walks off the pit.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Requires constant timber shoring from `Timber: Mine-Props`, cut and delivered by the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships bulk raw ore (`Ore: Raw-Iron-Ore`) down-valley to the **Charcoal Bloomery and Blast Furnace (`MINE-IRON-02`)**. The co-product `Mineral: Emery-and-Rouge` goes out as sorted abrasive grit to polishing trades such as the **Crown & Flint Lens-Grinding Lathe (`IND-OPTIC-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The cart track is the only way ore reaches the furnace; a washed-out bridge or a road toll stops the whole chain.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Falling Rock Slides:** Working on lower terraces without lookouts risks crushing rockfalls ($2\text{d}8$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Hematite Dust Inhalation:** Dry days produce choking mineral dust clouds; prolonged labor requires dampened bandanas or inflicts lung fatigue.
* **Flooded Pits:** A deep pit can fill with groundwater faster than the siphons clear it, trapping the diggers below.
