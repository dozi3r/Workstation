# Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TIMBER` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A rushing weir channels river current onto a large vertical waterwheel, driving a reciprocating crank mechanism that moves a vertically tensioned steel saw blade up and down. Logs are clamped onto sliding carriage frames and slowly fed into the screaming blade, slicing raw timber into uniform boards and sturdy mine props. This mechanical processing converts round logs into standardized construction materials. The required input is Timber: Hardwood-Logs, and the outputs are Timber: Sawn-Lumber and Timber: Mine-Props.

The saw runs about 120 strokes a minute, far faster than a sawpit crew, which could cut under 200 board feet a day; that speed is why hand sawyers resent the mill. Logs arrive on the spring flood, are sorted by end mark at the boom, and wait in the pond until the saw is ready.
- **Boom Pond:** holds logs sorted by owner.
- **Frame Saw:** blade in a wooden frame on guides, driven by the crank.
- **Drying Cribs:** stacked boards seasoning under cover.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Blade Tension (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, loose blades wander and ruin expensive hardwood logs. At `8–10`, taut, well-lubricated blades produce perfectly straight boards, but an over-tight blade overheats and snaps under a knot.
- **Quest Hook — *The Snapped Crank Shaft***: When the main cast-iron reciprocating crank shatters under a knotted oak log, Silas must coordinate emergency forge welds with the local blacksmith before construction projects stall; Ostrid Maule is the only man who knows the old pit gang was nearby that night, and the mines give three weeks before their props run out.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Saws `Timber: Hardwood-Logs` floated down from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Timber: Sawn-Lumber` to the **Master Cooperage (`IND-TIMBER-03`)**, the **River Barge Slipway & Canal Lock (`IND-BARGE-01`)** and the **Pelagic Fishing Fleet Wharf (`EXT-FISH-01`)**. Sends `Timber: Mine-Props` to every mine: the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**, the **Malachite Drift Mine (`MINE-COPPER-01`)**, the **Placer Sluice & Quartz Crush Mill (`MINE-GOLD-01`)**, the **Plumbago Drift Mine (`MINE-GRAPHITE-01`)**, the **Open-Cast Iron Ore Pit (`MINE-IRON-01`)**, the **Cinnabar Mine and Retort Furnaces (`MINE-MERCURY-01`)**, the **Galena Deep-Vein Mine (`MINE-SILVER-01`)** and the **Alluvial Cassiterite Streamworks (`MINE-TIN-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The weir. Whoever controls the water decides whether the saw runs and whether the mines get their props.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Saw Frame:** A hand caught between the carriage and the blade is crushed or severed ($2\text{d}6$ slashing damage).
* **Kickback Board:** A knot binds the blade and hurls a plank across the shed ($1\text{d}8$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Wheel Pit:** Falling into the race beside the wheel means drowning or crushing ($3\text{d}6$ damage).
