# Sugarcane Plantation and Harvest Fields (`AGRI-SUGAR-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SUGAR` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The sugarcane fields form the agricultural engine of the coastal river bottom, converting tropical humidity and deep volcanic loam into mountain-high stacks of dense, sucrose-rich cane.

The estate runs on forced labor. Enslaved field hands are divided into gangs by strength and task, each under a driver who is himself enslaved, and an absentee owner's attorney presses the overseer for quota. Cut cane spoils within a day or two, so at crop time cutting, carting and milling run without pause. Deaths among the enslaved outpace births, and the estate must keep buying new hands to hold its numbers.

* **Alluvial Drainage Canals:** Grid-cut earth trenches fed by wooden sluice gates. Regulates seasonal inundation and drains muddy water into the bay.
* **Cane Brake Stands:** Dense, 14-foot stands of thick-rind Saccharum cane, harvested by crews armed with weighted broad machetes.
* **Ox-Cart Weigh Station:** High-beamed timber scale house where bundled cane stalks are weighed, inspected for borer beetles, and bound with hemp rope for transport.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[3, 2, 4]` - **Coerced Harvest Regime** (`Clan / Hierarchy`). (Written for this conversion; the module had no authored coordinate.)
- **Sociological Rationale**: A plantation is held together by an owner's kin-like household at the top and by overseers and drivers enforcing quota on a bound workforce, so authority is rigid and dissent is risky and constant.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Bundled Cane Stalks** (`Commodity`): Weighed, hemp-bound bundles of ripe cane that must reach the mill within a day or two.
- **Cane Machete** (`Tool`): Weighted broad blade used by the cutting gangs; a dull blade slows the whole row.
- **Weigh-House Tally Book** (`Relic`): The overseer's record of bundles weighed per gang, used to set quota and punishment.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Cane Ripeness` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, green shoots carry thin sap and low sucrose, so the mill gets little for its fuel. At `8-10`, dry tassel over-ripeness turns the field into a fire hazard, and any spark ends in a firestorm that ruins the harvest.
- **Quest Hook - *The Short Tally***: Ama Dessouk comes to the party in secret: the weigh-house tally is four bundles-per-gang short, Thaddeus Varen will blame her gang, and Jarek Horn's cutters are ready to slow down. Corwin Ashdown's audit arrives in ten days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Requires uninterrupted fresh water from upstream headwaters (`Water: Baseline-Potable-Flow`), which the **Communal Artesian Well and Village Cistern (`T0-DOM-02`)** supplies, and bales and lashings of `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` from the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)** to bind cane bundles for transport.
* **Output Transmission:** Dispatches cartloads of `Crop: Raw-Sugarcane` to the **Cane Crushing Mill and Boiling House (`AGRI-SUGAR-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** Setting fire to the dry perimeter ditch-banks can ignite the entire estate within 30 minutes.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Razor Cane Cuts:** Moving through unharvested cane without heavy canvas gaiters inflicts slashing briar scrapes ($1\text{d}4$ damage) and risks swamp rot fever.
* **Tassel Fire Hazard:** Any open flame or spell spark in Level 3 tasseling conditions ignites a 30-foot cone of roaring fire.
* **Crop-Time Exhaustion:** Gangs worked day and night collapse in the rows; a worker who drops under the machete takes $1\text{d}6$ damage and the party's labor allies lose a round to carry them out.
