# Nixtamalization Vats & Stone Metate Mill (`IND-CORN-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CORN` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Equipped with large boiling vats and carved stone metate grinding stones, this processing station cooks dried maize kernels in an alkaline solution of slaked lime. This ancient chemical transformation softens the outer pericarp, releases bound niacin, and unlocks nutritional value before stone-grinding the softened maize into pliable masa dough and coarse meal. The required Crop: Raw-Maize, Chem: Slaked-Lime, Water: Baseline-Potable-Flow and Fuel: Wood-Fuel are turned into Food: Masa-Flour and Food: Cornmeal-Rations.

Dried kernels are cooked at or near the boil for minutes up to an hour, left to steep a few hours or overnight, then drained. The spent liquor is poured off and the grain washed thoroughly and hulled, because liquor left in the grain leaves a bad taste. Fresh nixtamal is ground on the metate the same day; rations are ground coarse for the stillhouse.
- **Cooking Vats:** boiling vessels over wood fire.
- **Metate Row:** basalt slabs and hand stones (mano) worked with the wrists.
- **Runoff Pit:** where the spent limewater drains.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 3, 2]` - **Operational Artisan** (`Market`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Precise soaking times and grinding rhythm determine the quality of staple food supplies for the entire settlement, and masa sold or bartered to households every day keeps the vats tied to local demand.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Alkaline Concentration` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, under-cooked corn remains tough and nutritionally locked. At `8-10`, excessive lime dissolves the grain into a bitter, unusable mush.
- **Quest Hook - *The Contaminated Spring***: The water source used for boiling turns brackish with sediment, threatening to ruin the entire daily nixtamal batch. Miller Xochitl Green-Corn has until the dawn pick-up to find clean water, or Market Steward Itzel Quemada takes the town's order to a rival mill.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Cooks `Crop: Raw-Maize` from the **Three-Sisters Maize Plantation (`FARM-CORN-01`)**; soaks it in `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)** and clean `Water: Baseline-Potable-Flow` from **The Communal Artesian Well and Village Cistern (`T0-DOM-02`)**; and fires the vats with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** `Food: Masa-Flour` is an end product that feeds the settlement. Coarse `Food: Cornmeal-Rations` go to the **Sour-Mash Stillhouse (`IND-CORN-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The wash water. Dirty or brackish water ruins a batch.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Lime Burn:** Handling caustic lime or hot limewater without care burns eyes and hands ($1\text{d}6$ damage and blurred sight for a day).
* **Vat Scald:** A tipped cooking vat sends boiling liquor across the floor ($2\text{d}6$ scalding damage).
* **Grinder's Ache:** A full day on the metate row without rest leaves wrists and shoulders swollen (-2 to fine work until rested).
