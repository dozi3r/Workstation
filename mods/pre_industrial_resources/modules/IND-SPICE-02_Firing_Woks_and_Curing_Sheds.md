# Firing Woks & Curing Sheds (`IND-SPICE-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SPICE` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Equipped with massive cast-iron woks, brick flues, and tiered bamboo drying racks, this processing station halts enzymatic decay in raw agricultural crops through controlled heat application. Artisans roast, steam, and ferment raw leaves and spices over steady charcoal fires before packing them tightly into sealed ceramic vessels for long-distance export. The `Crop: Raw-Spices-and-Tea` arrives from the gardens and is fired over `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` in woks of `Metal: Pig-Iron`, then sealed in `Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels` as `Luxury: Cured-Spices-and-Tea`.

Tea leaf is withered for hours, rolled, then fixed in the wok and dried, the cook taking great care not to over-fire it; nutmeg seeds are sun-dried for weeks and their red mace flattened and dried for ten to fourteen days. A sorter strips stems and grades each lot, and the company factor fixes the price it will pay. Rival firms and smugglers shadow the buyer.
- **Wok Line:** brick-flued iron woks over charcoal.
- **Withering Racks:** tiered bamboo racks for leaf and mace.
- **Grading Table:** where stems are stripped and lots graded.
- **Jar Store:** ceramic jars sealed with wax or clay.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Firing Temperature (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, under-dried goods mold in transit. At `8–10`, excessive heat scorches the crop, ruining delicate essential oils, and the sorter downgrades whole lots.
- **Quest Hook — *The Scorched Batch***: A sudden draft flares the primary flues, charring a high-value tea shipment intended for the provincial governor's palace. Bramen Crows must save what he can before Factor Leopold Veer grades the lot, and Dessa Orlin, who was on the fire, will be blamed unless the party can show the flue was fouled.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Fires `Crop: Raw-Spices-and-Tea` from the **Shade-Grown Spice & Tea Gardens (`FARM-SPICE-01`)**; burns `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**; packs in `Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels` from the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)**; and casts its woks from `Metal: Pig-Iron` from the **Charcoal Bloomery and Blast Furnace (`MINE-IRON-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Luxury: Cured-Spices-and-Tea` to the **Spagyric Apothecary & Quarantine Lazaretto (`IND-APOTH-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The factor's grade and price. A downgraded lot sells for a fraction.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Wok Burns:** A slip at the flue line burns hands and arms ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Smoke and Dust:** Spice dust and flue smoke in a shut shed choke the crews ($1\text{d}4$ damage per hour and a penalty to perception).
* **Seized Lot:** Goods caught moving outside the company's stamp are confiscated and the carrier is jailed or forced into labor.
