# Fulling Mill & Scouring Vat (`IND-SHEEP-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SHEEP` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Waterwheel-driven wooden trip hammers rhythmically pound wet woolen cloth inside heavy stone troughs filled with alkaline scouring soap and stale urine. Laborers scour raw fleece to strip natural lanolin before carding the clean fibers into parallel alignments. The facility transforms raw greasy fleece and scouring soap into clean, carded wool ready for spinning.

Raw wool arrives greasy and is scoured in warm water, alkali or soap and stale urine, then rinsed and dried. A cam shaft driven by the waterwheel lifts the heavy wooden stocks, which pound the wet cloth in the trough, and cloth is lifted out about every two hours to undo plaits. Finished cloth is stretched on tenters in the open drying ground.
- **Waterwheel and Cam Shaft:** lifts the wooden stocks.
- **Scouring Vats:** warm troughs of lye and lant.
- **Tenter Ground:** frames and hooks where cloth is stretched to dry.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Commodity: Soap** (`Commodity`): Alkaline cleansing blocks saponified from animal tallow and wood ash lye.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Scouring Alkalinity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, weak lye leaves residual lanolin that fouls the carding combs. At `8–10`, caustic over-treatment dissolves the wool proteins, reducing strong fibers to useless lint.
- **Quest Hook — *The Jammed Sluice***: Debris clogs the water intake of Millwright Orson Vance, halting the fulling hammers while Alis Wetherby's vats threaten to overflow with caustic scouring lye. The guild inspects the finished wool in three days, and Wash-Collector Gideon Marl's carts cannot reach the vats until the race is clear.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Scours `Animal: Raw-Fleece` from the **Highland Sheep Station (`FARM-SHEEP-01`)**, using `Commodity: Soap` from the **Caustic Soapboiler Vat (`IND-SOAP-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Textile: Carded-Wool` to the **Master Draper's Guildhall (`IND-SHEEP-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The millrace. A choked intake stops the hammers and halts the whole cloth chain.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Caustic Lye:** A vat splash burns skin and eyes ($1\text{d}6$ acid damage).
* **Trip Hammer:** A hand caught under a falling stock is crushed ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Wheel Race:** A fall into the millrace drags the victim under the wheel ($2\text{d}6$ damage).
