# Civic Bakehouse (`IND-WHEAT-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-WHEAT` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The civic bakehouse features cavernous brick-lined ovens heated by intense hardwood fires until the masonry radiates steady baking heat. Bakers mix refined flour with water and sourdough leaven in deep wooden troughs, knead the dough by hand, shape heavy loaves, and slide them onto the hot hearth using long-handled ash peels. This final thermal transformation converts milled flour (`Food: Wheat-Flour`) and fuel (`Fuel: Wood-Fuel`) into nourishing baked bread (`Food: Baked-Bread`) for the settlement.

A piece of each batch of dough is kept back to leaven the next. Ovens are costly and need careful tending, so they are often built apart from other buildings or outside the walls to limit fire risk. Loaf weight is fixed by law against the price of wheat: when wheat is dear the loaves get lighter, and the guild's weights are checked by sampling loaves and fining the short ones.
- **Baking Oven:** brick-lined chamber fired with hardwood.
- **Kneading Troughs:** where the dough is worked.
- **Wood Stack:** the fuel store the whole bake depends on.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Oven Thermal Retention (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, masonry cools prematurely, leaving dough raw and unrisen. At `8–10`, ideal radiant heat yields golden, crisp crusts, but the oven burns through its wood stack and a hot bake leaves no spare fuel for the next day.
- **Quest Hook — *The Scarcity Loaf***: When dwindling wood fuel threatens to shut down the ovens mid-baking, Martha must negotiate emergency timber allotments while guarding the remaining dough from desperate citizens. Assize Weigher Emrys Bellwether arrives to weigh the lightened loaves, and Wood-Carter Wat Tolliver swears the next cart is three days off; the party has until the court-leet sits to bring wood or save Martha from the fine.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Bakes `Food: Wheat-Flour` from the **Water-Powered Gristmill (`IND-WHEAT-02`)** and fires the ovens with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends out `Food: Baked-Bread` as an end product with no consuming module; it feeds the settlement directly.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The wood stack. Without fuel the flour is worthless.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Oven Burns:** Reaching into the hot hearth with a bare arm scorches the skin ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Bakehouse Fire:** Sparks or a cracked flue ignite flour dust and neighboring thatch ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and a spreading blaze).
* **Mob at the Door:** A hungry crowd during scarcity storms the loading door ($1\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and stolen loaves).
