# Master Draper's Guildhall (`IND-SHEEP-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SHEEP` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Multi-story timber-framed weaving halls housing numerous high-warp foot-treadle looms and dyeing vats. Master drapers supervise spinners, weavers, and dyers who fix natural pigments into carded wool using mineral mordants before weaving dense, windproof broadcloth. The facility transforms carded wool and mineral alum into luxurious, dyed broadcloth textiles.

Short-staple wool is carded, spun and woven on wide looms, about fifty to seventy-five percent wider than the finished cloth, which is then milled to shrink it to width. Pieces are sold in standard lengths of 24 or 30 yards. Under strict supervision the guild keeps the quality consistent, and export merchants buy by the piece.
- **Broad Looms:** wide frames worked by a team.
- **Dye Vats:** where mordant and dye are set into the wool.
- **Tenter Ground and Shearing Table:** where the cloth is stretched, the nap raised and shorn smooth.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[1, 1, 1]` — **Guild Chancellor** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Guild monopolies on weaving standards and color formulas enforce rigorous quality control, hierarchical apprenticeship, and market protectionism.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Textile: Broadcloth** (`Commodity`): Heavy, tightly woven woolen cloth with a felted, weather-resistant surface.
- **Chem: Mordant-Alum** (`Reagent`): Double-sulfate mineral salt used to lock organic dyes permanently into woolen fibers.
- **Guild Seal** (`Tool`): Lead or wax seal that certifies a piece as meeting the guild's width and weight.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Weave Tension` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, slack warp threads produce uneven, loose cloth that fails quality inspection. At `8–10`, excessive tension snaps threads constantly, stalling production and wasting yarn.
- **Quest Hook — *The Faded Batch***: A contaminated shipment of alum ruins the mordant bath of Master Dyer Isolde Vane, threatening Guild Master Geoffrey Vance's lucrative export contract with foreign merchants unless a replacement fix is discovered. Cloth Searcher Merrick Dole will seal no faded piece, and the merchant's ship sails in a week.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Weaves `Textile: Carded-Wool` from the **Fulling Mill & Scouring Vat (`IND-SHEEP-02`)** and sets the dye with `Chem: Mordant-Alum` from the **Alunite Quarry & Leaching Vats (`EXT-ALUM-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** `Textile: Broadcloth` is the chain's end product, sold by the piece to merchants and export houses.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The inspection seal. Without it no export merchant will buy the cloth.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Dye Vat Fumes:** Hot mordant and dye baths foul the air and sicken the dyers.
* **Loom Accident:** A snapped beam or heavy frame falls on a weaver ($1\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Hall Fire:** Lint, oil and a dropped lamp set the timber hall alight ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
