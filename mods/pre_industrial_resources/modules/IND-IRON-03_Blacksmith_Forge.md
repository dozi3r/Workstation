# Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-IRON` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
At the stone hearth with leather bellows, heavy anvil, water trough, and covered tallow bath, the smith and apprentice striker turn `Metal: Pig-Iron`, `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` and `Animal: Tallow` into `Metal: Forged-Iron` tools, hardware, and fasteners.

Bar iron from the finery and hammer works is reheated to cherry-yellow, drawn out, and shaped where the smith's hand hammer directs the striker's sledge. Work follows the fire from dawn lighting through the final quench in water or tallow.
- **Hearth and Bellows:** Charcoal hearth fed by leather bellows judged by iron colour.
- **Anvil Block:** Heavy steel-faced iron anvil set on an oak stump.
- **Quench Troughs:** Water trough and covered tallow bath beside the hearth.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 3, 4]` - **Guild Smithy** (`Market`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Operating as a direct nexus of tool commerce and municipal supply, the smithy emphasizes output velocity, material economy, and guild standard pricing.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Hearth Temperature & Draft` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, the charcoal bed cools, causing metal to fracture under the hammer. At `8-10`, overheating burns the carbon out of the iron, rendering it brittle.
- **Quest Hook - *The Contaminated Pig Batch***: Rufus Colbeck receives a shipment of high-sulfur pig-iron that crumbles into red-short shards under Nicol Audley's hammer. They must investigate the smelting camp upstream before the town guard's weapon requisition deadline passes, and Brannoch Tallowby, who carts the tallow that quenches the finished arms, has heard the furnace master blames the ore.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes `Metal: Pig-Iron` from the **Charcoal Bloomery and Blast Furnace (`MINE-IRON-02`)**, `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**, and `Animal: Tallow` for the quench baths from the **Civic Abattoir (`IND-CATTLE-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Metal: Forged-Iron` to the **Crucible Steelworks and Foundry (`IND-STEEL-01`)**, the **Drydock Slipway & Caulking Yard (`IND-SHIP-02`)**, the **Stamping Mill & Patio Amalgamation Floor (`IND-SILVER-02`)**, the **Cupellation Hearth & Sovereign Mint (`IND-SILVER-03`)**, the **Master Cooperage (`IND-TIMBER-03`)**, the **Water-Powered Gristmill (`IND-WHEAT-02`)**, the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**, the **Glassblower's Furnace (`IND-GLASS-02`)** and eleven other downstream trades that depend on iron tools and fittings.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Hot Iron Burns:** Handling bars or tongs at working heat without gloves scorches the hands ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Cracked Quench:** Plunging hot iron into a cold or fouled bath splits the piece and spits scalding tallow and steam ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
