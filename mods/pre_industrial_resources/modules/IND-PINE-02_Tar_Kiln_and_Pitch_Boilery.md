# Tar Kiln & Pitch Boilery (`IND-PINE-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PINE` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A terraced earthen mound built over a stone-lined drainage basin where pine roots and resinous billets are stacked, sealed with turf, and slowly smothered-burned to render dark wood tar. The raw tar is subsequently boiled down in heavy copper cauldrons to drive off volatile spirits, producing thick, waterproof pine pitch for ship-calking and timber preservation. Spent tar skimmings are burned beneath a cloth-hung soot chamber, and the fine lampblack that settles is scraped down for inkmakers. The kiln burns Resin: Raw-Pine-Resin and Fuel: Wood-Fuel in copper from Metal: Forged-Copper, and yields Chem: Pine-Pitch and Chem: Lampblack.

The wood is split into finger-sized billets, stacked densely on a floor that slopes to an outlet hole, and covered with earth and moss. The fire is lit on top; after a few hours the tar starts to run and keeps flowing for days. If air gets in, the wood catches fire and the batch is ruined. Tar goes out in barrels, and the navies are the largest buyers.
- **Kiln Floor:** sloped basin with an outlet hole where tar drains.
- **Boilery:** copper cauldrons over a bank of fires.
- **Soot Chamber:** cloth-hung hood where lampblack settles.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Pine Pitch** (`Commodity`): Thick, black, waterproof resinous compound used to calk timber hulls and seams.
- **Copper Cauldron** (`Tool`): Large heavy-lipped vessel used for boiling down crude tar into refined pitch.
- **Lampblack** (`Commodity`): Fine greasy carbon soot from burning pitch residues, the base pigment of printers' ink.
- **Branded Tar Barrel** (`Commodity`): Oak-hooped barrel of tar stamped by the buyer's agent, the unit in which the kiln is paid.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Kiln Smothering Rate` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, air leaks cause the entire wood stack to burst into open flame, destroying the batch. At `8-10`, oxygen starvation chokes the burn so completely that no tar drains, and the crew loses days waiting for a mound that will never run.
- **Quest Hook - *The Ruptured Mound***: When a turf seal collapses on the main tar kiln, Jorn must shovel wet clay into the blazing vent before the entire winter harvest ignites. Tar Factor Anselm Dreyer's barrel count is already short and the ice will close the river in two weeks; Pell Haldane offers silver for the lampblack if the party saves the soot chamber.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Burns `Resin: Raw-Pine-Resin` from the **Boreal Conifer Logging & Turpentine Camp (`EXT-PINE-01`)**, keeps the mound and cauldron fires fed with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**, and boils tar in cauldrons of `Metal: Forged-Copper` from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Chem: Pine-Pitch` to the **River Barge Slipway & Canal Lock (`IND-BARGE-01`)**, the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)**, the **Drydock Slipway & Caulking Yard (`IND-SHIP-02`)** and the **Tryworks Rendering Furnace (`IND-WHALE-02`)**, and scrapes `Chem: Lampblack` from the soot chamber for the **Movable-Type Printshop & Bindery (`IND-PAPER-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The tar monopoly. Whoever buys the barrels sets the price at which every hull is caulked.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Flashed Mound:** A sealed kiln that takes in air bursts into flame under the turf ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage to anyone at the vent).
* **Boiling Pitch Splash:** A tipped cauldron of hot pitch sticks and burns ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and the pitch must be cut off the skin).
* **Soot Lung:** Scraping lampblack from the soot chamber without a face cloth inflames the lungs ($1\text{d}6$ damage per hour and a cumulative cough).
