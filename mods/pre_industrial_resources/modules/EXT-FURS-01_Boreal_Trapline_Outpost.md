# Boreal Trapline Outpost (`EXT-FURS-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FURS` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A remote cluster of log-and-sod cabins situated along deep forest game trails in sub-zero taiga environments. Trappers set forged-iron spring traps across wide territorial beats, harvesting thick-furred winter animals, skinning carcasses on site, and salting raw pelts on wooden stretching frames before sled transport to trading posts. The required inputs of Metal: Forged-Iron and Commodity: Coarse-Salt supply the traps and the salting, yielding bundled Animal: Raw-Pelts.

Trappers work in small bands, each man setting a string of at least ten traps and walking it daily. The season opens with the first snow and ends in early spring, when animals are frozen hard enough that the hair will not slip. Skinners flesh, dry and salt each pelt; salted hides keep for weeks. At the post a factor prices the catch against trade goods, and the company or sovereign takes its share of every pelt.
- **Winter Lodge:** log-and-sod cabin where the band sleeps and works.
- **Trap Strings:** daily walking circuits of iron spring traps.
- **Stretching Frames:** wooden frames where salted pelts dry.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Trap-Line Coverage` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, traps go unvisited and ruined pelts rot in the snow. At `8-10`, overextended lines exhaust trappers and expose them to freezing storms.
- **Quest Hook - *The Poached Line***: When Silas finds his prime marten traps systematically robbed by an unknown trespasser, he must track the culprit across hostile winter ridges before the thaw; Factor Dmitri Orlenko will pay for the poacher's pelts without asking where they came from.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Takes `Metal: Forged-Iron` for traps from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)** and `Commodity: Coarse-Salt` for curing from the **Salt Boiling House & Granary (`IND-SALT-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sleds `Animal: Raw-Pelts` to the **Pelt Scraping & Furrier's Guild (`IND-FURS-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The frozen river road. When it breaks up, the pelts cannot leave and the salt cannot arrive.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Frostbite and Exposure:** A trapper caught out overnight in a storm risks losing fingers and toes ($1\text{d}6$ cold damage per night without shelter).
* **Spring Trap:** A careless hand in a set trap is crushed ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a lamed limb).
* **Cornered Predator:** A wounded bear or wolverine at the trap turns on the trapper ($2\text{d}6$ damage).
