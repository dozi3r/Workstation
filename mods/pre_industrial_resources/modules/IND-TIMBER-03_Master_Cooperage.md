# Master Cooperage (`IND-TIMBER-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TIMBER` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Master coopers shape sawn lumber into tapered, bevel-edged staves using drawknives and steaming boxes, arranging them inside iron hoops without adhesives or nails. Heat from wood-shaving fires softens the oak staves so they can be bent inward and locked tightly with heavy forged iron hoops, creating watertight casks for wine, beer, and dry goods. This intricate craft converts lumber and iron into essential storage vessels. The required inputs are Timber: Sawn-Lumber and Metal: Forged-Iron, and the output is Timber: Cooperage-Casks.

Tight (wet) work makes casks for wine, beer and oil, while slack (dry) work makes casks for flour and goods that only need to stay dry. A cooper marks every cask with a personal stamp, so a leak is traced back to the maker. Staves are steamed, toasted over a low fire and bent, then hooped by a hooper.
- **Steaming Box:** softens staves for bending.
- **Shaving Horse:** where staves are tapered with the drawknife.
- **Testing Trough:** where casks are filled to check for weeping.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Hoop Tension (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, loose hoops cause casks to collapse under liquid weight. At `8–10`, perfectly driven iron hoops seal wood staves into indestructible vessels, but over-driven hoops crack the bent staves.
- **Quest Hook — *The Leaking Vintage***: When a batch of newly manufactured wine casks begins weeping juice during storage, Gavin must re-hoop and reseal every barrel before the merchant guild rejects the entire shipment; Cellarman Bertram Oske gives him until the next market day before he bills the loss to the shop's mark.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Steam Box:** Opening the box carelessly scalds hands and face ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Slipped Drawknife:** The curved blade skids off a stave ($1\text{d}6$ slashing damage).
* **Hoop Spring:** A hoop driven too hard snaps back and strikes the worker ($1\text{d}8$ damage).
