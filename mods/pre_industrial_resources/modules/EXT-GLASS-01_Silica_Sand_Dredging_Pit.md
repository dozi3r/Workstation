# Silica Sand Dredging Pit (`EXT-GLASS-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-GLASS` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Open riverbanks and ancient lacustrine deposits are excavated by teams wielding iron-shod shovels and wooden screening frames. Coarse river gravel and heavy impurities are sluiced away in shallow wooden channels, leaving behind ultra-pure quartz sand washed clean of iron stains, ready for high-grade glassmaking. The pit has no inputs; its Mineral: Silica-Sand is the root of the glass chain.

Glassmakers prize sand of uniform grain from clean riverbeds, and some workshops also crush river quartz pebbles to powder. Diggers cut the bank, screeners remove gravel, and washers sluice the sand and check the settling bins for dark flecks. Wet weather and rising groundwater decide how many days the face can be worked.
- **Working Face:** the cut bank where sand is dug.
- **Screening Frames:** wooden frames that catch pebbles.
- **Settling Bins:** where washed sand drains and is inspected.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Wash Purity` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, sand contains iron impurities that discolor glass green or brown. At `8-10`, excessive washing washes away ultra-fine usable grains.
- **Quest Hook - *The Iron Vein***: Tessa discovers that upstream agricultural runoff has stained the latest sand deposit with rust, threatening the upcoming batch of royal crystal lenses; Pit Boss Garret Miller wants the stain kept quiet until the carts have gone, and the glasshouse inspects the next load in three days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Needs no inputs from other modules; the pit works a natural sand bank with picks and shovels.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Mineral: Silica-Sand` to the **Glassblower's Furnace (`IND-GLASS-02`)**, the **Mortar & Plaster Works (`IND-LIME-03`)** and the **Crown & Flint Lens-Grinding Lathe (`IND-OPTIC-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The wash. Dirty sand ruins every module downstream that needs clear glass.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Bank Collapse:** An undercut face slumps onto the diggers ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and buried until dug out).
* **Cold Water Work:** Standing in the river wash for hours chills and sickens the washers ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a fever penalty).
* **Flooded Pit:** Rising groundwater traps workers at the bottom of the cut ($1\text{d}6$ damage per round without escape).
