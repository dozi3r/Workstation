# Galena Deep-Vein Mine (`MINE-SILVER-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SILVER` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Carved deep into faulted limestone, the galena mine descends through dripping shafts and timbered adits where miners work lead-silver veins by tallow light. It consumes Timber: Mine-Props, Light: Tallow-Candles, and Livestock: Draft-Animals at the horse-gin, yielding silver-rich Ore: Silver-Ore and clean lead glance Ore: Argentiferous-Galena.

Foremen answer for each section while carriers climb ladders bearing hundred-pound loads with a candle at the brow. Since each piston pump lifts barely twenty-four feet, stacked waterwheel pumps fight rising groundwater while the sovereign claims a share of ore.
- **Horse-Whim Shaft Head:** Winding drum and ore skips turned by draft horses.
- **Pump-Wheel:** Waterwheel driving stacked pump rods down to the sump.
- **Drainage Adit:** Low downhill tunnel draining water out of the hillside.
- **Ore-Sorting Floor:** Surface yard separating silver-rich ore from clean galena.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

- **Sociological Rationale**: Constant peril in pitch-black tunnels binds miners into tight, insular brotherhoods suspicious of surface managers and royal auditors.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Tallow Dip Candle** (`Commodity`): Rendered animal fat candle providing essential illumination in firedamp-prone tunnels.
- **Oak Pit Prop** (`Tool`): Hand-hewn timber pillar wedged to support unstable overhead limestone strata.
- **Hand-Sorted Galena** (`Commodity`): Heavy, bright grey lead-silver ore picked clean of waste rock and carted to the lead smelting bole, while the silver-rich fraction goes to the stamping mill.
- **Pump Rod and Bucket** (`Tool`): Timber rod and leather-cupped piston that lifts the sump water one stage at a time.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Strata Stability` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, ceiling collapses trap shifts and flood lower levels. At `8–10`, stable rock permits steady, high-volume ore extraction, and the shareholders push to sink deeper levels faster than the pumps and timber can follow.
- **Quest Hook — *The Flooded Sump***: A pickaxe strike punctures an ancient subterranean aquifer, rapidly flooding the lower galleries and trapping Captain Crouch and his crew behind rising waters. Osric Talmage says the pump-wheel can buy six hours if someone clears a broken rod in the pump shaft, while Mine-Reeve Hedda Rauschenbach suspects the "accident" was struck to hide a rich pocket from the sovereign's share.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes `Timber: Mine-Props` from the **Water-Powered Sawmill (`IND-TIMBER-02`)** to shore every new gallery, `Light: Tallow-Candles` from the **Tallow Chandlery (`IND-CANDLE-01`)** for every hewer and carrier, and `Livestock: Draft-Animals` from the **Pasture Ranch (`FARM-CATTLE-01`)** to turn the horse whim.
* **Output Transmission:** Carts the silver-rich fraction as `Ore: Silver-Ore` to the **Stamping Mill & Patio Amalgamation Floor (`IND-SILVER-02`)** for crushing and amalgamation, and carts clean hand-sorted `Ore: Argentiferous-Galena` to the **Lead Smelting Bole & Plumbery (`IND-LEAD-01`)** for smelting.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The pump-wheel and its leat. Break the wheel or divert the stream and the deep levels drown within days.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Foul Air:** Bad air in dead-end headings and after blasting puts out candles and fells the unwary ($1\text{d}6$ damage per turn until carried to fresh air).
* **Ladder Fall:** Ore carriers climb long ladders under heavy loads; a slip in the shaft is often fatal ($3\text{d}6$ falling damage).
* **Run of Ground:** Rotten or skimped props give way without warning ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and the crew is cut off).
