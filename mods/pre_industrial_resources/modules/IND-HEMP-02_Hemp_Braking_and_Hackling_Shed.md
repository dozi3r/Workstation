# Hemp Braking & Hackling Shed (`IND-HEMP-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-HEMP` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
In the braking and hackling shed, dried and retted hemp stalks are crushed under heavy wooden break-mallets to shatter the brittle woody cores (hurds). Artisans then pull the clean fiber bundles through spiked iron hackling combs of increasing fineness, separating long line fibers from short tow and coarse oakum used for caulking and rough cordage. The required inputs of Crop: Raw-Hemp-Stalks and Metal: Forged-Iron, the latter drawn out into comb pins, become Textile: Oakum-and-Tow.

Breaking is followed by scutching, which scrapes off the last hurd, and then by hackling, where the hatchel's long iron pins straighten the fibre, with oil as a lubricant. Hemp dust ignites easily and fires in fibre works were frequent.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Oakum-and-Tow** (`Textile`): Coarse, fibrous hemp scrap and short fibers used for naval caulking and packing.
- **Hackling Comb** (`Tool`): Wooden board fitted with parallel rows of sharpened steel spikes for combing fiber.
- **Break-Mallet** (`Tool`): Heavy wooden mallet that shatters the woody core of the dried stalk.
- **Hatchel Oil Jar** (`Reagent`): Oil rubbed on the pins so the fibre slides; it is also the one thing in the shed that burns hotter than the dust.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Fiber Purity & Cleanliness` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, inadequate breaking leaves woody shives embedded in the fibers, ruining downstream rope quality. At `8–10`, excessive combing over-processes the batch, reducing total yield.
- **Quest Hook — *The Choking Dust***: Brendan Vance reports respiratory illness spreading among the brakers due to blocked ventilation shafts in the dusty shed. Surveyor Edmund Harkness inspects in a week; if the shed is shut for cleaning the quota fails, and if it is not, the dust and the lamps are a standing risk of fire.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Breaks and hackles retted `Crop: Raw-Hemp-Stalks` from the **Coastal Hemp Plantation (`FARM-HEMP-01`)**, and draws its hatchel pins from `Metal: Forged-Iron` made at the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships graded `Textile: Oakum-and-Tow` to the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)** and to the **Drydock Slipway & Caulking Yard (`IND-SHIP-02`)**, which packs it into ship seams.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The grading bench. The fibre inspector's verdict decides what goes to rope and what is sold as caulking.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Hemp Dust:** Breathing the thick air at the benches without a cloth over the face brings cough and wheeze ($1\text{d}6$ damage per shift and tired lungs).
* **Dust Fire:** A lamp or spark in the shed lights the fibre dust ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and flames running along the rafters).
* **Hatchel Pins:** A slipped hand across the hatchel is raked and pierced ($1\text{d}6$ piercing damage).
