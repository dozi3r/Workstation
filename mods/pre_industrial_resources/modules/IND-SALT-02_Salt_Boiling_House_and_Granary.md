# Salt Boiling House & Granary (`IND-SALT-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SALT` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The salt boiling house utilizes massive iron pans suspended over stone hearths to drive off remaining water from brine-slurry delivered by coastal pans. Wood-fuel stoked continuously by boilermen generates intense heat, forcing rapid crystallization of coarse salt crystals. The dried product is shoveled into adjacent timber granaries where it cures and stabilizes before export.

Brine is poured into shallow open pans, concentrated over fire, and the crystals raked out before more brine is added. Hearth fuel is the house's heaviest cost, so every wood contract matters. Salt sold by the barrel passes through the granary, where it is counted for the salt duty; the preservers of fish and fur buy it, and so does the silver patio.
- **Pan Hearths:** stone ranges that carry the iron pans.
- **Fuel Yard:** stacked seasoned wood, kept dry under cover.
- **Granary Bins:** raised timber bins where salt cures before barreling.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Furnace Heat Intensity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, wet wood chokes the hearths and halts boiling operations. At `8–10`, overheating cracks the expensive cast-iron boiling pans, halting production.
- **Quest Hook — *The Damp Reserve***: Ewen Pollard discovers rising damp threatening the central granary stock, requiring immediate relocation of tons of salt before it dissolves. Duty Collector Ambrose Treen arrives in three days to count the bins, and Master Boiler Bronwen Fane would rather lose a few barrels to damp than have them counted short.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Boils `Commodity: Brine-Slurry` from the **Tidal Salt Evaporation Pans (`EXT-SALT-01`)**; feeds its hearths with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; and hangs its pans on `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Commodity: Coarse-Salt` to the **Boreal Trapline Outpost (`EXT-FURS-01`)**, the **Salting Stages & Smokehouse (`IND-FISH-02`)** and the **Stamping Mill & Patio Amalgamation Floor (`IND-SILVER-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The wood contract. A late timber cart cools the hearths and starves every salt buyer downstream.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Scalding Brine:** A cracked pan or spilled bucket scalds the boilermen ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Hearth Smoke:** Long shifts in the smoky boiling house sour the lungs and slow the crew.
* **Granary Collapse:** A wet, overloaded bin gives way and buries the shovelers in salt ($1\text{d}6$ damage and suffocation if not dug out).
