# Caustic Soapboiler Vat (`IND-SOAP-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SOAP` (Stage 1: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Large lead-lined wooden vats are filled with water, potash, and slaked lime to causticize the mixture, generating lye. Olive oil is slowly added over gentle wood fires, and workers stir the boiling alkaline solution continuously with long wooden paddles until saponification occurs, casting the thick soap paste into wooden frames to cool and harden.

A full boil runs for days and the paste is judged by when it thickens and holds a trace. Poured paste is cut into cakes while soft, stamped, and left a fortnight or more to harden. In the trade's heartlands the great kettles hold several tons, and governments tax and regulate the work: surveyors lock the vats between boils, and a minimum batch size shuts small makers out.
- **Boiling Vat:** the big kettle over a wood firing pit.
- **Lye Tubs:** where potash and lime are leached and settled.
- **Mold Frames:** wooden frames where paste sets before cutting.
- **Curing Loft:** racks where cut cakes dry and harden.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Lye Causticity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, weak lye leaves oil unreacted, yielding greasy sludge. At `8–10`, overly caustic lye ruins equipment and irritates workers' skin, and burns hands and eyes badly enough that tenders refuse the paddle.
- **Quest Hook — *The Scorched Batch***: When a sudden wood-fire flare-up scorches the bottom of the master boiling vat, Dame Marta must skim the clean soap before the burnt odor ruins the entire export batch, and before Surveyor Hector Pell arrives at dawn to count and lock the vat; Jule Vane can hold the paddle for only a few more hours.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Boils `Oil: Olive-Oil` from the **Stone Olive Press (`IND-OLIVE-02`)**; leaches `Chem: Potash` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)**; causticizes with `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)**; and fires the vat with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Commodity: Soap` to the **Fulling Mill & Scouring Vat (`IND-SHEEP-02`)** for wool scouring.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The potash. Whoever holds the ash trade can starve the vats or squeeze the price.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Lye Burns:** Splashed lye eats skin and eyes ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a lasting scar or blindness on a bad splash).
* **Boil-Over Fire:** A runaway fire under the vat scatters scalding paste ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage in a small area).
* **Caustic Fumes:** Stirring an overstrong boil in a shut shed stings the throat and lungs ($1\text{d}4$ damage per hour without ventilation).
