# Roller Gin & Spinning House (`IND-COTTON-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-COTTON` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The roller gin and spinning house is a drafty industrial shed filled with double-roller churka gins and multi-spindle spinning wheels. Raw cotton bolls are fed between counter-rotating wooden rollers that crush seeds away from lint, after which carded fibers are drawn and twisted into durable yarn on spinning frames. Raw cotton boll inputs are transformed into neatly spooled spun-cotton-yarn ready for weaving looms.

A man and a woman working one gin clean roughly 28 pounds of lint in a day, so the shed runs many gins side by side. The roller must be narrow enough to push the seed out without crushing it, a skill that takes years. Lint is then carded and spun, mostly by women who are paid by the piece, and the yarn is wound into hanks.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Roller Slippage` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, smooth counter-rotation strips seeds instantly. At `8-10`, warped wooden rollers crush seeds into the lint, ruining entire batches.
- **Quest Hook - *The Warped Rollers***: Master Spinner Tariq Al-Din discovers that humidity has warped the primary gin rollers, halting production right before the merchant deadline. Yarn Broker Jivraj Mehta will cancel his advance in five days unless Roller Tender Amira Sen can find dry seasoned ironwood for new rollers.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Gins and spins `Crop: Raw-Cotton-Boll` from the **Cotton Plantation (`FARM-COTTON-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Delivers `Textile: Spun-Cotton-Yarn` in hanks to the **Calico Weaving & Print Works (`IND-COTTON-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The roller wood. A warped roller halts the gin row.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Roller Pinch:** A hand drawn between the counter-rotating rollers is crushed ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
* **Lint Dust:** Breathing carding and ginning dust all day leaves a lasting cough (-1 to endurance checks).
* **Spindle Fire:** Dry lint near a lamp flashes into a shed fire ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
