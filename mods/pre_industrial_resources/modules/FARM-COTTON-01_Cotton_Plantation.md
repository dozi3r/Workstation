# Cotton Plantation (`FARM-COTTON-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-COTTON` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The cotton plantation consists of sprawling sun-baked alluvial fields divided by drainage ditches and overseen from a central timber drying platform. Field hands labor under intense solar heat, manually picking fluffy white cotton bolls from thorny scrub shrubs. Unprocessed inputs are zero, while harvested raw cotton bolls are packed into burlap sacks and hauled to regional ginning houses.

Seed goes in at spring and picking runs after the bolls open, a long season of daily hand labor. The crews are bonded or enslaved people working under the factor's weight minimums, a system fed by the Atlantic trade in captives, and flight, slowdowns and quiet resistance are part of the work. Merchant syndicates buy the crop by contract, so every missed quota is felt first by the crews.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 4, 1]` - **Plutocratic Enterprise** (`Market`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Driven by cash-crop export incentives and strict volume quotas, plantation operations emphasize market efficiency, output maximization, and labor commodification.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Raw-Cotton-Boll** (`Commodity`): Unprocessed cotton fibers intertwined with stubborn seed pods straight from the field.
- **Weighing Scale** (`Tool`): Brass-balanced hanging scale used to measure daily picker quotas.
- **Merchant Contract Ledger** (`Relic`): The factor's book of advances, quotas and penalties, which decides the crew's rations.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Boll Blight Infestation` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, plants flourish untouched in dry conditions. At `8-10`, grubs and boll rot ravage the fields, destroying staple bolls before harvest, and the estate squeezes the crews harder to meet its contracts.
- **Quest Hook - *The Pestilence Spread***: Field Boss Kora Diallo warns Estate Factor Gideon Vance that uncontained boll rot and grubs threaten the entire western acreage. Picker Abena Quartey says the crews will not burn the western rows without a promise on rations, and Merchant Agent Lucan Pryor arrives in four days to inspect the bolls.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. This is a stage 1 extraction node and requires nothing from other modules.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Crop: Raw-Cotton-Boll` in sacks to the **Roller Gin & Spinning House (`IND-COTTON-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The weighing platform. The scale decides rations, rest days and who runs.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Heat Exhaustion:** Picking through the afternoon sun without shade or water ($1\text{d}6$ damage and collapse).
* **Thorn and Boll Cuts:** Picking cuts hands on the dry bolls ($1\text{d}4$ damage, and infection if left untended).
* **Overseers' Riders:** Armed riders chase anyone caught slipping away from the rows (-2 to escape checks).
