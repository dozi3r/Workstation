# Tin Blowing House (`IND-TIN-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TIN` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The tin blowing house is a thick-walled stone furnace shed equipped with a large leather-and-wood foot-or-water-driven bellows. Workers charge the granite hearth with alternate layers of washed cassiterite ore and charcoal fuel, producing high blast temperatures that reduce the oxide to molten white metal. The required inputs of Ore: Tin-Ore and Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal are smelted down and skimmed of slag with long-handled Metal: Forged-Iron tools, then cast into heavy stone moulds yielding pure Metal: Tin-Ingot.

A leat-fed wheel of about nine feet works the bellows, and each tide of work burns roughly two tons of charcoal for every ton of tin. The molten metal runs into a granite float and is ladled into mould-stones; slag and scum are restamped and resmelted. Cast blocks go to the coinage town, where an officer cuts a corner to test the metal before sealing.
- **Castle Furnace:** tall charge stack with a wrought-iron hearth-eye for the bellows.
- **Float and Mould-Stones:** granite trough and ingot moulds.
- **Slag Stamps:** water-driven stampers that crush slag for resmelting.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Blast Heat Intensity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, the furnace chills and freezes the charge into an unworkable iron-tin mass. At `8–10`, runaway temperatures melt the granite lining and crack the bellows.
- **Quest Hook — *The Arsenical Choke***: Dangerous arsenic vapors escape a cracked hearthstone, incapacitating Jory Polglase and threatening a stoppage unless Master Geoffrey procures refractory clay repairs; Isolde Penhallow gives the house until the next proving day before she moves her ore to another smelter.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Charges `Ore: Tin-Ore` from the **Alluvial Cassiterite Streamworks (`MINE-TIN-01`)**; burns `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**; and works the hearth and moulds with `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends sealed `Metal: Tin-Ingot` to the **Bell & Artillery Bronze Foundry (`IND-BRONZE-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The fuel. A house without charcoal or coal cannot smelt, and a merchant who controls the fuel controls the tin.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Arsenical Fume:** Breathing the vapors from a cracked hearth poisons the lungs ($1\text{d}6$ damage per hour and a cumulative tremor).
* **Molten Float:** A spill from the granite float scalds and burns ($3\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Bellows Run:** A loose belt or rod snaps in the wheel house ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
