# Kaolin & Petuntse Quarry (`EXT-PORC-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PORCELAIN` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Carved into weathered white hillsides, this extraction site features open-cast benches, pick-axes, and water-powered crushing mills. Miners unearth pristine kaolin clay and weathered petuntse (porcelain stone), which are pulverized, washed in settling pools, and filtered into dense, refined ceramic raw materials. The quarry needs no inputs and sends out two products, Mineral: Kaolin-Clay and Mineral: Petuntse-Stone.

Petuntse means "little white bricks," the form in which it travels to the potteries, and the kiln town mixes it with kaolin in set proportions: about equal parts for the finest ware and about two of stone to one of kaolin for common ware. When one digging runs out, the quarry opens new ones, so the benches keep moving along the hill.
- **Open-Cast Benches:** stepped cuts where white seams are quarried.
- **Mill Race and Pounding Mills:** water-driven hammers that break the stone.
- **Settling Pools:** where the washed slurry is allowed to clear and thicken into cakes and bricks.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Kaolin-Clay** (`Commodity`): Pure white, refractory clay essential for high-fire porcelain bodies.
- **Crushing-Sledge** (`Tool`): Heavy iron-headed hammer used to break raw stone blocks before wet milling.
- **Petuntse Stone** (`Commodity`): Weathered white porcelain stone, pulverized in water-powered mills to give porcelain its glassy body.
- **Petuntse Brick** (`Commodity`): Small pressed white brick of milled porcelain stone, the form in which it ships to the kilns.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Sediment Purity` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, iron contamination causes dark speckling and failure in high-fire kilns. At `8-10`, excessive washing wastes valuable fine fractions, and the quarry yields less for the same labor.
- **Quest Hook - *The Iron Vein Collapse***: Excavation hits an unexpected seam of iron pyrite, threatening to discolor an entire month's kaolin quota unless a new vein is opened. Davos Vane has two weeks before Kiln Buyer Wen Ashgrove's next loads are due, and Teodor Marsh's mill has already milled some of the stained rock.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The quarry has no `requires` tags and works from the hillside, its own picks and the mill race.
* **Output Transmission:** Carts `Mineral: Kaolin-Clay` and `Mineral: Petuntse-Stone` to the **Climbing Dragon Kiln (`IND-PORC-02`)**, which needs both to make porcelain.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The white seam. When it fails, the kiln town has no good body to throw.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Bench Collapse:** A cut face of weathered rock gives way on the quarry crew ($3\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Mill Crush:** A hand caught in the water-driven pounding hammers is crushed ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage and the hand is lost).
* **Dust Lung:** Breathing fine stone and clay dust day after day scars the lungs ($1\text{d}6$ damage per shift and a lingering cough).
