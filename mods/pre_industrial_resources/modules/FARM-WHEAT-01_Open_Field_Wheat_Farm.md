# Open-Field Wheat Farm (`FARM-WHEAT-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-WHEAT` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Open-field cultivation relies on communal crop rotation cycles, moldboard plows drawn by oxen teams, and manual broadcasting of grain seeds. Tenant laborers till the loam soil during early spring, maintain drainage furrows through summer rains, and harvest golden stalks in autumn using sickles and scythes before threshing them on stone floors. This relentless agrarian toil directly converts seasonal solar energy and fertile topsoil into raw harvested wheat.

The arable land lies in long strips (selions) grouped in furlongs, scattered so every family holds some good ground and some poor; a family might hold about seventy strips. Wheat goes into one field each autumn, spring crops into a second, and the third lies fallow and is grazed, its dung feeding the soil. Plow teams are shared among neighbors, and the lord's own demesne is worked by the tenants alongside their strips.
- **Furlongs and Strips:** the scattered tenant holdings.
- **Fallow Field:** grazed stubble that restores the soil.
- **Threshing Floor:** stone floor where the sheaves are beaten out.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Fallow Rotation Rate (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, soil exhaustion triggers severe blight. At `8–10`, disciplined three-field rotation restores robust nutrient balance, though less ground is sown each year and the lord's quota presses harder on what remains.
- **Quest Hook — *The Weevil Blight***: When a mysterious fungal rot infects the early granary reserves, Bailiff Vance presses the exhausted farm hands into emergency tilling before the autumn frost locks the soil. Strip-Warden Edda Harcastle swears the rot began in the wet strips Widow Oakhollow sowed on the fallow, and the party has until the first hard frost to prove whose grain is sound before the seed is seized.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. This is a source node working only soil, labor and seed; it needs no `requires` tags.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Crop: Raw-Wheat` by cart to the **Water-Powered Gristmill (`IND-WHEAT-02`)** and to the **Floor Malting Kiln (`IND-BREW-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The harvest window. A wet autumn decides how much grain reaches any mill at all.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Wet-Harvest Rot:** Damp sheaves or granary grain spoil and sicken those who eat them ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a fever penalty until the grain is cleaned).
* **Plow Team Accident:** A frightened ox or a snapped share crushes a plowman ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Sickle and Scythe Work:** Long harvest days with blades in crowded rows cut careless hands ($1\text{d}4$ slashing damage).
