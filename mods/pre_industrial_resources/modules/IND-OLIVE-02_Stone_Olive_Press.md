# Stone Olive Press (`IND-OLIVE-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-OLIVE` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A heavy circular stone basin and rotating edge-runner mill powered by draft animals crush harvested olives into a dense pomace. Laborers transfer the crushed mash into woven fiber mats, stacking them beneath a massive wooden lever-and-screw press to extract golden virgin oil. The facility transforms raw olives (`Crop: Olives`) and empty ceramic vessels (`Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels`), turned by `Livestock: Draft-Animals`, into high-grade `Oil: Olive-Oil` and storage jars.

A blindfolded mill beast keeps the stone turning to avoid dizziness. Paste is crushed for about thirty to forty minutes, then spread in rush or willow frails, stacked, and weighted; oil and watery lees (amurca) run into the vats and the oil is decanted off. Fruit should be pressed within a day of picking, and old paste left on mats ferments and taints the next batch, so the mats are scrubbed each run. Poor oil (lampante) goes to lamps and soap.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)

- **Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels** (`Commodity`): Kiln-fired amphorae sealed with pitch for long-term preservation.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Pressing Pressure` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, insufficient torque leaves residual oil trapped in the dry pomace. At `8–10`, excessive force shatters wooden press components or crushes bitter pits into the oil.
- **Quest Hook — *The Rusted Screw***: The bronze-threaded driving screw of Master Presser Nikolas Vance binds under maximum load, requiring Thalia Thorne to fabricate an emergency replacement pin before the stacked olive mash begins to ferment and ruin the batch. Marcello Danti's buyers wait on the jars, and Lucan Ferro says the paste will taint the vats within two days.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Crushes `Crop: Olives` from the **Terraced Olive Grove (`FARM-OLIVE-01`)**; stores the oil in `Commodity: Ceramic-Vessels` from the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)**; and turns the mill with `Livestock: Draft-Animals` from the **Pasture Ranch (`FARM-CATTLE-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Oil: Olive-Oil` to the **Caustic Soapboiler Vat (`IND-SOAP-01`)**, and sells the finest as an end product.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The harvest-to-press day. Fruit that waits is graded down to lamp oil.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Press Beam Failure:** A snapped beam or screw drops the weighted stack ($2\text{d}6$ crushing damage).
* **Mill Beast Kick:** A startled mill animal on the beam path ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
* **Tainted Batch:** Old fermented paste spoils a vat and sours a buyer's trust.
