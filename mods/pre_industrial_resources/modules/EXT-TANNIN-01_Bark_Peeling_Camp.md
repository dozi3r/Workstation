# Bark Peeling Camp (`EXT-TANNIN-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-TANNIN` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
During the spring sap run when bark slips easily from hardwood trunks, laborers use spuds and barking irons to strip thick oak and hemlock bark sheets from freshly felled logs. The stripped bark is dried in open air drying racks and bundled tightly for transport to leather tanneries. This specialized extractive forestry harvest captures the essential tannins required for hide preservation (the camp's `Timber: Hardwood-Logs` input and its `Commodity: Tree-Bark` output).

The season is short, roughly late April to mid-June in the oak woods, and sap is running when the trees are felled. Men and women both peel, working blunt scrapers around the trunk and lifting the bark off in lengths of about two feet, which are tied into bundles. Often the woodland owner sells the bark as one crop and the poles and logs as another.
- **Peeling Ground:** Felled trunks and coppice stools laid out for stripping.
- **Drying Racks & Bark House:** Raised racks in open air, with a roofed store for bundles awaiting the carts.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Sap Window (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, bark adheres stubbornly to dry wood, halting peeling. At `8–10`, peak spring sap allows effortless, pristine stripping, but the rush tempts crews to stack racks too thick, and any wet spell then rots the pile.
- **Quest Hook — *The Rains of Rot***: When an unseasonal damp spell threatens stacked drying bark with mold, Silas must mobilize every hand, including Jory, Widow Perpetua Hollin's hired woodcutters and Osric Fenwick's carts, to move tons of tannin sheets under shelter before the active compounds leach away; the spring window closes in about a week and the tannery's buyer will not pay for spoiled bark.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes `Timber: Hardwood-Logs` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**, the felled oak and hemlock trunks whose bark is stripped while the sap runs.
* **Output Transmission:** Dispatches bundles of `Commodity: Tree-Bark` to the **Bark Crushing Mill (`IND-TANNIN-02`)**, which pounds it into tanning extract.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The sap window. A missed or rained-out spring leaves the tanneries downstream without bark until the next year.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Falling Timber:** Rolling or slipping trunks on the peeling ground crush the careless ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Slipping Iron:** Wet, slick sapwood sends a barking iron skidding into hand or leg ($1\text{d}6$ slashing damage).
* **Rot and Mould:** Bark left wet on the racks is spoiled; the camp loses its pay and its buyer's trust.
