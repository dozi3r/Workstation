# Pelt Scraping & Furrier's Guild (`IND-FURS-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FURS` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A drafty, odor-heavy municipal workshop where raw, salted pelts are soaked in vats of astringent tree bark tannin, scraped clean of subcutaneous fat with curved draw-knives, and tumbled with chalk to soften the leather backing into supple, lustrous luxury garments ready for high-society tailoring. The required inputs of Animal: Raw-Pelts, Chem: Tannin, Metal: Forged-Iron for the knives, and Mineral: Chalk yield finished Luxury: Dressed-Furs.

Pelts are soaked, fleshed, scraped, tanned in baths of rising strength and softened. Frozen or salted pelts must be worked before hair slips. Furriers repair tears and bald spots and a pelt matcher pairs skins by size and colour so one garment looks cut from one animal. Sumptuary law reserves the finest furs for the nobility, so grading and provenance carry legal weight.
- **Soaking Vats:** tannin baths of increasing strength.
- **Fleshing Beam:** where the draw-knife clears fat and membrane.
- **Grading Table:** where lots are matched and stamped.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 2, 2]` - **Administrator** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Guild-regulated grading, exacting chemical soaking times, and precise knife work enforce strict quality controls and artisanal hierarchy.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Dressed Fur** (`Commodity`): Supple, cleaned, and conditioned animal pelt suitable for aristocratic garments.
- **Curved Fleshing Knife** (`Tool`): Double-handled steel blade used to scrape fat and membrane from raw pelts.
- **Chalk Tumbling Barrel** (`Reagent`): Barrel of powdered chalk in which dressed pelts are tumbled to soften and whiten the backing.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Tanning Bath Concentration` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, weak liquor leaves pelts stiff and prone to rotting hair slip. At `8-10`, overly strong acid eats the leather grain and ruins the batch.
- **Quest Hook - *The Spoiled Lot***: When a prized shipment of ermine pelts arrives water-damaged, Leofric Quince must rush emergency alum-and-chalk dressing to save the guild's royal commission; Ysolde Garrard, who must pass the matched lot, threatens to refuse it if the damaged skins are hidden.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Takes `Animal: Raw-Pelts` from the **Boreal Trapline Outpost (`EXT-FURS-01`)**; soaks them in `Chem: Tannin` from the **Bark Crushing Mill (`IND-TANNIN-02`)**; sharpens its fleshing knives with `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**; and tumbles the dressed backing with `Mineral: Chalk` from the **Limestone Quarry (`EXT-LIME-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Luxury: Dressed-Furs` to noble tailors and merchants; it is an end product with no consuming module.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The bark supply. Without tannin the vats sit idle and the pelts rot.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Tannery Fumes and Filth:** Long hours over the vats breed sickness and festering hands ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a cumulative penalty to fine work).
* **Fleshing Knife:** A slipped draw-knife on the beam opens a hand or forearm ($1\text{d}6$ slashing damage).
* **Vat Fall:** A slip into a deep tannin vat soaks and half-drowns the worker ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
