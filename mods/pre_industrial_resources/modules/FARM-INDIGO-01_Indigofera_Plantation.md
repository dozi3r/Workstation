# Indigofera Plantation (`FARM-INDIGO-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-INDIGO` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The Indigofera plantation consists of leveled tropical fields planted in dense rows of leguminous shrubbery adjacent to stone steeping vats. Field laborers harvest leafy branches at dawn before midday heat degrades the indican content within the foliage. Unprocessed inputs are zero, while harvested indigo-leaves are rushed to steeping vats to initiate pigment extraction.

The crop is sown in spring and cut around midsummer once the plants stand three to four feet high; new growth yields a second and sometimes a third cutting. Laborers on such plantations were often enslaved, and the stench and insects of the vat works shortened their lives to a few years. A colonial bounty on exports made the crop profitable.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[0, 1, 0]` — **Agricultural Enterprise** (`Market`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Driven by high commercial demand for natural blue dye, plantation workflows balance rapid harvest schedules with strict fermentation timing.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Indican Degradation Rate` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, leaves reach vats fresh with maximum pigment potential. At `8–10`, delayed transport causes leaves to ferment prematurely in baskets, ruining the dye yield.
- **Quest Hook — *The Wilting Carts***: Harvester Valli reports that broken transport carts are delaying leaf delivery, causing harvested indigo leaves to sour in the tropical sun. Cartwright Anselm Dray needs iron and timber to mend them, Planter Natesan has only until the noon heat to deliver the cut, and Factor Hollis Brandreth waits at the quay for the season's cakes.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. The plantation grows its own Indigofera crop from seed on leveled fields and needs no input from another module.
* **Output Transmission:** Carries fresh `Crop: Indigo-Leaves` the same day to the **Steeping Vat & Oxidation Beating Ponds (`IND-INDIGO-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The haul from field to vat. Every hour of delay spends pigment that no later stage can restore.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Field Heat and Insects:** Cutting through the heat of the day brings fever and bites ($1\text{d}6$ damage and fatigue).
* **Cutting Blades:** A slip of the sickle gashes a leg or hand ($1\text{d}4$ slashing damage).
* **Runaway Cart:** A loaded cart that loses a wheel on the track tips over its driver ($1\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
