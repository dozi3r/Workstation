# Mortar & Plaster Works (`IND-LIME-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-LIME` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
In shaded wooden mixing sheds, laborers shovel silica-grade sand and rich slaked lime putty into low circular stone troughs, blending them with measured streams of water. Working with heavy wooden hoes, craftsmen knead the viscous mix into durable masonry mortar and wall plaster, bagging or barreling the compound before it cures in the open air. The inputs `Chem: Slaked-Lime` and `Mineral: Silica-Sand` yield `Commodity: Masonry-Mortar`.

Old masons held to roughly one part putty to one and a half to three of washed sharp sand, and the lime must be fully slaked and rested, or it blows and blisters finished work. Mortar sets by carbonation in air and must dry slowly, not below about 5 degrees and not in frost. Plaster is laid in scratch, floating and setting coats with ox hair mixed in to hold the cracking. Mixed lime and sand may be banked for days and knocked up again before use.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Batch Consistency` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, sandy, watery mixes crumble upon drying. At `8–10`, overly lime-rich batches shrink and crack severely.
- **Quest Hook — *The Premature Set***: When an unexpected heatwave accelerates curing inside the storage casks, Hettie must rescue an entire shipment of royal fortification mortar before it hardens solid. Aldous Garrick gives her until the carts leave in two days, and Wynne Tarrow must knock up every banked lump before it stiffens.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Kneads `Chem: Slaked-Lime` from the **Calcining Lime Kiln (`IND-LIME-02`)** with `Mineral: Silica-Sand` from the **Silica Sand Dredging Pit (`EXT-GLASS-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Commodity: Masonry-Mortar` as an end product to masons and builders; no module consumes it.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The matured putty. Fresh lime cannot be rushed into mortar.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Lime Splash:** Wet lime on skin or in the eyes burns ($1\text{d}6$ damage and risk of blindness).
* **Blown Plaster:** Partly slaked lime blisters finished walls; a ceiling coat may drop on the crew ($1\text{d}6$ damage).
* **Frost Failure:** Fresh mortar caught by frost crumbles and the wall must be torn down.
