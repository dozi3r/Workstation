# Copper Alembic Distillery (`IND-VINE-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-VINE` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Hand-hammered copper pot stills featuring long cooling condensers and water jackets sit over controlled masonry hearths. Laborers manage wood-fuel combustion to gently vaporize fermented wine, capturing the concentrated alcohol vapor through precision fractional condensation. The facility transforms vintage wine, forged copper hardware, and wood fuel into potent, purified distilled spirits.

Spirit is made in repeated runs: the first fraction (heads) is harsh and very strong, the last (tails) is weak, and both are set aside and run again while the heart is kept for aging. Proof is tested by burning a spoonful or igniting gunpowder in it. Wine is distilled partly because it ships easier and, where tax is by volume, takes up less room.
- **Still Hearth:** A masonry firebox under the copper pot, fed with wood fuel.
- **Worm Tub:** A coiled copper tube in a tub of cold water, where vapor condenses.
- **Receiving Bench:** Where heads, heart and tails are caught in separate vessels.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Still Thermal Gradient` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, inadequate heat fails to vaporize the alcohol, stalling the run. At `8–10`, violent boiling scorches the wine, infusing the distillate with foul pyrolytic tars.
- **Quest Hook — *The Scorched Mash***: A sudden draft stokes the hearth of Bram Mason too fiercely, threatening to ruin Master Alchemist Vespera Drake's entire batch of high-grade spirit unless the heat is immediately damped and redirected. Wren Halloway warns the seams will not survive another surge, and Ivo Karrell's buyer sails with the tide tomorrow.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Takes `Food: Vintage-Wine` from the **Wine Press & Fermenting Cellar (`IND-VINE-02`)**, `Metal: Forged-Copper` for stills and worms from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**, and `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Carries `Chem: Distilled-Spirits` to the **Spagyric Apothecary & Quarantine Lazaretto (`IND-APOTH-01`)** for medicine and solvents.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** Copper repair and fuel supply decide how many runs the stills can make in a season.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Vapor Fire:** A leaking seam beside the open hearth ignites spirit vapor ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage across the still room).
* **Bad Heads:** Drinking unseparated heads sickens and blinds the unwary (a $1\text{d}6$ poison damage and a penalty to sight).
