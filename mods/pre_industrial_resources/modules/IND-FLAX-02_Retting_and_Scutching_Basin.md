# Retting & Scutching Basin (`IND-FLAX-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FLAX` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Here `Crop: Raw-Flax` yields `Textile: Flax-Fiber` and `Oil: Linseed-Oil`. Before retting, workers strip ripe seed bolls with a coarse rippling comb, roast the seed, and hammer it in a wedge press for oil. Stalk bundles are then weighted down in a slow stream until they feel slimy and the woody core springs free -- cleaner than a stagnant pond, though still foul downstream.

Retted stalks dry on grass on both sides, age indoors for weeks, and are crushed in a wooden brake, beaten with a blunt scutching knife, and drawn through hackling combs into strick bundles while short tow goes for sacking.
- **Retting Basin:** Weir-fed stream pool weighted with stones.
- **Grassing Field & Aging Loft:** Open meadow and indoor loft for drying retted straw.
- **Brake, Scutching Shed & Wedge Press:** Wooden brakes, scutching boards, and stamper press for linseed oil.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 3, 2]` — **Executioner** (`Market`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Stinky, labor-intensive water retting and aggressive scutching demand resilient, pragmatic workers who tolerate harsh chemical stenches and repetitive physical strain, paid by the bundle of fiber sold on to spinners and linen merchants.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Flax Fiber** (`Commodity`): Clean, combed bundles of golden bast fibers ready for spinning wheels.
- **Scutching Knife** (`Tool`): A flat wooden blade used to scrape away broken woody core fragments.
- **Linseed Oil** (`Commodity`): Raw golden seed oil pressed from rippled flax seed, used as a binder for paint, putty, varnish and printing ink.
- **Rippling Comb** (`Tool`): Coarse iron-toothed comb drawn through bundle tops to strip the seed bolls before retting.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - Retting Saturation (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, under-retted stalks remain bonded to woody shives, ruining the batch. At `8-10`, optimal soaking rot allows effortless fiber separation, but a day too long weakens the fibre and fouls the stream below.
- **Quest Hook - *The Foul Basin Poisoning***: When industrial runoff contaminates the retting ponds and kills the softening retting rot, Branick Culley must purge the basin upstream before the entire regional linen supply rots. Perrin Tace threatens the manor court within three days, and Nesta Reed swears the bundles were pulled on time.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Rets, scutches and presses `Crop: Raw-Flax` from the **Flax Farm (`FARM-FLAX-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Textile: Flax-Fiber` to the **Handloom Linen Weaver (`IND-FLAX-03`)** and to the **Tallow Chandlery (`IND-CANDLE-01`)**, and ships `Oil: Linseed-Oil` in casks to the **Movable-Type Printshop & Bindery (`IND-PAPER-03`)**, where it is boiled with lampblack into printing ink.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The retting water. A foul or blocked stream stops the whole fibre line.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Retting Stench:** Standing in the basin or breathing its fumes sickens the lungs ($1\text{d}4$ damage per hour and a penalty to concentration).
* **Brake Injury:** A hand caught in the breaking brake is crushed ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Roasting Fire:** Seed roasted over an open hearth can flare ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage and a chance the oil shed ignites).
