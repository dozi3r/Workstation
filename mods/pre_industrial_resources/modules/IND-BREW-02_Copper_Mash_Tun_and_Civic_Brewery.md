# Copper Mash-Tun & Civic Brewery (`IND-BREW-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-BREW` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The civic brewery houses immense riveted copper mash-tuns where crushed malted grain is steeped in hot water, mashed, and boiled with dried hop cones. Coopered timber casks receive the cooled, fermented wort, sealing the beverage into stable rations of nutritional ale for urban laborers and civic guards. The required inputs of Food: Malted-Grain, Metal: Forged-Copper, Timber: Cooperage-Casks, Fuel: Wood-Fuel and Crop: Hops yield stable Food: Barrelled-Ale.

The mash stands for an hour or two in the tun, then the wort runs to the copper and is boiled for an hour or so with the hop cones. It must be cooled fast: yeast dies in hot wort and slow cooling invites infection. Fermentation happens in open wooden vessels that are hard to keep clean, and the beer then goes into casks to settle in the cellar. Hopped beer keeps for months where plain ale lasts a week, and beer brewers make much larger batches than ale brewers. The ale-conner, chosen yearly at the court, tests the ale and reports short measures and over-pricing.
- **Mash Tun and Copper:** the main brewing vessels, heated by wood fire.
- **Coolship:** the shallow vessel where the wort cools quickly.
- **Fermenting Vats:** open wooden vessels.
- **Cask Cellar:** cool store where ale settles.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - Fermentation Ferocity (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, sluggish yeast action leads to flat, sweet swill. At `8-10`, runaway wild yeast infection spoils entire cellars of aging ale.
- **Quest Hook - *The Sour Cask Conspiracy***: Mysterious contamination ruins the municipal winter ale reserve just as the annual harvest festival begins. Hannelore and Joran must trace the tainted stave wood before civic inspectors shut down the brewery lanes, while Ale-Conner Dorcas Fenwick has three days to report the brewery to the court.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Mashes `Food: Malted-Grain` from the **Floor Malting Kiln (`IND-BREW-01`)**; boils in vessels of `Metal: Forged-Copper` from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**; stores the ale in `Timber: Cooperage-Casks` from the **Master Cooperage (`IND-TIMBER-03`)**; fires the copper with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; and flavours and preserves the beer with `Crop: Hops` from the **Hop Garden and Oast Kiln (`FARM-HOPS-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Delivers `Food: Barrelled-Ale` to taverns and the garrison; it is an end product with no consumer module.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The hop supply. Without hops the brewery returns to short-lived plain ale.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Scalding Wort:** A split copper or overflowing mash-tun scalds the brewers ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Cellar Gas:** Heavy gas collects over fermenting vats and in low cellars and overcomes anyone working there ($1\text{d}6$ damage and unconsciousness).
* **Infection:** One sour vat can spoil every cask in the cellar, and a bad cask sickens the drinkers ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a lost contract).
