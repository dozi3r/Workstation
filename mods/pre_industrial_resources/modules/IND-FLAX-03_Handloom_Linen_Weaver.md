# Handloom Linen Weaver (`IND-FLAX-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-FLAX` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Skilled artisans mount spun flax yarn onto wooden warping frames, thread heddles on complex counterbalance looms, and rhythmically pass heavy shuttles back and forth through alternating warp sheds. The interlocking threads are beaten tight with a wooden batten, creating durable, breathable linen cloth. This meticulous craft transforms refined fiber into finished garments and sailcloth. The required input of Textile: Flax-Fiber, spun to yarn, yields Textile: Linen-Cloth.

Yarn is spun in cottages on the wheel, mostly by women, with the flax kept moist and the distaff holding the fibre aligned. Yarn is sold by weight in 300-yard cuts. After weaving the cloth is bleached: boiled in a lye of ash, seaweed or fermented bran, rinsed, and spread on a grass bleach green by the river, then repeated. Finished bolts are brought before the linen board's inspector, who measures and stamps them for sale.
- **Warping Frame:** where the warp is laid out in hundreds of threads.
- **Handloom:** heddles, batten and shuttle.
- **Bleach Green:** riverside grass where cloth is laid to whiten in the sun.
- **Stamping Table:** where the inspector measures and marks each bolt.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — Warp Tension (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, uneven tension snaps warp threads constantly, halting production. At `8–10`, smooth, balanced tension creates flawless woven bolts, but overstrain cramps hands and snaps dry threads.
- **Quest Hook — *The Broken Heddle Frame***: When the master loom's primary heddle collapses during a rush royal order, Elenor must improvise emergency repairs using bone pins and silk cord before the bailiff inspects the workshop. Cuthbert Lyle arrives in two days and will refuse the bolts if the weave shows a flaw.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Weaves yarn spun from `Textile: Flax-Fiber` supplied by the **Retting & Scutching Basin (`IND-FLAX-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Textile: Linen-Cloth` as sailcloth and canvas to the **Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)**, and as rag stock to the **Retting Trough & Stamping Mill (`IND-PAPER-01`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The inspector's stamp. Unstamped cloth cannot be sold on the open market.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Lye Burns:** Handling hot bleaching lye scalds hands and eyes ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a penalty to fine work).
* **Loom Strain:** Long days at the beam cramp the back and wrists, imposing a cumulative penalty to craft checks.
* **Green Theft:** Cloth left out on the bleach green overnight may be stolen or soaked by rain.
