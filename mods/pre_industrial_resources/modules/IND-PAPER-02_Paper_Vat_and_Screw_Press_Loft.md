# Paper Vat & Screw-Press Loft (`IND-PAPER-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PAPER` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Centered on copper-heated pulp vats (`Metal: Forged-Copper`) and thirty- to fifty-ton oak screw presses, this loft turns `Material: Rag-Pulp`, `Chem: Mordant-Alum` and `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` into `Commodity: Laid-Paper`. Working two wire moulds and one deckle, a three-person team -- vatman, coucher, and layer -- forms and stacks thousands of wet sheets onto wool felts, pressing and exchanging packs four or five times.

Pressed sheets are dipped in warm gelatin size boiled from tanners' scrap and hardened with alum, then hung in spurs on hair ropes in the drying loft. Wood fuel and candles ward off winter frost, while summer heat quickly sours the size.
- **Vat & Press Floor:** Copper-heated pulp tub and heavy oak screw press.
- **Size Tub & Drying Loft:** Warm gelatin-alum bath and airy attic strung with hair ropes.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 2, 1]` — **Guild Artisan** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Sheet formation requires rhythmic muscular coordination, strict quality standards, and generational apprenticeship secrets.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Laid Paper** (`Commodity`): Durable writing and book sheets marked with distinct wire chain lines.
- **Wire Mould** (`Tool`): Fine brass mesh frame used to catch and drain fibrous slurry into sheets.
- **Alum Size Tub** (`Reagent`): Warm gelatin hardened with alum so ink does not bleed; spoils fast in heat.
- **Felt Blankets** (`Tool`): Wool felts that receive each couched sheet.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Press Pressure` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, waterlogged sheets warp and delaminate during drying. At `8–10`, excessive torque crushes the wet felts and tears the paper web.
- **Quest Hook — *The Faded Watermark***: A batch of custom crest moulds becomes misaligned, stamping blurred insignia onto the municipal tax rolls. Master Vatman Corin Whitaker must remake the wire and re-run the order before the city notary rejects it in five days, while Wenna Hale suspects someone bent the moulds on purpose.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Fills its vats with `Material: Rag-Pulp` from the **Retting Trough & Stamping Mill (`IND-PAPER-01`)**; hardens its size with `Chem: Mordant-Alum` from the **Alunite Quarry & Leaching Vats (`EXT-ALUM-01`)**; heats vats and loft with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; and heats its vats in `Metal: Forged-Copper` kettles from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Commodity: Laid-Paper` to the **Movable-Type Printshop & Bindery (`IND-PAPER-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The master vatman. If his shake fails the mill cannot make good paper.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Press Screw:** A kicked lever or collapsing pack breaks arms ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Hot Vat:** Scalding water from the copper pot burns the vat crew ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Loft Frost:** Freezing air in the drying loft ruins a whole load of sheets and the day's wages.
