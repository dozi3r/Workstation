# Paper Vat & Screw-Press Loft (`IND-PAPER-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-PAPER` (Stage 2: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Centered around heated vats of liquid pulp and towering oak screw-presses, skilled vatmen dip wire-mesh moulds into the slurry, expertly shaking them to interlock wet fibers into uniform sheets. The nascent paper sheets are couched onto felt blankets, stacked in multi-ton screw presses to expel excess water, and then loft-dried into durable laid paper. The required inputs of Material: Rag-Pulp, Chem: Mordant-Alum, Fuel: Wood-Fuel and Metal: Forged-Copper become Commodity: Laid-Paper.

A vat team of three works two moulds and one deckle: the vatman forms, the coucher turns each sheet onto a felt, and the layer strips the pressed sheets and stacks them; a skilled team forms thousands of sheets a day. Presses of thirty to fifty tons squeeze the packs, with sheets exchanged four or five times. The sheets are dipped in warm gelatin size boiled from tanners' scrap, hardened with alum, then hung in spurs on hair ropes in the loft. In winter the loft needs candles and wood fuel to keep sheets from freezing, and warm days sour the size.
- **Vat:** copper-heated tub of pulp.
- **Press Floor:** oak screw press for packs.
- **Size Tub:** warm gelatin and alum.
- **Drying Loft:** airy attic with hair ropes.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Press Pressure` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, waterlogged sheets warp and delaminate during drying. At `8–10`, excessive torque crushes the wet felts and tears the paper web.
- **Quest Hook — *The Faded Watermark***: A batch of custom crest moulds becomes misaligned, stamping blurred insignia onto the municipal tax rolls. Master Vatman Corin Finch must remake the wire and re-run the order before the city notary rejects it in five days, while Wenna Hale suspects someone bent the moulds on purpose.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Fills its vats with `Material: Rag-Pulp` from the **Retting Trough & Stamping Mill (`IND-PAPER-01`)**; hardens its size with `Chem: Mordant-Alum` from the **Alunite Quarry & Leaching Vats (`EXT-ALUM-01`)**; heats vats and loft with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; and heats its vats in `Metal: Forged-Copper` kettles from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Commodity: Laid-Paper` to the **Movable-Type Printshop & Bindery (`IND-PAPER-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The master vatman. If his shake fails the mill cannot make good paper.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Press Screw:** A kicked lever or collapsing pack breaks arms ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Hot Vat:** Scalding water from the copper pot burns the vat crew ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage).
* **Loft Frost:** Freezing air in the drying loft ruins a whole load of sheets and the day's wages.
