# Seasoning Timber Pond & Sawpit (`IND-SHIP-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-SHIP` (Stage 1: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Raw oak logs and towering pine mast-spars are submerged in coastal tidal seasoning ponds to leach out natural saps and sugars that attract wood-boring pests and rot. After months of soaking, pit-sawyers position the heavy timbers over deep sawpits, using long two-man rip-saws to cut precise structural frames, curved ribs, and planking for shipbuilding. The required inputs of Timber: Hardwood-Logs, Timber: Mast-Spars, and Metal: Forged-Iron yield Naval: Seasoned-Ship-Timber.

Fir spars and masts soak in salt-water ponds, while oak is stacked to air in covered sheds, sometimes holding years of stock. In the pits the top sawyer stands on the log and guides the saw while the bottom sawyer works below in falling sawdust, and a pair cuts only about a dozen boards a day. Sawyers are paid by the foot cut, with oak paying best.
- **Mast Pond:** salt-water soaking basin for spars.
- **Seasoning Sheds:** covered, ventilated stacks of oak.
- **Sawpits:** trenches where logs are held by iron dogs.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Seasoned Ship Timber** (`Commodity`): Cured, cut oak ribs and planking ready for structural hull assembly.
- **Rip Saw** (`Tool`): Heavy two-man steel saw used in sawpits for vertical timber planking cuts.
- **Log Dog** (`Tool`): G-shaped iron clamp hammered into a log to hold it steady over the pit.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Timber Seasoning Depth` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, premature removal leaves internal moisture, causing warping and dry rot inside finished hulls. At `8–10`, perfect curing yields exceptionally durable framing timbers, but stock sits for years in the sheds and the yard is always short of cured timber for the next hull.
- **Quest Hook — *The Split Rib***: Master Sawyer Ulfric Stone discovers a hidden hairline fracture running through the keelson timber, halting framing work on the new flagship. Purveyor Bryony Kerslake swears the log was cured, and the commissioner wants the launch date kept within the month.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Soaks and saws `Timber: Hardwood-Logs` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; takes `Timber: Mast-Spars` from the **Boreal Conifer Logging & Turpentine Camp (`EXT-PINE-01`)**; and relies on `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)** for saws, dogs and clamps.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Naval: Seasoned-Ship-Timber` to the **Drydock Slipway & Caulking Yard (`IND-SHIP-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** Time. Seasoning cannot be hurried, so a yard that launches early inherits rot.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Sawpit Collapse:** A kerf binds or a log slips from its dogs and crushes the bottom sawyer ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Dry-Rot Spores:** Working in a condemned shed leaves lungs and clothes fouled ($1\text{d}6$ poison damage per hour and spores carried into other stacks).
* **Pit Water:** A flooded pit chills and exhausts the crew (levels of fatigue until they climb out).
