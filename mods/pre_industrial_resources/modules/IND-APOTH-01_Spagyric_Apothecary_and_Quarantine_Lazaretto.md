# Spagyric Apothecary & Quarantine Lazaretto (`IND-APOTH-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-APOTHECARY` (Stage 1: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Positioned at the city gates, this fortress-like apothecary combines distillation alembics and herbal extraction vats with isolation wards for arriving travelers. Apothecaries steep rare spices and botanical extracts in high-proof distilled spirits and vinegar, sealing the curative tinctures in sterile glassware to combat urban plagues. The shop draws on `Chem: Distilled-Spirits`, `Luxury: Cured-Spices-and-Tea`, `Commodity: Glassware` and `Chem: Wine-Vinegar` and yields `Medicine: Apothecary-Tinctures`.

The shop works under guild rules: masters bind apprentices and the guild searches shops for bad stock. Its stock includes plague waters and theriac, a compound of dozens of ingredients. Arrivals are held for forty days, overseen by an armed guardian appointed by the health magistracy, while physicians who fled the city leave the apothecary to treat the sick.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)
- **Baseline Coordinate**: `[2, 2, 4]` - **Civic Guardian** (`Hierarchy`).
- **Sociological Rationale**: Managing public health and enforcing strict quarantine protocols require absolute regulatory control, rigorous sanitation, and civic defense oversight.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Distilled-Spirits** (`Reagent`): High-proof alcohol used as a primary solvent and preservative base.
- **Apothecary-Tinctures** (`Medicine`): Concentrated medicinal extracts formulated to treat fevers and infections.
- **Theriac Jar** (`Commodity`): A sealed jar of the long-compounded cure-all, prized and slow to make.
- **Guild Search Warrant** (`Relic`): The sealed paper letting guild officers enter the shop and test its stock.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Quarantine Sealing` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, lax sanitary barriers let infectious vectors slip past the lazaretto gates. At `8-10`, draconian lockdowns provoke civil unrest among quarantined merchant caravans.
- **Quest Hook - *The Contaminated Alchemical Stock***: A shipment of imported botanical spices arrives infested with mold, threatening to spoil the entire batch of plague preventative. Ansgar Vellacott and Gerolt Stannard must isolate the compromised reserves and synthesize an alternative tincture before a fever outbreak strikes the inner wards, while Orsolo Quill spreads word of the spoiled stock and Marco Dellavar holds the gate.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Consumes `Chem: Distilled-Spirits` from the **Copper Alembic Distillery (`IND-VINE-03`)**, `Luxury: Cured-Spices-and-Tea` from the **Firing Woks & Curing Sheds (`IND-SPICE-02`)**, `Commodity: Glassware` from the **Glassblower's Furnace (`IND-GLASS-02`)**, and `Chem: Wine-Vinegar` from the **Wine Press & Fermenting Cellar (`IND-VINE-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Dispatches sealed flasks of `Medicine: Apothecary-Tinctures` to the city's wards and physicians; it is an end product with no module consumer.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Contagion Exposure:** Entering the infected wards without fumigation risks a creeping fever ($1\text{d}6$ damage per day and a penalty to checks until treated).
* **Toxic Remedy:** A badly compounded or adulterated tincture, such as hellebore or a mineral nostrum, poisons the patient ($2\text{d}6$ damage).
* **Still Room Fire:** A cracked alembic over open flame ignites the high-proof spirit ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage).
