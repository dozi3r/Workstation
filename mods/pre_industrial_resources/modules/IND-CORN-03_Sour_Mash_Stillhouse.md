# Sour-Mash Stillhouse (`IND-CORN-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CORN` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
A timber-and-stone distillery equipped with copper pot stills, wooden fermenting mash tubs, and cooling worm coils fed by cold spring water. Fermented corn mash is boiled and distilled into potent corn whiskey, then aged in charred oak cooperage casks to mellow the spirit for trade and morale distribution. The stillhouse turns Food: Cornmeal-Rations into Food: Corn-Whiskey using Timber: Cooperage-Casks, Fuel: Wood-Fuel, Metal: Forged-Copper and Water: Baseline-Potable-Flow.

Frontier farmers turn bulky grain into spirit because it is easier to carry over the mountains and often passes as money. Each fermentation is started with a measure of spent mash from the last run, so the acid keeps bacteria down. The still runs in batches: the first spirit and the heads are thrown away, the middle is kept, and the last runnings are cut off. Duty is charged per still or per gallon, in coin.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Corn Whiskey** (`Commodity`): High-proof distilled spirit aged in charred oak casks for morale and trade.
- **Copper Pot Still** (`Tool`): Large alembic boiler and condenser worm used to vaporize and distill fermented mash.
- **Spent Mash Backset** (`Reagent`): Sour liquor drawn from the last run and stirred into each new tub to keep it clean.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Still Condenser Flow` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, cooling water fails, causing vapor blowouts and bitter low-grade spirit. At `8-10`, over-cooling stalls distillation and wastes fuel.
- **Quest Hook - *The Taxman's Knock***: When municipal revenue agents arrive unannounced during a heavy distillation run, Silas must scramble to conceal his hidden spirit casks before inspection. Excise Collector Ezra Pettibone will take the duty in coin, or the still, and Packer Hollis Brandt needs the casks loaded before nightfall.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Ferments `Food: Cornmeal-Rations` from the **Nixtamalization Vats & Stone Metate Mill (`IND-CORN-02`)**; ages and carries the spirit in `Timber: Cooperage-Casks` from the **Master Cooperage (`IND-TIMBER-03`)**; fires the still with `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` from the **Hardwood Logging Camp (`EXT-TIMBER-01`)**; builds and mends the still with `Metal: Forged-Copper` from the **Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)**; and cools the worm with `Water: Baseline-Potable-Flow` from **The Communal Artesian Well and Village Cistern (`T0-DOM-02`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** `Food: Corn-Whiskey` is an end product sold as currency and morale.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The collector. The stillhouse is seized or spared at his word.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Still Fire:** Spirit vapor builds in a poorly vented loft and ignites ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage to anyone inside).
* **Bad Cut:** Drinking from the first runnings or the tails sickens the drinker ($1\text{d}6$ damage and a day of blindness or fever).
* **Collector's Grievance:** Roughing up or driving off a revenue man draws armed men to the district within a week.
