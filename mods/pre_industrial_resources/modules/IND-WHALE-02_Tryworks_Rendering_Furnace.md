# Tryworks Rendering Furnace (`IND-WHALE-02`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-WHALE` (Stage 2: `refining`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The tryworks facility houses copper rendering pots set into brick masonry fireboxes backed by a heavy stone wall. Cut strips of `Animal: Whale-Blubber` are diced and boiled over `Fuel: Wood-Fuel` and crackling scraps in `Metal: Forged-Copper` try-pots, then ladled into cold water to cool and purify before barreling in `Timber: Cooperage-Casks` sealed with `Chem: Pine-Pitch`.

Rendering ("trying out") works like lard boiling: solid residue is skimmed when pots fill, and oil color shifts from honey yellow to dark brown depending on blubber freshness. Shore stations set several fireboxes in a row below the cooper's house.
- **Firebox Row:** Brick fireboxes holding copper try-pots against a stone back wall.
- **Cooling Trough:** Cold water bath where hot oil is ladled to cool and settle.
- **Cooper's Shed:** Workshop where oil casks are raised, pitched, and filled.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Rendering Yield & Purity` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, tainted blubber and wet fuel produce murky, foul-smelling oil that commands low market value. At `8–10`, overheating causes the vats to boil over, igniting catastrophic yard fires.
- **Quest Hook — *The Scorched Batch***: Ezra Trengove battles a runaway fire in the rendering sheds after a crackling-fed furnace overheats, threatening thousands of gallons of stored oil. Jory Pellew is trapped at the last firebox, Seth Gower's pitch kettle stands beside the flames, and Marguerite Valcourt's ship sails with the next tide.
