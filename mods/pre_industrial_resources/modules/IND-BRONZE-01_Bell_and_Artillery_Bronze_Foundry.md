# Bell & Artillery Bronze Foundry (`IND-BRONZE-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-BRONZE` (Stage 1: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The bronze foundry is a cavernous pit-kiln workshop centered around deep casting pits lined with fired bricks and clay loam. Master founders precisely proportion copper and tin ingots inside graphite crucibles, firing them with abundant charcoal fuel until liquid bronze forms. The required inputs of copper, tin, fuel, and refractory bricks are transformed into high-tensile Alloy: Cast-Bronze poured directly into complex loam-and-wax molds for ordnance and bells. The Metal: Copper-Ingot, Metal: Tin-Ingot, Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal, Commodity: Fired-Bricks, Mineral: Raw-Clay, Animal: Tallow and Mineral: Graphite all feed the pit.

A mould is built up around a core of brick covered in loam, shaped with a strickle board. The "false bell" is modelled on this core in wax and coated in several layers of fireproof clay; an outer mould is built around it and the wax is melted away to leave the cavity. Bell metal is about four parts copper to one of tin; gunmetal has less tin. Too much tin makes the bronze brittle. The metal is melted at roughly 1,100 degrees C and poured into pits dug in the floor, which steady the mould and slow its cooling. A bell is cast slightly thick and tuned by removing metal; none can be added. Guns are inspected at each stage of proof and can be rejected.
- **Casting Pit:** brick-lined pit holding the mould during the pour.
- **Melting Furnace:** charcoal-fired furnace and graphite crucibles.
- **Loam Store:** clay and loam for cores, false bells and mould coats.
- **Tuning Floor:** where the founder shaves a cast bell to pitch.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Alloy Ratio Precision` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, tin segregation causes brittle, cracking castings. At `8-10`, perfect metallurgical balance yields resonant bronze of exceptional tensile strength, but the strict ratio leaves no room to correct a bad pour and ruins any off-ratio batch.
- **Quest Hook - *The Cracked Recasting***: A massive siege gun mold fractures during the pour, releasing molten bronze across the pit floor and pitting Master Niccolò against desperate military inspectors. Proof Master Leopold Drach gives the foundry one month to recast the gun, or the contract is lost.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Melts `Metal: Copper-Ingot` from the **Copper Smelting Furnace (`IND-COPPER-02`)** with `Metal: Tin-Ingot` from the **Tin Blowing House (`IND-TIN-02`)**; fires the crucibles with `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**; lines the pit with `Commodity: Fired-Bricks` from the **Brickworks & Pottery Kiln (`IND-CLAY-02`)**; builds moulds from `Mineral: Raw-Clay` from the **Riverbank Clay Pit (`EXT-CLAY-01`)**; works the wax and mould coats with `Animal: Tallow` from the **Civic Abattoir (`IND-CATTLE-02`)**; and melts in crucibles tempered with `Mineral: Graphite` from the **Plumbago Drift Mine (`MINE-GRAPHITE-01`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Casts `Alloy: Cast-Bronze` for the **Clockwork & Navigational Instrument Guild (`IND-OPTIC-02`)**, the **Water-Powered Corning & Powder Mill (`IND-POWDER-03`)** and the **Admiralty Fitting-Out Basin (`IND-SHIP-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The tin and the graphite. Without tin the alloy fails; without graphite the crucibles crack.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Molten Bronze:** A cracked mould or split crucible sprays metal across the pit floor ($3\text{d}6$ fire damage and horrific burns).
* **Wet Mould:** A mould fired too cool retains moisture, and the steam blows it apart in the pour ($2\text{d}6$ damage to everyone near the pit).
* **Casting Pit:** A man stumbling into the open pit falls onto brick and hot mould ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
