# Coppersmith & Brazier Shop (`IND-COPPER-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-COPPER` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Reheating `Metal: Copper-Ingot` with `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` and `Metal: Forged-Iron` stakes, this shop hammers out seamless `Metal: Forged-Copper` kettles, alembics, and fittings. Because copper work-hardens and cracks when beaten cold, the brazier repeatedly anneals and quenches each piece between courses of hammer blows.

Braziers raise hollow ware from patterns and sheet copper, while redsmiths repair worn vessels and re-tin scoured cookware interiors against corrosion. Apprentices start young, breaking charcoal, scouring copper for the tin bath, and polishing stakes before they are trusted with a raising hammer.
- **Hammer Room:** Polished stakes, anvils, and raising hammers for sinking sheet copper.
- **Annealing Hearth:** Bellows-fed forge softening metal between hammer courses.
- **Tinning Bench & Pattern Rack:** Scouring station, molten tin bath, and templates for alembics and kettles.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Annealing Cycles` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, unannealed copper work-hardens and shatters under the hammer. At `8-10`, over-heating melts delicate soldered seams.
- **Quest Hook - *The Fractured Alembic***: When a crucial royal distillation helm cracks during final shaping, Matthias Kroll must reweld and anneal the vessel before the morning cargo deadline. Barnaby Holloway has already noted that the ingot used bears no guild mark, Hanno Ibsen's hammer arm is shaking with exhaustion, and Wilhelmina Pardew cannot re-tin the helm until the seam is sound.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Hammers `Metal: Copper-Ingot` from the **Copper Smelting Furnace (`IND-COPPER-02`)**, fires its annealing hearth with `Fuel: Charcoal-or-Coal` from the **Charcoal Burner's Mound (`IND-CHARCOAL-01`)** or the **Deep Colliery and Coking Ovens (`MINE-COAL-01`)**, and works with hammers, stakes and anvils of `Metal: Forged-Iron` from the **Blacksmith Forge (`IND-IRON-03`)**.

* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The annealing hearth. If it goes cold, every still, boiler and kettle in the region waits on repair.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Forge Burn:** A spilled hearth, hot tongs or flying scale burn the hands and face ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage and a penalty to fine work).
* **Hammer Strain:** A day of lead hammering without relief leaves the striker with a numb arm and a loose grip ($1\text{d}6$ damage and disadvantage on hammer checks).
* **Tinning Fumes:** Molten tin and scouring acid in a closed shop sicken the lungs ($1\text{d}6$ poison damage per hour and nausea).
