# Ropewalk & Sail Loft (`IND-HEMP-03`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-HEMP` (Stage 3: `manufacturing`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
The ropewalk is an exceptionally long, narrow covered gallery where spinners walk backward while feeding hackled hemp fibers onto spinning wheels, twisting them into high-tensile cordage. Adjacent in the loft, master sailmakers cut and hand-sew heavy linen cloth into expansive canvas sails, treating both products with pine-pitch to resist rot and salt spray. The required inputs of Textile: Oakum-and-Tow, Chem: Pine-Pitch and Textile: Linen-Cloth become Naval: Hemp-Cordage and Naval: Canvas-Sails.

Yarn passes from the white yarn house to a hot tarring house and a black yarn house, then back to the walk, where strands are twisted together, each layer laid opposite to the one beneath. The longest walks ran some hundreds of metres. Fire is a constant danger, and untarred rope that looks dry can be rotting inside.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial — `Cordage Tensile Strength` (`0–10`)**: At `0–3`, uneven twisting creates weak points prone to snapping under heavy wind loads. At `8–10`, superior cordage meets strict naval specifications, commanding top premium prices, but the walk runs night and day and the dust and kettles raise the risk of a fire.
- **Quest Hook — *The Rotten Twist***: Garreth Cole discovers moisture contamination in a primary batch of standing rigging just days before a major fleet launch. Tar-House Keeper Hester Brand swears the yarn was tarred dry, Sailmaker Isolda Wright wants her canvas rigged on sound lines only, and the admiralty will not wait past the launch day.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** Spins `Textile: Oakum-and-Tow` from the **Hemp Braking & Hackling Shed (`IND-HEMP-02`)**, tars its yarn and coats its canvas with `Chem: Pine-Pitch` from the **Tar Kiln & Pitch Boilery (`IND-PINE-02`)**, and cuts sails from `Textile: Linen-Cloth` woven at the **Handloom Linen Weaver (`IND-FLAX-03`)**.
* **Output Transmission:** Ships `Naval: Hemp-Cordage` to the **Sugarcane Plantation and Harvest Fields (`AGRI-SUGAR-01`)**, the **Pelagic Fishing Fleet Wharf (`EXT-FISH-01`)**, the **Offshore Whaling Flensing Slip (`EXT-WHALE-01`)** and the **Admiralty Fitting-Out Basin (`IND-SHIP-03`)**, and sends `Naval: Canvas-Sails` to the **Admiralty Fitting-Out Basin (`IND-SHIP-03`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The tarring house. Without pitch the rope looks sound and rots inside; the lie is found only at sea.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Walk Fire:** Hemp dust or hot tar catches and flame races down the covered lane ($2\text{d}6$ fire damage and the walk is lost for a season).
* **Parting Cable:** A cable under strain snaps and whips across the walk ($2\text{d}6$ bludgeoning damage).
* **Tar Scald:** A tipped kettle splashes hot pitch on the keeper's crew ($1\text{d}6$ fire damage and a lingering burn).
