# Three-Sisters Maize Plantation (`FARM-CORN-01`)
> **Industry Chain**: `CHAIN-CORN` (Stage 1: `extraction`)

## 1. Architectural Overview & Operational Loop
Organized around symbiotic polyculture mounds, this agrarian plantation interplants maize stalks, climbing beans, and sprawling squash together. Maize provides natural trellis support for nitrogen-fixing beans, while prickly squash leaves shade the soil to retain moisture and deter pests, yielding abundant raw maize cobs at harvest.

Soil is hilled up around each maize and bean plant every year, with squash set between the mounds. The ground is cleared first, then related women plant, weed and harvest in work parties and decide how the crop is shared. Maize goes in at spring; dry ears and beans go into community grain pits. Surplus is traded, and a buyer's demand sometimes pushes farmers toward growing maize alone.
- **Hilled Mounds:** warm, well-drained planting hills.
- **Grain Pit Cellar:** dry community storage for ears and beans.
- **Seed Row:** the saved ears of the old varieties.

## 2. Granular Disposition Baseline (`[S, E, C]`)

- **Sociological Rationale**: Sustained by reciprocal land stewardship, seasonal planting ceremonies, and collective harvest sharing.

### Material Stocks & Items (Item Prototypes)
- **Husked Maize Ears** (`Commodity`): Unprocessed corn ears with husks intact, ready for nixtamalization or granary drying.
- **Fire-Hardened Digging Stick** (`Tool`): Hardened wooden pole used for planting seed trios in mounded earth.
- **Seed Ear Braid** (`Relic`): The saved ears of the oldest maize varieties, hung dry from the pit-cellar rafters.

## 4. Operational Dials & Failure Modes (Quests & Crises)
- **Dial - `Companion Balance` (`0-10`)**: At `0-3`, aggressive squash chokes out young maize stalks. At `8-10`, nitrogen depletion stunts overall crop vigor, and a field planted for the buyer's demand tires the soil.
- **Quest Hook - *The Weevil Infestation***: A sudden outbreak of corn borers threatens the primary seed stock before Elder Tall-Pine can apply herbal deterrents. Pit-Keeper Marra Ashfall says the seed braid must be moved to a dry pit by the next new moon, and Grain Buyer Cedric Rushworth offers good coin for the ears if the elders will simply sell instead of save.

## 5. GM Socket Placement & Supply Synergy
* **Input Dependency:** None. This is a stage 1 extraction node and requires nothing from other modules.
* **Output Transmission:** Sends `Crop: Raw-Maize` to the **Nixtamalization Vats & Stone Metate Mill (`IND-CORN-02`)**.
* **Tactical Chokepoint:** The grain pit. Whoever holds the dry cellar and the seed braid holds next year's harvest.

## 6. Vague Game Hazards
* **Borer-Eaten Stalks:** Fields riddled with borers or weevils spoil the ears ($1\text{d}6$ ears of the daily haul lost per infected plot).
* **Silking Drought:** A dry spell when the silks appear halves the yield for the season.
* **Moldy Pit:** A damp grain pit breeds rot that sickens anyone who eats the ears ($1\text{d}4$ damage and a day of fever).
