### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Archaeological Grounding
Constructed upon the inter-tidal fringing reef flats off Temwen Island on the eastern perimeter of Pohnpei (Federated States of Micronesia), **Nan Madol** ("The Spaces Between") stands as the pre-industrial Pacific's most staggering monument of maritime megalithic engineering. Erected and expanded between roughly 1100 and 1628 CE under the autocratic reign of the Saudeleur Dynasty, the complex encompasses approximately 80 hectares of artificial land, functioning simultaneously as a fortified administrative citadel, royal necropolis, and ritual theater of theocratic terror.

Modern archaeological expeditions and radiometric surveys (notably by William Ayres, Rufino Mauricio, and J. Stephen Athens) demonstrate that the metropolis was not carved into terrestrial bedrock or built upon natural dry ground. Instead, the entire complex was raised directly upon living coral reef flats and submerged sand shoals through an engineered synergy of volcanic stone and marine limestone:

```
         [PONPEI MAINLAND: Volcanic Peaks, Mountain Streams & Sokehs Basalt Columns]
                                              │
                    (Open Lagoon Transit via Outrigger Raft Flotilla)
                                              │
                                              ▼
    ══════════════════════════════════════════╪══════════════════════════════════ (Reef Flat)
```

1. **The 92 Geometric Artificial Islets & Inter-Tidal Canals**: The city is organized into **92 distinct, artificial geometric islets**, segregated into two principal administrative districts: *Madol Pah* (the Lower City, housing the administrative compounds, priestly dormitories, and royal residences) and *Madol Powe* (the Upper City, dedicated to the mortuary sector, royal crypts, and sacred oracle sanctuaries). The islets are bounded by cyclopean retaining seawalls up to 8 meters (25 feet) high and separated by a labyrinth of seawater canals engineered to flush sediment on incoming and outgoing tides.
2. **The 750,000-Metric-Ton Columnar Basalt Logistic Chain**: The megalithic building material consists of natural **prismatic columnar basalt**—dense volcanic rock that fractured during prehistoric cooling into five-, six-, and eight-sided polygonal "stone logs." Ranging from 2 to 6 meters in length and weighing between 5 and 50 metric tons each, these columns were not quarried at Nan Madol. They were quarried from massive volcanic necks and plugs on the distant northern and western coasts of Pohnpei (such as Sokehs Rock), loaded onto heavy double-hulled rafts or towed across open coastal lagoons using buoyant timber outriggers, and transported across miles of treacherous reef breakers. Archaeologists estimate that over **750,000 metric tons** of basalt prisms were mobilized across centuries of corvée exploitation.
3. **The "Log-Cabin" Header-and-Stretcher Masonry**: Lacking lime mortar or metallic fasteners, builders devised a seismic- and wave-resistant masonry system mimicking timber log cabins. Monolithic basalt columns were laid in alternating courses of **headers** (perpendicular to the wall face) and **stretchers** (parallel to the wall face), creating rigid perimeter cribs. The hollow internal chambers of the islet foundations were subsequently backfilled with thousands of tons of coral gravel, porous basalt cobbles, and discarded mollusks. This porous aggregate ballast absorbed the hydraulic pounding of Pacific typhoon surges without blowing out the perimeter walls, permitting high-energy tides to drain harmlessly through the foundations.
4. **The Nandauwas Royal Mortuary Citadel & Sacred Moray Eel Crypt**: The architectural and ideological apex of the complex is **Nandauwas**, the royal mortuary enclosure in Madol Powe. Enclosed by double concentric perimeter walls of stacked basalt logs rising 8 meters above the lagoon, Nandauwas houses central vault chambers containing the stone cists of deceased Saudeleur dynasts. Beneath the primary ceremonial precinct lies a submerged saltwater chamber connected by subterranean reef tunnels to the open sea. Here, the theocracy maintained *Nan Samwohl*, a colossal sacred saltwater moray eel (*Gymnothorax javanicus*). High priests conducted nocturnal ceremonies where cooked turtle entrails and fatty liver portions were fed to the sacred predator, interpreting its thrashing strikes as divine omens from the sea deity *Nahn Samwohl*.
5. **The Fatal Metabolic Parasitism (Absence of Water and Soil)**: Despite its monumental grandeur, Nan Madol possessed an absolute metabolic vulnerability: **the artificial stone islets possess zero natural freshwater aquifers, zero freshwater springs, and zero arable agricultural soil**. The Saudeleur despots deliberately sited their citadel on the reef flats to isolate the royal court from peasant revolts, surrounded by a natural moat of deep saltwater canals. Consequently, the daily survival of the elite, their priesthood, and their garrison was 100% parasitic upon mainland Pohnpei. Every single gourd of drinking water, every basket of fermented breadfruit, and every root of giant swamp taro had to be paddled across the lagoon daily by mainland corvée canoe crews under penalty of torture and public execution.
6. **The Isokelekel Incursion & The Mangrove Necropolis (circa 1628 CE)**: Oral traditions and archaeological deposits converge on the systemic decapitation of the dynasty around 1628 CE. A semi-mythic war leader named **Isokelekel**, commanding 333 Kosraean sea-warriors in war canoes, entered the lagoon. Exploiting widespread mainland peasant mutinies, Isokelekel blockaded the freshwater canoe conduits, starving the Saudeleur defenders in their un-watered stone forts. In the resulting assault on Nandauwas, the last despot, *Saudemwohl*, was routed, fleeing into the stream of *Sapwalap* where he met his demise (legend holding he transformed into a small blue river fish). Isokelekel replaced the despotic theocracy with a decentralized, district-based chiefdom system (*Nahnmwarki*). Nan Madol, impossible to sustain without forced mainland tribute, was declared *tabu* (cursed/sacred) and permanently abandoned to the choking roots of red mangroves (*Rhizophora mangle*).

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "Our basalt log walls rest upon the living coral reef, secured by the divine oracle of the sacred moray eel; as long as mainland paddlers haul fresh water and taro through the saltwater canals, our royal dynasty will rule over Pohnpei for eternity."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** Starving mainland commoners rise in revolt and blockade the freshwater canoe conduits; trapped upon artificial stone islets with zero fresh water or soil, the Saudeleur priests watch in horror as Isokelekel's war-canoes breach the outer seawall.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Total Parasitic Reliance on Mainland Tribute} + \text{Inter-Tidal Megalithic Isolation} = \text{Freshwater Starvation \& Isokelekel Dynasty Overthrow}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** A monumental stone citadel built upon a barren reef and sustained entirely by forced tribute cannot survive when the mainland peasantry revolts and cuts off the water supply. When the freshwater canoes stop, the basalt palaces become un-watered tombs, reclaimed forever by the tidal mangrove swamp.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Saudeleur Supreme Despot):**  
  The absolute monarch of Nan Madol, dwelling within the innermost basalt log court of Nandauwas. Dressed in turmeric-dyed barkcloth and sacred shell beads, he views the fringing reef as the floor of heaven. When mainland tribute shipments fail and rebels blockade the canals, he orders mass sacrifices to the sacred moray eel, utterly blind to the structural collapse of his parasitic state.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Kosraean Warlord Isokelekel):**  
  A legendary seafarer and tactical genius who led 333 elite warriors across open ocean channels to decapitate the Saudeleur dynasty. Recognizing that Nan Madol's stone walls are impregnable to direct assault, he deploys a ruthless starvation strategy, cutting off all freshwater supply canoes and storming the breached islet walls at high tide.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (The Mainland Canoe Tribute Captain):**  
  A cynical high-ranking corvée captain who has spent decades hauling fresh water and heavy basalt logs across the lagoon. Despising the Saudeleur elite, he secretly guides Isokelekel's war-canoes through hidden reef channels and leaks palace defense schedules to the rebels, ensuring the dynasty's downfall.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (The Taboo Reef Mangrove Hermit):**  
  A solitary survivor who refused to leave Temwen Island after the Isokelekel conquest. Living amidst the tangled red mangrove roots that strangle the basalt walls, she harvests reef fish from the silent canals, acting as a ghostly guardian of the ruined ancestral necropolis.

### 4. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 4.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Tidal Starvation & Mangrove Encroachment
The hydrological survival, masonry stability, and political control of Nan Madol are tracked along a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Tidal Canal Shoaling & Mangrove Root Snag / Light Severity Tier - DC 10):**  
  * *Condition:* Low tide grounds outrigger canoes on coral shoals; red mangrove roots begin encroaching upon outer retaining seawalls; mainland tribute deliveries experience minor canoe delays.
  * *Mechanics:* Navigating shallow tidal canals during low water requires a **DC 10 Athletics or Land/Water Vehicles check**; failure grounds outrigger canoes, causing a 2-hour delay.
* **Level 2 (Basalt Log Stacking Collapse & Coral Cut / Moderate Severity Tier - DC 13, 2d8 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* Typhoon wave action pounds outer seawalls; multi-ton columnar basalt logs slip from their header-and-stretcher bedding; razor-sharp living coral lacerates waders.
  * *Mechanics:* Traversing submerged seawalls or swimming through tidal channels requires a **DC 13 Athletics or Reflex check**; failure inflicts **2d8 crushing and laceration damage** from shifting 10-ton basalt prisms and sharp reef coral.
* **Level 3 (Saudeleur Tyrannical Tribute Seizure & Freshwater Starvation / Severe Severity Tier - DC 16, 4d10 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* Mainland peasants rebel and halt freshwater canoe shipments; elite priests ration remaining well cisterns; desperate guards execute suspected mutineers in the central courtyards.
  * *Mechanics:* Enduring severe dehydration and political terror inside the un-watered stone citadel requires a **DC 16 Fortitude or Will check** each day; failure inflicts **4d10 thirst and psychological oppression damage** and imposes the *Exhausted* condition.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic Isokelekel Invasion & Mangrove Overgrowth / Lethal Climax Tier - DC 20, 8d12 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* Isokelekel’s 333 warriors breach the outer seawall at high tide; the Saudeleur dynasty is slaughtered; the megalithic complex is abandoned and permanently swallowed by choking red mangroves.
  * *Mechanics:* Complete military and civilizational collapse. Surviving the nocturnal storm assault on Nandauwas requires a **DC 20 Survival or Combat check**; failure inflicts **8d12 lethal combat and drowning damage** as seawalls collapse into the surging lagoon. The city is declared *tabu* and abandoned forever.

#### 4.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ]          Saudeleur Tribute Oppression Revolt Clock (4 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ]    Mangrove Canal Re-Wilding Meter (6 Ticks)
```

* **Saudeleur Tribute Oppression Revolt Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) when mainland commoners forced to haul fresh water and cooked taro suffer starvation, or when priestly tax-collectors execute canoe paddlers.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Mainland villages organize secret night meetings to discuss halting freshwater tribute deliveries.
    * *Tick 2:* A convoy of freshwater canoes is ambushed off Temwen Island; three royal tax collectors are killed.
    * *Tick 3:* Isokelekel’s war fleet arrives in the outer lagoon, welcomed by cheering mainland rebels who point out palace vulnerabilities.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Triggers **Dial Level 4: Catastrophic Isokelekel Invasion** immediately. The mainland uprising successfully breaches the island citadel.
  * *Player Mitigation:* Negotiating tribute relief, supplying medicine to exhausted paddlers, or exposing corrupt tax collectors (**DC 16 Diplomacy or Insight check**) clears 1 tick.
* **Mangrove Canal Re-Wilding Meter [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each operational season tidal canals silt up with coral sand, dredging crews are disbanded, or mangrove seedlings take root in basalt foundation joints.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* Red mangrove pneumatophores crack the outer limestone bedding of the retaining walls.
    * *Tick 4:* Canals narrow by 50%, forcing double-hulled canoes to single-file passage.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* The entire city is choked by dense red mangrove forests, transforming the stone canals into impassable swamps and erasing open-water visibility.
  * *Player Mitigation:* Organizing manual mangrove root clearing, dredging coral silt, or repairing basalt seawall foundations (**DC 16 Athletics or Crafting check**) clears 1 tick.

#### 4.3 Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Transit: Double-Hulled-Log-Raft-Flotilla`: Heavy timber outrigger rafts and double-hulled canoes capable of transporting 20-ton basalt columns across treacherous reef surf.
  * `Life-Support: Mainland-Freshwater-and-Taro-Tribute`: Daily paddle-convoys hauling gourds of drinking water, fermented breadfruit, and giant swamp taro from mainland Pohnpei.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Topography: Inter-Tidal-Canal-Artificial-Islet-Network`: 92 geometric artificial stone islets connected by flushed seawater canals across a coral reef lagoon.
  * `Structure: Columnar-Basalt-Log-Stacked-Fortress`: Cyclopean log-cabin header-and-stretcher basalt prism masonry providing supreme seismic and wave resistance.
  * `Ritual: Sacred-Reef-Eel-and-Turtle-Pool`: Submerged saltwater oracle vaults housing sacred marine predators and conducting royal mortuary liturgies.
* **Yields Resource (`yields_resource`):**
  * `Inter-Tidal Canal Architecture`: Advanced marine engineering blueprints for constructing artificial islands and self-flushing tidal canals.
  * `Columnar Basalt Log-Stacked Masonry`: Seismic-resistant dry-stone construction techniques utilizing natural prismatic volcanic columns without mortar.
  * `Sacred Saltwater Moray Reliquary`: Intricate ritual complexes and subterranean reef chambers designed to house divine animal oracles.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When the Saudeleur dynasty is overthrown and mainland tribute of fresh water and breadfruit halts, the module enters **Mangrove Necropolis Abandonment**:
    * The megalithic islets are uninhabitable due to total lack of fresh water or soil.
    * The tidal canal grid silts with coral sand; red mangroves engulf the 50-foot basalt log walls.
    * The city becomes a taboo ancestral spirit necropolis (*Nan Madol*), visited only by ghosts and reef birds.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Spatial Encounters

* **Encounter 1: The Freshwater Canoe Ambush (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* As the party’s outrigger canoe navigates the narrow, high-tide seawater canal leading into Madol Pah, they find the waterway blocked by three royal corvée canoes escorted by palace guards. The guards are viciously beating a mainland paddler who collapsed from dehydration and spilled a gourd of fresh spring water into the saltwater canal. The starving paddlers’ relatives lash out with paddles and digging sticks, initiating a violent canal brawl that traps the party's vessel in a crossfire of stone-tipped spears and heavy basalt blocks dropped from the terrace walls above.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party enters seeking safe harbor or diplomatic audience, but is trapped in a narrow saltwater kill-box under heavy stone fire from above.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The canoe captain screams that the water is sweet mountain rain for the king, while his crew frantically drinks from broken gourds knowing they will be executed before sundown anyway.
* **Encounter 2: The Sacred Moray’s Crypt Pool (Resource Dilemma & Moral Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* In the sunken seawater vault of Nandauwas, the party corners a dying rebel scout who has jumped into the sacred feeding pool with a ceramic vial of deadly mangrove root poison, preparing to poison the colossal sacred moray eel to break the religious morale of the royal defenders.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* If the party stops the poisoning, they preserve the unique sacred ecological oracle of the Pacific and gain the trust of the high priests, but allow the tyrannical dynasty to continue torturing mainland captives; if they allow the poisoning, the sacred eel thrashes in agony, snapping its teeth and collapsing the stone ceiling into the pool.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The blind eel-tender chants soothing sea prayers into the dark water, stroking the 12-foot moray’s head while pretending not to notice the blood already clouding the pool.
* **Encounter 3: The Breach of the Outer Seawall at High Tide (Severance Crisis & Tactical Climax)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* As high tide peaks and an incoming Pacific gale roars over the reef, Isokelekel’s 333 warriors ram their war-canoes directly into the outer seawall, dislodging a 5-ton hexagonal basalt column that creates a breach; water surges into the royal residential compound, threatening to wash thirty captive mainland children into the shark-infested lagoon.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party must choose between holding the breach against the invading shock-troops to save the drowning children from the rip-current, or fleeing through a secret submerged canal to recover the royal treasury of ancient greenstone adzes before the entire islet collapses into the surf.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The Kosraean warlord Isokelekel stands at the prow of his double-hulled canoe, pointing his shark-tooth spear at the palace and shouting that the stone sea-monsters of the Saudeleurs will drown in their own salt.
