### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Horological Engineering Grounding
Recovered from the tempest-battered shelf off the jagged Aegean islet of Antikythera, the **Antikythera Clockwork Astronomical Reliquary** represents the absolute pinnacle of ancient mechanical computation and astronomical science. Preserved within a cedar-framed bronze case measuring roughly 340 × 180 × 90 millimeters, the device is the earliest known mechanical analog computer. Dating to the 1st century BCE (circa 70–60 BCE, corresponding to a Roman grain-and-plunder merchant galley laden with looted Hellenistic bronze statues, Syrian glassware, and marble colossi bound from Rhodes or Pergamon toward Rome), it operationalizes complex mathematics into a physical, hand-cranked gearwork cosmos.

Rather than a static decorative dial, the mechanism contains **at least 37 precision bronze gears** cut from low-oxidation alpha-tin bronze sheets (average thickness 1.5–2.0 mm), featuring hand-divided triangular teeth with 60-degree angles and pitch radii as small as 1.5 millimeters.

```
       [MANUAL CROWN INPUT CRANK]
                   │
                   ▼ (1 Turn = 1 Solar Year)
       ┌────────────────────────┐
       │   MAIN DRIVE WHEEL b1  │ (64 Triangular Teeth)
       └───────────┬────────────┘
```

The engineering, mathematical, and mechanical reality of the reliquary rests upon four pillars:

1. **The Differential Gearbox and Pin-and-Slot Mechanism**: The crown achievement of the device—predating modern automotive differentials by nearly two millennia—is its incorporation of a **differential gear assembly**. Comprising gears mounted on a rotatable turntable cage, the differential allowed the device to subtract the position of the sun from the position of the moon, instantly calculating the lunar phase angle ($Moon - Sun$). Furthermore, to model the moon's variable orbital speed in accordance with Keplerian-like elliptic anomalies (Hipparchos's second anomaly), the mechanism utilized a brilliant **pin-and-slot (or crank-and-slider) escapement**: an eccentric drive pin rotating on one gear engaged a slotted follower arm on an adjacent coaxial gear, causing the simulated moon to smoothly accelerate and decelerate twice per lunar month.
2. **The Saros and Metonic Calendar Dials**: The back face of the bronze case features two massive, spiral display dials housed in protective hinged doors:
   * **The Metonic Dial**: A 5-column spiral dial spanning 235 synodic months (19 solar years), tracking the alignment of solar and lunar cycles, complemented by a subsidiary dial tracking the four-year Olympiad cycles and Panhellenic games (*Isthmian, Nemean, Pythian, Olympic*).
   * **The Saros Dial**: A 4-column spiral dial spanning 223 synodic months (18 years, 11 days), specifically designed to predict **solar and lunar eclipses**. The dial face features exquisite Greek glyph inscriptions marking exact eclipse hours, solar vs. lunar designations (*Hêlios, Selênê*), and color-coded hazard indicators.
3. **The Front Zodiac and Planetary Ephemeris Display**: The front face features concentric circular rings: a fixed 365-day Egyptian calendar ring (with an intercalary push-pull pin adjustment for leap years every four years) and a rotatable zodiac ring bearing Greek constellations. Beneath these faces rested planetary gear trains calculating the synodic loops of Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn using epicyclic gear wheels.
4. **The Plunder Galley Shipwreck Context**: The archaeological context of the shipwreck (*the Antikythera Wreck*) provides its dramatic framing. Sinking into a 45-meter deep-water trench off the rugged promontory of Glyphada on Antikythera, the merchant vessel struck jagged underwater limestone reefs during a nocturnal winter nor'easter. As the hull fractured and water flooded the bilges, the heavy bronze reliquary—housed in its cedar cabinetry along with bronze statues of philosophers and gods—tumbled into the benthic silt, where centuries of marine calcification fused the bronze gears into a solid, mineralized mass encased in green copper carbonate (*malachite*) and calcium crusts.

* **The Main Drive Crown & Axle Arbors**: Hand-forged square-ended bronze arbors fitted with removable winding keys.
* **The Differential Turntable Cage**: Three interlinked bevel and spur gears that compute lunar phase vectors dynamically.
* **The Saros Eclipse Spiral Face**: Engraved bronze plate tracking 223 lunar months of potential solar occultations.
* **The Benthic Abyssal Trench (45m depth)**: A cold, oxygen-poor underwater shelf where the reliquary rested undisturbed for over 2,000 years before being hauled up by sponge divers in 1900.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "The cosmos is an unsearchable mystery governed by capricious Olympian gods whose divine anger can only be placated by animal sacrifice and blind superstition."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** As a Roman plunder galley smashes against a midnight reef during a winter gale, the party discovers the Antikythera mechanism clicking within its cedar box; as they turn the hand-crank, the bronze gears align precisely to predict a total solar eclipse that begins at that exact second, plunging the stormy sea into terrifying daytime darkness.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Hellenistic Precision Differential Mechanics} + \text{Abyssal Shipwreck Plunder Environment} = \text{Deterministic Cosmic Predictability \& Horological Singularity}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** The universe is not a chaotic playground of arbitrary deities, but a sublime, rational clockwork governed by immutable mathematical ratios. When humanity captures this cosmic order in brass and bronze, it holds the blueprint of time itself—even as the physical world fractures into the sea.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Hellenistic Master Horologist):**  
  An elderly scholar and master craftsman from Rhodes who accompanied the cargo to explain its calibration to Roman patrons. Wearing a grease-stained linen tunic and wielding fine steel calipers, he views the reliquary as the crowning glory of human reason. He risks his life during the shipwreck to shield the bronze gear case from crushing amphorae and brutal Roman swordsmen.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Roman Proconsular Legate):**  
  An ambitious military commander overseeing the maritime transport of looted treasures to Rome. Decked in polished brass lorica musculata and a crimson cloak, he regards the Greek reliquary as a curious mechanical toy—useful perhaps as a dining-room novelty or proof of subjugated technical talent, but fundamentally subordinate to Roman military might and imperial plunder quotas.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (The Cypriot Benthic Salvage Diver):**  
  A cynical, hardened salvage operator working the dangerous 45-meter deep-water trench off Antikythera. Having located the sunken galley years after the storm, he hacks away at the encrusted bronze plates with a cold-chisel, caring nothing for its astronomical genius. He melts down delicate gear wheels for scrap metal or sells encrusted gear fragments to gullible alchemists as magical astrological talismans.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (The Cursed Abyssal Sponge Diver):**  
  A local Cretan sponge diver who ventured too close to the wreck site and claims he can hear the muffled, rhythmic clicking of bronze gear teeth echoing through the underwater clefts during dead calms. Driven half-mad by oxygen narcosis and superstitious terror, he preaches that the machine is an unholy trap forged by ancient daemons to bind the sun and moon in brass chains.

### 4. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 4.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Horological Precision & Abyssal Submersion
The mechanical integrity, operational calibration, and salvage state of the Antikythera reliquary are tracked along a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Gear Tooth Click & Bronze Canker Stiffening / Light Severity Tier - DC 10):**  
  * *Condition:* The manual crown turns smoothly, engaging all 37 bronze gears with a crisp, rhythmic clicking sound; the solar and lunar ephemeris dials track planetary positions accurately; light green copper oxidation (*patina*) dusts the gear teeth.
  * *Mechanics:* Operating the mechanism to calculate astronomical conjunctions or navigation ephemerides requires a **DC 10 Intelligence (Clockwork) check**; success grants advantage on celestial navigation checks for 24 hours. Without periodic oiling with fine olive oil, mechanisms stiffen, introducing minor tracking delays.
* **Level 2 (Pin-and-Slot Jam & Reef Gale Breakers / Moderate Severity Tier - DC 13, 2d8 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* Violent Aegean wave impact pitches the galley; the eccentric pin in the lunar escapement slips out of its slot; the differential gear train binds tightly; seawater spray enters the cedar case, forming corrosive white copper salts.
  * *Mechanics:* Forcing a jammed gear arbor requires a **DC 13 Sleight of Hand or Engineering check**; failure strips 1d4 delicate triangular gear teeth, permanently disabling planetary ephemeris tracking until repaired. Pounding waves crashing over the deck require a **DC 13 Vehicles or Athletics check** to maintain footing, or characters suffer **2d8 bludgeoning damage** against shifting cargo.
* **Level 3 (Hull Fracture & Differential Escapement Slip / Severe Severity Tier - DC 16, 4d10 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* The plunder galley fractures upon submerged limestone reef spikes; massive 100-pound terracotta wine amphorae break their lashings and roll across the cabin floor; the Saros eclipse dial spins wildly out of calibration as the internal arbors snap.
  * *Mechanics:* Dodging rolling amphorae and collapsing cabin bulkheads requires a **DC 16 Reflex or Athletics check**; failure inflicts **4d10 crushing and bludgeoning damage**. The reliquary takes severe impact trauma: internal arbors bend, requiring an intensive workshop restoration project to prevent permanent functional loss.
* **Level 4 (Catastrophic 45-Meter Plunge & Cosmic Eclipse Lock / Lethal Climax Tier - DC 20, 8d12 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* The ruined galley breaks in two and plunges vertically into the 45-meter seabed trench; simultaneously, the Saros clock reaches its index, triggering a total solar eclipse that plunges the storm-tossed sea into sudden, unnatural daytime darkness.
  * *Mechanics:* Complete maritime and horological disaster. The plunder galley sinks to the benthic floor. Surviving the sudden sinking and pitch-black eclipse waters requires a **DC 20 Survival or Athletics (Swimming) check**; failure inflicts **8d12 crushing and drowning damage** as suction drags characters down with the wreckage. The reliquary is entombed in underwater silt, initiating a 1,500-year dark age of lost mechanical engineering.

#### 4.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Saros Total Eclipse Countdown Clock (6 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ]       Galley Hull Plunge to 45m Abyss Clock (4 Ticks)
```

* **Saros Total Eclipse Countdown [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each round the manual crown is cranked forward toward the eclipse indices, whenever lightning flashes across the stormy horizon, or when magical divination is cast in the presence of the mechanism.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* The bronze lunar dial locks into position; the golden sun pointer begins to tremble.
    * *Tick 4:* Ambient daylight dims noticeably as the moon's simulated orbital node crosses the solar plane; sailors on deck drop to their knees in superstitious terror.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* A total solar eclipse locks into absolute reality. Daytime plunges into freezing, starlit darkness; magical divination spells are empowered by 200%, but all non-magical morale checks suffer disadvantage.
  * *Player Mitigation:* Unwinding the crown in reverse, oiling the differential escapement, or casting dispel magic (**DC 16 Arcana or Clockwork check**) clears 1 tick.
* **Galley Hull Plunge to 45m Abyss Clock [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each time jagged reef teeth tear another hull strake, bilge pumps fail, or rolling cargo weights breach the lower deck bulkheads.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Bilge water rises above the floorboards; rowers panic as lower benches flood.
    * *Tick 2:* Mainmast snaps under gale-force winds, crushing the starboard gunwale.
    * *Tick 3:* The bow detaches completely from the stern; the cargo hold lists at a 60-degree angle toward the open trench drop-off.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Triggers **Dial Level 4: Catastrophic 45-Meter Plunge** immediately. The vessel slides down the continental shelf into the benthic trench; the reliquary is lost beneath tons of mud and water.
  * *Player Mitigation:* Jettisoning heavy marble statues, shoring up bulkheads with timber beams, or operating emergency bilge pumps (**DC 16 Athletics or Survival check**) clears 1 tick.

#### 4.3 Logistical & Resource Flow Contract

* **Input Prerequisites (`requires`):**
  * `Metal: Low-Oxidation-Fine-Bronze-Gears`: High-purity alpha-tin bronze sheets cast without air bubbles, precision-hammered to uniform thickness, and hand-filed with triangular teeth.
  * `Mathematics: Hipparchian-Lunar-Calculus`: Advanced Hellenistic geometric models of epicycles, eccentric deferents, and lunar inequality calculations pioneered by Hipparchos of Rhodes.
* **Output Yields (`provides`):**
  * `Relic: Differential-Bronze-Clockwork-Computer`: The ultimate ancient mechanical analog computer capable of tracking lunar phases, eclipses, and planetary cycles with astonishing mathematical precision.
  * `Science: Saros-Eclipse-Predictor`: Unrivaled predictive capability for solar and lunar eclipses across 18-year cycles, granting profound strategic foresight over superstitious populations.
  * `Navigation: Precision-Zodiacal-Ephemeris`: Sophisticated stellar positioning tools granting unshakeable navigation accuracy across open ocean voyages.
* **Yields Resource (`yields_resource`):**
  * `37-Gear Differential Analog Computer`: Intricate mechanical marvel capable of unlocking ancient technological secrets or serving as an unequaled campaign artifact.
  * `Saros Eclipse Cycle Predictions`: Exact temporal charts of celestial occultations, usable to command religious awe or time tactical strikes.
  * `Olympic Games & Planetary Ephemeris Calibrations`: Calibrated horological plates marking Panhellenic athletic festivals and planetary retrograde loops.
* **Standalone Fallback Mode:**
  * When the plunder galley sinks into the 45-meter abyssal trench, the module enters **Benthic Submersion & Horological Dark Age**:
    * The differential mechanism corrodes beneath thick crusts of calcium carbonate and copper oxidation, fusing the 37 gears into a single unreadable mineralized lump.
    * Humanity loses the capacity for precision clockwork, gear-cutting, and mechanical analog computing for over 1,500 years, delaying the Industrial Revolution by millennia.
    * The shipwreck site becomes a guarded maritime anomaly where deep-sea sponge divers occasionally recover encrusted metallic fragments that whisper of lost technological gods.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Spatial Encounters

* **Encounter 1: The Turning of the Bronze Crank (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* In the violently pitching main cabin of the Roman plunder galley, the party discovers the cedar box. As a character touches the square bronze winding crown, Geminus of Rhodes lunges forward with a bronze cold-chisel, screaming that uninitiated hands will strip the gear arbors. Simultaneously, Proconsular Legate Lucius Licinius kicks the door open with drawn gladius, followed by four armored legionaries, ordering the execution of anyone who touches the prize.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party discovers what appears to be an ornamental chest of loot, only to trigger an instant three-way lethal standoff inside a pitching, water-logged cabin while the gear train begins clicking on its own.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* Licinius shouts about the majesty of the Roman Senate and the monetary value of Greek art, but his eyes reveal superstitious dread of the clicking gears, which seem to turn with occult life. Geminus screams insults at the soldiers, but his trembling hands cradle the delicate differential cage to protect it from stray blade strikes.
  * *Tactical Mechanics:* De-escalating the standoff requires a **DC 13 Persuasion or Intimidation check**. Intercepting Geminus's chisel without damaging the casing requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check**. Every round of melee combat inside the pitching cabin risks bumping the crank, advancing the *Saros Eclipse Clock* by +1 tick.
* **Encounter 2: The Reef Breakers of Antikythera (Hull Crisis / Environmental Dilemma)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* With a bone-shattering shudder, the galley impales its forward timber hull on a jagged underwater reef off the Antikythera cliffs. Freezing seawater geysers through the hold floorboards; dozens of heavy 100-pound terracotta wine amphorae snap their hemp lashing and roll across the slanting deck like battering rams.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Option A (Save the 37-Gear Clockwork Reliquary):* The party can dedicate their actions to wrapping the mechanism in waterproof oiled fleece, clearing the pinned gear train, and hauling the 30-pound bronze box up the slick companionway ladder (requiring a **DC 16 Athletics or Mechanics check** while dodging rolling amphorae, **DC 16 Reflex** or take **4d10 crushing damage**). This secures the greatest technological artifact of the ancient world; however, while the party secures the relic, forty enslaved rowers chained to their benches in the flooding lower hold drown in the rising black bilge water.
    * *Option B (Smash the Benches & Free the Chained Rowers):* The party can use their weapons and prybars to smash the iron chains and save the forty drowning rowers (requiring **DC 13 Athletics or Weapon Attack checks** against the chains). All forty human lives are spared; however, during the chaotic rescue, an unsecured marble statue of Hercules topples across the cedar box, crushing the bronze gear train into mangled scrap and stripping thirty teeth from the differential turntable.
* **Encounter 3: The 45-Meter Abyssal Plunge (Climax Salvage Dilemma / Eclipse Meltdown)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* The galley’s stern breaks free and hangs over the sheer edge of the continental shelf, beginning its final slide into the 45-meter seabed trench. Concurrently, the Saros clock expires: the total solar eclipse locks into place, extinguishing the sun and plunging the tempestuous reef into pitch-black midday midnight. In the screaming darkness, Captain Symeon’s sponge divers surface around the sinking stern with grappling hooks and harpoons, attempting to haul the chest away to their own craft while cutting the party’s mooring lines.
  * *Tactical Environment:* Pitch-black storm darkness; water churning over a sheer 45-meter drop; characters without light sources suffer blinded status. Free-diving sponge divers attack from the water using barbed trident poles and line-cutting knives.
  * *Resolution:* The party must secure a heavy hemp recovery line to the reliquary’s bronze iron-banded carrying ring, fight off the looters (**DC 13 Athletics/Combat**), and make a **DC 20 Athletics (Swimming) or Survival check** to swim clear of the sinking hull's suction before the 300-ton galley plunges into the 45-meter trench, burying the wreckage in benthic silt forever.
