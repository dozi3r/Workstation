### 1. Mechanical Architecture & Archaeological Grounding
Anchored in the mature urban phase of the Indus Valley Civilization (circa 2600–1900 BCE) along the great alluvial basins of the Indus and the now-extinct Ghaggar-Hakra river system (identified in archaeological hydrology and historical geography as the sacred Saraswati), this module operationalizes humanity's earliest and most sophisticated municipal sanitation and metric standardization engine. Spanning over one million square kilometers across major civic hubs—Mohenjo-Daro, Harappa, Dholavira, Rakhigarhi, and the tidal port of Lothal—this civilization rejected monumental palaces, dynastic tombs, and standing militarism in favor of an obsessive civic focus on public hygiene, hydraulic planning, and metric commerce.

```
                                   │
               (Perennial Inflow)   │   (Tectonic Avulsion: Diverted to Indus & Yamuna)
                                   ▼
                    [Ghaggar-Hakra / Saraswati River Channel]
                                   │
    ┌───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┐
    │                                                               │
```

The engineering, infrastructural, and hydrogeological integrity of Harappan civilization rests upon four foundational pillars:

1. **The Grid-Iron City Plan and Kiln-Baked Brick Masonry**: Harappan cities were laid out with mathematical precision along a rigid cardinal grid oriented to prevailing winds. Streets intersected at exact 90-degree angles, dividing urban blocks into standardized residential modules. Unlike contemporary Mesopotamian or Egyptian mudbrick cities that melted during monsoons, Harappans utilized uniform, kiln-baked mudbricks manufactured to strict dimensional ratios (4:2:1 length-width-thickness), bonded with mud mortar and bitumen waterproofing.
2. **The Advanced Covered Sanitary Drainage Network**: Every house was connected to an integrated, municipal sanitation system that remains unmatched in the ancient world. Bathing platforms and latrines on upper and ground floors drained into vertical terracotta wall-chutes that dropped wastewater directly into covered street-level brick drains. These drains ran down every major avenue, covered with snug fitting, removable stone slabs to allow inspection and cleaning. Sump pits (*cesspools*) were positioned at street corners to trap solid waste before greywater discharged into agricultural fields or river channels, preventing clogging and foul odors.
3. **The Great Bath and Waterproof Bitumen Sealing**: At the heart of Mohenjo-Daro lies the Great Bath—a massive public hydraulic basin measuring 39 × 23 feet with a depth of 8 feet. Descended via brick staircases with damp-proofing ledges, the basin was sealed against seepage using a 1-inch layer of natural bitumen (asphalt mined from local petroleum seeps) sandwiched between two layers of tightly fitted kiln-baked bricks. Water was supplied by a dedicated brick-lined well within an adjacent chamber, and discharged via a massive vaulted corbeled brick drain large enough for a person to walk through upright.
4. **The Saraswati River Desiccation and De-Urbanization**: The existential fragility of the Indus civilization lay in its total dependence on the perennial glacial flow of the Ghaggar-Hakra (Saraswati) river system. Fed by Himalayan glaciers and monsoonal runoff, the Saraswati supported over a thousand thriving Harappan settlements. However, between 2000 and 1900 BCE, regional tectonic uplifts and climate shifts triggered a devastating **river avulsion**: upstream tectonic tilting and shifting monsoon tracks diverted the Sutlej river system westward into the Indus, and the Yamuna eastward into the Ganges. Deprived of its glacial headwaters, the Saraswati dried into a string of seasonal, saline wadis. Without perennial water, deep wells went dry, agricultural irrigation failed, and the great cities were systematically abandoned as populations de-urbanized into small rural farming hamlets along the Himalayan foothills.

### 2. Thematic Premise & Syllogism

* **The Stasis / The Lie:** "As long as our street drains are covered with precisely cut stone slabs and our cubical chert weights measure out honest trade, the sacred waters of the Saraswati will flow forever through our brick-lined canals."
* **The Crucible / Conflict:** Tectonic tremors shift the Himalayan headwaters, turning the perennial Saraswati river into a string of dry, dusty wadis; underground wells fail, covered brick sewers choke with stagnant silt, and epidemic dysentery drives the population out of the planned metropolis into the wilderness.
* **Dramatic Syllogism:**  
  $$\text{Sophisticated Municipal Sanitary Grid} + \text{Glacial River Avulsion \& Desiccation} = \text{Choked Sewers, Well Failure \& Urban De-Urbanization}$$
* **The Truth / Synthesis:** Advanced public hygiene and standardized engineering cannot sustain an urban civilization when its hydrological lifeblood vanishes. When the river dies, even the cleanest brick city becomes a magnificent, dry tomb.

* **Corner 1: The Establishment (The Municipal Sanitary Praetor):**  
  An obsessive civic administrator draped in a pleated robe, carrying a standardized bronze measuring rod and cubical chert weight. He spends his days inspecting street drains, fining citizens for blocked cesspools, and maintaining the illusion of eternal municipal order. Even as the river level drops to a trickle, he insists that cleaning the sumps will restore divine favor.
* **Corner 2: The Zealot Opponent (The Canal Renegade Engineer):**  
  A former chief hydraulic planner who recognized the tectonic avulsion years before the priesthood. Disillusioned by the city's blind adherence to failing infrastructure, he leads a desperate faction of workers who dynamite municipal diversion sluices, redirecting the last remaining wadi trickles into private highland wheat terraces and leaving the central city sewers completely dry.
* **Corner 3: The Thematic Shadow (The Corrupt Wellhead Oligarch):**  
  A wealthy merchant who controls the four deepest municipal wells in the upper citadel. While commoners in the lower town suffer from dry pumps, he locks his courtyard gates, hoards fresh groundwater in immense copper cisterns, and trades brackish sips of well-water to desperate families in exchange for ancestral jewelry and land deeds.
* **Corner 4: The Tertiary Outcast (The Subterranean Drain Scavenger):**  
  A destitute citizen who took refuge within the immense, dark, vaulted brick sewers beneath the city. Immune to the prevailing filth, she crawls through the dry terracotta drainage tubes, salvaging lost carnelian beads, copper fishhooks, and bronze seals dropped through floor chutes centuries ago, living off moisture condensation and surviving the city's death.

### 4. Injected Tabletop Mechanics & Runtime Primitives

#### 4.1 4-Level Dynamic Dial: Sanitary Degradation & River Desiccation
The hydrological health, structural integrity, and civic order of the Harappan city are tracked along a 4-level dial strictly calibrated to Universal Hazard Tiers:

* **Level 1 (Drain Silt Accumulation & Low Well Head / Light Severity Tier - DC 10):**  
  * *Condition:* Street drains flow sluggishly; a faint, heavy stench hangs over residential blocks; underground well water levels drop by 10 feet; municipal inspectors demand strict adherence to cubical weights.
  * *Mechanics:* Navigating narrow brick streets requires a **DC 10 Athletics check** to avoid slick, organic-filmed paving stones. Inspecting trade goods or appraising ancient currency requires a **DC 10 Intelligence check** against minor metrological wear.
* **Level 2 (Sewer Backflow & Metrological Currency Friction / Moderate Severity Tier - DC 13, 2d8 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* Covered street drains choke completely with dry silt, causing greywater to backflow through bathroom floor-chutes; stagnant wastewater pools in courtyards; water carries mild bacterial pathogens.
  * *Mechanics:* Consuming untreated water or traversing flooded alleyways requires a **DC 13 Constitution check**; failure inflicts mild dysentery (disadvantage on physical checks for 24 hours) or **2d8 bludgeoning damage** from slippery collapse hazards. Trading in local markets incurs a 25% price penalty due to disrupted standard weights.
* **Level 3 (Typhoid Outbreak & Wellhead Blockade / Severe Severity Tier - DC 16, 4d10 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* Contaminated drinking water triggers an epidemic of typhoid and cholera; armed guards barricade the remaining deep wells; desperate citizens riot in the Great Bath courtyard as its basin dries to cracked clay.
  * *Mechanics:* Entering the city or drinking local water requires a **DC 16 Fortitude check**; failure inflicts **4d10 poison and fever damage** and inflicts the *Exhausted* condition. Navigating blockaded wellheads requires passing **DC 16 Intimidation or Persuasion checks** or fighting through desperate civic militias.
* **Level 4 (Complete River Avulsion & Desiccated Brick Tomb / Lethal Climax Tier - DC 20, 8d12 damage):**  
  * *Condition:* The Ghaggar-Hakra riverbed dries entirely to cracked dust and windblown salt; all municipal wells fail permanently; the city transforms into an uninhabitable, desiccated brick ghost town swallowed by desert dunes.
  * *Mechanics:* Complete environmental and civilizational collapse. Staying within the ruined city without external water transport requires a **DC 20 Survival check** each day; failure inflicts **8d12 lethal dehydration and plague damage**. The module permanently transitions into a ruinous archaeological wasteland.

#### 4.2 Segmented Threat Clocks

```
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ] Saraswati Glacial Avulsion Countdown (6 Ticks)
[ ][ ][ ][ ]       Municipal Sewer Sump Choke Meter (4 Ticks)
```

* **Saraswati Glacial Avulsion Countdown [6 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) each turn upstream seismic tremors occur, satellite river channels divert water into the Yamuna or Indus basins, or monsoonal rainfall averages drop by 30%.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 2:* River flow drops to ankle-deep trickles; waterwheels along the riverbanks grind to a permanent halt.
    * *Tick 4:* Main river branches fragment into disconnected, stagnant saline pools choked with dead fish and algal blooms.
  * *Consequence when full (6/6):* The river dries up completely. Triggers **Dial Level 4: Complete River Avulsion & Desiccated Brick Tomb** immediately.
  * *Player Mitigation:* Digging deep infiltration trenches in the dry riverbed, constructing upstream earthen dams, or utilizing water-condensing machinery (**DC 16 Survival or Engineering check**) clears 1 tick.
* **Municipal Sewer Sump Choke Meter [4 Ticks]:**  
  * *Trigger:* Ticks forward (+1) when municipal dredging crews go on strike, street settlement tanks overflow with solid waste, or heavy rains wash roof debris into vertical wall-chutes.
  * *Escalation Events:*  
    * *Tick 1:* Street drain inspection slabs heave upward from internal pressure; foul greywater bubbles out into residential courtyards.
    * *Tick 2:* Basements and lower-floor residential rooms flood with anaerobic sewage, forcing families to abandon ground-level living quarters.
    * *Tick 3:* Swarms of disease-bearing insects breed exponentially in the sumps, spreading lethal fevers throughout the city blocks.
  * *Consequence when full (4/4):* Triggers **Dial Level 3: Typhoid Outbreak & Wellhead Blockade** across all municipal sectors.
  * *Player Mitigation:* Clearing blocked brick drains manually, organizing sanitary dredging teams, or flushing sumps with reserve cistern water (**DC 16 Athletics or Crafting check**) clears 1 tick.

    * Standardized metrology and script writing fall into disuse, replaced by regional oral traditions and simple rural subsistence farming.

### 5. Scene Calculus & Tactical Spatial Encounters

* **Encounter 1: The Choked Drain Backflow (Arrival Collision)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Entering the city through the main western gate, the party steps onto a major residential avenue just as a massive subterranean pressure surge blows the heavy stone inspection slabs off the central street drain. A geyser of black, anaerobic sewage erupts twenty feet into the air, splashing foul greywater across passing merchants, sacred temple bulls, and the party’s equipment. Before the party can recover, municipal sanitary guards rush forward with bronze rods, accusing the party of dumping contraband material into the private wall-chute upstream and placing them under immediate arrest.
  * *Polarity Shift ($+ \to -$):* The party enters what is reputed to be the cleanest, most civilized metropolis in the ancient world, only to be drenched in sewage and wrongfully imprisoned amidst crumbling municipal infrastructure.
  * *Bifocal Subtext:* The sanitary guards demand an exorbitant bribery fee in standard chert weights to drop the charges, whispering that the city treasury hasn't paid their wages in three months because the tax collectors found nothing but dry dust in the grain silos.
  * *Tactical Mechanics:* De-escalating the guards requires a **DC 13 Persuasion or Intimidation check**. Evading arrest and leaping across the sewage torrent requires a **DC 13 Acrobatics check**; failure inflicts 1 level of poisoned condition and forces a **DC 13 Constitution check** against dysentery.
* **Encounter 2: The Great Bath Mud Crucible (Dilemma / Sabotage Crisis)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Seeking purification or vital water supplies, the party reaches the Great Bath citadel. Instead of sparkling water, they find the monumental brick basin half-filled with cracked, drying mud and stagnant green puddles at the deep end. Thirty desperate citizens are engaged in a vicious brawl around the dry brick intake well, smashing ceramic jars over each other's heads. At the head of the stone staircase, the Corrupt Wellhead Oligarch and his mercenaries point compound bows at the crowd, threatening to loose volley arrows if anyone steps closer to the copper-lined storage cisterns.
  * *The Dilemma:*
    * *Storm the Oligarch's Cistern:* The party can lead the desperate citizens in a frontal assault against the oligarch's mercenaries (**DC 16 combat encounter** against elite archers on stone stairs). Winning the fight secures fresh water for the entire neighborhood and liberates the copper cisterns; however, the violent struggle damages the historic bitumen waterproofing of the Great Bath, causing the remaining water to drain instantly into the bedrock.
    * *Negotiate a Capped Ration:* The party can use diplomacy or valuable trade goods to negotiate a restricted water share from the oligarch's reserves (**DC 16 Persuasion check** costing 50 gp or rare trade goods). This avoids bloodshed and preserves the architectural heritage, but forces the party to witness elderly citizens and children turned away empty-handed to die of dehydration in the alleys.
* **Encounter 3: The Silt-Choked Sewer Escape (Stealth / Survival Climax)**
  * *Collision ($><$):* Accused of inciting the wellhead riot by the panicked Sanitary Praetor, the party is hunted through the grid-iron streets by temple guards. Their only escape route is dropping down a vertical wall-chute into the subterranean brick drainage network. However, because the river has dried up, the covered main sewer is no longer a rushing river of greywater, but a dark, claustrophobic tunnel half-choked with dry silt, scorpions, and choking dust.
  * *Tactical Environment:* Pitch-black subterranean brick tunnels; low ceilings requiring crawling; pockets of poisonous carbon dioxide gas pooling in low sumps.
  * *Resolution:* Navigating the 500-yard sewer tunnel to the exterior city wall requires a coordinated **DC 16 Survival, Athletics, or Constitution check**. Failing checks exposes characters to scorpion stings (**2d6 poison damage**) or suffocating gas pockets (requiring a **DC 13 Constitution save** or unconsciousness). Bursting out through the drainage exit grate leads directly into the dry, dusty wadi bed outside the city walls, where the party must decide whether to follow the dying river upstream into the mountains or flee into the desert.

  * **Node 14 (Desiccated Frontier Sump / Ancient Hydraulic Monument)**: Placed as an archaeological ruin or a dying border settlement, where the party must navigate toxic subterranean sewer tunnels to uncover buried metrological relics and engineering plans.
* **Topological Synergies:**
  * **Macro Collapse Cascade**: Sockets directly downstream from `CWT-060` (The Systemic Interdependence Collapse Matrix), demonstrating how the severance of inter-regional bronze trade and grain logistics cascades into local municipal and hydraulic failure.
