## How to Use These Modules at the Table

1. **Macro Level (Faction Design):** Use **SOC-01** and **SOC-05** to determine which cultural quadrant and operational profile a faction belongs to. Know immediately who rules, what they value, and how they treat the party.
2. **Thematic Archetype Inspiration (Optional Supplement):** `SOC-08` (`08_Tarot_Sociology_Matrix.md`) and `SOC-09` (`09_Minor_Arcana_Edge_Matrix.md`) are preserved as standalone static markdown reference compendiums mapping the 64-cell `[S, E, C]` matrix to historical Major and Minor Arcana traditions for optional worldbuilding inspiration. They are completely decoupled from workstation software execution.
3. **Institutional Negotiation & Infiltration:** Use **SOC-02** and **SOC-06** (Friction Dial $F_p$) to see what levers work on the faction (bribes, technical relics, shared tears, or forged vouchers) and what errors trigger instant hostility.
4. **Emergent Crises & Sandbox Evolution:** When threats breach outer gates or disasters occur, use **SOC-03** to advance the Crisis Metamorphism Clock and watch the culture mutate into emergency despotism or splinter into feral raider gangs.
5. **Immediate Room & Settlement Skinning:** When the party arrives at an unexpected settlement or guildhall, roll 5d6 on **SOC-04** to generate the sound level, guard discipline, communication style, moral code, and physical atmosphere in 30 seconds.
