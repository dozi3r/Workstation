# MODULE SOC-04: THE 5-DIMENSION CULTURAL PENTAGON
## Granular Procedural Generator for Settlements, Outposts & Rooms

---

### 1. The Pentagon Architecture

When players cross the perimeter of a settlement, enter a guild hall, or infiltrate a military compound, culture is not an abstract concept—it is felt in the air, seen in the architecture, and heard in how people speak.

The Groysberg Cultural Pentagon breaks sociological reality into **Five Tangible Dimensions**:

```text
                           [1] BEHAVIORS
                      Discipline | Atmosphere
                            ▲
                           / \
                          /   \
                         /     \
      [5] ENVIRONMENT   /       \   [2] RELATIONSHIPS
       Layout | Decor  ◄─────────►   Authority | Comms
                        \       /
                         \     /
                          \   /
                           ▼ 
                    [4] VALUES ─── [3] ATTITUDES
                  Purpose | Metrics   Safety | Mindset
```

### 2. Procedural Settlement Generator Tables

Roll **1d6** for each dimension to instantly texture any settlement or faction room:

#### Dimension 1: Behaviors (How people act)
| Roll (d6) | Discipline Style | Atmosphere & Noise Level | Game Effect / Player Experience |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Strict Iron Whip:** Guards patrol with canes; public silence enforced; eye contact avoided. | Dead silence save for clicking boots and factory machinery. | Unscheduled speech triggers immediate questioning by patrol. |
| **2** | **Rigid Shift-Work:** Citizens move in marching formations; bells ring every hour. | Monotonous chanting, repetitive cadence, synchronized clattering. | Stepping out of pedestrian flow creates a pedestrian jam. |
| **3** | **Pragmatic Routine:** People mind their business; quiet professional courtesy. | Low murmur of trade, sizzling food stalls, cloaked travelers. | Standard urban stealth and rumor-gathering work normally. |
| **4** | **Boisterous Fellowship:** Friendly back-slapping, communal songs, shared tankards. | Loud laughter, bawdy tavern ballads, howling dogs. | Buying a round of grog grants +2 to gather local gossip. |
| **5** | **Frenetic Innovation:** Agitated debates in doorways; people sketching on walls. | Clanging hammers, hiss of steam valves, crackling chemical fires. | Easy to cause a distraction; everyone is already distracted. |
| **6** | **Feral Chaos:** Unchecked brawling; drunken duels; zero policing. | Screaming, gunshots into the air, feral revelry. | Weapons drawn in the open; any sign of weakness invites muggers. |

#### Dimension 2: Relationships (How power and secrets flow)
| Roll (d6) | Authority Gradient | Communication Protocols | Game Effect / Player Experience |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Autocratic God-Emperor:** Totalitarian decree; leaders never seen in public. | Sealed wax directives, public herald megaphones, iron edicts. | PCs cannot speak to decision-makers without high-tier noble contacts. |
| **2** | **Tiered Bureaucracy:** Power distributed across rigid clerk castes and seals. | Triplicate requisition forms, stamped badges, slow courier runners. | Forged seals or stolen transit vouchers are 100% effective. |
| **3** | **Mercenary Contract:** Bosses rule purely by controlling the treasury ledger. | Itemized invoices, debt tallies, written bounties on noticeboards. | Gold or valuable salvage instantly buys access to highest councils. |
| **4** | **Elder Consensus:** Decisions made by council of matriarchs/veterans. | Open town meetings, long debates around braziers, oral traditions. | Persuading one elder is useless; party must win over the council majority. |
| **5** | **Meritocratic Anarchy:** Whoever solved the last crisis holds command today. | Whiteboard scribbles, shouted technical debates, open challenges. | Proving technical competence or surviving a duel grants instant rank. |
| **6** | **Secret Brotherhood:** Covert syndicates operate behind dummy mayors. | Thieves' cant, disguised finger gestures, graffiti scratch-marks. | Finding the real boss requires passing an underworld awareness check. |

#### Dimension 3: Attitudes (Psychological safety & risk appetite)
| Roll (d6) | Psychological Safety | Stance Toward Foreigners | Game Effect / Player Experience |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Lethal Paranoia:** Mistakes are treated as treason; zero tolerance for error. | Outsiders are assumed to be saboteurs or stirge-bait carriers. | A failed social check results in immediate arrest and interrogation. |
| **2** | **Suspicious Scapegoating:** Blame is constantly shifted downward to lower castes. | Outsiders are subjected to strip searches and quarantine tolls. | Minor crimes trigger demands for exorbitant foreign bribe payouts. |
| **3** | **Wary Neutrality:** "Don't bother us, we won't bother you." | Foreigners allowed in designated perimeter bazaar only. | Safe as long as the party stays out of restricted inner districts. |
| **4** | **Inquisitive Opportunism:** Welcomes travelers if they bring exotic news or goods. | Foreigners invited into workshops to share technical tricks. | Strange magical items or rare ores fetch 150% standard value. |
| **5** | **Protective Kinship:** High psychological safety internally; mistakes forgiven. | Outsiders tested with grueling manual labor before gaining trust. | Aiding a commoner in distress earns the enduring gratitude of the clan. |
| **6** | **Desperate Recklessness:** Nothing to lose; embraces suicidal high-risk gambits. | Outsiders immediately conscripted as vanguard strike shock-troops. | Hired on the spot for deadly missions with absurd upfront bounties. |

#### Dimension 4: Values (What is sacred vs. expendable)
| Roll (d6) | The Sacred Core | The Expendable Currency | Moral Texture |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **The Law's Letter:** The rulebook is sacred; context is irrelevant. | Human comfort, mercy, individual innocence. | *"The execution stands; the statute contains no clause for starvation."* |
| **2** | **Production Quota:** Tonnage of ore mined, barrels of acetylene refined. | Worker lives, limb safety, structural longevity. | *"Three dead in the foundry? Did they meet the midnight iron quota?"* |
| **3** | **Silver Balance:** Profit margin, debt solvency, property rights. | Communal solidarity, oaths, abstract honor. | *"Your contract was bought out at noon. Clear your locker or be purged."* |
| **4** | **Survival of the Clan:** Preservation of the bloodline and veteran cadre. | Outside alliances, treaty promises made to foreign hubs. | *"We broke the pact because our children were hungry. We'd do it again."* |
| **5** | **The Grand Invention:** Breakthrough technical discovery or arcane relic. | Treasury coin, physical health, sleep, lab assistants. | *"The dynamo exploded, but look at the spectroscopic residue! Magnifique!"* |
| **6** | **Individual Liberty:** Freedom from shackles, absence of masters. | Civic order, public hygiene, long-term food stores. | *"No man tells me when to light my torch, even if it brings the stirges."* |

#### Dimension 5: Physical Environment (Sensory architecture & decor)
| Roll (d6) | Architectural Layout | Interior Decor & Sensory Atmosphere | Game Effect / Player Experience |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Fortified Panopticon:** Central watchtowers overlook narrow, sterile alleys. | Cold dressed granite; searchlights; smelling of caustic lye and bleach. | Climbing or hiding in shadows requires passing stealth checks with penalty. |
| **2** | **Siloed Bunkers:** Heavy bulkhead doors; barbed wire; partitioned districts. | Riveted iron plating; smelling of coal soot, grease, and ozone sparks. | Navigating between districts requires stamped physical gate tokens. |
| **3** | **Dense Merchant Shallows:** Overhanging awnings; modular shipping stalls. | Colorful canvas awnings; smelling of roasting salted eel, spices, and tar. | High crowds; pickpocketing checks and pursuit checks thrive here. |
| **4** | **Communal Longhouses:** Shared dining halls; central hearths; turf roofs. | Carved timber pillars; animal pelts; smelling of peat smoke and roasted mutton. | Resting here removes 1 extra fatigue point due to genuine warmth. |
| **5** | **Tinkerer's Hive:** Suspended rope walkways; towering scaffoldings. | Copper tubes, exposed gears; smelling of sulfur, battery acid, and sawdust. | High verticality; falling hazards on failed athletic checks. |
| **6** | **Sunken Ruins / Shanty Trenches:** Scrap-metal lean-tos dug into muddy ravines. | Rusted sheet metal, rotting timbers; smelling of stagnant mire and damp rot. | Movement is halved in muddy streets unless using stilts or boardwalks. |
