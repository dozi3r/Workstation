## 1. Executive Summary & Design Mandate

In sandbox campaigns and procedural balance engines, entities (biomes, settlements, syndicates, NPCs, and drawn Tarot cards) are frequently described using loose, evocative adjectives. While atmospheric, this creates severe drift: two different game masters (or the same designer over several months) will evaluate the disposition of the same faction or card in contradictory ways.

This module establishes the **Organizational Disposition Vector (ODV)**: a standardized, mathematically rigorous, system-agnostic coordinate system derived from academic organizational sociology (Cameron & Quinn, Daft, Duncan, Porter, Groysberg). 

It couples this coordinate system with a **Deterministic Diagnostic Decision Tree** engineered for **high inter-rater reliability**. Whether assessing a frontier boomtown, an authoritarian commandant, or the subtle theological differences between the *Rider-Waite-Smith*, *Crowley Thoth*, and *H.R. Giger* interpretations of the **Reversed Magician**, this framework ensures that any analyst will arrive at the exact same disposition score and mechanical game output.

## 2. The Organizational Disposition Vector (ODV) Specification

To remain 100% compliant with **ADR-07 (System-Agnostic Vague Game Standard)**, an entity's disposition is distilled into a 3-dial coordinate vector:

$$\mathbf{ODV} = [S, E, C]$$

Where each parameter is an integer dial from **1 to 4**:

```text
               [S] STABILITY / ENTROPY               [E] FOCUS / LOCUS                 [C] COUPLING / TOPOLOGY
        1: Rigid Clockwork (Immutable Order)   1: Hermetic Autarky (Insular Kin)   1: Atomized Silo (Autonomous)
        2: Codified System (Standard SOPs)     2: Mutualist Cohesion (Inward)      2: Decoupled Guild (Loose)
        3: Pragmatic Adaptation (Flexible)     3: Mercantile Exchange (External)   3: Federated Mesh (Networked)
        4: Fluid Entropy (Radical Flux)        4: Predatory Conquest (Extractive)  4: Total Matrix Hive (Integrated)
```

### 2.1 Parameter Definitions

#### 1. Dial $S$ — Structural Stability vs. Entropy ($S \in [1, 4]$)
*Measures the rate of internal reconfiguration, tolerance for operational error, and rigidity of authority.*
* **$S = 1$ (Rigid Clockwork):** Totalitarian stasis. Protocols are carved in stone. Error or deviation is treated as capital sabotage. Zero procedural variance.
* **$S = 2$ (Codified System):** Established chain-of-command with standardized operating procedures (SOPs). Governed by written charters; changes occur via slow, institutional review.
* **$S = 3$ (Pragmatic Adaptation):** Decentralized problem-solving. Policies are treated as rough heuristics. Mistakes are tolerated if they yield practical survival results.
* **$S = 4$ (Fluid Entropy):** Radical ad-hocracy, feral nomadism, or frantic constant metamorphosis. Roles dissolve and reassemble per crisis; absolute improvisation.

#### 2. Dial $E$ — Boundary Focus / Intent ($E \in [1, 4]$)
*Measures where the entity directs its energy, evaluates success, and positions itself relative to the outside world.*
* **$E = 1$ (Hermetic Autarky):** Hyper-internal centripetal focus. Outsiders are viewed as ideological, biological, or spiritual contagion. Victory is defined solely by member survival and sacred preservation.
* **$E = 2$ (Mutualist Cohesion):** Community-first focus. Protective of the inner circle; external relations are defensive and conducted at strictly regulated perimeter checkpoints.
* **$E = 3$ (Mercantile Exchange):** Centrifugal external focus. Evaluates success by contractual leverage, trade volume, asset acquisition, and competitive standing.
* **$E = 4$ (Predatory Imperialism):** Hyper-external extraction. Cannibalistic expansion, territorial annexation, debt peonage, or systemic stripping of surrounding nodes.

#### 3. Dial $C$ — Systemic Coupling / Interdependence ($C \in [1, 4]$)
*Measures how tightly the entity's functional units are wired together and how power/logistics flow.*
* **$C = 1$ (Atomized Silo):** Extreme independence. Lone master-apprentice workshops, cloistered hermits, autonomous corsair skiffs. Decapitating the leader leaves individual survivors completely functional.
* **$C = 2$ (Decoupled Guild):** Independent cells united by loose trade agreements or shared craft secrets. Sub-units can survive in total isolation for months.
* **$C = 3$ (Federated Mesh):** Coordinated regional confederation. Shared water, acetylene, or defensive garrisons; sub-units experience operational friction if decoupled.
* **$C = 4$ (Total Matrix Hive):** Hyper-interdependence. Deep matrix command, shared continental grids, or biological cybernetic symbiosis. Decapitation does not kill the system (matrix redundancy), but cutting off external pipeline feeds kills all units in days.

### 3.3 Duncan Environmental Uncertainty Stance ($U$)

Calculated by pairing the entity's internal entropy ($S$) with the surrounding node network complexity ($K_{\text{edges}}$):

$$U = \text{clamp}\left(1, 4, \left\lfloor \frac{S + \min(4, \text{Degree}(N))}{2} \right\rfloor \right)$$

* **$U = 1$ (Simple + Stable):** Interior farmland, sealed vault. Zero environmental stress.
* **$U = 2$ (Simple + Unstable):** Frontier shoreline, tidal salt flats. Economic volatility, simple hazards.
* **$U = 3$ (Complex + Stable):** Deep mountain monastic vaults, fungal cave networks. Layered biological hazards, predictable routines.
* **$U = 4$ (Complex + Unstable):** Titanwood apex canopy, Slaughter-Gorge stirge breeding rifts. Lethal chaotic volatility.

## 4. The Deterministic Decision Framework (The Diagnostic Battery)

To guarantee high inter-rater reliability, the analyst must not guess numbers based on mood. Instead, answer these **Six Standardized Diagnostic Inquiries (2 per dial)**. Each inquiry presents four mutually exclusive, testable conditions.

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE 6-STEP DIAGNOSTIC BATTERY                        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  DIAL E (FOCUS):                                                        │
│    [Test E.1] The Metric of Triumph (How is victory calculated?)        │
│    [Test E.2] The Stranger Encounter (How is an outsider processed?)   │
│                                                                         │
│  DIAL S (STABILITY):                                                    │
│    [Test S.1] The Deviance / Error Threshold (How are mistakes treated?)│
│    [Test S.2] Structural Reconfiguration Cadence (How fast do rules shift?)
│                                                                         │
│  DIAL C (COUPLING):                                                     │
│    [Test C.1] The Decapitation Vulnerability (What happens if head dies?│
│    [Test C.2] Logistical Autarky Horizon (How long can it self-sustain?)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.1 Scoring Dial $E$ (Boundary Focus & Intent)

#### Test E.1: The Metric of Triumph
*How does the entity's leadership determine that it has had a successful cycle?*
1. **Kin/Sacred Preservation:** Success = zero internal members lost, sacred lineage/doctrines preserved uncorrupted. No external expansion sought. $\to \mathbf{Score = 1}$
2. **Defensive Equilibrium:** Success = local stockpiles filled, internal morale stable, borders unbreached. Outside trade conducted strictly for needed survival staples. $\to \mathbf{Score = 2}$
3. **Ledger Dominance & Favorable Trade:** Success = trade surplus expanded, contractual liens collected, rival factions indebted or out-competed. $\to \mathbf{Score = 3}$
4. **Territorial / Resource Annexation:** Success = new outposts captured, external rivals crushed or enslaved, raw resources extracted to exhaustion. $\to \mathbf{Score = 4}$

#### Test E.2: The Stranger Encounter
*What happens to an unaccompanied outsider discovered at the entity's perimeter?*
1. **Hermetic Quarantine / Banishment:** Outsider is immediately blinded, quarantined, or executed on sight as an existential contaminant. $\to \mathbf{Score = 1}$
2. **Vouched Hospitality:** Outsider is barred until an existing member sponsors them under penalty of shared punishment; Restorative trial. $\to \mathbf{Score = 2}$
3. **Transactional Toll & Customs:** Outsider is escorted to a designated bazaar, assessed for tariffs, and evaluated strictly by what they can buy, sell, or pledge. $\to \mathbf{Score = 3}$
4. **Predatory Extraction:** Outsider is stripped of gear, conscripted into forward mine trenches, harvested for biomass/soul conduits, or swindled. $\to \mathbf{Score = 4}$

$$\mathbf{Dial\ E} = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{Test E.1} + \text{Test E.2}}{2}\right)$$

---

### 4.2 Scoring Dial $S$ (Structural Stability & Entropy)

#### Test S.1: The Deviance / Error Threshold
*How does the entity react when a subordinate, artisan, or pilot commits an unapproved procedural error or invents an unsanctioned shortcut?*
1. **Sacrilege / Capital Treason:** Immediate trial, execution, or lobotomy. Protocols are sacred; deviation is treated as infectious treason. $\to \mathbf{Score = 1}$
2. **Bureaucratic Reprimand:** Demotion, docked rations, or formal disciplinary hearing within the framework of official operational manuals. $\to \mathbf{Score = 2}$
3. **Pragmatic Assessment:** If the shortcut solved an immediate tactical problem, the error is quietly ignored or adopted as informal local practice. $\to \mathbf{Score = 3}$
4. **Celebrated Innovation:** The rulebook is discarded; the author of the breakthrough is given immediate resources to run further experiments. $\to \mathbf{Score = 4}$

#### Test S.2: Structural Reconfiguration Cadence
*How rapidly and easily does the institution reallocate command roles, operational stations, and foundational laws?*
1. **Millennial Granite:** Roles and caste tiers are ancestral and immutable. The command structure has not changed in generations. $\to \mathbf{Score = 1}$
2. **Codified Legislative Review:** Reorganizations require formal charters, jurisdictional approvals, and stamped authorization seals. $\to \mathbf{Score = 2}$
3. **Situational Committee Rotation:** Command shifts fluidly between veteran peers based on whoever has the relevant survival skill for the current crisis. $\to \mathbf{Score = 3}$
4. **Hourly Ad-Hocracy:** Zero permanent roles. Command belongs to whoever shouts the smartest command or seizes the wheel during an ambush. $\to \mathbf{Score = 4}$

$$\mathbf{Dial\ S} = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{Test S.1} + \text{Test S.2}}{2}\right)$$

---

### 4.3 Scoring Dial $C$ (Systemic Coupling & Topology)

#### Test C.1: The Decapitation Vulnerability
*What is the systemic status of the entity 24 hours after the supreme commander, guildmaster, or primary relay node is assassinated?*
1. **Immediate Cell Dispersal:** The structure shatters instantly; individual surviving agents flee into the wilderness with zero coordination. $\to \mathbf{Score = 1}$
2. **Autonomous Guild Schism:** Sub-workshops lock their doors and operate independently, competing to claim the dead master's assets. $\to \mathbf{Score = 2}$
3. **Orderly Council Succession:** A designated co-equal deputy or council convenes within hours; slight tactical delay, but operations hold. $\to \mathbf{Score = 3}$
4. **Seamless Matrix Absorption:** Redundant neural circuits, co-equal command matrixes, or deep institutional protocols absorb the loss without missing a single manufacturing shift. $\to \mathbf{Score = 4}$

#### Test C.2: Logistical Autarky Horizon
*If physical transit lines and communications to all neighboring nodes are completely cut by a landslide or siege, how long can an individual outpost survive?*
1. **Indefinite (Autarkic Enclave):** Fully self-sufficient. Has its own water distillery, seed vault, and repair forge. Can survive years decoupled. $\to \mathbf{Score = 1}$
2. **Seasonal (Stockpiled Independence):** Can survive 3 to 6 months on deep local caches before needing raw bulk inputs from neighbors. $\to \mathbf{Score = 2}$
3. **Fragile (Networked Interdependence):** Rations and fuel run dry within 2 to 4 weeks; requires regular convoy deliveries to operate defenses. $\to \mathbf{Score = 3}$
4. **Critical Parasitism / Grid-Lock (Total Matrix):** Shuts down within 48 to 72 hours without continuous high-head electrical current, pipeline feeds, or central relay signals. $\to \mathbf{Score = 4}$

$$\mathbf{Dial\ C} = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{Test C.1} + \text{Test C.2}}{2}\right)$$

## 6. Tabletop Runtime Rules: Integrating the ODV into Play

Once an entity's ODV vector $[S, E, C]$ is established, GMs use it to govern social rolls, stealth infiltration, and faction diplomacy without arbitrary improvisation.

### 6.1 The Player Friction Dial ($F_p \in [1, 4]$)
When player characters attempt to negotiate, bribe, or legally persuade an NPC or settlement, calculate the **Friction Dial**:

$$\Delta S = |S_{\text{party}} - S_{\text{target}}|, \quad \Delta E = |E_{\text{party}} - E_{\text{target}}|$$

$$F_p = \text{clamp}\left(1, 4, 1 + \left\lfloor \frac{\Delta S + \Delta E}{2} \right\rfloor \right)$$

* **$F_p = 1$ (Harmonic Resonance):** Negotiation flows naturally. Advantage on all social leverage rolls.
* **$F_p = 2$ (Minor Institutional Friction):** Standard social checks. Minor administrative delays or mild suspicion.
* **$F_p = 3$ (Severe Cultural Clashing):** Disadvantage on social rolls. Demands heavy collateral, hostages, or third-party guarantors.
* **$F_p = 4$ (Existential Incompatibility):** Verbal negotiation impossible without radical transformation of terms. Unsanctioned speech triggers immediate arrest or weapon draws.

### 6.2 Topological Network Influence Affinity

In the **Network Engine (`Run-NetworkEngine.ps1`)**, the natural influence transfer between two adjacent nodes $A$ and $B$ across a directed edge is weighted by their **Disposition Affinity**:

$$\text{Affinity}(A, B) = 1.0 - \left(\frac{|S_A - S_B| + |E_A - E_B| + |C_A - C_B|}{9}\right)$$

* If $\text{Affinity} \ge 0.75$: Node $A$ rapidly culturalizes Node $B$; influence propagates without smothering resistance.
* If $\text{Affinity} \le 0.35$: Severe cultural rejection. Edge triggers border skirmishes, refugee friction, and accelerated Conflation Rumors.

## 7. The Boon & Burden Matrix with Passives (All 8 S, E, C Combinations)

Every combination of **Stability ($S$)**, **External Focus ($E$)**, and **Coupling ($C$)** creates an inherent structural specialization. In accordance with organizational sociology, an institution cannot optimize one dimension without creating an exploitable operational vulnerability.

To support tactical tabletop play, macro-faction diplomacy, and procedural Tarot card generation, each of the **8 Core Combinations (Octants)** possesses:
* **The Core Boon:** The systemic advantage and foundational superpower of the entity.
* **The Core Burden:** The fatal structural vulnerability, blind spot, or failure condition.
* **Systemic / Faction Passive (Macro):** Affects regional rules, influence propagation, and resource dice.
* **Encounter / NPC Passive (Micro):** Affects direct tabletop negotiations, social friction, and combat reactions.
* **Tarot / Esoteric Passive (Archetypal):** The supernatural or card-modulator effect when assigned to a node, relic, or Tarot card.
* **Dial Gradient Scaling:** How dial extremes ($1$ and $4$) supercharge both the boon and the burden compared to moderate states ($2$ and $3$).

### 7.3 Dial Gradient Mechanics (Scaling from 2/3 to 1/4)

When integrating these combinations into the pointcrawl setting's rules-lite engine, use the **Dial Distance from Center** to scale mechanical intensity:

$$\text{Intensity} = \sum |D_i - 2.5|$$

* **Moderate States (Dials at 2 or 3):**
  * Resource Dice: **d6 / d8**
  * Clock Movement: Standard 1 tick per event
  * Friction Dial: Standard $F_p = 1\text{–}2$
  * Vulnerabilities are manageable through skilled roleplay and diplomacy.
* **Extreme States (Dials at 1 or 4):**
  * Resource Dice: **d10 / d12** (Supercharged Boons)
  * Clock Movement: Double-rate (2 ticks per shock) or instant catastrophic trigger
  * Friction Dial: Severe $F_p = 3\text{–}4$ (Near-zero diplomatic room)
  * Vulnerabilities are fatal structural blind spots that can destroy the entity in a single session.

## 9. Summary Diagnostic Cheat Sheet (GM Fast-Track)

When authoring a new region, city, or NPC during session prep, run this **10-second mental check**:

```text
1. FOCUS (E):
   Does it care about its OWN PEOPLE (E=1-2) or EXTRACTING FROM OTHERS (E=3-4)?

2. STABILITY (S):
   Does it punish MISTAKES AS TREASON (S=1-2) or IMPROVISE NEW RULES (S=3-4)?

3. COUPLING (C):
   Does it function as an ISOLATED SOLO CELL (C=1-2) or an INTEGRATED NETWORK (C=3-4)?
```

Write down the 3 numbers: `[S, E, C]`. Look up the quadrant and operational mode in Section 3. The entity is now mathematically grounded, behaviorally predictable, and fully balanced within the the pointcrawl setting network engine!
