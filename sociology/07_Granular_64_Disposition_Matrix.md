## Generalized Archetypal Taxonomy for Organizational Behavior, Entities & Tarot Hermeneutics

### 1. Architectural Foundation & Parameter Definitions

This framework provides a fully decoupled, system-agnostic decision matrix across 64 unique coordinates. It translates organizational sociology, behavioral psychology, and archetypal theory into a structured evaluation system without hardcoded game mechanics, dials, or setting-specific lore.

Every entity—whether a nation, city, faction, sub-unit, NPC, or esoteric card interpretation—is defined by three orthogonal parameters rated from **1 to 4**:

```text
===================================================================================================
PARAMETER S: STRUCTURAL ENTROPY & STABILITY (Governance & Reconfiguration)
===================================================================================================
1: Immutable Order        | Petrified doctrine, zero error tolerance, frozen hierarchy.
2: Codified Protocol      | Standardized operating procedures, legal charters, bounded evolution.
3: Adaptive Heuristics    | Empirical pragmatism, decentralized problem-solving, situational roles.
4: Hyper-Entropy          | Radical fluidity, continuous metamorphosis, ad-hocracy, volatile flux.

===================================================================================================
PARAMETER E: BOUNDARY FOCUS & INTENT (Direction of Purpose & Energy)
===================================================================================================
1: Hermetic Autarky       | Extreme inward preservation, sacred insularity, outsiders excluded.
2: Protective Cohesion    | Member welfare first, guarded perimeter, cautious defensive reciprocity.
3: Transactional Exchange | Contract-driven, outward mercantile engagement, resource bargaining.
4: Predatory Conquest     | Zero-sum expansion, aggressive annexation, extractive hegemony.

===================================================================================================
PARAMETER C: SYSTEMIC COUPLING & TOPOLOGY (Interdependence & Architecture)
===================================================================================================
1: Atomized Silo          | Fully self-contained autonomous cells/agents; decoupled survivability.
2: Loose Confederation    | Independent units with voluntary ties; decoupled fail-safes.
3: Integrated Network     | Interdependent logistical mesh, functional specialization, mutual reliance.
4: Monolithic Matrix      | Hardwired systemic integration, indivisible collective organism/hive.
```

#### Dual-Horizon Passive Architecture (Micro vs. Macro)

Every coordinate generates two distinct, concurrent passive effects that can be adapted into specific setting cultures or game systems:
* **Micro Passive (Internal Mechanics):** Operates strictly within the interior boundaries of the entity itself. Governs internal operational efficiency, damage redistribution, member psychology, self-preservation, structural resilience, and deviance handling.
* **Macro Passive (Influence Sphere):** Projects outward into the surrounding environment and adjacent network connections. Governs ambient territorial effects, border friction, tariff extraction, fear/ideological radiation, ecological contamination, and regional market manipulation.

### 7. Engine Translation Guide: Mapping Coordinates to Game Mechanics

To apply any `[S, E, C]` tag to your tabletop engine or balance calculator, use these universal mathematical translations:

1. **Social & Negotiation Friction ($F \in [1, 4]$):**
   $$F = \text{clamp}\left(1, 4, 1 + \left\lfloor \frac{|S_A - S_B| + |E_A - E_B|}{2} \right\rfloor \right)$$
   * $F = 1$: Natural cultural alignment (Advantage on social rolls).
   * $F = 4$: Existential ideological clash (Verbal negotiation impossible without leverage/hostages).

2. **Topological Influence Affinity ($\text{Affinity} \in [0.0, 1.0]$):**
   $$\text{Affinity}(A, B) = 1.0 - \left(\frac{|S_A - S_B| + |E_A - E_B| + |C_A - C_B|}{9}\right)$$
   * Measures how easily cultural traits, economic trade, or ideas propagate between two connected entities.

3. **Systemic Resilience vs. Innovation Speed:**
   * **Defensive Resilience Factor:** Directly proportional to $\mathbf{(5 - S) \times C}$. (Highest at `[1, *, 4]`, lowest at `[4, *, 1]`).
   * **Breakthrough Innovation Velocity:** Directly proportional to $\mathbf{S \times (5 - C)}$. (Highest at `[4, *, 1]`, lowest at `[1, *, 4]`).
