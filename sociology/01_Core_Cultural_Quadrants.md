### 1. The Core Sociological Axes

Every settlement, bastion, syndicate, or cult on the pointcrawl setting is defined by two fundamental institutional tensions. An organization cannot maximize both ends of either axis simultaneously; choosing one always creates an operational vulnerability.

```text
                  MORE FLEXIBLE / DYNAMIC
                             ▲
                             │
            CLAN             │          ADHOCRACY
         (Family)            │          (Creative)
      "Collaborate"          │           "Create"
                             │
   INTERNAL FOCUS ───────────┼─────────── EXTERNAL FOCUS
   (Unity, Harmony,          │           (Rivalry, Expansion,
    Human Capital)           │            Market Dominance)
                             │
          HIERARCHY          │            MARKET
          (Control)          │           (Results)
        "Control"            │           "Compete"
                             │
                             ▼
                  MORE STABLE / PREDICTABLE
```

* **Axis Y: Stability vs. Flexibility**
  * **Flexibility (High Entropy / Agility):** Organic adaptation, decentralized initiative, tolerance for error, high improvisation. Vulnerable to infighting, chaos, and lack of long-term infrastructure.
  * **Stability (Low Entropy / Order):** Predictable routines, rigid chain-of-command, repeatable processes, defensive fortifications. Vulnerable to stagnation, paralysis during sudden crises, and brittle breakages.
* **Axis X: Internal vs. External Focus**
  * **Internal Focus (Centripetal / People-Oriented):** Cohesion, mutual aid, shared welfare, loyalty, member preservation. Insular, suspicious of foreign alliances, slow to seize external opportunities.
  * **External Focus (Centrifugal / Competitive):** Resource conquest, territorial expansion, market share, out-competing rivals. Cannibalistic towards internal dissent, treats personnel as expendable capital.

### 2. The Four Cultural Archetypes

#### Quadrant A: The Clan ("Collaborate")
*Internal Focus + High Flexibility*
* **Motto:** *"We stand together, or we drown together."*
* **Sociological Core:** The extended family, veteran warband brotherhood, or monastic commune. High interpersonal loyalty, flat hierarchy, decisions reached via marathon elder councils or communal consensus.
* **Leader Type:** **The Facilitator / Mentor / Patriarch.** Leads through moral authority, shared suffering, and relational ties rather than legal decrees or brute force.
* **Value Drivers:** Commitment, interpersonal trust, human development, communal defense.
* **Reaction to Player Characters:**
  * *First Contact:* Warily welcoming if introduced by a known cousin; icy and guarded if unvouched.
  * *Justice System:* Restorative. Banishment or mandatory restitution rather than executions or fines.
  * *The Social Debt:* Once accepted, PCs are treated like kin—demanding unconditional mutual defense when raided.
* **Fatal Flaw:** **Consensus Paralysis.** When a rapid threat arrives (e.g. an aerial stirge breach), hours are lost debating consensus while the defenses burn.

#### Quadrant B: The Adhocracy ("Create")
*External Focus + High Flexibility*
* **Motto:** *"First to invent, last to surrender."*
* **Sociological Core:** The experimental workshop, rogue corsair flotilla, or radical arcane cabal. Constantly shifting task-forces, loose alliances, obsessive pursuit of breakthrough advantage or tactical surprises.
* **Leader Type:** **The Visionary / Artificer / Maverick.** Leads through charismatic breakthroughs, bold gambits, and technical or magical brilliance.
* **Value Drivers:** Innovation, rapid transformation, tactical opportunism, risk appetite.
* **Reaction to Player Characters:**
  * *First Contact:* Curious, transactional, eager to test strange foreign relics or recruit guinea pigs for experimental ventures.
  * *Justice System:* Pragmatic. Crimes are forgiven if the perpetrator invents a solution or provides valuable salvage.
  * *The Social Debt:* Loyal only until a better opportunity appears; contracts are fluid and easily renegotiated.
* **Fatal Flaw:** **Exhaustion & Anarchy.** Constant pivoting burns through resources and lives; no foundational institutions endure long-term.

#### Quadrant C: The Market ("Compete")
*External Focus + High Stability*
* **Motto:** *"Results justify the balance sheet."*
* **Sociological Core:** The merchant syndicate, mercenary company, or resource-extraction cartel. Obsessed with bottom lines, quotas, territorial extraction, and outmaneuvering rivals through contractual leverage or economic warfare.
* **Leader Type:** **The Hard-Driver / Producer / Cartel Boss.** Leads through contractual enforcement, quota performance, and aggressive incentive structures.
* **Value Drivers:** Market dominance, goal achievement, profit velocity, ruthless efficiency.
* **Reaction to Player Characters:**
  * *First Contact:* Coldly professional. PCs are evaluated purely by what resources, services, or leverage they bring to the ledger.
  * *Justice System:* Punitive & Financial. Debtor workhouses, crippling fines, bounty hunters, and contract liens.
  * *The Social Debt:* Everything has a price tag; friendship is non-existent, but signed parchment is sacred.
* **Fatal Flaw:** **Moral Attrition & Sabotage.** Ruthless internal competition fosters paranoia and backstabbing; subordinates sell out their masters to the highest bidder.

#### Quadrant D: The Hierarchy ("Control")
*Internal Focus + High Stability*
* **Motto:** *"Protocol is life; deviation is extinction."*
* **Sociological Core:** The imperial fortress, fortress monastery, or authoritarian administrative hub. Rigid caste tiers, standardized operating procedures, triplicate paperwork, surveillance networks, and defensive bastions.
* **Leader Type:** **The Coordinator / Monitor / High Arbiter.** Leads through legal mandate, office insignia, bureaucratic rank, and procedural enforcement.
* **Value Drivers:** Efficiency, uniformity, predictability, containment security, flawless record-keeping.
* **Reaction to Player Characters:**
  * *First Contact:* Bureaucratic processing. Mandatory quarantine, transit papers, baggage searches, and biometric silver wristbands.
  * *Justice System:* Inquisitorial. Summary trials, strict penal labor, public floggings, and strict legal codes.
  * *The Social Debt:* PCs are tracked numbers in an imperial ledger; obedience grants protection, while insubordination triggers military arrest.
* **Fatal Flaw:** **Brittle Rigidity.** Cannot handle anomalies that fall outside official manuals. When unexpected horrors emerge, guards wait for orders from dead superiors.

### 3. Vague Game Conversion Dials

When dropping any settlement into your rules-agnostic campaign, assign two ratings from **1 to 5**:

| Metric | Rating 1 | Rating 3 | Rating 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stability Dial** *(1 = Feral Anarchy, 5 = Totalitarian Clockwork)* | Dynamic nomadic warband; zero permanent laws. | Pragmatic council; customary laws with flexibility. | Imperial fortress; execution for curfew violations. |
| **External Focus Dial** *(1 = Hyper-Insular, 5 = Predatory Expansionist)* | Hidden valley tribe; foreigners blinded before entry. | Self-sufficient town; trades at perimeter gates only. | Voracious merchant empire; actively annexing neighboring hamlets. |

* **Friction Check (2d6):** When PCs attempt to persuade, negotiate, or bribe within a settlement, compare the PCs' proposal to the settlement's quadrant:
  * Proposing a collective sacrifice to a *Market* faction suffers **Disadvantage / -2**.
  * Proposing an innovative, rule-breaking shortcut to a *Hierarchy* faction triggers **Alarm / Immediate Arrest**.
  * Proposing a legally binding contract to a *Clan* faction induces **Deep Insult / Loss of Trust**.
