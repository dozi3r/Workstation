# Culture-and-organization-design.png (Visual Diagram / Cartographic Asset)

<!-- BEGIN_CUSTOM_NOTES -->
### 1. Architectural Purpose & Academic Origin
`sociology/Culture-and-organization-design.png` (`383.9 KB`) is a theoretical reference diagram from **Richard L. Daft's *Organization Theory & Design* (Cengage, 2017)**, cataloged in [[File:sociology/README.md]] and applied across [[File:sociology/02_Operational_Profiles_and_Power.md]] (`SOC-02`) and [[File:sociology/03_Environmental_Uncertainty_and_Crisis.md]] (`SOC-03`). It maps the relationship between **Strategic Focus** (*Internal* vs. *External*) and **Needs of the Environment** (*Flexibility* vs. *Stability*) to produce Daft's four organizational culture categories: **Adaptability Culture**, **Mission Culture**, **Clan Involvement Culture**, and **Bureaucratic Consistency Culture**.
