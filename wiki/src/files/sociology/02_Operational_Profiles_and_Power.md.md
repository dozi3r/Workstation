# MODULE SOC-02: OPERATIONAL PROFILES & POWER ARCHITECTURE

<!-- BEGIN_CUSTOM_NOTES -->
### 1. Architectural Purpose & Theoretical Foundation
`sociology/02_Operational_Profiles_and_Power.md` (**Module SOC-02**) defines the operational machinery, labor division, and power architecture of pointcrawl institutions. Grounded in Michael Porter's strategic trade-off theory, Boris Groysberg's operational alignment research, and Richard Daft's *Organization Theory & Design*, `SOC-02` answers how commands are executed and what every institution must sacrifice to survive.

Whereas [[File:sociology/01_Core_Cultural_Quadrants.md]] crosses Stability (`S`) with Boundary Focus (`E`), `SOC-02` crosses **Stability vs. Flexibility (`S` Dial)** with **Independence vs. Interdependence (`C` Coupling Dial in [[File:sociology/06_Disposition_Matrix_and_Tarot_Decision_Framework.md]])**:
- **Independence (`C <= 2`)**: Autonomous specialist cells, decoupled guild workshops, or isolated production silos that can survive severed from external grids.
- **Interdependence (`C >= 3`)**: Networked mutualist leagues or cross-functional matrix bastions wired into shared logistical pipelines and redundant command relays.
