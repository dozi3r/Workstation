### Academic & Architectural Provenance
`sociology/groysberg_5d_culture_radar.png` (formerly `https3A2F2Fblogs-images.png`, remediated under `[[Ticket:SYS-AUDIT-01]]`) provides Boris Groysberg, Jeremiah Lee, Jesse Price, and J. Yo-Jud Cheng's **5-Dimensional Cultural Radar Pentagon** (*Harvard Business Review*).

### Theoretical Foundations
The 5 dimensions form the baseline for procedural table-ready settlement generation:
1. **Behaviors**: Visible rituals, discipline, dress codes, civic protocols.
2. **Relationships**: Social hierarchies, kinship networks, patronage bonds, formal rank.
3. **Attitudes**: Collective disposition toward outsiders, danger, divine authority, and magic.
4. **Values**: Core ideological commitments, sacred taboos, what crimes are unforgivable.
5. **Environment**: Spatial architecture, smells, sounds, light levels, sensory density.

### Procedural Generation Integration
In `[[File:sociology/04_Sensory_Pentagon_Generator.md]]`, each of these 5 dimensions is mapped to d6 sensory roll tables, allowing a Game Master to flesh out sensory atmosphere, guard dispositions, and street-level encounters in seconds.
