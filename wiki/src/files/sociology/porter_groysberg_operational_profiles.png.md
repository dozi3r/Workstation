### Theoretical Foundations & Operational Trade-Offs
Every sovereign hub or faction outpost operates along a primary functional axis and is forced to make fundamental operational sacrifices:
1. **Design Focus (Innovation / Bespoke Craft)**: High customizability and esoteric discovery; sacrifices standardized high-volume efficiency.
2. **Service Focus (Customer / Retinue Intimacy)**: Deep interpersonal alignment and tailored assistance; sacrifices lowest-cost commodity delivery.
3. **Produce Focus (Low-Cost Mass Manufacture)**: Maximum volume, economies of scale, strict operational discipline; sacrifices custom adaptability.
4. **Deliver Focus (Logistics & Supply Lines)**: Unmatched distribution speed, road security, and conduit reliability; sacrifices unique product innovation.
