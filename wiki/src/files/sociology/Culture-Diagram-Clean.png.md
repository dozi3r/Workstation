# Culture-Diagram-Clean.png (Visual Diagram / Cartographic Asset)

<!-- BEGIN_CUSTOM_NOTES -->
### 1. Architectural Purpose & Academic Origin
`sociology/Culture-Diagram-Clean.png` (`128.9 KB`) is a clean, high-contrast reference diagram for [[File:sociology/01_Core_Cultural_Quadrants.md]] (**Module SOC-01**), indexed in [[File:sociology/README.md]]. Based on **Cameron & Quinn's (1999) Competing Values Framework**, it labels the four organizational culture quadrants using their functional governance descriptors: **Community (`Clan`)**, **Entrepreneurial (`Adhocracy`)**, **Bureaucratic (`Hierarchy`)**, and **Competitive (`Market`)**.

### 2. Mechanical Translation in Creator Studio
- Provides the visual archetype labels and core tension axes (`More Flexible` vs. `More Stable`; `Internal Focus` vs. `External Focus`) adapted into the ASCII diagram in Section 1 of [[File:sociology/01_Core_Cultural_Quadrants.md]] and [[File:sociology/SOCIOLOGY_MASTER_COMPENDIUM.md]].
- Directly underpins the `[S, E]` quadrant derivation in [[File:sociology/06_Disposition_Matrix_and_Tarot_Decision_Framework.md]] and [[File:tarot/generate_tarot_matrix.js]], and is included in the composite master graphic [[File:sociology/00 sociology all.png]] (see [[Series:series_c_sociology_and_tarot_hermeneutics]]).
