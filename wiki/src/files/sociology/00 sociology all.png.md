### 1. Architectural Purpose & Visual Synthesis
`sociology/00 sociology all.png` (`1.66 MB`) is the **Consolidated Master Reference Graphic** for **Volume VI: Sociology Hermeneutics & Tarot Matrix Engine**. Cataloged in [[File:sociology/README.md]], it combines all seven constituent academic organizational design diagrams into a single high-resolution composite sheet used during the design of the 3-dial `[S, E, C]` Organizational Disposition Vector ([[File:sociology/06_Disposition_Matrix_and_Tarot_Decision_Framework.md]]) and the Tarot Sociology Matrix ([[File:sociology/08_Tarot_Sociology_Matrix.md]]).

- **Competing Values Framework (Cameron & Quinn, 1999)**: Maps *Flexibility vs. Stability* (`S` dial) against *Internal vs. External Focus* (`E` dial) to produce `Clan`, `Adhocracy`, `Hierarchy`, and `Market` (individual plates: [[File:sociology/4_culture_types.png]], [[File:sociology/Culture-Diagram-Clean.png]], and [[File:sociology/1-s2.png]]).
- **Strategic Operations Alignment (Porter & Groysberg)**: Maps *Flexibility vs. Stability* (`S` dial) against *Independence vs. Interdependence* (`C` dial) to produce `Design`, `Service`, `Produce`, and `Deliver` (individual plate: [[File:sociology/https3A2F2Fblogs-imag4es.png]]).
- **Organizational Design & Strategic Focus (Daft, 2017)**: Aligns cultural postures with environmental demands (individual plate: [[File:sociology/Culture-and-organization-design.png]]).
- **Environmental Uncertainty Grid (Duncan, 1972)**: Crosses *Environmental Complexity* with *Environmental Change* to derive Uncertainty Tiers `U = 1..4` (individual plate: [[File:sociology/1520159723101.png]]).
