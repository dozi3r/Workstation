### Academic & Architectural Provenance
`sociology/duncan_environmental_uncertainty_matrix.png` (formerly `1520159723101.png`, remediated under `[[Ticket:SYS-AUDIT-01]]`) provides Robert Duncan's classic 1972 **Environmental Uncertainty Matrix** as applied to tabletop pointcrawl biomes.

### Theoretical Foundations
The diagram models organizational vulnerability and institutional adaptation along two axes:
1. **Simple vs. Complex (Environmental Domain)**: Number of external elements interacting with the settlement (monocultural agricultural outpost vs. cosmopolitan trade nexus).
2. **Static vs. Dynamic (Rate of Environmental Change)**: Stability of biological, geopolitical, and magical threats (stable climate vs. unpredictable disaster fronts and shifting faction frontiers).

### Pointcrawl Engine Application
* **Low Uncertainty (Simple + Static)**: Mechanistic, bureaucratic structures; low-level watch, predictable agricultural rhythm.
* **Moderate Uncertainty (Complex + Static)**: Standard division of labor; specialized guilds and legal frameworks.
* **Moderate Uncertainty (Simple + Dynamic)**: Rapid response militias; decentralized survivalist decision-making.
* **High Uncertainty (Complex + Dynamic)**: Organic, highly flexible governance; emergency war councils, esoteric crisis response teams.
