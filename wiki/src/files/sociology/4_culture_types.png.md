# 4_culture_types.png (Visual Diagram / Cartographic Asset)

<!-- BEGIN_CUSTOM_NOTES -->
### 1. Architectural Purpose & Academic Origin
`sociology/4_culture_types.png` (`28.2 KB`) is a primary visual reference diagram for [[File:sociology/01_Core_Cultural_Quadrants.md]] (**Module SOC-01**), indexed in [[File:sociology/README.md]]. It illustrates **Kim Cameron and Robert Quinn's (1999) Competing Values Framework (CVF)**, showing the 2x2 spatial grid formed by crossing **Flexibility & Discretion vs. Stability & Control** (`Axis Y`) with **Internal Focus & Integration vs. External Focus & Differentiation** (`Axis X`).
