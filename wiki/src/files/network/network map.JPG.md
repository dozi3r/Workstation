## Cartographic Evolution: Phase 1 (Conceptual Inception)
- **Visual Artifact Description**: An early hand-drawn and annotated conceptual map of the 22-node network.
- **Topological Significance**: Established the spatial clustering of regional settlements, identifying initial choke points, maritime coastlines, and mountain barriers.
- **Conduit Formalization**: Contains the first informal arrows indicating hegemonic dominance and trade migration, later formalized into `infBy` and `adj` arrays in [[File:network/generic_network_topology.json]].
- **Superseded By**: Refined digitally in [[File:network/network.png]], [[File:network/network v2.png]], [[File:network/network v3.png]], and ultimately implemented as an interactive vector graph in [[File:network/campaign_studio.html]].
