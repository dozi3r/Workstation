## Historical Conversion & Constitutional Crisis Mechanics
The module formalizes the political breakdown between Julius Caesar and the Roman Senate into playable tabletop dynamics:
1. **Constitutional Legitimacy vs. Martial Speed**: The spatial network models the Apennine divide: Caesar commands the rapid transit vectors of the Via Flaminia with Legio XIII Gemina, while Pompey and the Optimates command the maritime logistical hubs of Brundisium and eastern provincial grain routes.
2. **The Aerarium Saturni Vault Dynamic**: Models the historic abandonment of the state treasury in the Temple of Saturn when the consuls fled Rome in panic, leaving the Republic's emergency reserve gold (*aerarium sanctius*) unguarded.
3. **Escalation Output Dials**:
   - **Level 1 (Light Tier / DC 10)**: Constitutional Legalism & Curia Filibusters.
   - **Level 2 (Moderate Tier / DC 13 / 2d8)**: *Senatus Consultum Ultimum* & Emergency Militia Levies.
   - **Level 3 (Severe Tier / DC 16 / 4d10)**: Crossing the River Boundary & Patrician Baggage Flight.
   - **Level 4 (Lethal Climax / DC 20 / 8d12)**: Total Republic Succession Collapse & Street Gladiator Warfare.
4. **Threat Clocks**:
   - *Caesar's Rapid Column Advance* (4 Ticks): Advances on delayed dispatches or unfortified outposts.
   - *Senate Evacuation & Panic* (6 Ticks): Advances on failed rhetorical or diplomatic interventions.
   - *Subura Famine & Bread Riots* (6 Ticks): Advances whenever maritime grain deliveries from Ostia are halted.

## Dramatic 4-Corner Opposition (Historiographical Matrix)
- **1. Establishment / Hegemony**: *The Senatorial Optimates & Pompey* (constitutional legalism, *mos maiorum*, procedural paralysis).
- **2. Main Opponent / Challenger**: *Julius Caesar & Legio XIII Gemina* (*dignitas*, plebeian tribunes, lightning forced-march mobility).

- **4. Tertiary Outcast / Victim**: *The Disenfranchised Plebeians & Slaves* (Subura bread dependence, debt vulnerability, riot potential).
