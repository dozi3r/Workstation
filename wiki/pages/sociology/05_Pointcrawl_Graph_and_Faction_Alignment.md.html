1. Architectural Purpose & Role

sociology/05_Pointcrawl_Graph_and_Faction_Alignment.md (Module SOC-05) is the canonical, setting-agnostic methodology for mapping pointcrawl network topology ($G = (V, E)$) to organizational sociology. It supersedes the earlier setting-coupled file 📄 sociology/05_Node_and_Faction_Mapping.md (tracked under 🎫 SYS-AUDIT-01 and 🧭 Series G: Repository Hygiene Audit: Build-Time Mirrors, Legacy Scratch & Orphan Remediation).

By uniting CVF Cultural Quadrants (📄 sociology/01_Core_Cultural_Quadrants.md), Operational Profiles (📄 sociology/02_Operational_Profiles_and_Power.md), and Five-Color Ideological Axes (White, Blue, Black, Red, Green), SOC-05 provides deterministic rules for assigning institutional behaviors to nodes based on their spatial role in the graph.

3. Step-by-Step Topological Alignment Workflow

1. Node Elevation & Spatial Classification:

  • Perimeter / Gate Nodes $\to$ Hierarchy (White) to regulate entry traffic and quarantine external threats.
  • Energy / Industrial Generation Nodes $\to$ Market / Adhocracy (Blue / Black) driven by technical insight and extraction quotas.
  • Wilderness / Canopy Refuges $\to$ Clan (Green) where survival depends on mutual trust and ecological attunement.
  • Maritime / Smuggling Anchorages $\to$ Adhocracy / Clan (Red) governed by fluid prize-sharing pacts.

2. Edge Tension Inference:

  • Edges linking Hierarchy $\leftrightarrow$ Market nodes generate tariff disputes, smuggling arbitrage, and jurisdictional friction.
  • Edges linking Clan $\leftrightarrow$ Hierarchy nodes generate conscription resistance, tax evasion, and border skirmishes.

3. Dynamic Crisis Shifts: Integrates with 📄 sociology/03_Environmental_Uncertainty_and_Crisis.md so severe famine fractures Hierarchy nodes into cutthroat Market warlords, while existential external sieges compress feuding Clans into martial Hierarchy.
