Summary: BUILD SUITE - Centralized Asset Compiler, Zero-CORS Bundler & Agent Navigation Engine Authority: ADR-0001, ADR-0002, docs/navigation_manifest.json Functions:

The workstation registry emits compact launcher summaries (summary) and lightweight metadata into start.html and network/platform_audit_dashboard.html while externalizing each campaign's deduplicated preset and creatureEvidence payload to campaigns//campaign_bundle.js for zero-CORS on-demand tags via .replace(/<\/script>/gi, '<\\/script>') and avoids regex $ dollar sign replacement hazards using functional replacers () => replacement.

  • Build-Time Mirror Note (🎫 SYS-AUDIT-01): Lines 487–494 write an identical mirror of 📄 network/platform_audit_dashboard.html to 📄 tickets/creative/platform_audit_visualizer.html, tracked under Category A of 🧭 Series G: Repository Hygiene Audit: Build-Time Mirrors, Legacy Scratch & Orphan Remediation.
  • Module Prose & Industry/Resource Enrichment (Entity Explorer Overhaul): parseModuleMarkdownFile, enrichModuleEntities, and applyResourceConsumerIndex extract YAML and prose metadata (dials, custom output_dial_key, threat_clocks, node_prerequisites, mechanical_architecture, extraction_lore, four_corners), synthesize grounded prototype lore/method/handling text without boilerplate strings, classify resource vs. industry prototypes cleanly, and resolve cross-module downstream consumer links.

    • Operational Verification

    To execute asset compilation and verify zero-CORS compliance:

    powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Build-Suite.ps1
