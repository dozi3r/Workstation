1. Architectural Context & Single Source of Truth Invariant

Ground Rule #2 of 📄 AGENTS.md establishes the Single Source of Truth Invariant: every topological graph, Tarot deck, sociological matrix, and worldbuilding module must have a single canonical authority on disk, while downstream HTML distributions are compiled deterministically via 📄 Build-Suite.ps1.

During the full repository inventory conducted for the Technical Manual & Bidirectional Codebase Wiki, 📄 wiki/build_wiki.js scanned every file in the workspace and cross-referenced byte hashes, compiler write targets, and inbound link graphs. That audit (codified in 📄 tickets/system/SYS-AUDIT-01_duplicate_mirror_and_orphan_file_remediation.md and building upon the earlier 📄 docs/audit/2026-10-creator-studio-audit-dossier.md) identified four distinct categories of duplicate mirrors, overlapping drafts, stale outputs, and legacy .scratch/ scripts.

To protect backwards compatibility prior to destructive file deletion, 📄 wiki/build_wiki.js maintains an explicit AUDIT_FLAGS registry (lines 224–300) that automatically badges every affected file's wiki page and generates bidirectional links to its canonical counterpart and 🎫 SYS-AUDIT-01.
