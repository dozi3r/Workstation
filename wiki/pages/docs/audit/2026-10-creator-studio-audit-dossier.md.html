Summary: The **Creator Studio** is a tabletop campaign authoring workstation designed to generate procedural pointcrawl networks, sociological faction profiles, and modular worldbuilding components.

Architectural Role & Subsystem Context

docs/audit/2026-10-creator-studio-audit-dossier.md is the Foundational October 2026 System Audit & Assessment Dossier within Volume I: System Architecture, Governance & Ontology. Authored by the DeepMind Antigravity Advanced Agentic Team, this 300-line forensic report diagnosed the prototype Creator Studio across five architectural dimensions, uncovered the critical 26-link vs. 52-link Adjacency Smothering divergence between the browser UI and PowerShell CLI, audited all 48 Opal Island modules to discover the hidden industrial supply-chain graph, and decomposed the remediation into the 12 tracer-bullet tickets stored in .scratch/creator-remediation/issues/.

It is the historical precursor to 🎫 SYS-AUDIT-01 and is cross-referenced throughout 🧭 Series A: Pointcrawl Topological Mathematics & Network Centrality, 🧭 Series D: The 5-Tier Module Pipeline, Resource Contracts (provides/requires) & Fallbacks, 🧭 Series E: Zero-CORS Offline Bundling, Compass Headers & Self-Updating Wiki Architecture, and 🧭 Series G: Repository Hygiene Audit: Build-Time Mirrors, Legacy Scratch & Orphan Remediation.

Core Audit Findings & Architectural Scorecard

The dossier evaluated the pre-remediation repository across five dimensions:

  • 1. Runtime Seams & Zero-CORS (Grade: B- -> Remediated via 📄 build_suite.js):
  • Identified the Multiple Source of Truth Hazard where the 22 nodes and 52 links were copy-pasted across 📄 network/generic_network_topology.json, 📄 network/Run-NetworkEngine.ps1, 📄 network/campaign_studio.html, and 📄 network/network_visualizer.html.
  • Identified that network/modules_manifest.json previously started with const MODULES_MANIFEST = [ (invalid pure JSON) and contained raw prompt provenance strings (MyActivity.html) in ESOT-04, ESOT-05, and ESOT-06.
  • 2. Algorithmic & Mathematical Parity (Grade: C+ -> Remediated via Tickets 02 & 03):
  • Uncovered the Critical Adjacency Smothering Divergence: CANONICAL_LINKS_DATA in campaign_studio.html previously defined only 26 links instead of the full 52 directed links in 📄 network/generic_network_topology.json (missing links between Node 1 and Nodes 2, 6, and 20), causing getNodeAdjacents() in the browser to fail to smother adjacent hub candidates that PowerShell (Run-NetworkEngine.ps1) properly smothered.
  • Identified amalgamation rounding divergence (Math.round(... * 10) / 10 1-decimal in JS vs. [Math]::Round(..., 2) 2-decimal in PowerShell) and non-deterministic tie-breaking in b.score - a.score.
  • 3. 48-Module Dependency & Typology Audit (Grade: C -> Remediated via 📄 docs/adr/0002-dual-srd-and-tiered-module-architecture.md):
  • Provided the Master 48-Module Diagnostic Matrix (Section 4.1) classifying every module (INFRA-01..ESOT-06) into its true 5-Tier Worldbuilding Pipeline tier (Tier 1..Tier 5), extracting explicit provides and requires tags, and proving that FACT-01, FACT-02, and FACT-04 are macro system rules while EDGE-01..EDGE-05 are edge transit corridors.
  • Mapped the core Industrial Defense Ecosystem supply chain: High-Head Water $\to$ 📦 INFRA-01 (Pelton Wheel, 60V DC 24kW) $\to$ 📦 INFRA-02 (Arc Smelter, CaC2 Pellets) $\to$ 📦 INFRA-03 (Argent-Fume Smokewall repelling 📦 ECOL-01 Stirge Swarms) and 📦 INFRA-06 (RF Radios).
  • 4. Portability & Setting Agnosticism (Grade: B -> Remediated via Tickets 01, 05 & 11):
  • Flagged stale hardcoded file:///c:/Users/Milo/Documents/Antigravity/Creator/... links in 📄 README.md and 📄 modules/README.md and recommended extracting setting canon into network/presets/.
  • 5. Specifications & SRD Structure (Grade: A- -> Codified in Volume II):
  • Established the Dual SRD architecture (📄 docs/srd/authoring_engine_srd.md and 📄 docs/srd/adventure_framework_srd.md).
