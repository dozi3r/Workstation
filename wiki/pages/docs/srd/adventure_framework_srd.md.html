Architectural Role & Subsystem Context

docs/srd/adventure_framework_srd.md is the System-Agnostic Tabletop Runtime System Reference Document (SRD) within Volume II: System Reference Documents (The Dual SRD & Substratum). Created under 📄 docs/adr/0002-dual-srd-and-tiered-module-architecture.md and 📄 .scratch/creator-remediation/issues/07-universal-adventure-framework-srd.md, it forms the second half of the Dual SRD architecture alongside 📄 docs/srd/authoring_engine_srd.md. Whereas the Authoring Engine SRD governs compile-time and studio-time network mathematics, the Universal Adventure Framework SRD defines the lightweight tabletop procedures—Threat Clocks, Universal Hazard & Difficulty Tiers, Logistical Supply Units, Clock-Driven Supply Severance Degradation, Crisis Gate Windows, and the Multi-Campaign Sourcebook Ecosystem—referenced by every worldbuilding module in campaigns/*/modules/ and tickets/creative/backlog/.

Core Tabletop Runtime Invariants & Contracts

Every worldbuilding module (injected_mechanics) and creative writing ticket (CWT-, T0-DOM-) must adhere to the mechanical standards codified in this SRD:

1. Universal Hazard & Difficulty Severity Matrix (Section 3 & 7.3)

To remain interoperable across d20 (D&D 5e, Knave, OSR), 2d6 (PbtA, Traveller), and dice-pool (Blades in the Dark / FitD) systems without embedding proprietary stat blocks, all checks and hazards use four canonical severity tiers mapped 1:1 to the 4-Level Module Output Dial (Section 7.1):

Dial LevelSeverity Tierd20 DC Equivalent2d6 TargetDamage Die (Avg)Tabletop Consequence Scale
Level 1LightDC 106+1d6 (3 dmg)Minor delay, superficial injury, routine friction
Level 2ModerateDC 138+2d8 (9 dmg)Moderate wound, resource depletion, +1 Threat Tick
Level 3SevereDC 1610+4d10 (22 dmg)Crippling injury, equipment shattered, node lockdown
Level 4LethalDC 2012+8d12 (52 dmg)Instant downing/death, permanent structural ruin / Climax Trigger

2. Segmented Threat Clock Engine (Section 2 & 7.2)

  • 4-Tick Clock ([ ][ ][ ][ ]): Imminent tactical crises, stealth alarms, or supply severance degradation.
  • 6-Tick Clock ([ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ]): Operational or node-wide environmental shifts.
  • 8-Tick Clock ([ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ]): Macro-geopolitical mobilization or campaign climax countdowns.
  • Tick Triggers: Failed checks / soft moves (+1), hazardous corridor traversal (+1), or per-turn supply/fuel neglect (+1). Players can reverse clocks by expending abstract supply units (Fuel Casks, Silver Vouchers) or completing sabotage/relief objectives.

3. Campaign Turn Economy, Q2 Supply Severance & Q3 Crisis Gate Window (Section 4, 5 & 8)

  • Campaign Turn Unit: 1 Turn = 1 Travel Watch (8 Hours) or 1 Long Rest.
  • Clock-Driven Supply Severance Degradation (Q2 Standard, Section 8.3): Severing a physical transit corridor ($E_{adj}$) does not cause instant binary ruin; instead, downstream dependent modules advance a 4-Segment Threat Clock at +1 Tick per Campaign Turn:
  • Tick 0/4: Reserves Holding (normal operation).
  • Tick 1/4: Depleted Reserves (-1 mechanical yield/advantage).
  • Tick 2/4: Operational Strain (rationing, +1 regional peril modifier).
  • Tick 3/4: Critical Distress (infrastructure buckling, secondary stress on neighbors).
  • Tick 4/4: Threat Threshold reached $\to$ activates Dial 8: Climax Trigger Protocol.
  • Climax Trigger Protocol (Q3 Standard, Section 8.4):
  • Mode A (Crisis Gate Window — Default): Reaching 4/4 opens a mandatory 1-Session Heroic Crisis Window. Heroic completion reduces the clock by -2 Ticks (back to 2/4); neglect or failure results in permanent Level 4 ruin.
  • Mode B (Hardcore Cataclysm): Immediate, irreversible Level 4 collapse at 4/4.

4. Modular Campaign Sourcebook Ecosystem (Q1 Architecture, Section 10)

Defines the campaigns// package standard (campaign.json, preset.json, modules_manifest.json, modules/) supporting both Isolated Mode (single setting) and Aggregated Multi-Book Mode with cross-campaign Weighted Affinity Scoring (+10 Tarot, +5 Culture Match, -5 Culture Clash, +3 Color Match).

Data Flow & Subsystem Consumers

  • Module Regex Extraction: Stage 3 of 📄 build_suite.js scans module Markdown files for the [N Ticks] Clock syntax and Level 1..4 Dial syntax defined in this SRD to populate CAMPAIGN_REGISTRY and 📄 network/platform_audit_dashboard.html.
  • Wiki Metadata Extraction: 📄 wiki/build_wiki.js (lines 520–530) parses Threat Clocks and 4-Level Dials from every module and ticket page using this SRD's formatting rules.

Mechanical Contracts, Dials & Real-World Reference Data

Reference Foundations & Design Provenance (For GM / Downstream Expansion)

  • Real-World Engineering: Lester Allan Pelton impulse water turbine (1870s high-head hydro-electric generation) & early direct-current dynamos.
  • Historical Metallurgy: Enkomi & oxhide copper ingot smelting monopolies of Late Bronze Age Cyprus.
  • Indus Valley Civilization: Mohenjo-daro & Dholavira standardized baked-brick municipal sewers and Ghaggar-Hakra monsoon river desiccation.
  • Tabletop & CRPG Design Classics: Might & Magic III: Isles of Terra (1991), B2 Keep on the Borderlands, and X1 The Isle of Dread.

Systemic Escalation Dials (Levels 1–4)

  • Level 1 (Dormant / Folklore / Marginal)**: Baseline operational state, rustic rumor, or minimal energy. Routine difficulty checks (Light Tier).
  • Level 2 (Standard / Operational / Supervised)**: Default active condition. Reliable output with moderate environmental side effects (Moderate Tier).
  • Level 3 (Overdriven / Exposed / Unstable)**: Surge capacity or political revelation. Doubled outputs accompanied by hazardous fallout, active opposition, or severe risk (Severe Tier).
  • Level 4 (Catastrophic / Eschatological / Meltdown)**: Structural failure, bio-weapon activation, or total revolt. Triggers immediate Lethal Tier hazard consequences.
