Architectural Role & Subsystem Context

docs/srd/authoring_engine_srd.md is the Primary Mathematical & Algorithmic System Reference Document (SRD) within Volume II: System Reference Documents (The Dual SRD & Substratum). Established under 📄 docs/adr/0002-dual-srd-and-tiered-module-architecture.md and 📄 .scratch/creator-remediation/issues/06-authoring-engine-srd.md, it codifies the formal graph theory, 64-cell sociological algebra, 7-wave Major Arcana balancing protocol, Amalgamation Score formula, Adjacency Smothering algorithm, Living Campaign fog-of-war subgraph rules, 5-Tier socket capacities, weighted module Recommendation Scoring, and BFS supply-flow reachability algebra that govern 📄 network/campaign_studio.html, 📄 network/network_builder.html, 📄 network/network_visualizer.html, 📄 network/contradiction_matrix_dashboard.html, 📄 network/platform_audit_dashboard.html, and 📄 network/Run-NetworkEngine.ps1.

It is the core specification for 🧭 Series A: Pointcrawl Topological Mathematics & Network Centrality and 🧭 Series D: The 5-Tier Module Pipeline, Resource Contracts (provides/requires) & Fallbacks, and pairs with 📄 docs/srd/adventure_framework_srd.md (which governs tabletop runtime play).

Core Mathematical Invariants & Contracts

Any modification to 📄 src/campaign_studio/scripts/stage_4_balancing.js, 📄 src/campaign_studio/scripts/stage_5_slotting.js, or 📄 network/Run-NetworkEngine.ps1 must strictly preserve the mathematical contracts defined in this SRD:

SectionFormal Rule / EquationInvariant & Implementation Contract
Section 2: Pointcrawl Graph $G = (V, E_{adj}, E_{inf})$22 nodes ($1 \le i \le 22$), 52 directed adjacency pairings (26 bidirectional corridors in $E_{adj}$), directed influence vectors $E_{inf}$.Dual-layer edge routing (`layer: "transit""hegemony""both"): transit governs $E_{adj}$ (movement, BFS supply flow, Adjacency Smothering); hegemony governs $E_{inf}$ (Amalgamation Score). Nodes 12, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, and 22 are hardcoded Eligible = false`.
Section 3: [S, E, C] Sociological AlgebraOrganizational Disposition Vector $[S, E, C] \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}^3$ yielding 64 archetypes across the 4 CVF Quadrants (Clan, Adhocracy, Market, Hierarchy).Maps 1:1 to 📄 sociology/06_Disposition_Matrix_and_Tarot_Decision_Framework.md and 📄 sociology/07_Granular_64_Disposition_Matrix.md.
Section 4.1–4.3: Amalgamation Formula$\text{BaseWeight}(K, O) = \pm(K + 1)$ for $K \in [0, 21]$; $\text{IncomingScore}(i) = \sum_{s \in InfBy(i)} 0.5 \times \text{BaseWeight}(s)$; $\text{TotalScore}(i) = \text{Round}(\text{BaseWeight}(i) + \text{IncomingScore}(i), 2)$.Must round strictly to 2 decimal places (Math.round(val * 100) / 100 in JS; [Math]::Round(..., 2) in PowerShell).
Section 4.4: Adjacency SmotheringSort eligible candidates by descending TotalScore, tie-breaking by ascending ID. Elect up to 5 Sovereign Hubs $\mathcal{H}$; any candidate $c$ adjacent in $E_{adj}$ to an already-elected $h \in \mathcal{H}$ is smothered ($c \in \mathcal{S}$).Identical candidate ordering and smothering across 📄 src/campaign_studio/scripts/stage_4_balancing.js and 📄 network/Run-NetworkEngine.ps1.
Section 4.5–4.6: Color Wheel & Card 22Ranks 1–5 map to White, Blue, Black, Red, Green. Card 22 Reversed triggers Global Inversion.Evaluated at completion of Wave 7 (or when Node 22 is revealed in Living Campaign mode).
Section 5: Living Campaign Subgraph ($R \subseteq V$)When fogOfWarMode = true, all balancing formulas evaluate exclusively over the revealed node set $R$ (InfBy(i) \cap R).Default Known Anchors are Nodes 1, 2, 6, and 20. When $R = V$, results match Omniscient mode and Run-NetworkEngine.ps1 identically.
Section 6–7: Socket Allocation & Recommendation Score $RS(m, v)$Sovereign Hub = 4 sockets; Satellite = 3 sockets; Hazard/Enclave = 2 sockets; Transit Corridor = 1–2 sockets. $RS(m, v) = 3\mathbb{I}_{\text{Tarot}} + 2\mathbb{I}_{\text{Culture}} + 1\mathbb{I}_{\text{Color}} + 3\mathbb{I}_{\text{SupplyIn}} + 2\mathbb{I}_{\text{SupplyOut}} - 5\mathbb{I}_{\text{Slotted}}$.Recommends curated Module Bundles (Carbochemical Defense Complex 📦 INFRA-01+📦 INFRA-02+📦 INFRA-03, Ordnance Foundry, Resonant Telecom Grid, Glacial Sanctuary).
Section 8: Supply Reachability & Severance$\text{Supplied}(v) = \text{LocalYields}(v) \cup \bigcup_{u \in \text{Reachable}(v, k)} \text{LocalYields}(u)$ ($k = 3$ hops over unblocked $E_{adj}$).Unmet material requires tags place module $m$ into Severed Supply Status, activating its standalone_fallback mode.
