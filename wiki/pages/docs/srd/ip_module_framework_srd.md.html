Architectural Role & Subsystem Context

docs/srd/ip_module_framework_srd.md is the Universal Cross-Domain Ingestion & IP Translation Standard within Volume II: System Reference Documents (The Dual SRD & Substratum). Functioning as the "Rosetta Stone" of Creator Studio under 📄 docs/adr/0001-dual-mode-preset-architecture.md and 📄 docs/adr/0002-dual-srd-and-tiered-module-architecture.md, it specifies how any external Intellectual Property (historical eras, shonen anime, sci-fi universes, or classic tabletop modules) is translated into system-agnostic, mechanically interoperable pointcrawl modules without prose bloat or ruleset lock-in.

It is the primary theoretical and practical foundation for 🧭 Series F: Authoring Custom Campaign Presets, IP Conversions & Scenario Primitives, 📄 templates/IP_MODULE_TEMPLATE.md, and the three canonical cross-domain reference exemplars:

  • Historical Exemplar: 📄 templates/examples/HIST-01_The_Crossing_of_the_Rubicon.md (Julius Caesar Crossing the Rubicon, 49 BC).
  • Anime / Sci-Fi Exemplar: 📄 templates/examples/DBZ-01_The_Siege_of_Planet_Namek.md (Dragon Ball Z: The Siege of Planet Namek).
  • Classic Tabletop Exemplar: 📄 templates/examples/DND-01_The_Mists_of_Barovia.md (Ravenloft / Curse of Strahd: The Mists of Barovia).

Core Invariants & The 7-Component Translation Contract

When translating any setting or IP element into a Creator Studio module, the author must map narrative lore into seven structured components:

ComponentSchema Field / SectionStructural Rule & Cross-Domain Examples
1. Pipeline Tier Classificationpipeline_tier: 1..5Tier 1 (Macro Governance: Roman Lex Cornelia, Frieza Force Quotas, Barovian Mist Lockdown); Tier 2 (Biome/Corridor: Rubicon River Barrier, Namek Coral Archipelago, Old Svalich Road); Tier 3 (Supply Chain: Ostia Grain Silos, Scouter Relay Grid, Wizard of Wines); Tier 4 (Sparks/Clocks: Plebeian Bread Riots, Namekian Village Cull, Feast of St. Andral); Tier 5 (Climax Engine: Dictator Apotheosis, Namek 5-Minute Core Detonation, Heart of Sorrow).
2. Pointcrawl Spatial Anchorhub_eligibility (candidate / never / always)Determines whether the location can win Tarot wave elections (candidate), is a dependent satellite/hazard (never), or locks a Sovereign Hub slot (always), plus 1-Way ($u \to v$) vs. 2-Way ($u \leftrightarrow v$) tethering.
3. Sociological CVF & Tarot Affinityculture_quadrant, operational_profile, mtg_color, tarot_affinityMaps faction culture to Hierarchy (Control / White), Adhocracy (Create / Blue-Red), Market (Compete / Black), or Clan (Collaborate / Green).
4. Typed Resource Contractsprovides: [...], requires: [...]Machine-readable [Category]: [Specific-Asset-Name] strings across Power, Material, Bio, Tech, Authority, Labor, and Climax.
5. 4-Level Output Dial & Clocksinjected_mechanics.output_dial, threat_clocksScales across Level 1 (Light DC 10 / 1d6), Level 2 (Moderate DC 13 / 2d8), Level 3 (Severe DC 16 / 4d10), and Level 4 (Lethal DC 20 / 8d12), paired with 4/6/8-tick Threat Clocks (📄 docs/srd/adventure_framework_srd.md).
6. 4-Corner Dramatic Opposition WebMarkdown Section 2 (four_corners)Pits Establishment (The Power), Main Opponent (The Challenger), Thematic Shadow (The Profiteer), and Tertiary Outcast (The Victim / Pariah) in irreconcilable conflict.
7. Standalone Fallback Invariantstandalone_fallbackMandatory narrative/mechanical clause explaining how the module operates if severed from external supply edges or run as an isolated one-shot.

Data Flow & Execution Pipeline

1. Authoring: Worldbuilders or AI agents copy 📄 templates/IP_MODULE_TEMPLATE.md into campaigns//modules/ and populate the 7 components defined in this SRD.

2. Validation: 📄 campaigns/Test-CampaignPackage.ps1 verifies frontmatter conformance against this SRD.

3. Compilation & UI Ingestion: 📄 build_suite.js (Stage 3) parses the Markdown body to extract threat_clocks, dials, and four_corners, injecting them into CAMPAIGN_REGISTRY for live filtering, slotting, and reader inspection in 📄 network/campaign_studio.html and 📄 network/platform_audit_dashboard.html.

AI Agent Maintenance Checklist

  • When converting an external IP or authoring new campaign modules: Consult docs/srd/ip_module_framework_srd.md alongside 📄 templates/IP_MODULE_TEMPLATE.md and 🧭 Series F: Authoring Custom Campaign Presets, IP Conversions & Scenario Primitives.
  • Never invent ungrounded generic fantasy filler: Ground every module in concrete institutional sociology, material supply chains (provides / requires), and 4-corner dramatic opposition.

Mechanical Contracts, Dials & Real-World Reference Data

Reference Foundations & Design Provenance (For GM / Downstream Expansion)

  • Real-World Engineering: Lester Allan Pelton impulse water turbine (1870s high-head hydro-electric generation) & early direct-current dynamos.
  • Historical Archaeology: 1177 BC Late Bronze Age Collapse (tin/copper trade network severance, palace economy cascade failure).
  • IP Conversion Exemplar: Dragon Ball Z (Frieza/Namek Saga) translated into a 7-MacGuffin asymmetric triangulation race and planetary destruction clock.
  • IP Conversion Exemplar: Ravenloft (I6 / Curse of Strahd) translated into a closed mist-border gothic domain with an omnipresent apex predator.
  • Historical Campaign Exemplar: Julius Caesar crossing the Rubicon (49 BC) — rapid legionary blitzkrieg vs. senatorial treasury evacuation.

Truby 4-Corner Opposition Matrix

RoleFaction / Actor Vector
EstablishmentThe entrenched authority defending the current order.
OpponentThe active revolutionary, invader, or warlord seeking disruption.
ShadowThe corrupt merchant, mercenary, or parasite exploiting the clash.
OutcastThe collateral bystander, forgotten minority, or subterranean exile caught in between.

Systemic Escalation Dials (Levels 1–4)

  • Level 1 (Dormant / Routine)**: Baseline passive state. Low difficulty checks (**Light Tier: DC 10 / 1d6 damage**).
  • Level 2 (Operational / Supervised)**: Default active throughput. Moderate friction (**Moderate Tier: DC 13 / 2d8 damage**).
  • Level 3 (Overdriven / Crisis)**: Surge production or public discovery. Dangerous fallout (**Severe Tier: DC 16 / 4d10 damage**).
  • Level 4 (Catastrophic / Eschatological Meltdown)**: Total containment breach or collapse (**Lethal Tier: DC 20 / 8d12 damage**).
