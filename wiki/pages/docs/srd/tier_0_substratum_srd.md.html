Architectural Role & Subsystem Context

docs/srd/tier_0_substratum_srd.md is the Authoritative System Reference Document Governing Mundane Settlement Survival, Caloric Carrying Capacity, and the Dual-Mode Tier 0 Substratum within Volume II: System Reference Documents (The Dual SRD & Substratum). While Tiers 1–5 of 📄 docs/adr/0002-dual-srd-and-tiered-module-architecture.md model macro-governance, biomes, industrial alchemy, social dilemmas, and eschatological climaxes, Tier 0 establishes the material baseline (food, water, vernacular shelter, parish watch, barter, leechcraft, sanitation, hearth vigils) that prevents "phantom starvation" in pointcrawl networks.

It serves as the primary specification for 📄 tickets/unified_ticket_tracker.html, 📄 tickets/tier_0/README.md, 📄 tickets/tier_0/tier_0_manifest.json, and the 8 Tier 0 domain modules (T0-DOM-01 through T0-DOM-08 in tickets/tier_0/tickets/).

Core Invariants & Architectural Contracts

1. The Dual-Mode UX Architecture (enable_tier_0_substratum)

To balance fast high-concept worldbuilding against gritty logistical survival simulation, Tier 0 enforces a two-mode switch in 📄 network/campaign_studio.html and 📄 network/Run-NetworkEngine.ps1:

  • Mode A: Smooth Generic ("Checkbox Through" / enable_tier_0_substratum: true):
  • Every settlement node (Hub, Settlement, Outpost) automatically receives implicit baseline resources: Food: Baseline-Subsistence-Rations, Water: Baseline-Potable-Flow, and Shelter: Baseline-Vernacular-Hearth.
  • Consumes zero socket slots and suppresses mundane starvation deficits so authors can focus immediately on Tiers 1–5.
  • Mode B: Granular Modularity (Deep Authoring / Survival Core / enable_tier_0_substratum: false):
  • Settlements require explicit Tier 0 (T0-DOM-01..08) or Tier 2/3 food and water providers within $k \le 2$ graph hops.
  • Unmet baseline needs advance active Starvation, Drought, or Unrest Threat Clocks.

3. Pre-Industrial Carrying Capacity & The Agrarian Radius Law (Section 4)

  • 5 Settlement Scale Tiers: Thorp (20–60 pop), Hamlet (60–250 pop, 3 sockets), Village (250–1,000 pop), Market Town (1,000–5,000 pop), and Sovereign Hub (5,000–25,000+ pop, 4 sockets).
  • The 3-Mile Ox-Cart Transport Boundary & Sovereign Hub Deficit Law: Any Sovereign Hub ($>5,000$ pop) runs a structural caloric and hydrological deficit (-2,000 Cal net balance) within its municipal walls and must maintain 2 to 4 unsevered transit edges connecting it to agricultural/hydrological Satellite Nodes (+250 Cal Surplus).

Mechanical Contracts, Dials & Real-World Reference Data

Reference Foundations & Design Provenance (For GM / Downstream Expansion)

  • Historical Metallurgy: Enkomi & oxhide copper ingot smelting monopolies of Late Bronze Age Cyprus.
  • Indus Valley Civilization: Mohenjo-daro & Dholavira standardized baked-brick municipal sewers and Ghaggar-Hakra monsoon river desiccation.

Systemic Escalation Dials (Levels 1–4)

  • Level 1 (Bountiful / Secure / Routine)**: Full root cellars, flowing artesian wells, alert town watch. Checks succeed automatically or require DC 10 Light tests.
  • Level 2 (Normal / Baseline Operation)**: Standard rations (2,000 kcal/day), mild seasonal mud, minor equipment wear. Checks require DC 13 Moderate tests (2d8 damage or minor resource loss).
  • Level 3 (Strained / Seasonal Hunger Gap)**: Spring hunger gap, dry spells, well silt buildup, rations halved to 1,000 kcal/day. Checks require DC 16 Severe tests (4d10 damage, fatigue, unrest checks).
  • Level 4 (Exhausted / Famine / Lockdown)**: Seed-grain eaten, draft animals slaughtered, wells tainted with brine, bread riots erupt. Triggers DC 20 Lethal Climax consequences.

Active Threat Clocks

  • Caloric Exhaustion Clock [4 Ticks]**: Advances each campaign week a settlement is isolated from agricultural satellites; full clock triggers Level 4 Famine riots.
  • Contamination Clock [4 Ticks]**: Advances on neglected maintenance or runoff floods; full clock triggers dysentery outbreaks.
  • Timber Rot Clock [6 Ticks]**: Advances in damp seasons without replacement timber; full clock collapses perimeter defense.
